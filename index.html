Frequently asked questions

Are the solved notes really free?

Yes — completely free. Every solved note is free to read on this website. No download needed, no payment, no sign-up. Just open a book and start reading — come back any time to revise.

Which mediums are available?

Solved notes are available in Sindhi, Urdu, English, Arabic, Punjabi and Farsi mediums, across four boards — Sindh, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan. 381 books for Classes 1–12 are live now, and 297 more are coming soon.

Do the solved notes follow the official textbook?

Yes. Every solved exercise follows the official textbook of its own board — Sindh, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa or Balochistan — chapter by chapter, in the book's own format.

Can I request a book that is not listed?

Yes. Use the contact page to request any board, class, subject or medium. Requested books are prepared first.

What is Solved Books?

Solved Books gives free previews of solved textbook exercises for four boards: Sindh, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan. The catalogue lists 678 books, of which 381 are live to read now and 297 are coming soon. Find your class, subject and medium, open a book, and read the solved exercises on this website without signing up or paying.

How do I read a solved book?

  1. Search by class, subject or medium on this page, or open the catalogue and filter by board, class, subject, medium and status.
  2. Open a live book to reach its preview page.
  3. Scroll through the solved exercises in the preview, and use the zoom buttons to read small writing.

What is inside a solved book preview?

Each preview shows the solved exercises of one book — one board, one class, one subject and one medium — in the book's own format, as a page-by-page PDF preview. Page counts differ by book and are shown on the book's preview page. Every preview carries a note that the exercises were solved with the help of AI models, so answers should be checked before use.

Which books are not ready yet?

297 books in the catalogue are marked Coming Soon, including most of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa list, which has 9 live books so far. A coming-soon book shows its details and a request link on the catalogue page. No date is promised for any coming-soon book.
