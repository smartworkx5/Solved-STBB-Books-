AKU-EB September Examinations Result 2026 Announced — Check by Roll Number & Date of Birth

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 2044 words · 10 min read

The Aga Khan University Examination Board (AKU-EB) has announced the results of its September Examinations 2026. According to the board's own official notice, the results were published on Friday, 6 November 2026, at 11:00 a.m., with marks lists, candidate pass slips and subject-wise score breakdowns going live at the same time on the board's digital examination portal, examinationboard.aku.edu. The September session is the board's second examination session of the year: it is the session in which students clear backlogs, re-appear in papers they did not pass in the annual examinations, or upgrade their grades. This guide explains exactly what was announced, how to check your result by roll number and date of birth step by step, who the September session was for, and what to do next whether you passed, need to re-appear, or want a paper rechecked.

The announcement: AKU-EB September Examinations 2026 result, 6 November 2026

The Aga Khan University Examination Board announced the results of its September Examinations 2026 on Friday, 6 November 2026, at 11:00 a.m. The board's own website carried the notice that the September Examinations 2026 results would be announced at that time, and the announcement was confirmed the same day by result reporting. The results cover both levels the board examines, the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) and the Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC), for candidates who sat the September session.

The September examinations themselves were held from 21 to 26 September 2026, according to the final timetable for the SSC and HSSC September examinations 2026 published on the board's own forms page. That is a short, focused session: unlike the annual examinations, which run for weeks across the country, the September session is a compact sitting for students with specific papers to clear. AKU-EB is a private national examination board, not one of the provincial BISE boards, and it examines SSC and HSSC students through affiliated schools in Karachi, Gilgit, Chitral and other cities. The board says it runs 132 examination centres in more than 60 cities for about 26,000 candidates in its examination cycle, so the September session is a small sitting inside a much larger annual operation. Students should therefore check only the AKU-EB portal, examinationboard.aku.edu, because no BISE portal holds AKU-EB records. Our earlier post on the Karachi Board 11th Class Result 2026 explains how a BISE announcement works, but your AKU-EB roll number will only work on the AKU-EB system.

No overall pass percentage, appeared and passed counts, or position-holder names for the September session were published in the announcement material available at the time of writing, so this post reports none of them. If the board publishes session statistics or a position list later, it will appear through the board's own channels. What is confirmed is the date, the time, the portal, and the exact steps to check your result, and those are set out below.

How to check your AKU-EB September 2026 result online

AKU-EB results are checked online with two pieces of information: your candidate roll number and your date of birth. There is no fee and no CNIC needed to view your result on the portal. Keep your admit card beside you before you start, because the portal asks for the roll number exactly as printed on it, and a single wrong digit will show no record. If the portal is slow in the first hours after the announcement, wait a few minutes and try again rather than re-entering different numbers; heavy traffic on result day is normal and does not mean your result is missing.

Follow these steps in order. First, open the official AKU-EB portal at examinationboard.aku.edu on your phone or computer and click Check Results. Second, stay on the Candidate Results tab, which is the tab for individual students; the other tab, School Results, is for institutions. Third, select the exam from the list, choosing the September Examinations 2026 session you sat, whether SSC or HSSC. Fourth, type your candidate roll number exactly as printed on your admit card and select your date of birth as required. Fifth, press View Results and wait for your result to load, then read every subject's marks carefully and download or print your digital marks slip for your own record. The board also sends hard-copy marksheets and certificates through your institution or to your registered postal address after the online release, so keep the printed slip safe until the original arrives.

  • Official portal: examinationboard.aku.edu, then click Check Results
  • Use the Candidate Results tab, not the School Results tab
  • You need your roll number exactly as printed on your admit card, plus your date of birth
  • Select the September Examinations 2026 session, SSC or HSSC, before you submit
  • Download or print your digital marks slip, and keep it until the hard copy arrives

Who the September session was for

The September session exists for students who did not finish their business in the annual examinations. AKU-EB announced its annual SSC and HSSC results in early August 2026, and the September session, held from 21 to 26 September, gave those students a second chance within the same academic year instead of making them wait for the next annual sitting. That is why the reporting around the announcement describes the session as the route for clearing academic backlogs and upgrading final grades: a student who failed one or two papers in August could re-appear in just those papers in September, and a student who wanted a better grade in a paper could try again.

Clearing a backlog in September matters because it keeps the student on track. A pass in the September session means the student meets the secondary or higher secondary prerequisite for the next step, whether that is admission to Class 11, admission to a college, a university enrolment cycle, or a technical diploma application, without losing an academic year. If you sat the September session, your result is therefore not a footnote to the annual result; it is the result that decides whether you move forward now. Read it subject by subject: a paper you passed in September stands, and only the papers you did not pass need a further plan.

AKU-EB's September session is different from the supplementary or second annual examinations of the provincial boards, which follow their own schedules. Punjab's boards, for example, run their second annual examinations separately, as explained in our guide to the Punjab Boards 11th Class Result 2026. Do not mix the two systems: AKU-EB dates, portals and procedures apply only to AKU-EB candidates, and the board has warned candidates to rely only on its official digital channels and verified communication handles, and to ignore third-party rumours about marks.

Marksheets and rechecking after the result

First, save your result and check it against your expectations. Download or print the digital marks slip from the portal as soon as you have viewed it, and read every subject's marks, not just the total. If any subject's marks look far below what you expected, or if a subject you sat is missing from the slip, note the exact subject and marks before you contact anyone, because a clear note makes the enquiry faster. Your school can confirm the details of your entry, and the board's own channels are the only place to raise a correction.

Second, ask about rechecking only after reading your result carefully. The board provides a rechecking route for candidates who believe a paper was not marked as it should have been, and your school can tell you how to apply and what it costs for this session. Rechecking re-examines the marking of a paper; it does not re-teach the subject, so it is the right step when a mark looks wrong, and the wrong step when the paper simply needs more preparation. Do not pay any agent or third party who offers to change a result for a fee; no genuine result change happens outside the board's own process.

  • Download and print your digital marks slip, and keep it until the hard copy arrives
  • Ask your school about the board's rechecking route if a mark looks wrong
  • Never pay an agent who claims to change a result; only the board's own process counts

Planning your next step after the September result

If you passed everything you sat in September, collect your hard-copy marksheet when your institution issues it and proceed with your admission or enrolment plans at once, because the September result was timed so that successful candidates do not lose the year. The online marks slip is your proof until the original arrives, so keep the printed copy with your admit card and your school records in one folder.

If you still have papers outstanding, ask your school immediately about the next examination opportunity and its registration dates, and start revising those subjects now rather than waiting for the registration notice. Split your time between keeping up with your current class and revising the outstanding subjects, with a teacher's help to prioritise the weakest ones first.
