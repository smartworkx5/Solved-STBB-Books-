BISE Sukkur 11th Class Result 2026 Announced — Check by Roll Number & Gazette

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 2513 words · 13 min read

The Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education Sukkur (BISE Sukkur) has announced the results of the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) Part-I, or 11th class, 1st Annual Examination 2026. The result gazettes are dated Wednesday, 11 November 2026, and are signed by the board's Controller of Examinations, Rizwan Ahmed Memon. They are published on the board's own official website, bisesuksindh.edu.pk, on the Examination Results page. The announcement covers three groups — Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical and Computer Science — for the board's three districts: Sukkur, Khairpur and Ghotki. This guide explains exactly what was announced, how to find your result in the official gazette step by step, the pass figures from the board's own statistics, what the gazette categories and withheld results mean, and what to do next.

The announcement: BISE Sukkur 11th class result 2026, 11 November 2026

BISE Sukkur published the HSC Part-I (11th class) 1st Annual Examination 2026 results as district-wise, group-wise gazettes on its official website. The gazettes for Sukkur district in all three groups are dated 11-11-2026 and carry the signature of Rizwan Ahmed Memon, Controller of Examinations, with the note “SYSTEM GENERATED REPORT”. The same gazettes for Khairpur and Ghotki are listed on the same Examination Results page of the board's website, so the announcement covers the whole board at once: the three groups, Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical and Computer Science, across the three districts the board serves, Sukkur, Khairpur and Ghotki.

This is a gazette-only release. Unlike some boards that run an online roll-number search, BISE Sukkur has published the 11th class result as PDF gazettes that list candidates by seat number. There is no online roll-number lookup form and no SMS code shown on the board's homepage or Results page, so the gazette is the way to check your result. Do not trust any SMS code or portal link passed around on social media for this result; the board's own website is the only source named in this guide.

The Sukkur announcement comes after the other Sindh boards released their 11th class results earlier in the season. The Board of Intermediate Education Karachi announced its HSC Part-I results on 26 October 2026, with Pre-Engineering announced separately on 2 October, as explained in our post on the Karachi Board 11th Class Result 2026. Punjab's nine boards announced together on 22 October 2026, covered in our Punjab Boards 11th Class Result 2026 guide. Each Sindh board announces on its own date, so Sukkur students should follow only the Sukkur board's own gazettes, not another board's dates or links. The board's 12th class result was announced earlier in the year; see our BISE Sukkur 12th Class Result 2026 check guide for how that result works.

How to check your BISE Sukkur 11th class result in the gazette

Your result is in a PDF gazette on the board's official website. The gazette lists seat numbers under headings such as “PASSING IN Six (6) PAPERS”, “PASSING IN Five (5) PAPERS” and so on, plus sections for absent candidates and results withheld for queries or verification. Keep your admit card or roll-number slip beside you before you start, because you need your exact seat number as printed on it, and a single wrong digit will show no match.

Follow these steps in order. First, open the official BISE Sukkur website, bisesuksindh.edu.pk, on your phone or computer and go to the Examination Results page. Second, find the section headed “HSC PART-I 1st ANNUAL EXAMINATION 2026”. Third, pick the gazette for your district — Sukkur, Khairpur or Ghotki — and your group, Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical or Computer Science, and open that PDF. Fourth, search inside the PDF for your seat number: on a computer press Ctrl+F and type the number, or on a phone use the PDF reader's search option. Fifth, read the heading above your seat number to see how many papers you passed, and save or print that page for your own record. If the website is slow in the first hours after the announcement, wait a few minutes and try again; heavy traffic on result day is normal and does not mean your result is missing.

  • Official website: bisesuksindh.edu.pk, then the Examination Results page
  • Open the gazette for your own district (Sukkur, Khairpur or Ghotki) and group (Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical or Computer Science)
  • Search the PDF for your seat number exactly as printed on your admit card
  • Read the heading above your seat number: it tells you how many papers you passed
  • Save or print the gazette page that shows your seat number

Key statistics: 45,177 appeared, 38,093 passed all six papers

The board's own statistical sheets for the HSC Part-I 1st Annual Examination 2026 give the full picture. Across the three groups, 45,968 candidates registered, 45,177 appeared, and 38,093 passed all six papers, which is 84.3 per cent of those who appeared. These are the board's own figures, taken from the official Statistics PDFs published with the gazettes, and the group-wise breakdown is set out below.

In the Pre-Engineering group, 13,252 candidates registered and 12,992 appeared; 10,697 passed all six papers, which is 82.3 per cent of those who appeared. A further 1,707 candidates passed five papers, 425 passed four, 111 passed three, 30 passed two and 19 passed one paper, while 390 failed in all subjects and 139 results were withheld. In the Pre-Medical group, the largest of the three, 31,799 candidates registered and 31,278 appeared; 26,568 passed all six papers, which is 84.9 per cent. A further 3,220 passed five papers, 871 passed four, 347 passed three, 138 passed two and 105 passed one paper, while 483 failed in all subjects and 234 results were withheld. In the Computer Science group, 917 candidates registered and 907 appeared; 828 passed all six papers, which is 91.3 per cent, the highest of the three groups. A further 68 passed five papers, 9 passed four, one passed three and one passed two papers, while 24 failed in all subjects and 18 results were withheld.

The figures also show where the candidates came from. In Pre-Medical, Khairpur district alone accounted for 18,532 of the 31,278 candidates who appeared, against 7,231 from Sukkur district and 5,515 from Ghotki district. In Pre-Engineering, Ghotki had the most candidates at 5,894 appeared, followed by Sukkur at 3,830 and Khairpur at 3,268. In Computer Science, Sukkur district had 604 of the 907 candidates who appeared. Girls did well in the smaller groups: in Pre-Engineering, 1,202 of the 1,244 girls who appeared passed all six papers, and in Computer Science, 316 of the 339 girls who appeared passed all six papers. No position-holder list was published with the gazettes, so this post names no toppers.

What the gazette categories mean — and the withheld results

The gazette sorts every candidate by how many of the six papers they passed. “Passing in Six (6) Papers” means the candidate cleared every paper and has passed the 11th class outright. “Passing in Five Papers”, “Four Papers” and so on mean the candidate passed that many papers and still has the remaining papers to clear; those candidates should read the heading carefully rather than assuming a pass or a fail from the total alone. “Fail in All Subjects” means no paper was passed. Separate sections list candidates who were absent in all papers and candidates whose results are withheld.

A total of 391 results were withheld across the three groups: 139 in Pre-Engineering, 234 in Pre-Medical and 18 in Computer Science. In the Pre-Engineering gazette for Sukkur district, seat number 480292 is held for unfair means, and a number of other seat numbers are withheld because of pending queries or verification. If your seat number appears under a withheld heading, your result has not been cancelled; it is held while the board completes a check. Contact the BISE Sukkur examination office directly with your seat number and admit card details to find out what is needed, and do not pay any agent who claims to release a withheld result for a fee. Only the board's own process can release it.

  • Passing in six papers means you cleared every paper of the 11th class
  • Passing in fewer papers means those papers are cleared and the rest still need to be passed
  • Fail in all subjects means no paper was passed in this examination
  • 391 results are withheld in total: 139 Pre-Engineering, 234 Pre-Medical, 18 Computer Science
  • Withheld candidates should contact the BISE Sukkur examination office with their seat number

What to do next: marksheet, rechecking and the next examination

First, save your result. Download or print the gazette page that shows your seat number, and keep it with your admit card. Your detailed marks card (DMC) and marksheet are issued through your college or the board after the online release, so ask your college when to collect them. Until the original arrives, the printed gazette page is your record of the result.

Second, if a mark looks wrong, ask about rechecking. No rechecking dates or fee details were published with the result gazettes, so this post states none. Watch the board's official website, bisesuksindh.edu.pk, for the scrutiny or rechecking notice, and ask your college office how to apply once the board announces it. Rechecking re-examines the marking of a paper; it does not re-teach the subject, so it is the right step when a mark looks wrong and the wrong step when the paper simply needs more preparation.

Third, if you still have papers to clear, plan for the next examination opportunity. The board runs a second annual (supplementary) session for students who did not clear one or more papers, and its schedule is announced on the board's website. Do not wait for the notice to start revising: begin with the papers you did not pass, and ask a teacher to help you prioritise the weakest ones first. For study help with the subjects you need to retake, browse the solved notes on Solved Notes and the full book list on Books, and read more board guides on Blog.

  • Collect your DMC and marksheet from your college or the board when they are issued
  • Watch bisesuksindh.edu.pk for the rechecking notice; no dates were published with the result
  • If papers are still outstanding, start revising them now and ask a teacher to help you prioritise
  • Never pay an agent who claims to change or release a result; only the board's own process counts

How this result fits the Sindh 11th class season

Sindh's boards do not announce on one shared day. The Board of Intermediate Education Karachi announced most of its HSC Part-I groups on 26 October 2026, with Pre-Engineering earlier on 2 October, while BISE Sukkur's gazettes are dated 11 November 2026. That gap is normal for Sindh: each board finalises its own marking and publishes its own gazettes when they are ready. Students should therefore check only their own board's website and ignore dates and links belonging to other boards.

The same pattern holds across the country. Punjab's nine boards announced their 11th class results together on 22 October 2026 under the Punjab Boards Committee of Chairmen, while the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa boards announced their HSSC Part-I results on 21 and 22 September 2026 and the Federal Board announced on 9 September 2026. The Aga Khan University Examination Board took a different route again, announcing its September Examinations 2026 result on 6 November 2026, as covered in our AKU-EB September Examinations Result 2026 post. Whatever board you appeared under, the rule is the same: the board's own website is the only source that counts, and a result is real only when the board itself has published it.
