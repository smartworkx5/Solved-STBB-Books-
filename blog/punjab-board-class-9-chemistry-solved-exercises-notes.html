Punjab Board Class 9 Chemistry Solved Exercises & Notes

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 828 words · 4 min read

473Solved Notes

Class 9 Chemistry is the first time Punjab Board students meet the subject as a full science — atoms, the periodic table, chemical bonding and numericals all arrive in one year. This guide shows you how to use the solved exercises and chapter-wise notes for the Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board (PCTB) Class 9 Chemistry book so every chapter is prepared the way the exam demands.

What the Punjab Board Class 9 Chemistry book covers

The PCTB Class 9 Chemistry book has eight chapters: Fundamentals of Chemistry, Structure of Atoms, Periodic Table and Periodicity of Properties, Structure of Molecules, Physical States of Matter, Solutions, Electrochemistry, and Chemical Reactivity. The first three chapters build the language of the subject — elements, compounds, symbols, formulae and the mole — and everything after them uses that language.

Start from the book itself: open the Punjab Board Class 9 Chemistry book page to read the solved exercises chapter by chapter, free on this website. Each chapter's exercise is solved in the order the book asks it, so you can match every answer to the exact question in your textbook.

How to study each chapter with the solved exercises

Use a three-pass method for every chapter. First pass: read the chapter in the textbook and underline the definitions. Second pass: read the solved exercise for that chapter and note how each answer is built — definition first, then explanation, then example. Third pass: close everything and write two or three answers from memory, then check them against the solved exercise.

Keep the downloadable notes beside you while you study. The notes compress each chapter into the points that matter — definitions, laws, and the differences between similar terms (element vs compound, atom vs molecule, physical vs chemical change). On revision days, the notes are all you need to re-read.

Numericals and equations: where students lose easy marks

Class 9 Chemistry numericals mostly come from the first chapter — moles, molar mass, Avogadro's number and percentage composition. Solve them in four written steps: what is given, what is required, the formula, then the calculation with units. A numerical with steps earns marks even if the final figure slips; a bare answer earns nothing when it is wrong.

Chemical equations must be balanced, and balancing is a skill of practice, not of reading. Write five equations from the chapter every day and balance them by counting atoms on both sides. The solved exercises show the balanced form of every equation in the book — copy the method, not just the answer.

Finish every chapter with an MCQ test

After the written exercise, test the same chapter on the MCQs practice page. Chemistry MCQs punish vague understanding: the difference between atomic number and mass number, or between a period and a group, is exactly what the options test. Read the explanation under every answer — especially the ones you got wrong.

Keep a small error notebook. Every MCQ you miss goes into it with the correct fact in one line. Before the exam, that notebook is the most personal revision material you own, because it contains only the things you personally got wrong.

5 key points you must know

  • Learn the definitions with one example each: element, compound, mixture, atom, molecule, ion. The first chapter's definitions return in almost every later chapter's exercise.
  • For the periodic table, learn trends as comparisons: atomic size increases down a group and decreases across a period. Comparison answers earn more than one-line answers.
  • Balance every chemical equation by counting atoms on both sides. Never change a formula's subscript to balance — only add coefficients.
  • Write numericals in four steps: given, required, formula, calculation with units. Steps carry marks even when the final answer slips.
  • Revise each chapter in this order: textbook reading, solved exercise, notes, then the chapter's MCQs. Skipping the MCQ step leaves your objective preparation to chance.

Frequently asked questions

Are the Punjab Board Class 9 Chemistry solved exercises free?

Yes. You can read the complete solved exercises for the PCTB Class 9 Chemistry book free on this website, chapter by chapter, from the book page. No sign-up and no payment.

Which Class 9 Chemistry chapters need the most practice?

Fundamentals of Chemistry (numericals on the mole), Structure of Molecules (bonding), and Electrochemistry (cells and electrolysis) need the most written practice because their questions combine definitions with steps. The solved exercises for these chapters show the full method.

How should I prepare Chemistry numericals?

Learn the formula, then solve the book's worked examples with the book closed. Write given, required, formula and calculation for every numerical. After the chapter, test yourself with the numerical-based MCQs on the MCQs page.

Can I download the Class 9 Chemistry notes as a PDF?

Yes. Chapter-wise notes for Punjab Board books are available on the Notes page, where you can read them online and download the notes PDF for offline revision.
