Sindh Class 9 Islamiat Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 680 words · 3 min read

503Solved Exercises

Class 9 Islamiat is a scoring subject for Sindh Board students — but only for those who write complete, well-ordered answers. The solved exercises on this site cover every chapter of the Class 9 Islamiat book in Sindhi and Urdu medium, question by question, so you can check your own answers and learn the right way to present them. Use this guide to work through the book properly before the board paper.

Start with the solved exercises, chapter by chapter

Open the Class 9 Islamiat book page and work through one chapter at a time. Read the lesson first, then try every exercise question in writing before you look at the solution — an answer you attempt yourself stays in memory far longer than one you only read.

Compare your answer with the solved one line by line. Notice the order: a short definition first, then the explanation, then the example or reference. Copy that order into your own answers and your paper will read like a prepared student's paper.

Learn the Quranic references and Hadith by heart

Islamiat answers are incomplete without the right reference. For every chapter, keep a small notebook with the Quranic verses and Hadith quoted in the lesson, written in Arabic with their translation. Revise this notebook every week — it is the fastest way to lift your marks.

Do not memorise references you do not understand. Read the translation, learn what the verse or Hadith teaches, and then learn the words. An examiner can tell the difference between a student who understands and one who only repeats.

Practise short and long questions separately

Short questions need one clear fact each — a name, a definition, a date, a teaching. Practise them from the solved exercises until you can answer each in one or two lines without looking. Speed here leaves time for the long questions.

Long questions need structure: introduction, main points in order, and a closing line. Practise two long questions a week from the solved exercises, in full, by hand, and time yourself. The Notes page carries chapter notes you can use to plan each answer before you write it.

Revise with MCQs and past paper practice

In the final weeks, test yourself on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 9 Islamiat book and work through the questions with the correct answer and explanation shown for every one — it catches the small facts that long-question revision misses.

Finish every chapter with a self-test: close the book, write the chapter's key points from memory, then check against the solved exercises. Whatever you missed goes into your revision notebook for the last week.

5 key points you must know

  • Attempt every exercise question in writing before checking the solved answer.
  • Keep a notebook of Quranic verses and Hadith with translations and revise it weekly.
  • Answer short questions in one or two clear lines and long questions with a planned structure.
  • Practise two long questions a week in full, by hand, under time.
  • Finish each chapter with a closed-book self-test and note every miss in a revision notebook.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Sindh Board Class 9 Islamiat solved exercises?

On the Class 9 Islamiat book page. The solved exercises are free to read chapter by chapter, with notes on the Notes page.

How should I answer long questions in Class 9 Islamiat?

Start with a short introduction, then write the main points in order with the relevant Quranic reference or Hadith where it fits, and close with one concluding line. Practise this structure from the solved exercises.

How do I memorise Quranic references for Islamiat?

Write each verse with its translation in a small notebook, understand the meaning first, then learn the words. Revise the notebook weekly and test yourself by writing the reference from memory.

Can I practise Class 9 Islamiat MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 9 Islamiat book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.
