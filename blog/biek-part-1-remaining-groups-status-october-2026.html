BIEK Part 1 Remaining Groups: Result Status, October 2026

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 912 words · 5 min read

The BIEK Part 1 remaining groups result status in October 2026 is a waiting game for thousands of Karachi students. After the Board of Intermediate Education Karachi published the Pre-Engineering Part 1 gazette on 2 October 2026, the other Part 1 groups, Pre-Medical, Commerce, and Humanities, were not yet listed on the board's recent-results page. This status check explains what is known, what remains an expectation, and what waiting students should do now. You will also learn what the waiting groups should study now, and how to check your result calmly when your group is finally announced.

What the Status Check Found

When the Pre-Engineering gazette was checked after 2 October 2026, the other Part 1 groups were not yet listed on the board's recent-results page. One group published, three still waiting, is a normal pattern.

The practical consequence is that Pre-Medical, Commerce, and Humanities students should not expect results simply because Pre-Engineering is out. Each group has its own timeline, and the board announces each one separately. Checking the recent-results page once a day is enough. Our solved notes can help you start Part 2 topics early.

Pre-Medical: An Expectation, Not a Confirmed Date

Pre-Medical Part 1 is expected towards the end of October, with 20 October or later mentioned as an expectation. This is an expectation, not a confirmed board date, so trust no specific day until the board announces it.

Prepare as if the result could arrive any day from late October onward, without fixing on a single date. Keep your roll number slip safe, check the official board website once a day, and ask your college for guidance. Use the waiting weeks to revise biology, chemistry, and physics foundations that Part 2 will build on. The books page can help you find Part 2 material.

Commerce and Humanities: No Date to Report

For Commerce and Humanities, no date is stated here, because no date was part of the verified snapshot. Students in these groups should not trust any date they see online unless the board or their college confirms it. The absence of a date is not a sign of a problem. It simply means the board has not announced a timeline for these groups yet.

Commerce students can use the waiting period to strengthen accounting, business, and mathematics foundations, while Humanities students can revise the subjects that carry into Part 2. In both cases, starting Part 2 topics early is far more useful than waiting idle. A short daily study block keeps your routine intact.

What Waiting Students Should Do Now

The most useful step while waiting is to keep studying. Begin Part 2 topics, revise weak Part 1 chapters, and set a weekly plan with one target per subject. Even one focused hour a day puts you ahead.

Alongside study, keep your documents in order and your sources clean. Keep your roll number slip safe, since you will need it to check the result. Check only the official board website and your college for announcements, and ignore forwarded messages claiming confirmed dates. If a message makes you anxious, ask your college whether it is true.

  • Start Part 2 topics now instead of waiting idle.
  • Revise the Part 1 chapters that felt weakest.
  • Keep your roll number slip safe and easy to find.
  • Check the official board website once a day.

How to Check When Your Group Is Announced

When your group's result is announced, check it on the official board website by roll number. Enter the number exactly as printed, read your scorecard line by line, and save a copy.

After checking, take time to understand the scorecard before deciding anything. Note which subjects you passed, which marks you received, and whether any subject shows a compartment. Discuss the pattern with teachers before any formal step. See our BIEK gazette guide and our blog for more guidance.

  • Check on the official board website by roll number.
  • Read every subject entry before deciding next steps.
  • Save or print your scorecard for future use.
  • Discuss compartments or rechecking with your college first.

5 key points you must know

  • After 2 October, Pre-Medical, Commerce, and Humanities were not yet listed.
  • Pre-Medical is expected towards the end of October, not confirmed.
  • No date is stated for Commerce or Humanities results.
  • Waiting students should keep studying and start Part 2 topics.
  • Check only the official board website and your college for announcements.
