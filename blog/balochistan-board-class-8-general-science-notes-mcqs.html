Balochistan Board Class 8 General Science Notes & MCQs

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 744 words · 4 min read

486Subject Guides

Class 8 General Science is the bridge between primary science and Matric science: the human body, force and motion, energy, matter and the environment, all in one book. Balochistan Board students who study the notes chapter by chapter and then test themselves with MCQs arrive at the exam knowing both the concepts and the exact facts. This guide shows how to use the Class 8 General Science notes and MCQs together.

What the Class 8 General Science book covers

The Balochistan Board Class 8 General Science book covers the human body systems, force and motion, energy and its forms, matter and its states, and the living environment around us. Each chapter mixes understanding with facts: the body chapters need labelled diagrams, the physics chapters need worked examples, and the environment chapters need clear definitions.

Study chapter by chapter from the Balochistan Board Class 8 General Science book page, where the solved exercises are free to read, and keep the chapter notes from the Notes page beside your textbook.

Diagrams first: the body and the environment

For the human body chapters, draw the diagram before reading the text: the digestive system, the respiratory system, the circulatory system. Label every part and write one line for what each part does. Most exam questions in these chapters ask you to name a part or state its function — a diagram you drew yourself answers both.

For environment chapters, learn definitions exactly as the book gives them, then add one example from daily life. An ecosystem defined in the book's words, plus the example of a pond or a field near your home, is an answer the examiner can mark full.

Physics chapters: work the examples

Force, motion and energy chapters cannot be learned by reading alone. Work every solved example in writing, with units at every step, then try the exercise questions the same way. A formula learned without a worked example collapses in the exam; a formula practised on three examples stays.

Keep a formula sheet: one line per formula, with the meaning of every symbol and its unit. Revise the sheet before every practice set — it turns a blank moment in the exam into a remembered line.

Testing yourself with MCQs

After each chapter's notes and solved exercises, test yourself on the MCQs practice page. The objective questions check whether the facts are exact — a name, a unit, a definition — and the explanation under each answer is a short lesson in the point the examiners probe.

Write every missed fact into an error notebook, one line each. In the final weeks, your error notebook and your formula sheet are the whole revision: they contain exactly what you personally were getting wrong.

5 key points you must know

  • Study chapter by chapter from the notes and solved exercises: body systems, force and motion, energy, matter and the environment, in the book's order.
  • Draw the body diagrams yourself and label every part with one line for its function. Diagram questions ask for a name or a function, and a sketch answers both.
  • Learn environment definitions exactly as the book gives them, then add one example from daily life to make the answer complete.
  • Work every physics example in writing with units at each step, and keep a formula sheet with the meaning and unit of every symbol.
  • Test each chapter with MCQs after its solved exercises, and write every missed fact as one line in an error notebook.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Balochistan Board Class 8 General Science notes?

On the Balochistan Board Class 8 General Science book page, with chapter notes and downloadable PDFs on the Notes page.

How should I study the human body chapters in Class 8 Science?

Draw each system first — digestive, respiratory, circulatory — label every part and write one line for what it does. Most exam questions in these chapters ask for a name or a function.

Can I practise Class 8 General Science MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 8 General Science book and practise chapter by chapter; every MCQ shows the correct answer with an explanation.

What is the best final revision for Class 8 General Science?

Your error notebook and your formula sheet. They hold precisely the facts and formulas you personally missed, which is the most efficient final revision.
