Sindh Board Class 7 Mathematics Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 755 words · 4 min read

487Solved Notes

Class 7 Mathematics is the year the subject becomes serious: sets and integers, fractions and decimals, the first algebra, geometry and ratio. Sindh Board students who work through the solved exercises question by question — writing every step, not just reading the answers — build the habit that carries them through Classes 8, 9 and 10. This guide shows how to use the Class 7 Mathematics solved exercises properly.

What the Class 7 Mathematics book covers

The Sindh Board Class 7 Mathematics book covers sets, integers and rational numbers, fractions and decimals, squares and square roots, algebra, ratio and proportion, geometry, and an introduction to mensuration. The chapters build on each other: number work powers algebra, and algebra powers geometry.

Work chapter by chapter from the Sindh Board Class 7 Mathematics book page, where the solved exercises are free to read. Never skip a chapter's exercises to reach the next one — in Mathematics, a skipped chapter is a weak foundation for everything after it.

Write every step — never read the answer first

The single most important habit in Class 7 Mathematics is writing. Solve each question on paper first, showing every step, and only then compare with the solved answer. Reading a solution feels like understanding; writing it proves whether you understand.

When your answer is wrong, do not just copy the correct one. Find the exact step where yours went wrong — a sign error, a wrong fraction step, a missed bracket — and write that mistake in an error notebook. Most Class 7 mistakes repeat the same three or four causes.

Fractions, algebra and the chapters students fear

Fractions and decimals cause more lost marks than any other Class 7 topic. Practise them daily in small sets — ten questions a day, checked at once — until the steps are automatic. Speed in fractions is not talent; it is ten minutes a day for a month.

Algebra begins in Class 7, and the students who enjoy it later are the ones who learned its rules exactly now: like terms combine, signs follow the operation, and every step is written. Keep a one-page list of the algebra rules with one example each, and revise it before every practice set.

Geometry and the final weeks

Geometry in Class 7 rewards drawing: construct the figure with instruments, label it, and only then measure or calculate. A neat, labelled figure earns marks even when the calculation that follows has a slip — and a careful figure usually prevents the slip.

In the final weeks, test each chapter on the MCQs practice page and re-work the questions in your error notebook. On the final day, revise only the error notebook and your algebra rules page — they contain exactly what you personally were getting wrong.

5 key points you must know

  • Work chapter by chapter in the book's order — sets, integers, fractions, algebra, ratio, geometry — and never skip a chapter's exercises to reach the next one.
  • Solve every question on paper first, showing every step, and compare with the solved answer only after you finish. Reading a solution is not understanding it.
  • When an answer is wrong, find the exact step that failed and write the mistake in an error notebook. Most Class 7 mistakes repeat the same few causes.
  • Practise fractions and decimals ten questions a day until the steps are automatic. Speed in fractions is ten minutes a day for a month.
  • Draw geometry figures with instruments and label them before calculating. A neat, labelled figure earns marks and usually prevents the slip.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Sindh Board Class 7 Mathematics solved exercises?

On the Sindh Board Class 7 Mathematics book page. The solved exercises are free to read chapter by chapter, with notes on the Notes page.

How do I stop making sign mistakes in Class 7 Mathematics?

Write every step and check the sign at each step before moving on. Keep an error notebook of your sign mistakes — seeing the same cause three times is what stops the fourth.

Which Class 7 Mathematics topics need daily practice?

Fractions and decimals, and algebra. Ten questions a day, checked at once, builds the automatic steps that the exam rewards.

Can I practise Class 7 Mathematics MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 7 Mathematics book and practise chapter by chapter; every MCQ shows the correct answer with an explanation.
