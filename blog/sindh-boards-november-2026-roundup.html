Sindh Boards November 2026 Roundup: Results, Supplementary Exams and Inquiries

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-11-04 · 1712 words · 9 min read

November 2026 has been a busy month for Sindh boards, with fresh Class 9 results, supplementary examinations still in progress, a university admission window opening, and an official inquiry under way, and students can easily lose track of what is verified and what is only rumour. This roundup brings the verified stories together in one place, with dates and figures taken only from confirmed sources, so that students, parents, and teachers can see the full picture at a glance. Read each section for the detail that affects you, and follow the official board websites for any later update.

BSEK Karachi Class 9 Science Result 2026

The Board of Secondary Education Karachi has declared the Class 9, or SSC Part I, Science Group Annual Examination Result 2026, announced by Chairman Prof. Musharraf Ali Rajput with Controller of Examinations Ahmed Khan Chhutto, with the source page updated on 3 November 2026. Students check this result on the official BSEK website, bsek.edu.pk, using the roll number from their admit card, and they should record their marks subject by subject before planning Class 10 preparation. This result matters most to Karachi students moving into Class 10, because it shows which science and mathematics foundations are strong and which need revision first.

For this particular result, no pass percentages or candidate counts were verified in the sources seen, so this roundup deliberately does not quote any such figure for BSEK Class 9 Science. Students should be wary of any post that adds a percentage or a candidate total to this result without an official source, because unverified numbers spread quickly on result days and are often wrong. The reliable action is simple: check your own result on bsek.edu.pk, discuss it with a teacher, and build a short revision plan. You can revise with STBB books and solved notes after checking.

BISE Benazirabad Class 9 Result and Verified Figures

The Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education Shaheed Benazirabad announced Class IX Annual Examinations 2026 results for Science and General groups, approved by Chairman Dr. Asif Ali Memon and Controller Shah Muhammad Farooq Rashdi, and reported on 1 November 2026. The verified Science group figures are clear: 52,260 candidates registered, 51,546 appeared, and 41,425 were declared successful, giving an overall pass percentage of 79.02 percent. Results are available on www.bisesba.edu.pk and the board's Android app, and the same under-40 percent Ungraded rule that applies in BIEK Part I also applies to Class IX Part-I this year.

These figures give Benazirabad students useful context, because they show both the scale of the examination and the proportion who succeeded, while also reminding students that a paper below 40 percent is treated as Ungraded rather than passed. A student who passed should still look at weak papers honestly, since Class 10 builds on Class 9, and a student who did not pass a paper should ask the school office promptly about the next procedure. Either way, the result is a starting point for a plan, not a reason to stop studying. The blog and about us pages offer study guidance for the next step.

BIEK Supplementary Exams and Part I Grading Rule

The Board of Intermediate Education Karachi supplementary examinations 2026 run from 28 October to 21 November 2026 for all groups, with papers from 2pm to 5pm and admit cards issued by the board, covering Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, Science General, Home Economics, Commerce, Arts, and special candidates. Students still sitting papers should keep a paper-by-paper revision plan, protect their sleep, and practise written answers daily, because the middle and final weeks of a series reward steady preparation more than last-minute cramming. The afternoon timing means mornings should be used for light revision only, with a normal lunch and an early arrival at the centre.

Alongside the supplementary series, BIEK announced Intermediate Part I Annual Examinations 2026 results on 26 October 2026, with a new grading rule under which a candidate scoring less than 40 percent in a paper is declared Ungraded and is not considered to have passed that paper. Results are available on biek.edu.pk and the board's official Android app, and a 178-page result gazette was published for the Pre-Medical group, listing roll numbers and marks of candidates who passed one or more papers. Students reading any gazette should look for their own roll number carefully and understand the Ungraded rule before judging a result.

Hyderabad Inquiry: What Is Known So Far

The Sindh government ordered an inquiry into alleged irregularities in BISE Hyderabad's Intermediate Part-II results on 23 October 2026, after complaints and social-media reports, with the notification issued by Secretary Universities and Boards Abbas Baloch after approval from Minister Muhammad Ismail Rahoo. Deputy Controller of the Top Secret Branch Ejaz Ali Kaka was suspended, and a three-member committee headed by Professor Dr. Arbeela Bhutto, with Additional Secretary Ameer Fazal Owaisi and Dr. Nasrullah Pirzada, must report and make recommendations to the minister within 30 days, pointing to around 22 November 2026. Students should follow this timeline without assuming an outcome in advance.

Crucially, no cancellation of results has been announced in the sources seen, and no student is named in connection with the inquiry, so students should not repeat cancellation claims or name classmates online. The responsible response is to verify every new claim against an official notification, keep personal documents safe, and continue studying normally while the committee completes its work. Rumours help no student, and a calm, source-based approach protects both individual students and the wider student community. For study continuity, use STBB books and solved notes as usual.

BSEK General Group First Position and What Comes Next

In a separate BSEK announcement, the board declared SSC Part-II, or Class X, Annual Examinations 2026 General Group results for Regular and Private candidates, the first Sindh board to declare General Group results this year, announced by Controller Ahmed Khan Chhutto on the directives of Chairman Prof. Musharraf Ali Rajput. The first position went to Tooba Fatima of Hifaz Girls Secondary School, Block B, North Nazimabad, with 90.27 percent marks, among 15,446 General Group registered candidates. Her result is a reminder that consistent, general study habits, such as regular revision, solved practice, and steady written work, can lead to strong outcomes.

Looking ahead, students should watch three things: the BIEK supplementary series finishing on 21 November 2026, the Hyderabad inquiry report expected around 22 November 2026, and the Sukkur IBA Foundation Semester application deadline on 26 November 2026 with its entrance test on 6 December 2026. Each of these affects a different group of students, so the wise approach is to note only the dates that apply to you, set a reminder for them, and keep your daily study plan running. Check official board and university portals for updates, and use the blog and about us pages for further guidance.
