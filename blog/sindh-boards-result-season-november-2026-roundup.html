Sindh Boards Result Season November 2026: Every Verified Result So Far and What Is Still Pending

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-11-01 · 1888 words · 9 min read

November 2026 opens in Sindh with several board stories at very different stages: some results are announced and can be read, some inquiries are still running, and some results have no confirmed date at all. This roundup brings those threads together in one place, using only verified facts from the 1 November 2026 snapshot, so that students and parents can see the whole result season without mixing announcements, fears, and rumours. The verified results so far include BIEK HSC Part-I on 26 October 2026 and Shaheed Benazirabad Class IX on 31 October 2026, while the Hyderabad inquiry report is pending within 30 days of 23 October 2026, and Hyderabad Class IX and XI results have no confirmed date. Students looking for study material can start with [solved notes](/solved.html) for their class, but for board news, this roundup's rule is simple: if a date or figure is not verified, it is labelled pending, not guessed. A clear map of what is done and what is still open is the best antidote to result-season anxiety.

BIEK Part-I: Announced 26 October 2026

The largest verified result in this roundup is the BIEK HSC Part-I Annual Examination 2026, with most remaining groups announced on Monday 26 October 2026 under Controller Zarina Rashid and Chairman Faqir Muhammad Lakho. The groups announced on that date included Pre-Medical, Science General, Commerce, Arts, Special Candidates, and Home Economics, while Pre-Engineering had been announced earlier, on 2 October 2026. The passed-all-papers figures give a clear picture of the cohort: Pre-Medical 11,966 of 24,544 appeared, Science General 15,036 of 30,386 appeared, Commerce Regular 9,332 of 22,693 appeared across seven papers, Arts Regular 3,748 of 10,678 appeared, and Special Candidates 58 of 97 appeared. Results are available on biek.edu.pk and the official BIEK Android app, and the Pre-Medical gazette runs to 178 pages while the Commerce gazette runs to 172 pages, which shows the scale of the exercise.

For BIEK students, November is therefore a follow-up month, not an announcement month: reading gazettes, understanding Ungraded papers under the 40 percent rule, and following the board's own notices for any further procedure. The new grading rule, under which a score under 40 percent in a paper is Ungraded, applies to these results, and students should read their results paper by paper rather than as a single pass or fail. If you are a BIEK Part-I student, your most useful November task is to turn your individual result into a study list for Part-II or for clearing any Ungraded paper, rather than waiting for a new announcement. The group figures are context; your paper outcomes are the plan.

Shaheed Benazirabad Class IX: Announced 31 October 2026

The freshest verified result is the Shaheed Benazirabad Class IX Annual Examinations 2026, announced on 31 October 2026 for Science and General Groups, approved by Chairman Dr Asif Ali Memon with Controller of Examinations Shah Muhammad Farooq Rashdi, and reported on 1 November 2026. Science Group figures are fully verified: 52,260 registered, 51,546 appeared, and 41,425 successful, giving a 79.02 percent pass rate. General Group reporting gives only a 52 percent overall pass rate, without registered or appeared counts, so this roundup repeats that figure with its caveat intact rather than filling in missing numbers. Results can be checked on the board's official website, www.bisesba.edu.pk, and the board's Android application, which are the channels students should use.

For Benazirabad Class IX students, November is the first full month after the result, and the priorities are reading the result carefully, understanding the 40 percent Ungraded rule, and following the board's own notice for any next step. A Science student who passed can begin strengthening weak subjects for Class X, while a student with an Ungraded paper can begin focused re-study while awaiting official instructions. Parents should avoid comparing the Science and General percentages directly, because the groups differ in papers, cohort, and reporting completeness. The Benazirabad result is a completed announcement with a clear next-step task for each student, which makes it the most straightforward item in this roundup.

BSEK Class 9 Science: 28 October in Prior Coverage

This roundup also notes, carefully, that prior snapshot coverage placed a BSEK Class 9 Science result on 28 October, and the task for this article allows only that date to be carried forward unless figures are re-verified from the current snapshot. The current 1 November snapshot does not re-verify BSEK figures, so this roundup states only the date as prior knowledge and gives no BSEK percentages, counts, or paper details. That restraint is deliberate: a roundup that mixes verified current figures with unverified recalled figures would mislead readers about what is actually confirmed today. Students seeking BSEK Class 9 Science details should check the board's official website, bsek.edu.pk, and the site's earlier BSEK guide for that result, rather than relying on this roundup for BSEK numbers.

The lesson here is about how to read a roundup itself. A good roundup labels its sources and its limits, so that a reader can tell which items rest on today's verified snapshot and which items rest on earlier coverage. If a future snapshot re-verifies BSEK figures, they can be added with their source and date; until then, the honest entry is a date with a caveat, not a set of invented statistics. Students should apply the same test to any roundup they read elsewhere: does it say where each figure came from, and does it admit what it does not know? A roundup that never says pending is probably guessing, and guessing is the one thing result-season reporting must not do.

Hyderabad: Inquiry Pending, IX and XI Unconfirmed

Hyderabad contributes the two pending items in this roundup, and they must not be confused with each other. First, the inquiry into alleged irregularities in BISE Hyderabad Class XII 2026 results is pending, with a three-member committee headed by Professor Dr Arbeela Bhutto due to report within 30 days of the 23 October 2026 order. The matters are allegations, not findings, results have not been cancelled, and Deputy Controller Ijaz Ali Kaka was suspended during the inquiry. Second, and separately, Hyderabad Class IX and XI result compilation is subject to fears of further delay, linked in reporting to temporary leadership and certificate signature delays, but no Class IX or XI result date is confirmed. Keeping these two pendings separate matters, because the inquiry concerns Class XII results already announced, while the IX and XI concern is about results not yet compiled or announced.

For Hyderabad students, November therefore requires two different kinds of patience. Class XII students should continue normal academic life while watching for the inquiry report through official channels, without acting as if their results have been cancelled. Class IX and XI students should keep studying for the next stage while waiting for an official result date, without trusting forwarded dates. The Chromebook scheme concern for Class IX students remains a fear, not a confirmed outcome, and should be discussed in those terms. The books page can support continued study in both cases, but no study resource can substitute for an official board announcement when one is awaited.

What Is Still Pending Across Sindh

Pulling the pending items together gives a short, honest list. Pending: the Hyderabad inquiry report, due within 30 days of 23 October 2026, with its findings unknown. Pending: Hyderabad Class IX and XI result dates, with no date confirmed and further delay feared but not dated. Pending: any general supplementary or rechecking dates and fees for the newly announced BIEK and Benazirabad results, which this roundup does not invent and which must come from each board's own notice. Not pending, because already announced: BIEK Part-I groups on 26 October 2026, Pre-Engineering on 2 October 2026, and Benazirabad Class IX on 31 October 2026. That distinction, announced versus pending, is the whole value of a roundup, and students should return to it whenever a new rumour appears.

The practical advice for November follows directly from that list. If your result is announced, work from your paper outcomes and your board's notice. If your result is pending, keep studying, keep records safe, and check official channels rather than chat groups. If a claim arrives without a board source, a date, or a verifiable notice, treat it as unconfirmed until proven otherwise. Sindh's result season will continue into the coming weeks, with Punjab second annual papers also running through November, and the students who navigate it best will be those who separate verified facts from fears, who read their own results carefully, and who keep studying through both announcements and waits. For broader planning, study guides offer general techniques that apply across boards and classes.

Frequently asked questions

Which Sindh results are verified as announced in this roundup?

BIEK HSC Part-I, with most groups on 26 October 2026 and Pre-Engineering on 2 October 2026, and Shaheed Benazirabad Class IX on 31 October 2026. Both are covered with their verified figures in this roundup.

What is still pending in Sindh in November 2026?

The Hyderabad inquiry report, due within 30 days of 23 October 2026, and Hyderabad Class IX and XI result dates, for which no date is confirmed. General supplementary dates and fees for new results are also not given and must come from board notices.

What do we know about BSEK Class 9 Science in this roundup?

Only that prior coverage placed it on 28 October. The current 1 November snapshot does not re-verify BSEK figures, so this roundup gives no BSEK percentages or counts.

Have Hyderabad Class XII results been cancelled?

No. Results have not been cancelled, and the inquiry concerns allegations, not findings. Students should treat their results as standing unless an official decision changes them.

Where should I check for the next official update?

Check your own board's official website, such as biek.edu.pk, bisesba.edu.pk, or bsek.edu.pk, and ask your school to confirm which notice is current. Do not rely on forwarded messages for dates.
