Punjab Class 10 Urdu Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 554 words · 3 min read

545Solved Exercises

Class 10 Urdu has four parts — poetry, prose, grammar and writing — and most students prepare only the first two and lose marks in grammar and letters. These solved exercises cover all four parts in the paper's own pattern. This guide shows how to use them so no part of the paper is left unprepared.

Tashreeh first: mafhoom, lafzi maani, pas-e-manzar

Open the Class 10 Urdu book page and answer every ghazal and nazm question in three parts: the shair's mafhoom, the lafzi maani of difficult words, and the pas-e-manzar. Leave out one part and the answer is incomplete.

Learn the tashreeh of the first few ash'aar of each ghazal by heart — they are the ones the paper asks most often. Write each tashreeh once from memory before checking it against the solved answer.

Nasr answers start from the book, then your own words

For every nasri sabaq question, begin with a sentence from the book, then explain it in your own words. The first sentence shows you read the lesson; the rest shows you understood it.

Learn a three-line khulasa of every sabaq. If the khulasa is ready, no question on that lesson feels difficult.

Grammar needs five words a day, not one night

Practise five grammar words daily — isam, fail, harf, wahid-jama, muzakkar-muannas — instead of cramming grammar the night before. Grammar is learnt by daily practice, not by memorising rules.

Keep a small list of the grammar questions that appear every year and revise only that list in the last week. The paper repeats its grammar patterns.

Letters and essays earn marks for format

For khat and mazmoon, the format itself carries marks: address, tareekh, khitab and akhtitam in a letter; tamheed, headings and natija in an essay. A correct format secures half the marks before the content is read.

Before writing a mazmoon, make a khaka: tamheed, three headings, natija. An essay without a khaka reads scattered; with one, it reads planned.

5 key points you must know

  • Answer every tashreeh in three parts: mafhoom, lafzi maani and pas-e-manzar.
  • Learn the first few ash'aar of each ghazal by heart — the paper asks them most often.
  • Start nasr answers with a sentence from the book, then explain in your own words.
  • Practise five grammar words every day instead of cramming grammar in one night.
  • Make a khaka before every mazmoon: tamheed, three headings, natija.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a full-marks tashreeh in Class 10 Urdu?

Write three parts: the shair's mafhoom, the lafzi maani of difficult words, and the pas-e-manzar. All three parts together make a complete answer.

Which ghazal ash'aar should I prepare first?

The first few ash'aar of each ghazal — the paper asks them most often, so learn their tashreeh by heart.

How do I prepare Urdu grammar for the board exam?

Practise five words a day — isam, fail, harf, wahid-jama, muzakkar-muannas — and revise the questions that appear every year in the last week.

How many marks does format carry in letters and essays?

Format carries a real share: address, tareekh, khitab and akhtitam in a letter, and tamheed, headings and natija in an essay — get the format right and half the marks are secure.
