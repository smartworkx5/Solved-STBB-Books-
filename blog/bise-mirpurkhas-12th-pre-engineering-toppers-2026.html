BISE Mirpurkhas 12th Pre-Engineering Toppers 2026

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-10-18 · 993 words · 5 min read

According to reports, the BISE Mirpurkhas 12th Pre-Engineering toppers 2026 list is led by a joint first position on 957 marks, followed by second on 956 and third on 955. The Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education Mirpurkhas is reported to have announced the 12th class Pre-Engineering annual result 2026, and this article reports the topper names, schools, and grade breakdown exactly as the single-source report presents them. Treat every figure here as reported, not board-confirmed. You will also learn what the reported grade breakdown shows, what the results mean for Pre-Engineering students, and how to confirm the reported figures.

Joint First Position: 957 Marks

According to reports, the joint first position went to Saim of Government Boys Degree College Sindhri and Tanzila Kumari of Sindh Public Higher Secondary School Umerkot, both on 957 marks.

The tie carries a useful lesson for students aiming high. Top marks in Pre-Engineering come from steady work across mathematics, physics, and chemistry, not strength in one subject. A student who neglects one subject will struggle to reach the top, because every paper contributes to the total. Our solved notes show how to balance all three subjects.

Second and Third Positions

According to reports, the second position went to Vim Lalwani of Government Higher Secondary School Naukot, on 956 marks, just one mark behind the joint leaders. The third went to Ghansham Das of Government Boys Degree College Sindhri, on 955 marks, just two marks behind. The narrow gaps show how much small details matter at the top.

Students preparing for Pre-Engineering should take this as encouragement to polish the basics. Finishing the syllabus early, practising full papers under timed conditions, and reviewing every mistake protect those final few marks. Presentation matters too: clear working, labelled diagrams, and organised answers help markers follow your reasoning. The books page can help you find the right material.

Reported Grade Breakdown

According to reports, the grade breakdown was: A-grade 549, B-grade 1,757, C-grade 1,545, D-grade 515, E-grade 34, and failed 826. This is reported, not board-confirmed, so do not quote it as official.

The most useful takeaway is that middle grades are common, and moving up one grade is a realistic goal for many students. A focused term of revision, targeted at your weakest topics and backed by timed practice, can shift a borderline result into the next grade. Discuss your grade pattern with teachers, and set one improvement goal per subject.

What These Results Mean for Pre-Engineering Students

The reported toppers show what sustained Pre-Engineering study can achieve, but they should not become a source of pressure. Every student's starting point, school, and circumstances differ, and the right comparison is with your own previous performance. If your marks improved this year, that improvement is real progress, even without a position.

Pre-Engineering study rewards patience and structure. Break each subject into chapters, set a weekly target, and test yourself with short questions after each session. Keep a mistake notebook recording every error and the correct method, and review it before each new test. These habits, repeated over months, produce steady improvement.

  • Compare your result with your own past performance first.
  • Break each subject into chapters with weekly targets.
  • Keep a mistake notebook and review it before tests.
  • Test yourself with short questions after each session.

Confirming the Reported Results

Because these figures come from a single source, confirm them on official BISE Mirpurkhas channels before quoting them. The board website is authoritative for announcements and scorecards.

For your own result, the same rule applies: check your scorecard on the official board website by roll number, read every subject entry, and keep a copy safe. If you are planning admissions, gather documents early. See our blog and the BISE Sukkur check guide for more guidance.

  • Confirm toppers and figures on the official board channels.
  • Check your own scorecard by roll number on the official site.
  • Keep a copy of your scorecard for admissions.
  • Do not quote reported figures as board-confirmed.
