Sindh Class 8 Islamiat Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 627 words · 3 min read

554Solved Exercises

Class 8 Islamiat builds on what you learned in Classes 6 and 7 — Tauheed, Risalat, the life of the Prophet (peace be upon him), and the manners of a Muslim. The paper rewards students who write complete, ordered answers, not those who write the longest answers. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises chapter by chapter so every answer earns its marks.

Start from the book page, not from a guide

Open the Class 8 Islamiat book page and read the chapter in the textbook first, then check the solved exercise. An answer you understand from the book stays in the exam; an answer copied from a guide without reading the chapter is forgotten under pressure.

Work one chapter at a time: read the lesson, answer the exercise questions in your notebook, then compare with the solved answers. The questions you got wrong are the ones to revise again the next day.

Write belief answers with a daleel

For Tauheed and Risalat questions, always support the answer with one Quranic daleel or one Hadith reference. A belief answer without a daleel reads like an opinion; with a daleel it reads like Islamiat, and examiners reward that.

Keep a small list of five short references — one for Tauheed, one for Risalat, one for Akhirah, one for the Quran and one for prayer — and reuse them across chapters. You do not need a new reference for every question.

Learn Seerat in sequence: event, year, result

For Seerat questions, answer in the same order every time: what happened, when it happened, why it happened, and what resulted from it. The Class 9 Islamiat solved exercises use the same sequence for the longer Seerat questions you will meet next year.

Practise the sequence on two or three events — for example the Hijrat and the Battle of Badr — until it becomes automatic. In the exam you will write the answer in order without having to think about the order.

Akhlaq answers need one example from daily life

For Akhlaq chapters such as honesty, respect for parents and good manners, finish the answer with one practical example from a student's daily life. If the question is about truthfulness, end with how a student shows it at school or at home.

That one closing example is what separates A-grade answers from average ones. The examiner sees that you understood the lesson, not just memorised its words.

5 key points you must know

  • Read the textbook chapter first, then check the solved exercise — never copy answers without reading the lesson.
  • Support Tauheed and Risalat answers with one Quranic daleel or Hadith reference.
  • Answer Seerat questions in sequence: event, year, reason, result.
  • Finish every Akhlaq answer with one practical example from daily life.
  • Revise the questions you got wrong the next day — those are the ones the paper will ask again.

Frequently asked questions

How do I prepare Class 8 Islamiat for the Sindh Board exam?

Read each textbook chapter, write the exercise answers in your notebook, then compare with the solved exercises and revise the questions you got wrong.

What is the best way to answer Tauheed and Risalat questions?

State the belief clearly and support it with one Quranic daleel or Hadith reference — a belief answer without a daleel reads like an opinion.

How should I write Seerat answers?

In sequence: what happened, when it happened, why it happened, and what resulted from it. Keep the same order for every Seerat question.

How many chapters should I revise daily?

One chapter a day, with the wrong answers revised the next day — steady daily work beats a last-week rush.
