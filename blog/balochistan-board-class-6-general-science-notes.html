Balochistan Board Class 6 General Science Notes

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 655 words · 3 min read

496Solved Notes

Class 6 General Science is where science stops being stories and starts being a subject: living things, matter, energy and the world around us, with questions that need exact answers. Balochistan Board students who read each chapter and work its solved exercises in writing build the foundation every later science class stands on. This guide shows how to use the Class 6 General Science notes properly.

Read the chapter, then work the exercises

Open the Class 6 General Science book page and read each chapter once for understanding before you touch the exercises. Science in Class 6 is about seeing the idea — what a living thing is, how matter changes — and the questions only make sense after the idea is clear.

Then answer every exercise question in writing and compare with the solved answer. A question answered in writing is learned; a question only read is forgotten by the next chapter.

Learn the key terms exactly

Every chapter has key terms — living and non-living, solid, liquid and gas, energy, environment — and the paper asks for them by name. Learn each term with its meaning in one line, and keep a term list per chapter that you revise weekly.

For definitions, write them as the book states them, then practise writing them from memory. A definition written from memory, in your own neat hand, is a definition you own.

Diagrams make science easy

Draw the diagrams the chapter uses — a plant, the water cycle, a simple circuit — with labels, and practise them until you can draw them without the book. In Class 6, a labelled diagram often carries the whole answer.

Keep your diagrams in one notebook, one page per chapter. That notebook becomes your quickest revision: ten labelled diagrams, revised the night before the paper, cover most of the book.

Observation and daily revision

Science is around you: notice the living things, the weather, the materials at home, and connect them to the chapter you studied. A student who links the book to the world remembers the book.

In the final weeks, test each chapter on the MCQs practice page and re-read the chapter notes on the Notes page. Every wrong answer points at a chapter to read once more — and that is the whole of revision.

5 key points you must know

  • Read each chapter once for understanding before touching the exercises — the idea must be clear before the questions make sense.
  • Answer every exercise question in writing, then compare with the solved answer and note what yours missed.
  • Learn each chapter's key terms with a one-line meaning, and keep a term list per chapter to revise weekly.
  • Draw every diagram with labels until you can draw it without the book — in Class 6 a labelled diagram often carries the whole answer.
  • Link the book to the world around you, and test each chapter with MCQs in the final weeks.
