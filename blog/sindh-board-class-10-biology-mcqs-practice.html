Sindh Class 10 Biology MCQs with Answers

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 653 words · 3 min read

524Subject Guides

Biology carries a large MCQ share in the Class 10 board paper, and most of those MCQs come straight from the textbook — definitions, diagrams, life processes and the scientists behind them. Students who only read the chapters often lose marks on small facts they never tested themselves on. This guide shows how to practise Class 10 Biology MCQs chapter by chapter so the facts stay ready for the paper.

Start with the high-weight chapters

Open the Class 10 Biology book page and give the most practice time to the chapters on life processes, reproduction, heredity and ecology — these are where the paper draws its MCQs again and again. Work one chapter at a time: read the chapter, then answer its MCQs before moving on.

For every MCQ you get wrong, go back to the exact line in the book and write the correct fact in a notebook. A wrong answer you understand is worth more than ten right answers you guessed.

Learn the diagrams the MCQs ask about

Many Biology MCQs describe a diagram and ask what it shows — the heart, the nephron, the flower, the human eye. Practise by covering the labels and naming each part from memory before checking. A diagram you can label is a set of MCQs you will not miss.

Keep a small list of one-line facts from each diagram: what each part does, in your own words. The paper rarely asks for a full diagram in the MCQ section — it asks what one part does.

Memorise the scientists and terms, not just the story

MCQs love the names: who discovered what, which scientist gave which theory, what a term means. Make a two-column list — term on the left, meaning on the right — and test yourself from the term side, then from the meaning side.

Use the MCQs practice page to test each chapter under time. A chapter you can finish in ten minutes with most answers right is a chapter you are ready for.

Practise a full MCQ set before the exam

In the final weeks, sit with a full mixed-chapter MCQ set and a timer, notes closed. Mark every question you guessed, even the ones you got right, and revise the rule or fact behind each one the same day.

Repeat the weak chapters only — do not re-read the whole book. The chapters you keep getting wrong are the ones that decide your Biology score.

5 key points you must know

  • Practise MCQs chapter by chapter from the Class 10 Biology book, starting with life processes, reproduction and heredity.
  • For every wrong answer, find the exact line in the book and write the correct fact in a notebook.
  • Cover diagram labels and name each part from memory — the heart, nephron, flower and eye return in MCQs.
  • Keep a two-column term-and-meaning list for scientists, theories and definitions, and test both directions.
  • Finish with a full mixed-chapter MCQ set under time, notes closed, and revise only the weak chapters after.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I practise Sindh Board Class 10 Biology MCQs?

On the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 10 Biology book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.

Which chapters matter most for Class 10 Biology MCQs?

Life processes, reproduction, heredity and ecology supply most of the MCQs. Give these chapters the most practice time, then cover the rest chapter by chapter.

How do I stop guessing in Biology MCQs?

For every guessed answer, find the exact line in the textbook and write the fact in a notebook. Guessing without learning the fact repeats the same mistake in the paper.

Are the Class 10 Biology MCQs on the site chapter-wise?

Yes. Open the Class 10 Biology book page and use the MCQs practice page to practise chapter by chapter.
