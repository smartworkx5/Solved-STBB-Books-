Class 8 English Vocabulary: 100 Useful Words

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-11-04 · 1736 words · 9 min read

Class 8 English rewards stronger writing: essays need opinions with evidence, paragraphs need linking words, and letters need formal respect, so this general list gathers stronger words for essays and letters with simple meanings and example sentences for the Class 8 level. It is not an official answer key for any single lesson, and its value lies in daily use: pick a theme, learn its words, and use them in one essay or letter each week. With steady practice through the [solved notes](/solved.html) and [books](/books.html) pages, these words become a natural part of your writing instead of a list you memorise and forget.

Words That Build a Strong Essay

Class 8 essays need more than simple sentences: they need words that state an opinion, support it and finish clearly, and the solved notes pages show how such words turn scattered ideas into a well-organised essay. Opinion means what you think about something. For example, In my opinion, reading daily is the best habit for students.. Argument means a reason given to support an idea. For example, His argument for early study was strong and clear.. Evidence means facts that prove an idea is true. For example, Good essays give evidence, not only opinions.. Introduction means the opening part of an essay. For example, The introduction should tell the reader the main topic..

A clear conclusion matters as much as a good opening, because it reminds the reader of your main point and leaves a strong final impression, so practise ending every essay with one short paragraph that repeats your idea in new words. Conclusion means the final part that sums up ideas. For example, The conclusion repeated the main point in simple words.. Paragraph means a group of sentences about one idea. For example, Each paragraph of the essay should cover only one main idea.. Topic means the subject you write or talk about. For example, Choose a topic you know well before you start writing.. Title means the name given to a piece of writing. For example, A good title tells the reader what the essay is about..

Linking Words for Smooth Writing

Linking words join your ideas smoothly, so your essay reads like one connected piece instead of separate sentences, and examiners notice this flow because it shows clear thinking and careful planning behind your writing. However means used to show a contrast. For example, The task was hard; however, the students completed it well.. Therefore means used to show a result. For example, He studied daily; therefore, he passed with good marks.. Although means used to show a surprising contrast. For example, Although it rained, the students reached school on time.. Furthermore means used to add another point. For example, Furthermore, reading improves spelling and vocabulary..

Use linking words sparingly and correctly, because one well-placed however or finally is stronger than five linking words used without meaning, and every linking word must match the real connection between your two ideas. Finally means used to introduce the last point. For example, Finally, we should thank our teachers for their guidance.. Meanwhile means used to show things happening at the same time. For example, Meanwhile, the other students prepared their charts.. Instead means used to show a replacement or choice. For example, Instead of worrying, he made a simple study plan.. Otherwise means used to show what happens if not. For example, Revise daily; otherwise, you may forget the lesson..

Strong Verbs and Qualities

Strong verbs and quality words make essays and letters lively, because they show effort, character and results, and they help you describe people and actions with precision instead of repeating the same weak words again and again. Achieve means to reach a goal through effort. For example, With daily practice, she achieved excellent marks in English.. Improve means to make something better. For example, Reading aloud every day will improve your pronunciation.. Succeed means to reach a wanted result. For example, Students who plan well usually succeed in examinations.. Encourage means to give someone hope and confidence. For example, Teachers encourage students to ask questions freely..

Character words also help in essays about ideal students, good citizens and role models, so learn them with examples from real life, and use them when you describe people you admire in your class or family. Appreciate means to value and thank for something good. For example, We appreciate our teachers for their hard work every day.. Responsible means trusted to do a job well. For example, A responsible student completes homework on time.. Honest means truthful and fair in behaviour. For example, An honest student admits mistakes and learns from them.. Confident means sure of yourself in a calm way. For example, Practice makes a student confident during class presentations..

Society and Environment Words

Essays in Class 8 often ask about society, environment and the future, so these words help you write sensibly about real problems and solutions, from pollution and community life to education and the duties of a good citizen. Environment means the natural world around us. For example, We must protect our environment by planting more trees.. Pollution means harmful substances in air, water or land. For example, Pollution harms health, so we must keep our streets clean.. Citizen means a member of a country or city. For example, A good citizen follows rules and helps neighbours.. Community means a group of people living together. For example, Our community works together to keep the school clean..

Future and knowledge words complete this group, because essays about progress need words for learning, freedom and growth, and these ideas appear again and again in school essays, speeches and letters. Freedom means the right to act and speak freely. For example, Freedom comes with responsibility towards other people.. Knowledge means facts and skills gained by learning. For example, Knowledge grows when students read, ask and practise daily.. Education means learning and teaching in schools. For example, Education opens doors to a better and brighter future.. Future means the time that is yet to come. For example, Hard work today builds a strong future for every student..

Formal Words for Letters

Letters need formal words that show respect and clarity, and learning them early helps Class 8 students write applications, requests and invitations correctly, with more examples on blog and in solved letter guides. Address means the place where someone lives or works. For example, Write your address at the top of a formal letter.. Salutation means the greeting at the start of a letter. For example, Dear Sir is a common salutation in formal letters.. Subject means a short line stating the purpose. For example, The subject line tells the reader why you are writing.. Sincerely means used to close a formal letter politely. For example, End your application with Yours sincerely and your name..

End letter practice by writing one short application each week, checking the greeting, subject and closing carefully, because a neat formal letter shows respect for the reader and earns good marks in examinations. Request means asking for something politely. For example, I request you to grant me leave for two days.. Permission means allowing someone to do something. For example, Students must take permission before leaving the classroom.. Apology means saying sorry for a mistake. For example, A sincere apology can repair a small misunderstanding.. Invitation means asking someone to come to an event. For example, We sent an invitation to our parents for the school function..

5 key points you must know

  • Essay words state an opinion, support it, then conclude.
  • Use one linking word per idea, not one per sentence.
  • Formal letter words show respect and clarity.
  • Learn society words with real-life examples.
  • Write one essay or letter weekly using new words.

Frequently asked questions

Why do Class 8 students need linking words?

Linking words join ideas smoothly, so an essay reads as one connected argument instead of separate sentences, and examiners reward this flow because it shows clear thinking, planning and control over English writing. Short daily practice works better than one long session, so revisit this point tomorrow and again at the end of the week for lasting progress.

Is this list from a specific Class 8 lesson?

No, this is a general word list for the Class 8 level, covering essay words, linking words, formal letter terms and society vocabulary, and it is not presented as an official answer key for any single lesson. Write the point in your own words in a notebook, because explaining it yourself shows whether you truly understand it or only recognise it.

How many new essay words should I learn weekly?

Eight to ten new words per week is enough if you use each one in a real sentence or paragraph, because words used in writing stay in memory, while long lists copied without use are forgotten quickly before tests. Try the idea in class or homework this week, and note what felt easy and what still needs practice before your next revision.

What makes an essay paragraph strong?

A strong paragraph starts with one main idea, supports it with a reason or example, links its sentences smoothly, and ends with a clear point, so the reader can follow your thinking without confusion or repetition. Ask your teacher about any part that stays unclear, because a quick question now prevents a bigger confusion before the examination.

How do I choose between formal and informal letter words?

Use formal words like salutation, subject and sincerely for teachers, officers and applications, and use friendly informal words only for close friends and family, because the reader and purpose decide the correct level of formality. Practise with a friend when possible, since explaining an idea to someone else is one of the strongest ways to remember it well.
