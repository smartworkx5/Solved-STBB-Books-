How to Prepare for Practical Exams

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 400 words · 2 min read

584Study Tips

Practical exams test whether you can do the experiment, record readings neatly and explain what you did. Students who revise the method before the day stay calm in the laboratory. This guide shows how to prepare for practical exams in Science subjects across all boards.

Revise the method first

Read each experiment's aim, apparatus and method until you can state the steps in order. Use your subject notes to refresh the theory behind every experiment.

Knowing the method before you touch the apparatus prevents confusion during the practical. Calm preparation shows in your work.

Practise diagrams and readings

Draw each experiment's diagram from memory and label it fully. Then practise writing sample readings in a neat table with correct units.

Neat tables and labelled diagrams make your practical file clear. Clarity helps the examiner follow your work easily.

Prepare viva answers

For every experiment, prepare short answers to likely viva questions: aim, principle, precautions and result. Say them aloud once before the exam day.

Spoken practice makes viva answers steady. Keep answers brief and direct instead of memorising long paragraphs.

Stay calm on the day

Listen to the instructions fully, set up carefully, and record readings as you go. If a reading looks wrong, repeat it instead of guessing.

Browse the books library the night before only for quick revision, then rest. A fresh mind performs better in the lab.

5 key points you must know

  • Revise each experiment's aim, apparatus and method in order.
  • Draw and label every diagram from memory.
  • Practise neat reading tables with correct units.
  • Prepare short viva answers and say them aloud.
  • Record readings carefully and repeat doubtful ones.

Frequently asked questions

How do I prepare for a board practical exam?

Revise each experiment's method, practise labelled diagrams and reading tables, prepare short viva answers, and record readings carefully on the day.

What should I revise the night before a practical?

Only quick revision: methods, diagrams, precautions and viva points. Then rest, because a fresh mind works better in the laboratory.

How do I answer viva questions well?

Keep answers short and direct: aim, principle, precautions and result. Practise saying them aloud before the exam day.

What if a reading looks wrong during the practical?

Repeat the reading instead of guessing. Careful repetition protects your result and shows honest practical work.
