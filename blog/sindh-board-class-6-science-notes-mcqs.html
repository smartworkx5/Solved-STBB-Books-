Sindh Board Class 6 Science Notes & MCQs — Complete Guide

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 740 words · 4 min read

480Subject Guides

Class 6 Science is the first full science book Sindh Board students study — living things, materials, energy and our environment, all in one year. The students who enjoy it are the ones who learn it unit by unit, with notes and questions together. This guide shows how to study Class 6 Science with the unit notes, solved exercises and MCQs on this website.

What Class 6 Science teaches

The Sindh Textbook Board Class 6 Science book introduces the big ideas of science: living and non-living things, plants and animals and their habitats, materials and their properties, simple machines and energy, and the Earth and environment around us. Nothing in it is beyond a Class 6 student — but the new scientific words must be learned properly, because Class 7 and 8 build directly on them.

The book is available in English, Sindhi and Urdu mediums, and all three are solved on this website. Start from the Sindh Board Class 6 Science book page — Sindhi and Urdu medium students will find their own medium's book in the books catalogue.

Learn the words of science first

Every unit has its own vocabulary: habitat, nutrient, conductor, evaporation, and many more. Learn each scientific word as a pair — the word and one example from daily life. A conductor is not just 'a material that lets electricity pass'; it is that, plus 'the copper wire in our home'. The example makes the definition stick.

The unit notes on the Notes page collect these words with their meanings in one place. Read the notes before the unit's solved exercises, and the exercises become reading instead of struggling.

Study each unit in a fixed order

Use the same order for every unit: first the textbook reading, then the notes, then the solved exercises, then writing two answers from memory. Science answers in junior classes should be short and exact — a definition, a reason, an example. Long copied paragraphs are not needed and not rewarded.

Draw the unit's main diagram once by hand: a plant's parts, a food chain, a simple circuit. A hand-drawn diagram teaches more than ten minutes of looking at the printed one, and junior science papers love diagram questions.

Test every unit with MCQs

After each unit, take its set on the MCQs practice page. Class 6 MCQs are friendly — they ask the exact facts the unit taught — but they punish guessing. Read the explanation under each answer; in junior classes, the explanation often teaches the next fact too.

Keep a small science notebook for missed facts, one line each. By the end of the book, that notebook is a complete personal revision sheet for the annual exam.

5 key points you must know

  • Learn every scientific word with one daily-life example: habitat, conductor, evaporation, nutrient. Word plus example is remembered; word alone is forgotten.
  • Study each unit in a fixed order: textbook, notes, solved exercises, then two answers written from memory. Short exact answers are what junior science rewards.
  • Draw each unit's main diagram by hand once — plant parts, food chain, simple circuit. Hand-drawn diagrams are remembered far better than looked-at ones.
  • Use your own medium: the Class 6 Science book and its solved exercises are available in English, Sindhi and Urdu mediums on this website.
  • Take the unit's MCQ set after finishing it, and write every missed fact in one line in a science notebook.

Frequently asked questions

Are Sindh Board Class 6 Science notes free?

Yes. Unit-wise notes are on the Notes page, and the solved exercises are free to read from the book page. Notes PDFs can also be downloaded for offline study.

Is Class 6 Science available in Sindhi and Urdu medium?

Yes. The STBB Class 6 Science book exists in English, Sindhi and Urdu mediums, and each medium's book is solved separately on this website. Find yours in the books catalogue.

How should a Class 6 student study Science?

Unit by unit: read the textbook, read the notes, do the solved exercises, write two answers from memory, then take the unit's MCQs. The fixed order matters more than long hours.

Where can Class 6 students practise Science MCQs?

On the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 6 Science book of your medium and practise unit by unit — every MCQ shows its answer with an explanation.
