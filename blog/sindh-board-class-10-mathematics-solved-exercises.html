Sindh Class 10 Maths Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 599 words · 3 min read

544Solved Exercises

Class 10 Maths decides a large part of the Matric result, and it is the subject where step-by-step working earns marks even when the final answer goes wrong. Students who copy solutions without writing them out themselves lose those working marks. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises so every chapter's method stays clear until the paper.

Start with quadratic equations — they carry the most weight

Open the Class 10 Maths book page and give quadratic equations the first and longest practice slot: factorisation, completing the square and the quadratic formula. These methods return in almost every later chapter, so a weak start here costs marks again and again.

Solve each exercise question on paper first, then compare it with the solved answer step by step. Where your working differs, find the exact step where it went wrong — that step is the lesson, not the final answer.

Write every step in trigonometry

Trigonometry answers lose marks when steps are skipped: write the given values, the ratio you are using, the substitution and the answer. A full line of working is easier to mark than a correct answer that appears from nowhere.

Learn the standard angles by heart — 0, 30, 45, 60 and 90 degrees — and practise heights and distances with a labelled diagram every time. A diagram with the angle marked is half the solution.

Practise geometry constructions by hand

Geometry constructions cannot be learnt by reading: draw each one with compass and ruler at least three times — angle bisector, perpendicular bisector, circle through three points. The hand remembers what the eye forgets.

For theorems, learn the statement in your own words first, then the proof in short steps. A theorem answer that states the result clearly and then proves it in order earns full marks.

Revise statistics with one worked example per formula

Statistics is the easiest chapter to score in if the formulas are ready: mean, median, mode and standard deviation. Keep one worked example for each formula on a single page and revise that page the night before the paper.

Do the exercise questions in order, without skipping the word problems. Word problems are where students lose marks in statistics — read the question twice and underline what it asks for before starting.

5 key points you must know

  • Solve every exercise question on paper first, then compare it with the solved answer step by step.
  • Give quadratic equations the most practice time — factorisation, completing the square and the quadratic formula.
  • Write every step in trigonometry: given values, ratio, substitution, answer with units.
  • Draw each geometry construction by hand at least three times with compass and ruler.
  • Keep one worked example per statistics formula on a single page for last-night revision.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use Class 10 Maths solved exercises for the board exam?

Solve each question on paper first, then compare your working with the solved answer step by step and fix the exact step where you went wrong.

Which Class 10 Maths chapter should I prepare first?

Quadratic equations — the methods return in almost every later chapter, so a strong start there helps the whole paper.

How do I avoid losing marks in trigonometry?

Write every step: the given values, the ratio you use, the substitution and the answer. Skipped steps lose working marks.

How should I prepare geometry constructions?

Draw each construction by hand with compass and ruler at least three times. Constructions are learnt by the hand, not by reading.
