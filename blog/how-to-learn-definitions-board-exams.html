How to Learn Definitions for Exams

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 355 words · 2 min read

594Study Tips

Definitions appear in almost every board subject, and exact wording carries their marks. Students who write definitions from memory instead of only reading them remember far more. This guide shows how to learn definitions for board exams in any subject.

Understand before memorising

Read the definition with its topic until you can explain the idea in your own words. Understanding first makes exact wording easier to hold.

Use your subject notes to see each definition inside its chapter, not as an isolated line.

Write from memory, then check

Close the book and write the definition from memory. Then compare word by word and underline any missing key words.

The comparison step is where learning happens. Rewrite the corrected definition once immediately.

Repeat missed definitions next day

Keep a short list of definitions you missed and rewrite them the next day without looking. One corrected repetition usually fixes a definition.

Clear the list daily so it stays short. A short list is easy to finish every day.

Collect definitions chapter-wise

File your corrected definitions by chapter and subject. That file becomes your fastest revision before the paper.

Browse the books library to check any chapter's definitions against the book itself.

5 key points you must know

  • Understand the idea before memorising wording.
  • Write each definition from memory, book closed.
  • Compare word by word and underline missing key words.
  • Rewrite missed definitions the next day.
  • Collect corrected definitions chapter-wise.

Frequently asked questions

How do I learn definitions for board exams?

Understand the idea, write the definition from memory, compare word by word, rewrite corrections, and repeat misses the next day.

Why does writing beat reading for definitions?

Writing exposes missing key words immediately, while reading hides gaps. The correction you write is what fixes the definition.

How many definitions should I practise daily?

A short daily set from the current chapter, plus yesterday's misses, keeps definitions fresh without overload.

How do I revise definitions before the exam?

Use your chapter-wise corrected file. Rewrite any definition you cannot produce exactly from memory.
