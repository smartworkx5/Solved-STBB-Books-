KPK Class 10 Biology MCQs with Answers

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 599 words · 3 min read

520Subject Guides

Class 10 Biology carries a large share of objective questions, and the MCQs test the small facts — a term, a number, a step in a process — that long answers skip over. Students who only read the chapters often know the story but miss the exact term the MCQ asks for. This guide shows how to practise MCQs so the small facts stick.

Practise chapter by chapter, not all at once

Open the Class 10 Biology book page and read one chapter properly, then practise its MCQs on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 10 Biology book and work the chapter's questions with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.

Read the explanation for every question — including the ones you answered correctly. The explanation often adds the fact the next question will ask, and reading it turns one question into two facts learned.

Collect your mistakes in one place

Keep an MCQ mistake notebook: the question, the correct answer, and one line explaining why. Revise it every few days — the questions you got wrong are the questions most likely to come back in a different form.

When two options look alike, write down the difference between them. MCQs test exactly that difference: two similar terms, two close numbers, two steps in the wrong order. Knowing the difference is knowing the answer.

Learn the diagrams and processes

Biology MCQs love diagrams and processes: the parts of a flower, the steps of respiration, the stages of the life cycle. Draw each diagram from the notes with labels, and write each process as numbered steps — then the MCQ only asks you to recognise what you already drew.

Learn the key terms exactly as the book gives them. A term half-remembered is a wrong option chosen — write each new term with its definition and revise the list weekly.

Test yourself under time before the exam

In the final weeks, practise a full set of MCQs in one sitting, timing yourself, with the notes closed. This builds the speed the paper needs — reading, deciding and moving on without getting stuck on one question.

Revise the chapter notes on the Notes page alongside the MCQs — one chapter a day, mistake notebook open beside you. The notes give the story, the MCQs give the exact facts, and together they cover the whole paper.

5 key points you must know

  • Practise MCQs chapter by chapter right after reading the chapter.
  • Read the explanation for every question, even the ones you got right.
  • Keep an MCQ mistake notebook and revise it every few days.
  • Draw labelled diagrams and write processes as numbered steps.
  • Practise a full MCQ set under time in the final weeks with notes closed.
