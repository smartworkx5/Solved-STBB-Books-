KPK Class 8 English Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 521 words · 3 min read

540Solved Exercises

Class 8 English is where good exam habits are built — read the lesson, answer the exercise, and learn the grammar rule behind each question. Students who copy answers without understanding them struggle in every class after. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises so the English stays with you.

Read the lesson before the exercise

Open the Class 8 English book page and read the lesson first, slowly, looking up every word you do not know. An exercise answered from a lesson you understood is worth ten answered from memory.

After reading, close the book and say the lesson's main idea in one sentence. If you cannot, read it once more before touching the exercise.

Learn grammar one rule at a time

The grammar questions in the solved exercises — tenses, articles, prepositions, voice — teach the rule through an example. Work each question, then write the rule in your own words beside it.

Keep a small notebook of the rules you get wrong. Five minutes a day on that notebook fixes more grammar than an hour of reading rules you already know.

Build vocabulary from the lessons

Each lesson gives you new words in real sentences. Write five new words a day with their meanings and one sentence of your own for each — a word used in your own sentence is a word you own.

Revise the week's words every Friday. Ten minutes of revision keeps a word; without it, the word is gone by Monday.

Practise writing every week

The writing section — paragraph, letter, story — is where Class 8 students lose the most marks. Write one short paragraph and one letter a week, timed, and compare them with the solved examples.

Keep the solved exercises guides open while you practise, so the model answers are one click away when you need to check a format.

5 key points you must know

  • Read each lesson fully before answering its exercise, and summarise it in one sentence.
  • Learn each grammar rule from the exercise example, then write the rule in your own words.
  • Learn five new words a day from the lessons, each with a sentence of your own.
  • Revise the week's vocabulary every Friday for ten minutes.
  • Write one timed paragraph and one letter every week and compare them with the solved examples.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find KPK Board Class 8 English solved exercises?

On the Class 8 English book page on solvedbooks.pk, with lesson-wise solved exercises, grammar and writing examples.

How do I improve English grammar for Class 8?

Work the grammar questions in the solved exercises, write each rule in your own words, and revise the rules you get wrong daily.

How can I build English vocabulary?

Learn five new words a day from the lessons, write a sentence of your own for each, and revise the week's words every Friday.

How can I score well in the English writing section?

Practise one timed paragraph and one letter every week and compare them with the solved model answers.
