Punjab Class 8 General Science Notes

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 501 words · 3 min read

549Solved Notes

Class 8 General Science is the last science paper before the board classes, and it covers the whole range — living things, matter, energy and the Earth. Students who only read the chapters forget the definitions and the diagrams by exam time. This guide shows how to use the notes so the concepts and the diagrams both stay clear.

Learn each chapter through its diagram

Open the Class 8 General Science book page and for every chapter, draw its main diagram first — the cell, the plant, the circuit, the water cycle. A diagram drawn once is remembered longer than a page read three times.

Write the labels in your own words beside each part. Labels you wrote yourself are the ones you remember in the paper.

Definitions in one line, examples in the next

Learn every definition in one line, then add one example in the next line. A definition with an example is a complete answer; a definition alone is easy to forget.

Keep a definition page per chapter and revise it every two days. Ten minutes of revision beats an hour of re-reading.

Experiments: aim, method, result

For every experiment in the book, learn it in three parts: aim, method, result. Examiners ask experiments in exactly this order, so a three-part answer fits the question perfectly.

Practise writing one experiment answer a day from memory, then check it against the notes. The parts you miss are the parts to revise.

Short questions every day, ten at a time

Do ten solved short questions a day from the notes, covering one chapter at a time. Short questions are the fastest way to find the chapters you have not learnt.

Say each answer aloud before writing it. If you cannot say it, you do not know it yet — read that part again before moving on.

5 key points you must know

  • Draw each chapter's main diagram first and write the labels in your own words.
  • Learn every definition in one line, with one example in the next line.
  • Learn experiments in three parts: aim, method, result.
  • Do ten solved short questions a day, one chapter at a time.
  • Say each answer aloud before writing it — if you cannot say it, you do not know it yet.

Frequently asked questions

How do I prepare Class 8 General Science for exams?

Learn each chapter through its diagram, keep definitions to one line with one example, and do ten solved short questions a day.

How do I learn science diagrams?

Draw the diagram yourself and write the labels in your own words. A diagram drawn once is remembered longer than a page read three times.

How should I answer an experiment question?

In three parts: aim, method, result. Examiners ask experiments in exactly this order.

How many short questions should I practise daily?

Ten a day, one chapter at a time, saying each answer aloud before writing it.
