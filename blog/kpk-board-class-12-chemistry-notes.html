KPK Class 12 Chemistry Notes for Exams

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 502 words · 3 min read

526Solved Notes

Second-year Chemistry is the paper that decides the intermediate result — electrochemistry, chemical kinetics, organic chemistry and transition elements all carry heavy marks. Students who memorise reactions without working the numericals find the paper impossible. This guide shows how to use the notes so the reactions and the numbers both stay clear.

Master electrochemistry and kinetics first

Open the Class 12 Chemistry book page and give electrochemistry and chemical kinetics the most practice time: cell EMF, Nernst equation, rate laws and half-life. These chapters carry the numericals, and the numericals carry the marks.

Solve each numerical by writing the given data, the formula, the substitution and the answer with units. Practise five of each type until the order is automatic.

Learn organic reactions as families

Do not memorise reactions one by one. Group them: alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, aldehydes, carboxylic acids — and learn what each family does with each reagent. One family rule covers ten reactions.

Draw every reaction with its mechanism in short form. A reaction you can draw is a reaction you can write in the paper without guessing.

Keep a short-notes book for definitions

Chemistry definitions are exact: one wrong word can lose the mark. Write each definition in the book's words, then in your own words underneath, and test yourself from your own words first.

Revise the short notes every night for two weeks before the paper. Short daily revision beats one long session the night before.

Practise a full paper before the exam

In the final weeks, sit with a past-style paper and a timer, notes closed. Attempt every question, including the numericals, and mark the time each section takes.

Afterwards, check every answer against the notes and list the chapters where marks were lost. Revise only those chapters — the rest is already ready.

5 key points you must know

  • Give electrochemistry and chemical kinetics the most time — their numericals carry the marks.
  • Group organic reactions by family instead of memorising them one by one.
  • Write each definition in the book's words, then in your own words underneath.
  • Keep a short-notes book and revise it every night for two weeks before the paper.
  • Finish with a full timed paper, notes closed, and revise only the chapters where marks were lost.

Frequently asked questions

Where are KPK Board Class 12 Chemistry notes?

On the Class 12 Chemistry book page, with solved numericals and chapter-wise notes for the board exam.

Which chapters carry the most marks in Class 12 Chemistry?

Electrochemistry, chemical kinetics and organic chemistry. Their numericals and reactions together decide a large part of the paper.

How do I learn organic reactions for Class 12 Chemistry?

Group reactions by family — alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, aldehydes, carboxylic acids — and learn what each family does with each reagent, drawing each reaction once.

Are the Class 12 Chemistry notes chapter-wise?

Yes. The Class 12 Chemistry book page organises the notes chapter by chapter, following the textbook order.
