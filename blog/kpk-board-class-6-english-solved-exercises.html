KPK Class 6 English Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 494 words · 2 min read

530Solved Exercises

Class 6 English is where good exam habits start: read the lesson, answer the exercise, and learn the grammar rule behind each question. Students who copy answers without understanding them struggle in every class after. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises so the English stays with you, not just in the notebook.

Read the lesson before the exercise

Open the Class 6 English book page and read the lesson first, slowly, looking up every word you do not know. An exercise answered from a lesson you understood is worth ten answered from memory.

After reading, close the book and say the lesson's main idea in one sentence. If you cannot, read it once more before touching the exercise.

Do the exercise in your own words first

Try every question in your own words before checking the solved answer. Even a wrong attempt in your own words teaches more than a correct answer copied from the book.

Then compare with the solved answer: keep your words where they are right, and fix the grammar where they are wrong. The difference between the two is exactly what you need to learn.

Learn one grammar rule per lesson

Each lesson hides a grammar rule — tenses, articles, plurals, pronouns. Write the rule in a notebook in your own words with two examples from the lesson.

Revise the rules every week. A rule revised four times in a month is a rule you will use in the paper without thinking.

Practise comprehension the exam way

For comprehension questions, find the answer in the passage first and underline it before writing. The answer is always in the passage — never guess from memory.

Write short, complete answers in your own words, and check the spelling of every new word against the lesson.

5 key points you must know

  • Read the lesson first and say its main idea in one sentence before the exercise.
  • Try every question in your own words before checking the solved answer.
  • Write one grammar rule per lesson in a notebook with two examples from the lesson.
  • Find comprehension answers in the passage and underline them before writing.
  • Revise the grammar rules weekly — four short revisions beat one long session.

Frequently asked questions

Where are KPK Board Class 6 English solved exercises?

On the Class 6 English book page, with lesson-wise solved exercises and answers.

How should Class 6 students use solved exercises?

Read the lesson first, try each question in your own words, then compare with the solved answer and fix only the grammar that was wrong.

Which grammar rules matter most in Class 6 English?

Tenses, articles, plurals and pronouns. One rule per lesson, written in your own words with two examples, is enough.

Are the Class 6 English exercises lesson-wise?

Yes. The Class 6 English book page organises the solved exercises lesson by lesson, following the textbook order.
