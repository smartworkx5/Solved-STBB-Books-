Class 11 Biology Solved Exercises — All Boards

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 696 words · 3 min read

499Solved Notes

First year Biology is a big step up: cells, diversity of life, human physiology, with long chapters and questions that need exact, well-organised answers. Students of Sindh, Punjab and Balochistan boards all work from the same core ideas, and the solved exercises on this site cover each board's book chapter by chapter. This guide shows how to use the Class 11 Biology solved exercises properly, whichever board you study under.

Find your board's book and work chapter by chapter

Sindh Board students open the Class 11 Biology book page, Punjab Board students the Class 11 Biology book page, and Balochistan Board students the Class 11 Biology book page. Work each chapter in the order your book gives it — cells first, then diversity of life, then human physiology.

Study the chapter, then work its solved exercises in writing before comparing with the solved answers. In first year, reading alone is not enough: an answer written in your own hand, in the paper's format, is the only proof you know the chapter.

Long answers need a plan

First year Biology answers are long, so plan before you write: a definition first, then the points in order, then a labelled diagram where the chapter uses one. The solved exercises show this structure — study the shape of the answer, not only its facts.

Practise one long answer a day, timed, and compare its structure with the solved model. A long answer with a clear structure earns marks even when a fact or two is short; a long answer without structure loses them.

Diagrams are half the paper

Draw every diagram the chapter uses — cell structure, the human heart, the digestive system — with labels, and practise them until you can draw them without the book. In Class 11 Biology, a labelled diagram is often worth as much as the words around it.

Keep your diagrams in one notebook, one page per chapter. That notebook becomes your quickest revision: twenty labelled diagrams, revised the night before the paper, cover most of the book.

Definitions, differences and final revision

Learn definitions exactly as the book states them, and learn differences as pairs in a small table — mitosis and meiosis, photosynthesis and respiration. A pair learned together is remembered together.

In the final weeks, test each chapter on the MCQs practice page and re-read the chapter notes on the Notes page. Revise only your own documents in the final week — your diagram notebook, your error notebook, and your one-page chapter summaries.

5 key points you must know

  • Find your board's Class 11 Biology book page and work each chapter in the order your book gives it — cells, diversity of life, human physiology.
  • Plan every long answer before writing: definition first, points in order, labelled diagram where the chapter uses one.
  • Practise one long answer a day, timed, and compare its structure with the solved model.
  • Draw every diagram with labels until you can draw it without the book, and keep them in one notebook per chapter.
  • Learn differences as pairs in a small table — mitosis and meiosis, photosynthesis and respiration — and test each chapter with MCQs.
