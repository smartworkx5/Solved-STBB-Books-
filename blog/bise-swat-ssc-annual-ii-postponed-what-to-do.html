BISE Swat Postpones SSC Annual-II 2026: What Affected Students Should Do Next

By Solved STBB Books Team · Updated 2026-10-16 · 1174 words · 6 min read

The Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Saidu Sharif, Swat, has postponed the SSC Annual-II Examination 2026. The exams were scheduled to begin on 12 October 2026 and are postponed until further notice, with a revised schedule to be announced later. This article explains what is confirmed, what is not, and the calm, practical steps affected students should take next.

Important: BISE Swat Is Not a Sindh Board

First, an important clarification. BISE Swat — the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Saidu Sharif, Swat — is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, not Sindh. This postponement does not apply to Sindh boards such as Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, or Mirpurkhas. If you study under a Sindh board, this news does not change your exam schedule.

We cover this story because board news travels fast across social media, and a postponement in one province is often misread as a postponement everywhere. If you are a Sindh student, keep following your own board's announcements and your normal exam preparation plan. If you are a Swat board student, the rest of this article is for you.

  • BISE Swat is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, not Sindh.
  • The postponement applies only to BISE Swat students.
  • Sindh board exam schedules are not changed by this news.
  • Always check which board a news post is actually about.

What Is Confirmed

Here is what is confirmed. BISE Saidu Sharif Swat postponed the SSC Annual-II Examination 2026. The exams were scheduled to begin on 12 October 2026 and are postponed until further notice. A revised schedule, or a new date sheet, will be announced later by the board.

That is the full confirmed picture. No new exam dates have been announced yet, and no revised date sheet exists at the time of writing. Any post that claims to show the new dates should be treated as unverified until the board itself publishes it. Students are advised to follow the board's official website and official Facebook page, and to avoid unverified information.

  • SSC Annual-II Examination 2026 postponed by BISE Swat.
  • Exams were scheduled to begin on 12 October 2026.
  • Postponed until further notice; revised schedule to be announced later.
  • No new exam dates have been confirmed yet.

Where to Find Official Updates

The board has advised students to follow its official website, bisess.edu.pk, and its official Facebook page. These are the only sources that can announce a revised schedule. A screenshot, a forwarded WhatsApp message, or a post from an unknown page is not an official announcement, even if it looks convincing.

A good habit is to check the official website once a day and to ask your school to confirm anything you see elsewhere. If your school has a notice board or an official group, that is a second reliable channel. If a message cannot be traced back to the board itself, treat it as a rumour. For general advice on staying organised during uncertain exam periods, see our guide on how to manage your time during exams.

  • Check the board's official website, bisess.edu.pk.
  • Check the board's official Facebook page.
  • Ask your school to confirm anything you see elsewhere.
  • Treat untraceable forwards and screenshots as rumours.

What to Do With the Extra Time

A postponement gives you something valuable: more time. The students who benefit most are the ones who use it for steady revision rather than stopping altogether. Keep a simple daily routine — a fixed study time, a fixed sleep time, and short revision blocks for each subject — and work through your weakest topics first while the pressure is lower.

Practise past papers under timed conditions, revise your notes, and go back over the chapters you found hardest. Avoid the trap of treating the postponement as a holiday and then cramming when the new dates arrive. A little revision every day keeps you ready whenever the revised schedule is announced. Our guide on how to revise well the night before an exam has a complete revision plan for the final stretch.

  • Keep a fixed daily study and sleep routine.
  • Revise your weakest topics first.
  • Practise past papers under timed conditions.
  • Do a little revision every day instead of cramming later.

What Not to Do

Do not spread unverified dates. Forwarding a fake date sheet, even with good intentions, creates panic and confusion for other students. If you are not sure a message is real, do not forward it — check the official website first.

Do not pay anyone who claims they can get you the new date sheet early, change your centre, or guarantee a result. The board announces its schedule through official channels, and no middleman can change it. Do not stop studying either. A postponed exam is still an exam, and the students who stay ready are the ones who perform best when the new dates arrive. If anxiety is building, talk to a parent or teacher rather than scrolling rumour posts.

  • Do not forward unverified date sheets or rumours.
  • Do not pay anyone for early dates, centre changes, or results.
  • Do not stop studying until the revised schedule is announced.
  • Talk to a parent or teacher if you feel anxious.
