Punjab Board Class 7 General Science Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 653 words · 3 min read

498Solved Notes

Class 7 General Science builds on Class 6 and asks for more: the human body, plants, matter and energy, with questions that need exact answers and labelled diagrams. Punjab Board students who read each chapter and work its solved exercises in writing — instead of only reading them — build the science foundation that Classes 8, 9 and 10 stand on. This guide shows how to use the Class 7 General Science solved exercises properly.

Work chapter by chapter, in the book's order

Open the Class 7 General Science book page and work each chapter in the order the book gives it. Read the chapter once for understanding, then answer every exercise question in writing before comparing with the solved answer.

Do not skip the chapters that look easy. The easy chapters carry the definitions and diagrams that return in later classes — and the paper asks for them by name.

Diagrams and the human body

Draw every diagram the chapter uses — the human body systems, plant parts, the water cycle — with labels, and practise them until you can draw them without the book. In Class 7, a labelled diagram often carries the whole answer.

For the human body chapters, learn each part with its function in one line. A part without its function is only half an answer — the paper asks what it does, not only what it is called.

Key terms and short questions

Learn each chapter's key terms with a one-line meaning and keep a term list per chapter to revise weekly. Science terms are the vocabulary of the paper — a term learned exactly is a mark earned exactly.

Answer short questions in complete sentences, with the fact and the reason together. A short answer that states the fact but not the reason is an incomplete answer.

Observation, MCQs and revision

Science is around you: notice the plants, the weather, the materials at home, and connect them to the chapter you studied. A student who links the book to the world remembers the book.

In the final weeks, test each chapter on the MCQs practice page and re-read the chapter notes on the Notes page. Every wrong answer points at a chapter to read once more — and that is the whole of revision.

5 key points you must know

  • Work chapter by chapter through the solved exercises: read the chapter, answer in writing, then compare with the solved answer.
  • Draw every diagram with labels until you can draw it without the book — in Class 7 a labelled diagram often carries the whole answer.
  • Learn each body part with its function in one line, and each chapter's key terms with a one-line meaning.
  • Answer short questions in complete sentences, with the fact and the reason together.
  • Link the book to the world around you, and test each chapter with MCQs in the final weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Punjab Board Class 7 General Science solved exercises?

On the Class 7 General Science book page. The solved exercises are free to read chapter by chapter, with notes on the Notes page.

Are diagrams important in Class 7 General Science?

Yes. Draw every diagram with labels until you can draw it without the book. In Class 7 a labelled diagram often carries the whole answer, so keep them in one notebook.

How do I learn the human body chapters in Class 7 Science?

Learn each part with its function in one line. The paper asks what a part does, not only what it is called — a part without its function is only half an answer.

Can I practise Class 7 General Science MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 7 General Science book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.
