Punjab Class 11 English Notes Guide

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 555 words · 3 min read

559Solved Notes

Class 11 English decides more of your first-year result than students expect — the lessons, the grammar and the writing section together carry the paper. This guide gives a complete study plan for the Punjab Board Class 11 English book: how to read each lesson, build vocabulary, master grammar and write essays that earn marks.

Read each lesson twice, for two different things

Open the Class 11 English book page and read each lesson twice: first for the story and its message, then for the difficult words and the questions at the end. The first reading gives you the summary; the second gives you the exam answers.

After the second reading, write the lesson's summary in five lines without looking at the book. If you can summarise it, you understand it.

Build a vocabulary notebook

Write every new word with its meaning and one sentence of your own using it. Ten words a week, revised every Sunday, gives you more than three hundred words by the exam — and those words appear in your essays too.

Use the new words in your own writing the same week you learn them. A word used once in an essay is learnt; a word only read in a list is forgotten.

Grammar: one rule, five sentences

For each grammar rule — tenses, active and passive voice, direct and indirect narration — write five sentences of your own using the rule. Five sentences written by you teach more than fifty read in a guide.

Correct your own sentences the next day with the book open. The mistakes you find yourself are the ones you stop making.

Practise essays in the exam format

Write one essay every two weeks in the full exam format: introduction, three body paragraphs, conclusion. A practised structure is worth more than a memorised essay, because the paper's topic is never the one you memorised.

Keep a list of essay outlines — five lines per topic — instead of memorising whole essays. An outline lets you write on any topic the paper gives.

5 key points you must know

  • Read each lesson twice: first for the story, then for difficult words and end questions.
  • Keep a vocabulary notebook: ten new words a week, each used in a sentence of your own.
  • Learn grammar by writing five sentences of your own for every rule.
  • Write one essay every two weeks in the full exam format.
  • Keep essay outlines — five lines per topic — instead of memorising whole essays.

Frequently asked questions

How do I prepare Class 11 English for the Punjab Board?

Read each lesson twice, keep a vocabulary notebook, practise grammar by writing your own sentences, and write one essay every two weeks.

How many vocabulary words should I learn weekly?

Ten words a week, each with its meaning and one sentence of your own — more than three hundred by the exam.

Should I memorise essays for Class 11 English?

No. Keep essay outlines of five lines per topic — a practised structure lets you write on any topic the paper gives.

What is the best way to learn grammar rules?

Write five sentences of your own for each rule, then correct them the next day with the book open.
