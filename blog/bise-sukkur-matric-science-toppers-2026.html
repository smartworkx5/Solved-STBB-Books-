BISE Sukkur Matric Science Toppers 2026: Position Holders

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-10-18 · 1033 words · 5 min read

The BISE Sukkur Matric Science toppers 2026 list recognises the highest achievers in the SSC Part II Science group, according to the board's published position-holder notification. This article names the boys and girls top three, with their marks and schools, exactly as that notification reports them. It also explains what position-holder lists do and do not tell you, and how ordinary students can learn from the study habits behind these performances. You will also learn what a position-holder list really shows, what lessons ordinary students can take from it, and how to check your own result.

Boys Top Three in Matric Science

According to the board's published position-holder notification, the boys first position went to Umar Riyan of The Excellence School and College, Pano Akil, with 1009 marks and an A-1 grade. The second position went to Zaryan Ahmed, also of The Excellence School and College, Pano Akil, with 1006 marks and an A-1 grade. The third went to Sahil Abbas of Govt. Boys Campus Higher Secondary School, Dara Wahan, with 1004 marks and an A-1 grade.

The gap between first and third is only a handful of marks, showing how much careful revision and neat presentation matter. Students aiming high should finish the syllabus early and practise full papers under timed conditions.

Girls Top Three in Matric Science

According to the board's published position-holder notification, the girls first position went to Emaan Hafeez of School of Excellence Sukkur, with 1009 marks and an A-1 grade. The second went to Asifa of Govt. High School, Mulla Ali, with 1006 marks and an A-1 grade. The third went to Ayesha Abbas of Mazhar Muslim Model Higher Secondary School, Ranipur, with 1001 marks and an A-1 grade.

The girls list carries the same lesson as the boys list: consistent, careful work across all subjects beats last-minute effort in one or two. The top three come from different schools, a reminder that strong results are possible from many kinds of institutions. The books page can help you find the right material for each subject.

What a Position-Holder List Really Shows

A position-holder notification names the highest scorers in a group, and nothing more. It does not give the pass percentage or candidate count, and this article does not invent those figures.

The list is most useful as a source of motivation and method. It shows what sustained effort can achieve, and invites students to ask how top performers organise time, revise weak topics, and handle pressure. The answer is rarely a secret technique. It is usually a simple routine repeated for months: attend classes, revise regularly, and ask teachers for help early.

Lessons Ordinary Students Can Use

The first lesson is to finish the syllabus early enough to leave real time for revision. Top performers have seen every chapter before the final weeks, so those weeks go to weak areas and practice papers.

The second lesson is to practise under exam conditions. Solve full papers with a timer, write answers in full, and mark them honestly against the textbook. The third lesson is presentation: write clearly, label diagrams, show working in mathematics and science, and leave no question blank if you can attempt it.

  • Finish the syllabus early to leave time for revision.
  • Practise full papers with a timer and mark them honestly.
  • Review every mistake and fix the topic behind it.
  • Write neatly, label diagrams, and show your working.

How to Check Your Own Sukkur Result

If you appeared in the BISE Sukkur Matric examinations, check your own result on official board channels. Your scorecard describes your performance, so read each subject entry and discuss the pattern with teachers.

Once you understand your own result, plan your next step. If you are moving to 11th class, choose your group with care and start building its foundations. If you have compartments, speak to your school about the correct procedure. See our BISE Sukkur 12th class check guide and our blog for more guidance.

  • Check your own scorecard on the official board channels.
  • Note your strong and weak subjects before planning ahead.
  • Discuss your result pattern with your teachers.
  • Choose your next group or step with care, not in a rush.
