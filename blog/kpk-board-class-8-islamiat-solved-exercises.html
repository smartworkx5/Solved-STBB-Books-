KPK Board Class 8 Islamiat Solved Exercises — Full Guide

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 787 words · 4 min read

476Solved Notes

Class 8 Islamiat is the last step before the board classes, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa students who master its answer style now find Class 9 and 10 Islamiat easy. The KPTBB Islamiat Lazmi book covers Quran and Hadith, beliefs, worship, Seerat and Akhlaq. This guide shows how to study its solved exercises so your answers carry the translation, explanation and references that earn full marks.

What the KPK Class 8 Islamiat book covers

The Islamiat Lazmi book for Class 8 is built in sections: selected Quranic Surahs and Ayaat with translation, selected Ahadith, beliefs (Imaniyat), worship (Ibadaat) such as Namaz, Roza, Zakat and Hajj, the Seerat of the Holy Prophet (peace be upon him), and Akhlaq — the manners and character Islam teaches. Each section needs a slightly different answer style, and the solved exercises show all of them.

Read the complete solved exercises chapter by chapter on the KPK Board Class 8 Islamiat book page, free on this website. The answers follow the Urdu-medium textbook exactly, so what you learn is what the paper asks.

The answer style that earns full marks

For Quran questions, write the translation first, then the tashreeh in your own simple words. For Hadith questions, write the Arabic text if you can, then the translation, then two lines of explanation. For belief questions, add one daleel — a Quranic Ayat or a Hadith reference. An answer with a daleel is a proof; an answer without one is an opinion.

Compare your written answers with the solved exercises on this site. You will notice every long answer has the same skeleton: definition or introduction, main points, one reference, one daily-life example, and a closing line. Learn that skeleton once and every Islamiat answer becomes easier to write.

Seerat and Akhlaq: sequence and example

Seerat answers must move in sequence: the event, its time, its reason, and its result. For example, the Hijrat answer flows from the persecution in Makkah, to the journey, to the building of the first Islamic community in Madina. A sequence answer never misses a step, and examiners reward it.

Akhlaq answers must end in daily life. If the topic is truthfulness, honesty at school and at home is the example. If the topic is the rights of neighbours, one real act of help is the example. The solved exercises model this ending in every Akhlaq answer — copy the habit, in your own words.

Build the base for Class 9 and 10 now

Board-class Islamiat repeats the Class 8 foundations at a deeper level. The Surahs you memorise with translation now, the worship chapters you understand now, and the Seerat timeline you learn now will all return. Study Class 8 as if it were a board class: read the solved exercise, write from memory, then test yourself with the chapter's questions on the MCQs practice page.

Keep the chapter notes from the Notes page for revision days. One page per chapter — key points, one reference, one example — is enough to revise the whole book in the last week.

5 key points you must know

  • Quran answers: translation first, then tashreeh in your own words. Hadith answers: Arabic text, translation, then explanation — the three-step format is the safest in the paper.
  • Every belief answer needs one daleel from the Quran or Hadith. Keep a short list of references — one for each major topic — and reuse them.
  • Seerat answers follow the sequence: event, time, reason, result. Sequence is what separates a story from an answer.
  • End every Akhlaq answer with one practical example from home or school life. That final line completes the answer.
  • Revise in three rounds: read the solved exercise, write it from memory, then check against the book. Three rounds turn notes into exam marks.
