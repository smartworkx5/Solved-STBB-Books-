KPK Class 8 Computer Science Notes

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 687 words · 3 min read

505Solved Notes

Computer Science in Class 8 is a mix of definitions, diagrams and practical steps — and students lose marks when they treat it like a reading subject. The KPK Board book expects you to know what each part of a computer does, how data moves, and the basic ideas of programming. These notes and solved exercises help you learn it the way the paper tests it.

Learn definitions exactly, then explain them in your own words

Start on the Class 8 Computer Science book page. Every chapter opens with terms — hardware, software, input, output, memory, processor — and the paper asks for them word for word. Learn each definition exactly as the book gives it.

Then practise explaining the same term in your own words. If you can explain what a processor does to a classmate who has not read the chapter, you understand it; if you can only repeat the book's sentence, you do not — yet.

Draw the diagrams until you can draw them from memory

Block diagrams of a computer system, the flow of data, and input-output devices appear in almost every paper. Draw each diagram from the notes with labels, then close the book and draw it again. A labelled diagram in your answer earns marks even when your written explanation is short.

Keep a small diagram notebook: one diagram per page, drawn neatly, with a one-line caption under it. Revise the whole notebook in a single sitting the night before the paper.

Practise the practical steps on a real computer

Steps for using software, saving files, and writing simple programs cannot be learned by reading alone. Wherever the book gives steps, follow them on a real computer — at school, at home, or at a computer lab. A step you have done yourself is a step you will remember in the paper.

If you have no computer at home, practise the steps on paper as a written procedure: first click, then menu, then option, in order. Writing the sequence down is the next best thing to doing it.

Use the solved exercises and MCQs to check yourself

After each chapter, work the solved exercises on the book page and check your answers against the solutions. Short questions on definitions and functions are the backbone of this paper — do not skip them for the long ones.

Finish with the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 8 Computer Science book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question. The chapter notes on the Notes page carry the key points to revise before each test.

5 key points you must know

  • Learn every chapter's definitions exactly as the book gives them, then explain them in your own words.
  • Draw each block diagram with labels until you can reproduce it from memory.
  • Follow the book's practical steps on a real computer wherever you can.
  • Work the solved exercises after every chapter and check each answer against the solution.
  • Keep a diagram notebook — one labelled diagram per page — and revise it the night before the paper.
