Sindh Class 8 Urdu Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 476 words · 2 min read

573Solved Exercises

Class 8 Urdu builds the habits Matric Urdu will demand — khulasa writing, tashreeh of nazm and ghazal, grammar and mazmoon. Students who practise all four parts weekly find Class 9 Urdu familiar instead of frightening. This guide shows how to work through the solved exercises for the Sindh Board Class 8 Urdu book.

Khulasa in your own words

Open the Class 8 Urdu book page and read the full sabaq first. Then close the book and write the khulasa in five or six lines of your own, and compare it with the solved exercise.

Your own words prove understanding. A khulasa copied line by line proves only copying — and collapses in the exam hall.

Tashreeh in three steps

For every she'r or band, follow three steps: difficult words and their meanings, the meaning in simple Urdu, and one line on the poet's message. The same three steps serve from Class 8 to Matric.

Write the difficult words of each nazm in a notebook with meanings. A growing word-list quietly improves every other part of the paper.

Grammar five questions a day

Grammar — mutradif, mutazad, wahid-jama, islah-e-jumla, muhavaray ka jumla — is learnt by daily doing. Solve five grammar questions a day from the solved exercises and correct them the same day.

A grammar mistake corrected in writing disappears; one only noticed returns. Correct in writing, every time.

One mazmoon a week

Write one mazmoon weekly in exam format: introduction, three body paragraphs, conclusion. Structure practised weekly carries any topic the paper gives.

The Class 10 Urdu solved exercises continue this same four-part method at Matric level — habits built in Class 8 are marks banked for Class 10.

5 key points you must know

  • Read the full sabaq, then write the khulasa in your own words.
  • Do tashreeh in three steps: words, meaning, poet's message.
  • Keep a notebook of difficult words with meanings.
  • Solve five grammar questions daily and correct mistakes in writing.
  • Write one mazmoon a week in the full exam format.

Frequently asked questions

How do I prepare Class 8 Urdu for the Sindh Board?

Four parts weekly: khulasa in your own words, tashreeh in three steps, five grammar questions daily, and one mazmoon a week in exam format.

How do I write a good khulasa?

Read the full sabaq, close the book, and write five or six lines in your own words. Then compare with the solved exercise and note what you missed.

What is the easiest way to learn Urdu grammar?

Five questions a day, corrected in writing the same day. Small daily practice beats one long weekly session.

Does Class 8 Urdu help in Matric?

Directly — khulasa, tashreeh, grammar and mazmoon are the same four skills Matric Urdu examines, at a harder level.
