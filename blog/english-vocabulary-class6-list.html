Class 6 English Vocabulary: 100 Useful Words

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-11-04 · 1884 words · 9 min read

English feels hard only when the words feel new, so this general word list gives Class 6 students a friendly set of everyday words with simple meanings and short example sentences, grouped by theme so revision feels easy and useful, and it is not presented as an official answer key for any single lesson. Work through one group at a time, say each word aloud, copy its meaning, and write one sentence of your own, because that small habit turns a list into real vocabulary you can use in class, in homework and in daily conversation. You can also practise alongside the [solved notes](/solved.html) and [books](/books.html) pages on this site for extra reading material.

Why Everyday Words Matter First

Class 6 English becomes much easier when you know the words you meet every day at school and at home, because reading, writing and speaking all depend on the same small set of useful words, and the solved notes on this site show how simple words build clear sentences. Book means a set of printed pages for reading or study. For example, I keep my English book in my school bag every day.. Pencil means a thin tool used for writing or drawing. For example, She writes her homework neatly with a sharp pencil.. Bag means a container for carrying books and other things. For example, Ali carries his lunch and books in a strong bag.. Water means the clear liquid we drink every day. For example, Please drink enough water during the hot afternoon..

When you learn a word, always learn its meaning and one sentence with it, because a word without a sentence is easy to forget, while a word used in your own sentence stays in your memory for a long time and becomes ready for tests and class work. Door means the opening through which you enter a room. For example, Please close the door quietly when you leave the classroom.. Window means an opening in a wall that lets in light and air. For example, The teacher opened the window because the room felt warm.. Chair means a seat with a back, used by one person. For example, Every student sits on a chair during the lesson.. Table means a flat piece of furniture with legs. For example, We place our notebooks on the table before the lesson starts..

School Words You Use Every Day

School words are the most useful words for a Class 6 student, because you hear them in every lesson, and once you know them well you can follow instructions, ask questions and write answers with confidence, just like the learners who practise with books every week. Teacher means a person who teaches students in a school. For example, Our teacher explains every new lesson with simple examples.. Lesson means a period of learning or teaching. For example, Today's English lesson is about describing our school.. Notebook means a book of blank pages for writing notes. For example, Write the new words in your notebook after each lesson.. Classroom means a room where a class is taught. For example, The classroom looks clean because every student helps to keep it tidy..

Homework and questions are part of daily school life, so learn these words until you can use them without thinking, and practise them by writing two sentences of your own for each word before you move on to the next group. Homework means school work that you do at home. For example, I finish my homework before I watch television in the evening.. Question means a sentence that asks for information. For example, The teacher asked a question and every student tried to answer it.. Answer means a reply to a question. For example, Write your answer in a complete sentence, not in one word.. Friend means a person you like and trust. For example, My friend helps me learn new English words every day..

Home Words for Daily Life

Home words help you talk about your family and your daily routine in English, and because you use these words at home every day, they are the easiest words to remember once you start saying them in real sentences with your family members. Kitchen means the room where food is cooked. For example, Mother cooks delicious food in our kitchen every evening.. Garden means a piece of land where flowers and plants grow. For example, We water the plants in our garden every morning.. Breakfast means the first meal of the day. For example, I eat breakfast with my family before going to school.. Mother means a female parent. For example, My mother wakes me early so I can revise my lesson..

Daily actions at home give you many chances to practise English, so name what you are doing in English while you do it, and soon these words will feel as natural as the names of the rooms in your own house. Brother means a boy or man who has the same parents as you. For example, My brother and I read our lessons together after school.. Clean means free from dirt. For example, Please keep your room clean and put your books in place.. Cook means to prepare food by heating it. For example, Father can cook rice and vegetables very well.. Sleep means to rest with your eyes closed. For example, Children should sleep early so they can wake up fresh..

Nature Words Around You

Nature words describe the world outside your home, and Sindh gives you many examples every day, from rivers and fields to trees, birds and clouds, so try to name five things you see on your way to school in English each morning. River means a large natural stream of water. For example, The river flows slowly past the green fields near our village.. Mountain means a very high area of land. For example, Snow covers the top of the mountain during the winter season.. Flower means the coloured part of a plant. For example, The red flower in our garden smells sweet in the morning.. Rain means water that falls from clouds. For example, Rain makes the fields green and fills the ponds with water..

Weather and nature words also appear in reading passages and picture descriptions, so learn them with their colours, sounds and seasons, and use them in short descriptions of what you see from your window each day. Sun means the star that gives light and heat to the Earth. For example, The sun rises in the east and gives light to the whole village.. Tree means a tall plant with a trunk and branches. For example, Birds build their nests in the big tree near our school.. Bird means an animal with wings and feathers. For example, A small bird sings sweetly on the branch every morning.. Cloud means a white or grey mass of water in the sky. For example, Dark clouds gathered in the sky before the rain started..

Feelings and Good Actions

Feeling words help you describe yourself and other people honestly, and action words like help and share teach good behaviour, so learn them together and use them when you talk about your day, your friends and your family. Happy means feeling pleased and glad. For example, I feel happy when I learn a new lesson well.. Kind means gentle and caring towards others. For example, A kind student helps a new student find the classroom.. Brave means not afraid in a difficult situation. For example, The brave boy crossed the road carefully and helped his sister.. Help means to make something easier for someone. For example, Please help your friend carry the heavy books to class..

End your practice by writing a short paragraph using five words from this list, then read it aloud to a family member, and keep revising from blog and your notebook so the words grow into a strong vocabulary for Class 6 and beyond. Share means to give part of something to others. For example, Good friends share their books and pencils with each other.. Laugh means to make a happy sound when something is funny. For example, The children laugh happily when the teacher tells a funny story.. Careful means giving attention to avoid mistakes. For example, Be careful while writing, and check your spelling at the end.. Honest means truthful and fair. For example, An honest student always tells the truth, even when it is hard..

5 key points you must know

  • Learn words in theme groups, not in random order.
  • Always learn a meaning plus one example sentence.
  • Say each new word aloud and write it once.
  • Revise yesterday's words before learning new ones.
  • Use five new words in a short paragraph every week.

Frequently asked questions

How many words should a Class 6 student learn each week?

Ten to fifteen new words per week is a healthy target for Class 6, because it leaves time to practise each word in sentences and to revise older words, while a much larger list usually leads to fast forgetting and confusion during tests and class work.

Is this list taken from one STBB lesson?

No, this is a general useful word list for the Class 6 level, grouped by everyday themes such as school, home, nature and feelings, and it is not presented as an official answer key or as the word list of any single lesson in any book.

What is the best way to remember a new word?

Read the word, say it aloud, learn its simple meaning, and then write your own sentence with it, because using the word yourself connects it to your memory far better than only reading it again and again from a list. Try the idea in class or homework this week, and note what felt easy and what still needs practice before your next revision.

Should I learn spellings at the same time?

Yes, copy each new word carefully once or twice and check its spelling against the list, because correct spelling helps in written tests, and a word you can spell correctly is usually a word you can also read and use confidently. Ask your teacher about any part that stays unclear, because a quick question now prevents a bigger confusion before the examination.

How can parents help with vocabulary practice?

Parents can ask the child to explain five words at dinner, listen to one short paragraph using new words, and praise correct sentences, because short daily praise and practice build confidence much faster than one long weekly revision session. Practise with a friend when possible, since explaining an idea to someone else is one of the strongest ways to remember it well.
