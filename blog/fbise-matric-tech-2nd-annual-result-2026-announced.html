FBISE Matric Tech 2nd Annual Result 2026 Announced — Check by Roll Number, SMS & Portal

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 2104 words · 11 min read

The Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE), Islamabad, has announced the result of the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) Matric Tech 2nd Annual Examination 2026. The result was published on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, and it is listed on the board's official result portal, where technical-stream students can now search their result by roll number. This guide explains exactly what was announced, how to check your Matric Tech result online step by step, the SMS method the board uses, what the announcement does and does not include, and what to do next.

The announcement: FBISE Matric Tech 2nd Annual result 2026, 18 November 2026

FBISE published the SSC Matric Tech 2nd Annual Examination 2026 result on 18 November 2026. The board's official result portal carries a card headed “RESULT OF SSC MATRIC TECH 2nd ANNUAL EXAMINATIONS 2026” with a search button, and the portal's results list includes separate entries for “SSC-I (MATRIC-TECH) 2nd Annual” and “SSC-II (MATRIC-TECH) 2nd Annual”. That listing is the confirmation that the result is live: the board itself has published it on its own portal, and students of the technical stream can now look up their individual results.

Matric Tech is the technical stream of the Secondary School Certificate under FBISE. It is the matriculation examination for students in technical institutions and polytechnic institutes, with technical subjects alongside the general papers. The 2nd Annual session is the second annual examination of the year, the supplementary round for students who needed another attempt after the first annual examinations. This announcement covers the Matric Tech stream only. The general SSC Part I and Part II 2nd Annual results are separate listings on the same portal, so Matric Tech students should open the Matric Tech option, not the general SSC option.

The Matric Tech result is the latest in FBISE's 2026 results season. The board declared the SSC First Annual Examination 2026 result on 29 July 2026 and the HSSC Part I First Annual Examination 2026 result on 9 September 2026. Across the country, other boards have announced on their own dates: Punjab's nine boards announced their 11th class results together on 22 October 2026, covered in our Punjab Boards 11th Class Result 2026 guide, and BISE Sukkur announced its 11th class result on 11 November 2026, covered in our BISE Sukkur 11th Class Result 2026 guide. Each board announces when its own marking is complete, so students should follow only their own board's portal.

How to check your FBISE Matric Tech 2nd Annual result by roll number

The confirmed way to check this result is the board's official result portal. The portal address is result.fbise.edu.pk, which opens the results page on portal.fbise.edu.pk. Do not use unofficial result websites or links shared on social media: they can show wrong data or ask for personal details. The board's own portal is the only source named in this guide.

Open the portal and find the card headed “RESULT OF SSC MATRIC TECH 2nd ANNUAL EXAMINATIONS 2026”, then open the search. In the results list, choose the option that matches your class: “SSC-I (MATRIC-TECH) 2nd Annual” for Part I or “SSC-II (MATRIC-TECH) 2nd Annual” for Part II. Enter your roll number exactly as printed on your roll-number slip, because one wrong digit will return no record, and submit. Your result will show your marks and your pass or fail status. Save or print the page for your own record, and keep your roll-number slip beside you until you have checked. If the portal is slow in the first hours after the announcement, wait a few minutes and try again; heavy traffic on result day is normal and does not mean your result is missing.

  • Choose the option for your class: SSC-I (MATRIC-TECH) 2nd Annual or SSC-II (MATRIC-TECH) 2nd Annual
  • Enter your roll number exactly as printed on your roll-number slip

Check by SMS: FBISE's standard SMS method

FBISE runs a standard SMS result service. For the board's SSC and HSSC results, the method is to type FB, a space, and your roll number, and send the message to 5050, for example “FB 123456”. That is the code the board uses across its results, and it is the method to try if you have no internet access on result day. Standard SMS charges apply, and the reply can take a few minutes when traffic is heavy.

One caution: no separate SMS code was published with the Matric Tech 2nd Annual announcement itself, and the announcement does not confirm that the SMS service returns the Matric Tech result. The online portal is the confirmed method for this result, and it is the one to use if the SMS reply does not arrive or does not show your technical-stream result. Never send your roll number to any other code passed around on social media; only the board's own channels count.

  • Type FB, a space, and your roll number, for example FB 123456
  • Send the message to 5050, the board's standard SMS code
  • Standard SMS charges apply and the reply can take a few minutes
  • If no reply arrives, or it does not show your Matric Tech result, use the online portal

Key facts about the Matric Tech 2nd Annual result

This announcement is a short one: it declares the result and points students to the portal. No pass percentage, no appeared or passed counts, and no position-holder list were published with it. This post therefore states none of those figures, because inventing them would mislead students. If the board publishes statistics later, they will appear on fbise.edu.pk.

What the announcement does confirm is the scope: the SSC Matric Tech 2nd Annual Examination 2026, for both Part I and Part II of the technical stream. The portal lists both parts separately, so a Part I student and a Part II student each have their own option to choose. Students who appeared in the general SSC 2nd Annual examinations should not use the Matric Tech option; their results are listed separately on the same portal.

  • The result covers the SSC Matric Tech 2nd Annual Examination 2026, Part I and Part II
  • No pass percentage or appeared and passed counts were published with the announcement
  • No position-holder list was announced with this result
  • The official portal is the only confirmed source for checking this result

What to do next: mark sheet, rechecking and the next session

First, save your result. Print or save the portal page that shows your marks, and keep it with your roll-number slip. Your detailed marks certificate (DMC) and mark sheet are issued through your institution or the board after the online release, so ask your institution when to collect them. Until the original arrives, the printed portal page is your record.

Second, if a mark looks wrong, ask about rechecking. No rechecking dates, fee, or application details were published with this announcement, so this post states none. Watch the notifications on fbise.edu.pk for the rechecking notice, and ask your institution how to apply once the board announces it. Rechecking re-examines the marking of a paper; it is the right step when a mark looks wrong, not when the paper simply needs more preparation.

Third, plan the next step. If you passed, you can move on to the next stage, HSSC or a technical diploma, as your institution advises. If papers remain, the board's next annual session is the route to clear them, so start revising the papers you did not pass now rather than waiting for the date sheet.   • If papers are still outstanding, start revising them now and ask a teacher to help you prioritise

How this result fits the FBISE 2026 season

FBISE's 2026 season began with the SSC First Annual result on 29 July 2026, when the board reported an overall pass rate of 91.33 per cent for SSC Part II, and continued with the HSSC Part I result on 9 September 2026. The Matric Tech 2nd Annual result now closes the loop for the technical stream's second annual session. The general SSC 2nd Annual examinations ran from 24 September to 24 October 2026, and their results are listed separately on the portal.

The wider picture is the same across Pakistan: every board announces on its own timetable. Punjab's nine boards moved together on 22 October 2026 for the 11th class, while Sindh's boards announced one by one, with Karachi's BIEK on 26 October and Sukkur on 11 November. Whatever board you appeared under, the rule is the same: the board's own website is the only source that counts, and a result is real only when the board itself has published it.
