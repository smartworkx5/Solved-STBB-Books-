Sindh Class 10 English Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 661 words · 3 min read

510Solved Exercises

Class 10 English is a matric paper where grammar and writing carry as much weight as the lessons — and it is the paper students most often leave until the last week. The Sindh Board solved exercises cover all four parts: comprehension, grammar, vocabulary and composition. This guide shows how to use them together, week by week.

Read the lesson, then answer from the text

Open the Class 10 English book page and read the lesson once for meaning before touching the questions. Then answer the comprehension questions by finding the exact lines in the lesson that support your answer — never guess from memory alone.

Write every answer in your own words, in a complete sentence, keeping the lesson's facts exact. Copying sentences from the lesson word for word shows no understanding; a short answer in your own words does.

Treat grammar as a daily habit, not a chapter

Grammar — tenses, voice, narration, prepositions, correction of sentences — is tested in every paper and learned by use, not by reading rules. Take one rule at a time from the solved exercises, learn it, and then write three sentences of your own using it. A grammar notebook with one page per rule is worth more than a week of reading.

Correct your own sentences the next day. The mistakes you find in your own writing are the mistakes the paper would have caught — fixing them yourself is the fastest way to stop repeating them.

Build vocabulary from the lessons themselves

Every lesson has words you will meet again in the paper. Keep a vocabulary notebook: the word, its meaning, and one sentence of your own using it. Ten words a lesson is enough — the words come from the book, so they are the words the paper uses.

Revise vocabulary in both directions: word to meaning, and meaning to word. A word you can recall from its meaning is a word you can use in your own writing.

Practise essays, letters and applications weekly

Composition is the part students leave until the last week — and it shows. Write one essay, letter or application every week from the solved exercises, in full, by hand, and time yourself. Learn the format first: heading, salutation, body in paragraphs, closing.

After writing, check your work against the solved version for format and ideas, then correct your own grammar. The Notes page carries chapter notes to revise from, and the MCQs practice page is the place to test grammar and vocabulary under time.

5 key points you must know

  • Read each lesson for meaning, then answer comprehension questions from the exact lines of the text.
  • Learn one grammar rule at a time and write three of your own sentences using it.
  • Keep a vocabulary notebook from the lessons — word, meaning, and your own sentence.
  • Write one essay, letter or application every week, in full and by hand.
  • Correct your own sentences the next day to stop repeating the same mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I answer comprehension questions in Class 10 English?

Read the lesson once for meaning, then find the exact lines that answer the question. Write the answer in your own words in a complete sentence, keeping the lesson's facts exact.

How do I improve English grammar for the board paper?

Learn one rule at a time and practise it with your own sentences. Keep a grammar notebook with one page per rule and correct your own sentences the next day.
