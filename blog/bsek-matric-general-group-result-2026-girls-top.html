Girls Sweep the Top Positions in BSEK Matric General Group Result 2026

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-10-17 · 1730 words · 9 min read

The Board of Secondary Education Karachi (BSEK) has announced the SSC Part II (Class X) General Group Result 2026 for regular and private candidates, and the headline is one that schools across Karachi are celebrating: according to the board's announced results, the top three positions were all taken by female students. The first position went to Tooba Fatima of Hifaz Girls Secondary School, North Nazimabad, with 90.27 percent. The second position went to Amina Asad of Lycee Bahadurabad School with 87.82 percent, and the third position went to Safia Munawar of Hifaz Girls Secondary School with 87.73 percent. This article reports those announced results, explains what they mean, and sets out what General Group students should do next after their result.

What BSEK Announced in the General Group Result 2026

BSEK announced the SSC Part II Class X General Group Result 2026 for regular and private candidates. The General Group covers students who chose subjects outside the Science Group, including combinations built around general science, humanities, commerce-related, and arts subjects depending on the school and the board scheme. These students sit the same Matriculation examination structure as Science Group students, with papers set from the prescribed syllabus, and their results carry the same standing for college admission in the streams that match their subjects.

The result is checked through the board's official channels by roll number, in the same way as the Science Group result. If you have not yet checked your own result, go to the official BSEK website and use the result section for SSC Part II General Group. Keep your admit card with you, because you will need your roll number, and save or print your result card once it appears. For step-by-step checking help, see our guide on how to check the BSEK Matric Science result, which explains the same official checking process.

  • Board: Board of Secondary Education Karachi (BSEK)
  • Examination: SSC Part II (Class X) General Group Result 2026
  • Candidates: regular and private
  • Check: through the board's official channels by roll number

The Top Three Positions: All Female, According to the Announced Results

According to the board's announced results, the first position in the General Group went to Tooba Fatima of Hifaz Girls Secondary School, North Nazimabad, with 90.27 percent. The second position went to Amina Asad of Lycee Bahadurabad School with 87.82 percent. The third position went to Safia Munawar of Hifaz Girls Secondary School with 87.73 percent. We phrase this as according to the board's announced results because the report rests on a single source, and careful reporting keeps that qualification visible. The percentages are close at the top, which shows how competitive the leading positions were this year.

Two of the top three positions went to students of the same school, Hifaz Girls Secondary School, which is a credit to its students and teachers. But the wider lesson is not about one school. It is that General Group students, who sometimes feel that their stream receives less attention than the Science Group, can reach the very top of the board. A General Group result is not a lesser result. It is a different set of subjects, with its own demands in writing, comprehension, and understanding, and students who master those demands deserve the same recognition as any Science Group topper.

Why This Result Matters Beyond the Headline

When girls take all three top positions in a board result, it matters for reasons that go beyond celebration. It tells younger girls in Karachi that the top of the board is open to them, and it tells families that investing in a daughter's education produces visible results. It also challenges the quiet assumption, still heard in some homes, that girls should aim lower than boys or leave study earlier. Results like this one give teachers and parents a concrete example to point to when a girl wonders whether her hard work will be recognised. The answer, in this year's General Group, is yes.

The result also matters because it corrects a common misunderstanding about the General Group. Some students choose the General Group as a fallback and then undervalue their own subjects. That is a mistake. General Group subjects demand strong reading, clear written expression, and careful understanding of ideas, and the students who master them build skills that colleges and workplaces value. Whether your result was at the top of the board or in the middle of the class, your General Group subjects taught you how to read, write, and think, and those skills will carry into every stream you choose next.

What General Group Students Can Learn From the Toppers

We do not have verified details about the toppers' daily routines, so this article does not invent them. What we can say with confidence is that the habits behind strong General Group performance are the same habits that work in every stream: full syllabus coverage, regular revision, and practice in writing complete answers under time pressure. In General Group subjects, written expression matters even more than in Science papers, because the examiner can only mark what is clearly written. Students who organise answers with a short introduction, a clear middle, and a tidy conclusion, and who answer the question asked rather than writing everything they know, consistently score better.

Two practical habits deserve special attention. First, read the question twice before you start writing, because General Group papers often contain questions with two parts, and students lose marks by answering only one. Second, practise writing answers by hand within a time limit, because neat, complete writing under pressure is a skill that only improves with practice. Use your STBB textbooks and Solved Notes to build model answers for the chapters you found hardest, and ask your teacher to mark one practice answer each week so you learn exactly where marks are gained and lost.

  • Cover the full syllabus; do not skip chapters you find dull
  • Revise regularly with active recall, not only re-reading
  • Read each question twice; many General Group questions have two parts
  • Practise writing complete, timed answers by hand

What Comes Next After the Class 10 General Group Result

If you passed, your next step is choosing a college stream that fits your subjects, interests, and marks. General Group students commonly consider Arts, Commerce, Humanities, and related streams, depending on the subjects they studied and the requirements of the college they want. Do not choose a stream only because friends chose it. Read the subject combinations each stream offers, talk to a teacher about what each combination leads to, and think about what you want to study in two years, not only next month. A calm, informed choice now saves a painful change of stream later.

If you missed a paper or want to improve your marks, find out the board rules for reappearance or improvement and the deadlines that apply to you, and speak to your school about the fastest honest route. If you believe a paper was marked unfairly, learn how scrutiny and rechecking work in Sindh before you apply. And if you are moving to college, read our guide on what to do after the Matric Science result, whose planning advice on documents and stream choice applies to General Group students as well.

  • Read each stream's subject combinations before you decide
  • Missed a paper: check board rules for reappearance and deadlines
  • Unhappy with marking: read the scrutiny and rechecking process first
