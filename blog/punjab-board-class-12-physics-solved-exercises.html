Punjab Class 12 Physics Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 621 words · 3 min read

519Solved Exercises

Second-year Physics is the heaviest paper of intermediate science — long derivations, numericals and conceptual questions across electrostatics, magnetism, electronics and modern physics. Students who only read the notes find the numericals impossible, and students who only practise numericals lose the theory marks. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises for both.

Learn derivations by writing them

Open the Class 12 Physics book page and learn each derivation by writing it, not by reading it. Cover the solution, write the derivation step by step from the starting point, then compare — every missing step shows a gap in understanding.

Keep a derivation list: the name, the starting equation and the final result. Revise one derivation a day by writing it from memory. A derivation written ten times from memory is a derivation that survives the paper.

Solve numericals by method, not memory

For every numerical type, follow the same order: given data with units, the formula, substitution, calculation and answer with units. Practise five numericals of each type from the solved exercises until the order is automatic — then a new problem is just a new set of numbers.

After solving, check the answer's size: does it make sense? A current of a million amperes or a wavelength of a kilometre is a signal to recheck. This one habit catches most calculation mistakes before the paper does.

Connect concepts across chapters

Second-year chapters are linked: electrostatics feeds current electricity, magnetism feeds electromagnetic induction, and modern physics builds on all of them. When you revise a chapter, write down which earlier chapter it uses — the links are what the paper's conceptual questions test.

Learn the definitions exactly as the book gives them, then explain each one in your own words as well. The paper asks for the book's definition and for its meaning — both are needed, and both come from the same study.

Revise with the notes and MCQs

In the final weeks, revise the chapter notes on the Notes page — one chapter a day, derivation list open beside you — and redo the solved numericals with the book closed. Whatever you cannot solve goes into a short revision list for the last days.

Finish with the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 12 Physics book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question — it catches the definitions, units and formula points that long-answer revision misses.

5 key points you must know

  • Learn each derivation by writing it from memory, then comparing step by step.
  • Solve numericals in order: data, formula, substitution, answer with units.
  • Check every answer's size — an impossible value means a mistake to find.
  • Write down how each chapter links to earlier chapters when you revise.
  • Revise one chapter a day in the final weeks and redo numericals with the book closed.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Punjab Board Class 12 Physics solved exercises?

On the Class 12 Physics book page. The solved exercises are free to read chapter by chapter, with notes on the Notes page.

How do I prepare for the Class 12 Physics paper?

Learn derivations by writing them, solve numericals by method, connect concepts across chapters, and test yourself with the notes and MCQs.

How do I learn Physics derivations for Class 12?

Write each derivation from memory step by step, compare with the solution, and keep a derivation list to revise one a day. Writing beats reading every time.

Can I practise Class 12 Physics MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 12 Physics book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.
