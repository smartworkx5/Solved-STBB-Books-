Class 10 English Vocabulary: 100 Useful Words

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-11-04 · 1688 words · 8 min read

Class 10 English expects mature writing: formal letters need respectful precision, essays need linking words and strong ideas, and character sketches need exact adjectives, so this general list gathers essay and letter words with simple meanings and example sentences for the Class 10 level. It is not an official answer key for any single lesson, and its purpose is practical: use these words in real essays and letters, not only in a notebook. Practise one essay or letter each week through the [solved notes](/solved.html) and [books](/books.html) pages, and your writing will grow steadily towards board standard.

Formal Letter Words

Class 10 letters are judged on formality, so the words you choose must show respect, purpose and clarity from the first line to the last, and the solved notes letter guides show these words in complete model letters. Formal means following official and polite rules. For example, Use formal language when writing to the principal.. Informal means friendly and relaxed in style. For example, Use informal language only for letters to close friends.. Salutation means the greeting at the start of a letter. For example, Dear Sir is the correct salutation for an unknown officer.. Subject means a short line stating the purpose. For example, Write the subject clearly so the reader knows your purpose..

Closings and enclosures complete a formal letter, because they show manners and completeness, and a letter without a correct closing feels unfinished even when its main message is perfectly clear and polite. Sincerely means used to close a formal letter politely. For example, Close the application with Yours sincerely and your full name.. Respectfully means showing respect in a polite way. For example, I respectfully request you to consider my application.. Enclosure means a document sent with a letter. For example, Mention the enclosure at the end of your application.. Signature means your name written by hand. For example, Put your signature below the closing line of the letter..

Essay Linking Words

Class 10 essays need mature linking words that add, contrast and conclude ideas, because they show the examiner that you can organise a long argument, not only write correct sentences one after another. Furthermore means used to add a stronger point. For example, Furthermore, libraries build a habit of independent learning.. Moreover means used to add another supporting point. For example, Moreover, daily reading improves writing and spelling.. Nevertheless means used to show contrast despite a point. For example, Nevertheless, the students completed the project on time.. Consequently means used to show a result. For example, Consequently, the school won praise for its clean campus..

Similarly, eventually, overall and indeed give your essay a finished, thoughtful tone, because they help you compare ideas, reach a result, sum up fairly and stress a point that truly matters to your argument. Similarly means used to show a similar idea. For example, Similarly, other schools started their own reading clubs.. Eventually means used to show a final result after time. For example, Eventually, hard work brought success to the whole class.. Overall means used to give a general judgement. For example, Overall, the programme improved reading habits in the school.. Indeed means used to stress that something is true. For example, Indeed, teachers play the greatest role in student success..

Strong Ideas for Essays

Strong idea words help you write about progress, problems and solutions, because essays on education, health and society need words like benefit, challenge and solution used correctly and with real examples. Significant means important and meaningful. For example, Clean water has a significant effect on student health.. Benefit means an advantage gained from something. For example, The main benefit of reading is a larger vocabulary.. Challenge means a difficult task that needs effort. For example, Poverty is a big challenge for many families.. Solution means a way to solve a problem. For example, Planting trees is a simple solution to dusty streets..

Opportunity, responsibility, development and progress complete this set, because they let you write hopefully and honestly about the future, without exaggeration and without repeating the same weak words in every paragraph. Opportunity means a chance to do something good. For example, Every student deserves an opportunity to learn well.. Responsibility means a duty you must carry out. For example, Keeping the classroom clean is our shared responsibility.. Development means growth and improvement over time. For example, Education drives the development of every village and city.. Progress means movement towards a better state. For example, Daily practice shows clear progress in writing skills..

Describing People Well

Describing people well matters in essays, stories and character sketches, because precise adjectives show character, effort and feeling, while vague words like good and nice tell the reader almost nothing about the person. Impressive means making a strong good effect. For example, Her impressive speech won praise from all the teachers.. Remarkable means unusual and worthy of notice. For example, His remarkable memory helps him learn lessons quickly.. Excellent means extremely good in quality. For example, Excellent handwriting makes any answer look neat and clear.. Sincere means honest and true in feeling. For example, A sincere apology shows real respect for the other person..

Generous, patient, curious and determined describe the habits behind success, so use them for role models, heroes and ideal students, and support each adjective with one action that proves it is true. Generous means willing to give and share. For example, A generous student shares books with classmates who need them.. Patient means calm while waiting or facing difficulty. For example, A patient teacher explains the lesson again without anger.. Curious means eager to learn and ask questions. For example, Curious students ask good questions in every lesson.. Determined means firm in reaching a goal. For example, A determined student never gives up after one failure..

Exam Writing Skills

Exam writing skills decide how well your vocabulary reaches the paper, because an outline, a draft and clear paragraphs turn good words into good marks, with extra guidance on blog for students who want to practise weekly. Outline means a short plan of main points. For example, Make a short outline before writing a long essay.. Draft means a first version to improve later. For example, Write a quick draft, then improve it before the final copy.. Paragraph means a group of sentences about one idea. For example, Keep one idea in each paragraph of your essay.. Coherence means ideas joined in a clear, logical order. For example, Coherence makes your essay easy to follow from start to end..

Clarity and expression are the final pair, because clear writing says exactly what you mean, and good expression says it in a mature, natural way, so always read your answer once after writing it and fix unclear lines. Clarity means being clear and easy to understand. For example, Clarity matters more than difficult words in an answer.. Expression means the way ideas are put into words. For example, Good expression comes from reading and daily writing practice.. Introduction means the opening part of a piece. For example, A strong introduction states the topic in two sentences.. Conclusion means the final part summing up ideas. For example, End with a conclusion that repeats your main point briefly..

5 key points you must know

  • Formal letters need respectful, precise words.
  • Linking words organise a long essay argument.
  • Strong idea words need real examples behind them.
  • Describe people with exact adjectives, not vague ones.
  • Outline, draft, then revise every long answer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between formal and informal letters?

Formal letters use respectful words, a clear subject and a polite closing for officers, teachers and applications, while informal letters use friendly, relaxed words for close friends and family, and the reader always decides which style is correct. Short daily practice works better than one long session, so revisit this point tomorrow and again at the end of the week for lasting progress.

Is this list from a specific Class 10 lesson?

No, this is a general word list for the Class 10 level, covering formal letters, essay linking, strong ideas and exam writing, and it is not presented as an official answer key for any single lesson. Write the point in your own words in a notebook, because explaining it yourself shows whether you truly understand it or only recognise it.

How do I use linking words without overusing them?

Use one linking word where two ideas truly connect, such as adding a point or showing a result, and avoid placing one in every sentence, because natural links help the reader while forced links make writing feel stiff and artificial. Try the idea in class or homework this week, and note what felt easy and what still needs practice before your next revision.

What makes a good essay outline?

A good outline lists the topic, three main points with one example each, and a short conclusion, because planning first keeps the essay focused, balanced and complete within the time allowed in the examination. Ask your teacher about any part that stays unclear, because a quick question now prevents a bigger confusion before the examination.

How can I improve expression in English writing?

Read good essays and stories regularly, copy useful sentence patterns into a notebook, and write one short piece weekly, because expression grows from reading strong models and practising them, not from memorising difficult words alone. Practise with a friend when possible, since explaining an idea to someone else is one of the strongest ways to remember it well.
