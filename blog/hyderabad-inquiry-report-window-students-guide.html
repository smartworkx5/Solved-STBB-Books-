Hyderabad Inquiry Report Window: What Students Waiting on Results Should Expect

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-11-03 · 1467 words · 7 min read

Students waiting on BISE Hyderabad results and certificates are living with an unusual kind of uncertainty: an inquiry is under way, a report is due within a window, and meanwhile their own results and documents are delayed. This article explains what the inquiry report window actually means for those students, what is verified and what is not, and what to do while waiting. It is written carefully, because the inquiry concerns allegations, not proven findings, and because no student should be named or judged in connection with it.

What Happened: Suspension, Committee and the 30-Day Window

The Sindh government suspended the BISE Hyderabad Deputy Controller of the Top Secret Branch, Ijaz Ali Kaka, and formed a three-member inquiry committee under a notification issued by Universities Secretary Abbas Baloch and approved by Provincial Minister Muhammad Ismail Rahoo. The committee is headed by Professor Dr Arbeela Bhutto. Its report is due within 30 days of the order dated 23 October 2026. This article deliberately states the window as within 30 days of that order, rather than naming a confirmed calendar date, because the verified information gives the window, not a single date, and students should not treat a calculated date as an official deadline.

The scope of the inquiry is alleged irregularities in the Class XII (Intermediate Part-II) annual examination 2026 results, in the pre-medical and pre-engineering groups, covering position holders, top-10 and A-1 and A grade students, and the processes of checking, tabulation and announcement. These are allegations only. They are not proven findings, and the results have not been cancelled. Any report or message that presents the allegations as proven facts, or that names individual students, should be ignored, and students should not repeat such claims.

What the Window Means for Students Waiting on Class 9 and 11 Results

The most direct effect on waiting students is delay. Sources have warned that the compilation of Class IX and XI results could be delayed further, and no Class IX or XI result date for Hyderabad is confirmed. That means students waiting for those results should not trust any date circulating without an official board announcement, and should not plan admissions, admissions-related steps or further study choices around a rumoured date. The waiting period is frustrating, but acting on an invented date causes more harm than waiting for the real one.

The reason for the delay is connected to the wider disruption at the board. According to a Tribune report of 27 October 2026, the chairman, controller and secretary posts at BISE Hyderabad lack permanent appointments and are held on additional charge or deputation, with BIEK Chairman Faqir Muhammad Lakho holding additional charge as Hyderabad chairman and Mehran University Assistant Professor Shakeel Laghari serving as controller on deputation. A board operating under additional charge and an ongoing inquiry moves more slowly than usual, and students should understand that the delay reflects administrative disruption, not any judgment about their own papers.

Certificates Delayed by Missing Signatures

A separate but related problem affects students waiting for certificates: certificate issuance has been delayed by missing signatures. A certificate that is otherwise ready cannot be issued until the required signatures are in place, and when senior posts are held on additional charge, that step can stall. Students who need certificates for admission or employment should therefore plan for extra waiting time, keep their existing documents, such as marksheets and admit cards, safe and ready, and ask their school or college office what interim proof, if any, the board or institution accepts while a certificate is pending.

If you are in this position, keep a written record of your requests: the date you applied or enquired, the office you visited, and what you were told. That record is useful if you need to follow up, and it protects you against confusion when different offices give different answers. Do not pay any unofficial fee or use any unofficial channel that promises faster certificates, because no such channel is part of the official process, and unofficial payments create risk without any guarantee.

What Students Should Do During the Window

First, rely only on official board announcements for dates, and check the board's official channels rather than social media forwards. Second, keep studying or revising in general terms for your next class or next examination, because waiting time spent on study is never wasted, whatever the inquiry's outcome. Third, if you are a Class 9 or 11 student waiting for a result, keep your school informed that you are waiting, so that the school can advise you as soon as genuine news arrives, and so that any school-level planning for the next class includes you.

Fourth, protect your privacy and that of others. Do not share or repeat lists of names connected with the inquiry, and do not treat any named student as guilty of anything, since the inquiry has made no findings and no student has been named in any verified source used here. Fifth, if the wait is causing real anxiety, talk to a teacher, a parent or a school counsellor rather than carrying it alone. Uncertainty is stressful, and asking for support is a sensible response, not a weakness. For general study help while you wait, visit solved notes and the blog listing.

What the Report Window Does Not Mean

The report window does not mean that results will automatically appear when the window closes, and it does not mean that existing results will be cancelled. The committee's report is an input to a decision, and the government and board must then act on it, which can take further time. Students should therefore expect the window to end with a report, not necessarily with instant results, and should avoid disappointment by keeping that distinction clear. Equally, the window does not mean that every result is under suspicion. The inquiry's scope is specific to the alleged irregularities in the Class XII pre-medical and pre-engineering results, and it does not cast doubt on every student's paper.

What students can reasonably expect is information: the submission of a report within the stated window, and then official communication about what follows. Until that communication arrives, the safest course is patience plus preparation. Keep your documents ready, keep your studies going, and check official channels regularly. A calm, documented wait is the strongest position a student can hold while an inquiry runs its course.

5 key points you must know

  • The inquiry committee, headed by Professor Dr Arbeela Bhutto, must report within 30 days of the 23 October 2026 order
  • The inquiry concerns allegations only; no findings have been made and results have not been cancelled
  • Class IX and XI result compilation may be delayed further; no confirmed date exists
  • Certificate issuance is delayed by missing signatures; keep existing documents safe
  • Trust only official board announcements, and never repeat or share names connected with the inquiry

Frequently asked questions

When is the Hyderabad inquiry report due?

The committee's report is due within 30 days of the order dated 23 October 2026. This article states the window rather than a calculated calendar date, because the verified information gives the window, not a confirmed single date.

Have the Hyderabad results been cancelled?

No. The inquiry concerns alleged irregularities, which are allegations, not proven findings, and the results have not been cancelled.

Why are Class 9 and 11 results delayed?

Sources warn that Class IX and XI result compilation could be delayed further amid the inquiry and the board's administrative disruption, including senior posts held on additional charge or deputation. No confirmed Class IX or XI result date exists.

Why are certificates delayed?

Certificate issuance has been delayed by missing signatures. Keep your marksheet, admit card and other documents safe, and ask your school or college what interim proof is accepted while a certificate is pending.

Should I trust dates about Hyderabad results shared on social media?

No. Trust only official board announcements. Dates circulating without an official notice are unverified, and planning around them can cause wasted effort and worry.
