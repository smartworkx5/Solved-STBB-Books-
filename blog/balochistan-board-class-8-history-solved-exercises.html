Balochistan Class 8 History Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 615 words · 3 min read

521Solved Exercises

Class 8 History is a story told in order — and students lose marks when they learn dates without the story or the story without the dates. The paper asks what happened, when it happened, who did it and why it mattered. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises to learn the story and the facts together.

Learn history as a story in order

Open the Class 8 History book page and read each chapter as a story: what happened first, what happened next, and why. Then write the main events with their dates on a timeline — a timeline you made yourself is easier to remember than a list of dates from the book.

For every event, learn the reason as well as the date. The paper asks why things happened as often as when — and a student who knows the reason can answer even when the exact date slips.

Learn people with their work

History papers ask about personalities: who they were and what they did. For every important person, learn one line — name, role, and one achievement. Keep these one-liners in a notebook, one page per chapter, and revise them weekly.

Group people by movement or period, not one by one. People learned in groups — the reformers together, the leaders of one movement together — are far easier to recall than a long separate list.

Answer in writing, with dates and reasons

Work the solved exercises in writing, giving the date and the reason in every answer. Writing the date with the event fixes both in memory; reading them once fixes neither.

Practise the long questions by planning first: three points, in order, each with its fact. A planned answer is a complete answer — an unplanned one wanders and misses the point the paper asks for.

Revise with the notes and MCQs

In the final weeks, revise the chapter notes on the Notes page — one chapter a day, timeline open beside you — and test yourself with the solved exercises and the book closed. Whatever you cannot answer goes into a short revision list for the last days.

Finish with the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 8 History book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question — it catches the dates, names and facts that long-answer revision misses.

5 key points you must know

  • Learn history as a story in order, with a timeline of events and dates.
  • Learn every event's reason as well as its date.
  • Keep one-line notes for each personality: name, role, one achievement.
  • Answer exercises in writing, giving the date and reason in every answer.
  • Revise one chapter a day in the final weeks and test yourself with the book closed.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Balochistan Board Class 8 History solved exercises?

On the Class 8 History book page. The solved exercises are free to read chapter by chapter, with notes on the Notes page.

How do I prepare for the Class 8 History paper?

Learn the story in order with a timeline, learn each event's reason, keep one-line personality notes, and answer exercises in writing with dates and reasons.

How do I remember dates for Class 8 History?

Make your own timeline for each chapter and revise it weekly. Dates learned with the story and the reason behind the event are remembered far longer than dates learned alone.

Can I practise Class 8 History MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 8 History book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.
