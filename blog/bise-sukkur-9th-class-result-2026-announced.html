BISE Sukkur 9th Class Result 2026 Announced — Check by Seat Number & Gazette

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 2821 words · 14 min read

The Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education Sukkur (BISE Sukkur) has announced the results of the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) Part-I, or 9th class, 1st Annual Examination 2026. The result gazettes are dated Monday, 23 November 2026, and are signed by the board's Controller of Examinations, Rizwan Ahmed Memon. They are published on the board's own official website, bisesuksindh.edu.pk, in the Examination Results section. The announcement covers the Science and General groups for the board's three districts: Sukkur, Khairpur and Ghotki. This guide explains exactly what was announced, how to find your result in the official gazette step by step, the figures from the board's own statistics sheets, what the gazette categories and withheld results mean, and what to do next.

The announcement: BISE Sukkur 9th class result 2026, 23 November 2026

BISE Sukkur published the SSC Part-I (9th class) 1st Annual Examination 2026 results as district-wise, group-wise gazettes on its official website. The Examination Results section lists the result under the heading 'Secondary School Certificate Part-I — 1st Annual Examination, 2026 — Science, General', with Statistics links for the Science and General groups and a separate Result gazette link for each district and group. The gazettes themselves carry the date 23-11-2026 and the signature of Rizwan Ahmed Memon, Controller of Examinations. The Sukkur district Science gazette runs to 50 pages and the Khairpur district Science gazette to 90 pages, each listing candidates by seat number under headings such as 'PASSING IN Seven (7) Papers'.

The listing covers the whole board at once. For Sukkur district it lists Science, General Regular and General Private gazettes; for Khairpur district it lists Science, General Regular and General Private gazettes; and for Ghotki district it lists Science and General Private gazettes. Statistics are published separately for the Science group and the General group, and both statistics sheets are signed by the Controller of Examinations together with the board's assistant and deputy controllers for secret work. Students should open only the gazette for their own district and group, because a seat number that appears in one district's gazette will not be found in another district's file.

The Sukkur announcement follows the other Sindh boards' 9th class results earlier in the season. The Board of Secondary Education Karachi announced its SSC Part-I Science Group (Regular) result on 28 October 2026, as explained in our post on the Karachi Board 9th Class Science Result 2026, and BISE Shaheed Benazirabad announced its SSC Part-I result on 31 October 2026, covered in our Shaheed Benazirabad Board 9th Class Result 2026 post. BISE Sukkur's own 11th class result came earlier this month, in gazettes dated 11 November 2026; see our BISE Sukkur 11th Class Result 2026 guide. Each Sindh board announces on its own date, so Sukkur students should follow only the Sukkur board's own gazettes, not another board's dates or links.

How to check your BISE Sukkur 9th class result in the gazette

Your result is in a PDF gazette on the board's official website. The gazette lists seat numbers under headings such as 'PASSING IN Seven (7) Papers', 'PASSING IN Six (6) Papers' and so on, down to one paper, together with the results the board has withheld. Keep your admit card or roll-number slip beside you before you start, because you need your exact seat number as printed on it, and a single wrong digit will show no match. If you do not have your seat number, ask your school office for it before searching, since the gazette is arranged by seat number and not by name.

Follow these steps in order. First, open the official BISE Sukkur website, bisesuksindh.edu.pk, on your phone or computer and select Results to open the Examination Results section. Second, find the section headed 'Secondary School Certificate Part-I — 1st Annual Examination, 2026 — Science, General'. Third, open the Result gazette for your district — Sukkur, Khairpur or Ghotki — and your group, Science or General. Fourth, search inside the PDF for your seat number: on a computer press Ctrl+F and type the number, or on a phone use the PDF reader's search option. Fifth, read the heading above your seat number to see how many papers you passed, and save or print that page for your own record. If the website is slow in the first hours after the announcement, wait a few minutes and try again; heavy traffic on result day is normal and does not mean your result is missing.

  • Official website: bisesuksindh.edu.pk, then select Results to open Examination Results
  • Find the section headed Secondary School Certificate Part-I, 1st Annual Examination 2026, Science and General
  • Open the gazette for your own district (Sukkur, Khairpur or Ghotki) and group (Science or General)
  • Search the PDF for your seat number exactly as printed on your admit card
  • Read the heading above your seat number: it tells you how many papers you passed, then save the page

SMS code and online roll-number checking: what the board has published

For this result, the board's Examination Results listing publishes gazette PDFs and statistics sheets. No SMS code and no online roll-number search form are shown with the SSC Part-I 2026 listing on the board's official website, so the gazette is the confirmed way to check your result. This post therefore prints no SMS code: a code that the board itself has not published for this result cannot be trusted, and sending your seat number to an unverified code wastes your balance and tells you nothing reliable.

Third-party result websites list different SMS codes for Sukkur board results, and those pages do not agree with each other. Treat every such code as unconfirmed unless BISE Sukkur publishes it in its own result notice on bisesuksindh.edu.pk. The same caution applies to any social-media post that promises your result by name, by message or for a fee. The board's own website is the only source named in this guide, and the gazette page showing your seat number is the record to save.

  • Confirmed method: the official gazette PDFs in the Examination Results section of bisesuksindh.edu.pk
  • No SMS code was published with the SSC Part-I 2026 listing on the board's website
  • No online roll-number search form was shown with this listing
  • Do not send your seat number to any code passed around on social media
  • If the board later publishes an SMS service, it will appear in the board's own notice on its website

Key statistics: 56,509 appeared, 41,182 passed all seven papers

The board's own statistics sheets for the SSC Part-I 1st Annual Examination 2026 give the full picture. Across the two groups, 56,509 candidates appeared and 41,182 passed all seven papers, which is 72.9 per cent of those who appeared. The board does not print a single overall pass percentage on the sheets; it reports how many candidates passed in seven, six, five, four, three, two and one papers, together with fail-in-all-subjects and withheld counts. The group-wise figures below are taken from the official Statistics PDFs published with the gazettes.

In the Science group, 57,614 candidates registered, 1,217 were absent and 56,397 appeared. Of those who appeared, 41,110 passed all seven papers, which is 72.9 per cent. A further 9,393 candidates passed six papers, 3,299 passed five, 1,442 passed four, 598 passed three, 302 passed two and 193 passed one paper. The Science sheet also lists 60 candidates as fail in all subjects and 737 results withheld under CC. In the General group, 118 candidates registered, 6 were absent and 112 appeared. Of those who appeared, 72 passed all seven papers, which is 64.3 per cent. A further 30 passed six papers, 2 passed five, 4 passed four, 1 passed three, 2 passed two and 1 passed one paper, and the General sheet lists 6 candidates as fail in all subjects with no results withheld.

The figures also show where the candidates came from. In the Science group, Khairpur district accounted for 27,093 of the 56,397 candidates who appeared (17,404 boys and 9,689 girls), followed by Sukkur district with 14,970 (9,290 boys and 5,680 girls) and Ghotki district with 14,334 (10,589 boys and 3,745 girls). In the General group, 55 candidates appeared from Sukkur district, 41 from Khairpur district and 16 from Ghotki district. No position-holder list was published with the gazettes or the statistics sheets, so this post names no toppers.

What the gazette categories mean — and the withheld results

The gazette sorts every candidate by how many of the seven papers they passed. 'Passing in Seven (7) Papers' means the candidate cleared every paper of the 9th class examination. 'Passing in Six Papers', 'Five Papers' and so on mean the candidate passed that many papers and still has the remaining papers to clear; those candidates should read the heading carefully rather than assuming a pass or a fail from the total alone. The statistics sheets use the same seven-to-one-paper breakdown, which is why this post reports the board's figures in that form instead of a single pass-or-fail split.

The Science statistics sheet lists 737 results withheld under CC, while the General sheet lists no withheld results. A withheld result has not been cancelled; it is held while the board completes its process. If your seat number does not appear under a passing heading, first search the gazette again to be sure you opened the correct district and group file and typed the seat number exactly. If your result is withheld, contact the BISE Sukkur examination office directly with your seat number and admit card details to find out what is needed, and do not pay any agent who claims to release a withheld result for a fee. Only the board's own process can release it.

  • Passing in seven papers means you cleared every paper of the 9th class examination
  • Passing in fewer papers means those papers are cleared and the rest still need to be passed
  • 737 Science group results are listed as withheld under CC; the General group lists none
  • Search the correct district and group gazette again before assuming your result is missing
  • Withheld candidates should contact the BISE Sukkur examination office with their seat number

What to do next: marksheet, rechecking and the next examination

First, save your result. Download or print the gazette page that shows your seat number, and keep it with your admit card. Your detailed marks card (DMC) and marksheet are issued through your school or the board after the online release, so ask your school office when to collect them. Until the original arrives, the printed gazette page is your record of the result, and it is also the page to show if anyone asks which papers you passed.

Second, if a mark looks wrong, ask about rechecking. No rechecking dates or fee details were published with the result gazettes, so this post states none. Watch the board's official website, bisesuksindh.edu.pk, for the scrutiny or rechecking notice, and ask your school office how to apply once the board announces it. Rechecking re-examines the marking of a paper; it does not re-teach the subject, so it is the right step when a mark looks wrong and the wrong step when the paper simply needs more preparation.

Third, if you still have papers to clear, plan for the next examination opportunity. The board announces its second annual (supplementary) schedule on its official website, and the papers you did not clear are the ones to prepare. Do not wait for the notice to start revising: begin with the papers you did not pass, and ask a teacher to help you prioritise the weakest ones first. For study help with the subjects you need to retake, browse the solved notes on Solved Notes and the full book list on Books, and read more board guides on Blog.

  • Save or print the gazette page showing your seat number, and keep it with your admit card
  • Collect your DMC and marksheet from your school or the board when they are issued
  • Watch bisesuksindh.edu.pk for the rechecking notice; no dates were published with the result
  • If papers are still outstanding, start revising them now and ask a teacher to help you prioritise
  • Never pay an agent who claims to change or release a result; only the board's own process counts

How this result fits the Sindh 9th class season

Sindh's boards do not announce on one shared day. Karachi's secondary board announced its 9th class Science Group (Regular) result on 28 October 2026 and BISE Shaheed Benazirabad announced on 31 October 2026, while BISE Sukkur's SSC Part-I gazettes are dated 23 November 2026. That spread is normal for Sindh: each board finalises its own marking and publishes its own gazettes when they are ready. Students should therefore check only their own board's website and ignore dates, codes and links belonging to other boards.

The same rule holds for every board in the country: a result is real only when the board itself has published it. Sukkur students who also follow the board's intermediate results can compare this release with the board's 11th class announcement earlier this month in our BISE Sukkur 11th Class Result 2026 guide, which was likewise published as dated gazettes on the board's own website. Whatever your district or group, keep the gazette page with your seat number, collect your marks card through your school, and rely on bisesuksindh.edu.pk for every next step.
