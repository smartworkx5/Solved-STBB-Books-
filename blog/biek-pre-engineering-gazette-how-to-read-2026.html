BIEK Pre-Engineering Gazette 2026: How to Read Your Result

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-10-18 · 1034 words · 5 min read

Learning how to read the BIEK Pre-Engineering gazette 2026 is the fastest way to understand your Part 1 result, because the gazette itself is the official result. The Board of Intermediate Education Karachi announced the HSC Part I Science Pre-Engineering Annual Examination 2026 result on 2 October 2026, and published it as a college-wise PDF gazette. There is no separate roll-number search box for this result, so students must open the gazette and search it themselves. This guide explains every step. This guide also explains what each papers-passed grouping means, so you can move from finding your number to planning your next step with confidence.

What the Gazette Actually Is

The gazette is a college-wise PDF listing passing candidates of Pre-Engineering Part 1. It runs to 129 pages, with about 12,584 roll numbers, each under a heading showing papers passed.

Think of the gazette as the result itself, not a supplement to it. The board publishes no separate online search tool for this result, so the PDF is the authoritative record. Open it from the official BIEK website, under recent results, where the Pre-Engineering Part 1 entry is listed.

Step by Step: Finding Your Roll Number

Start by opening the gazette PDF from the official BIEK website, under the recent result declarations. Download the file to your phone or computer rather than reading it in a small browser preview, because a downloaded copy searches faster. Keep your roll number slip beside you so you copy the six-digit number exactly.

Next, use the search function inside your PDF reader. On a computer, press Ctrl and F together and type your six-digit roll number. On a phone, use the search icon in your reader app. Check the match carefully, because similar numbers can appear nearby, and confirm the heading above it.

  • Keep your roll number slip beside you while you search.
  • Type all six digits exactly, with no spaces.
  • Check the heading above your number, not just the number.
  • If the first match looks wrong, keep searching.

Understanding the Papers-Passed Groupings

The gazette groups passing candidates by how many papers they passed, from one paper up to six papers. A student under the six-paper heading has passed all six papers of Part 1. A student under a smaller heading, such as three papers, has passed three and still has the rest to deal with.

Board figures show 15,044 registered, 14,683 appeared, and 6,310 passed all six papers. Those figures describe the whole cohort, not your result, so focus on your own heading.

What Your Gazette Entry Means for Part 2

If your number sits under the six-paper heading, you move into Part 2 study with a clear mind. Use the momentum to build strong routines early, especially in mathematics, physics, and chemistry, where Part 2 builds directly on Part 1. Revisit Part 1 notes briefly at the start of each new topic.

If your entry shows fewer than six papers, treat the result as a map, not a verdict. List the papers you still need to clear, then ask your school about the correct procedure for supplementary papers. Begin Part 2 study at the same time, so you do not fall behind. Our solved notes can help you plan this balance.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

The most common mistake is searching for the wrong roll number, often typed from memory with a digit wrong. Another is stopping at the first similar-looking number instead of checking all six digits and the heading above it. Students also read cropped screenshots from social media instead of the official gazette. Always work from the official PDF itself.

A second trap is trusting rumours about supplementary dates or admissions that circulate before the board announces them. The board announces its own schedule through official channels, and your school can confirm details. For study material, see the books page and our blog.

  • Never rely on a cropped screenshot instead of the full gazette.
  • Do not trust result dates shared by unofficial pages.
  • Keep your roll number slip safe for future applications.
  • Ask your school to confirm any supplementary step.

5 key points you must know

  • The gazette itself is the official BIEK Pre-Engineering Part 1 result.
  • Search the PDF for your exact six-digit roll number.
  • Your heading shows how many papers you passed, from one to six.
  • Students who passed all six papers move fully into Part 2 study.
  • Confirm supplementary and rechecking steps through your school.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find the BIEK Pre-Engineering Part 1 gazette?

Open the official BIEK website and look under recent result declarations for the Pre-Engineering Part 1 entry. Download the PDF from there rather than trusting screenshots shared elsewhere.

How do I search for my roll number inside the gazette?

Download the PDF, then use your reader's search function. On a computer press Ctrl and F and type your six-digit roll number. On a phone, use the search icon in your PDF reader app.

What does it mean if my number is under the six-paper heading?

It means you passed all six papers of Part 1. You can move into Part 2 study with a clear record, while still revising the Part 1 foundations that support the new syllabus.

What if my number is under a smaller papers-passed heading?

It means you passed that many papers and still have others to clear. Ask your school about the correct supplementary procedure, and keep studying Part 2 topics at the same time.
