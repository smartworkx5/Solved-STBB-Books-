Punjab Class 10 Maths Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 672 words · 3 min read

504Solved Exercises

Class 10 Mathematics decides a large part of your matric result, and it is the subject where solved exercises help most. Every question in the Punjab Board book has a fixed method, and once you have worked it once with the steps in front of you, the same method answers every similar question in the paper. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises to build that method, chapter by chapter.

Work every exercise with the steps, not just the answer

Open the Class 10 Mathematics book page and take one exercise at a time. Cover the solution, solve the question yourself, then uncover the steps and compare — step by step, not just the final answer. A right answer with a wrong method will not survive the next question.

Keep a formula sheet for each chapter as you go: every formula used in the solved exercises, written once, with one worked example under it. That sheet becomes your revision for the last week.

Give algebra and trigonometry daily practice

Algebra and trigonometry are the chapters where students lose the most marks, and both are learned by practice, not by reading. Solve five questions a day from these chapters, even on days when you revise other subjects — short daily practice beats a long session once a week.

When a question goes wrong, do not just read the correct solution. Work it again from the start, in writing, until you can reach the answer without looking. The questions you get wrong twice are the ones that appear in your error notebook.

Draw geometry properly and label everything

Geometry and construction questions need a ruler, a compass and a pencil — never freehand. Draw every figure with instruments, label every point and angle, and write the steps of construction beside the figure. A neat, labelled figure is part of the answer, not decoration.

Practise the standard constructions until you can do them without the book: bisecting angles and lines, drawing tangents, and constructing triangles from given measurements. The solved exercises show the steps — repeat them until they are automatic.

Revise with mixed sets and MCQs

In the final month, stop revising chapter by chapter and start mixing: one set of questions from algebra, one from geometry, one from trigonometry, as the paper will mix them. The chapter notes on the Notes page help you spot which chapter a mixed question belongs to.

Test the objective part on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 10 Mathematics book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown — quick questions on formulas and definitions are exactly what the objective section tests.

5 key points you must know

  • Solve each question yourself first, then compare every step with the solved exercise.
  • Keep a formula sheet per chapter with one worked example under each formula.
  • Practise five algebra or trigonometry questions every day, even on other subjects' days.
  • Draw geometry figures with ruler and compass, labelled, with construction steps written beside them.
  • Re-work every question you get wrong until you can solve it without looking.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Punjab Board Class 10 Mathematics solved exercises?

On the Class 10 Mathematics book page. The solved exercises are free to read chapter by chapter, with notes on the Notes page.

How do I prepare for the Class 10 Mathematics board paper?

Work every exercise step by step, keep a formula sheet per chapter, practise algebra and trigonometry daily, and re-work every question you get wrong until you can solve it unaided.

How do I learn geometry constructions for Class 10 Mathematics?

Draw every figure with ruler and compass, labelled, until you can do it without the book. Practise the standard constructions — bisection, tangents, triangles — from the solved exercises.

Can I practise Class 10 Mathematics MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 10 Mathematics book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.
