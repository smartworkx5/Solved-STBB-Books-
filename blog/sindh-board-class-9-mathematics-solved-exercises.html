Sindh Class 9 Mathematics Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 568 words · 3 min read

565Solved Exercises

Class 9 Mathematics is where board Maths truly begins — matrices, algebraic expressions, geometry theorems and trigonometry all appear in one year. Students who solve every exercise step by step find the paper familiar; students who only read solutions lose marks on method. This guide shows how to work through the solved exercises so every chapter is exam-ready.

Solve the example, then the exercise

Open the Class 9 Mathematics book page and take one exercise at a time: read the worked examples, solve them again with the book closed, then attempt the exercise questions. The examples teach the method; the exercise tests it.

Write every step on its own line. In board marking, each correct step earns its mark even if the final answer slips — steps written down are marks protected.

Theorems need figures and reasons

For geometry theorems, draw the figure first, label it, write 'given' and 'to prove', then each step with its reason beside it. A theorem without reasons is an incomplete proof, and examiners mark it that way.

Practise each theorem twice: once with the book open to learn the order of steps, once closed to prove you own it. The second attempt is the one that stays in memory.

Keep formulas on one page per chapter

At the end of each chapter, write every formula on one page with one solved example beside each. Revise that page before starting the next chapter — formulas revised weekly are formulas remembered in the exam hall.

The Class 10 Mathematics solved exercises build directly on these Class 9 chapters, so a strong formula page now saves weeks next year.

Redo wrong questions the next day

Mark every exercise question you got wrong and redo it the next morning before new work. A mistake corrected once in writing rarely returns; a mistake only read returns in the paper.

Keep a small error notebook: the question, where the step went wrong, and the correct step. Revise it every Sunday — it is your personal syllabus of weak points.

5 key points you must know

  • Solve worked examples with the book closed before attempting the exercise.
  • Write every step on its own line — each correct step earns its mark.
  • Draw and label the figure, then write each theorem step with its reason.
  • Keep one formula page per chapter and revise it weekly.
  • Redo wrong questions the next day and record them in an error notebook.

Frequently asked questions

How do I prepare Class 9 Mathematics for the Sindh Board?

One exercise at a time: examples first with the book closed, then the exercise with every step written. Keep a formula page per chapter and redo wrong questions the next day.

Are geometry theorems important in Class 9 Maths?

Yes — theorems carry steady marks. Draw the figure, write given and to-prove, and give a reason beside every step for full marks.

How many hours a day should I give Class 9 Maths?

One focused hour daily beats three hours once a week. Maths is learnt by daily solving, not by weekend reading.

What should I do when an exercise question will not solve?

Read the solved solution once, close the book, and solve it again from memory. If it still fails, note it in your error notebook and ask your teacher the next day.
