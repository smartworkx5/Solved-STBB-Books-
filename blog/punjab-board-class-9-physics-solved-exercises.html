Punjab Board Class 9 Physics Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 689 words · 3 min read

494Solved Notes

Class 9 Physics is the first real physics paper, and it rewards students who work the exercises instead of only reading the chapter. Punjab Board students who solve every numerical in writing — with units at every step — and check against the solved answers find the paper far more predictable than it first looks. This guide shows how to use the Class 9 Physics solved exercises properly.

Work chapter by chapter, in the book's order

Open the Class 9 Physics book page and work each chapter in the order the book gives it — measurements, motion, forces, work and energy, and the rest. Study the chapter's solved examples first, then work the exercise questions yourself before comparing with the solved answers.

Do not jump to the numericals before the concepts. A numerical solved without understanding the concept is a guess; a concept understood makes the numerical a routine calculation. Read the chapter, then work it.

Numericals: units at every step

For every numerical, write the given data, the formula, the substitution with units, and the final answer with its unit. Most lost marks in Physics are unit slips and missing steps, not wrong concepts — and the solved exercises show the full step-by-step working to copy.

Keep a formula sheet with every formula, the meaning of each symbol, and its unit. Revise it daily in the last month: a formula you can write from memory is a numerical you can finish in the paper.

Definitions and short questions

Learn definitions exactly as the book states them, then practise writing them from memory. Short questions in Physics usually ask for a definition, a reason, or a one-line difference — and the solved exercises give the exact wording the paper expects.

For 'why' questions, answer with the reason, not just the fact. A reason stated plainly, in one or two lines, is what earns the mark — memorising the fact alone is only half the answer.

Diagrams and final revision

Draw every diagram the chapter uses — ray diagrams, force diagrams, graphs — with labels, and practise them until you can draw them without the book. A labelled diagram in the paper earns marks even when the words around it are short.

In the final weeks, re-work every numerical you once got wrong and test each chapter on the MCQs practice page. Keep your formula sheet and your error notebook beside you — they hold precisely what you personally need to revise.

5 key points you must know

  • Work chapter by chapter through the solved exercises: study the solved examples first, then work the exercise questions in writing before comparing.
  • For every numerical, write given data, formula, substitution with units, and the final answer with its unit — never skip steps.
  • Keep a formula sheet with every formula, the meaning of each symbol, and its unit, and revise it daily in the last month.
  • Learn definitions exactly as the book states them, and answer 'why' questions with the reason, not just the fact.
  • Practise every labelled diagram until you can draw it without the book, and re-work every numerical you once got wrong in the final weeks.
