Class 7 English Vocabulary: 100 Useful Words

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-11-04 · 1809 words · 9 min read

Class 7 English asks for more than everyday words: reading passages grow longer, grammar terms appear in questions, and answers need thinking words like describe and explain, so this general list gathers useful textbook-level words with simple meanings and example sentences for the Class 7 level. It is not an official answer key for any single lesson, and every word here is chosen because it helps in reading, writing or class discussion. Learn one theme at a time, write your own sentence for each word, and revise weekly through the [solved notes](/solved.html) and [books](/books.html) pages for steady progress.

Reading Words for Class 7

Class 7 reading passages use longer texts than Class 6, so you need words that describe the parts of a book and the work you do with it, and the solved notes pages show how these words appear in real summaries and answers. Chapter means one part of a book. For example, We read the second chapter of the story in class today.. Paragraph means a group of sentences about one idea. For example, Write your answer in one clear paragraph with five sentences.. Summary means a short version giving only the main points. For example, Write a summary of the lesson in your own words.. Example means something that shows how a rule or idea works. For example, The teacher gave an example to explain the new rule..

When you meet a reading word, notice how it is used in the passage, copy the sentence into your notebook, and then try the same word in a sentence about your own school day, because that transfer from reading to writing is what builds real understanding. Exercise means a set of questions for practice. For example, Complete the exercise at the end of the chapter carefully.. Dictionary means a book that gives meanings of words. For example, Use a dictionary to find the meaning of a difficult word.. Sentence means a group of words that makes complete sense. For example, Every sentence in your answer should start with a capital letter.. Story means an account of real or imagined events. For example, The story teaches us that hard work always brings success..

Grammar Words You Must Know

Grammar words name the building blocks of sentences, so learning them helps you understand questions, follow grammar rules and correct your own writing, which is why every Class 7 student should know them before moving to harder work. Verb means a word that shows an action or state. For example, Run, read and sleep are all verbs in these sentences.. Noun means a word that names a person, place or thing. For example, Teacher, school and book are nouns in this sentence.. Adjective means a word that describes a noun. For example, The adjective tall describes the boy in the sentence.. Pronoun means a word used instead of a noun. For example, He and she are pronouns that take the place of names..

Tense and punctuation words also matter in every test, because they decide whether a sentence talks about the past, present or future, and whether it ends correctly, so practise them with short sentences of your own every day. Tense means the form of a verb showing time. For example, The present tense talks about actions happening now.. Punctuation means marks such as full stops and commas. For example, Use punctuation carefully so your sentences are easy to read.. Plural means the form of a word for more than one. For example, The plural of book is books, and of child is children.. Opposite means a word with the reverse meaning. For example, Hot is the opposite of cold in this pair of words..

School Life Words

School life words help you talk about places, people and routines in your school, and because you meet them every day, they are perfect for speaking practice with classmates during breaks and group work. Library means a room or building with books for reading. For example, I borrow story books from the school library every week.. Laboratory means a room for science experiments. For example, Students work carefully in the science laboratory.. Assembly means a gathering of students and teachers. For example, The morning assembly starts with a short prayer and news.. Principal means the head of a school. For example, The principal visits every classroom once a week..

Examination words can feel stressful, but knowing them early removes fear, because you understand what a timetable means, what revision involves and what a result shows, so you can plan your study calmly instead of worrying. Timetable means a plan showing times for lessons. For example, Our timetable shows English first on Monday morning.. Examination means a formal test of knowledge. For example, The examination will test reading, writing and grammar skills.. Revision means studying again to remember well. For example, Revision the night before the test helps you stay confident.. Result means the outcome of a test or examination. For example, Good results come from regular study, not from last-day worry..

Daily Life and the World

Words about journeys, markets, weather and health connect English to life outside school, and when you learn them you can describe your town, your family routines and the seasons in simple, correct English sentences. Journey means a trip from one place to another. For example, Our journey to the city took two hours by bus.. Market means a place where goods are bought and sold. For example, Mother buys fresh vegetables from the market every week.. Neighbour means a person who lives near you. For example, Our neighbour is kind and helps us in difficult times.. Weather means the state of the sky and air. For example, The weather today is cool and pleasant for playing outside..

Health and habit words are equally useful, because they appear in essays, letters and health lessons, and they also help you talk about your own routine honestly, from exercise and sleep to food and daily habits. Season means one of the four parts of the year. For example, Summer is the hottest season in our part of the country.. Health means the state of being free from illness. For example, Good health comes from clean food, exercise and enough sleep.. Energy means the strength to do work or be active. For example, A good breakfast gives us energy for the whole school day.. Habit means something you do regularly. For example, Reading for twenty minutes daily is a very good habit..

Thinking Words for Better Answers

Thinking words help you write better answers, because questions often ask you to describe, explain, compare or imagine, and if you know what each word asks for, you can shape your answer correctly instead of guessing, with extra practice from blog. Describe means to say what something is like. For example, Describe your school in five clear sentences.. Explain means to make an idea clear and easy to understand. For example, Please explain the rule with one simple example.. Compare means to show how two things are alike or different. For example, Compare summer and winter in four sentences.. Imagine means to form a picture in your mind. For example, Imagine a village with clean streets and happy children..

Finish by choosing one thinking word each day and writing a short answer with it, then checking your work for spelling and full stops, because steady daily practice builds the confident writing that Class 7 tests reward. Remember means to keep something in your mind. For example, Remember the meanings by revising them every evening.. Decide means to choose something after thinking. For example, Decide your study time and follow it every day.. Improve means to make something better. For example, Daily practice will improve your English writing quickly.. Respect means to show honour and care for others. For example, Respect your teachers, elders and classmates every day..

5 key points you must know

  • Learn reading words first, then grammar words.
  • A word is learned only when you can use it in a sentence.
  • Revise older words every week, not only new ones.
  • Use thinking words to match what a question asks.
  • Keep a personal word notebook with meanings and examples.

Frequently asked questions

How is Class 7 vocabulary different from Class 6?

Class 7 vocabulary adds textbook and grammar words such as chapter, paragraph, tense and summary, because reading passages grow longer and questions start asking students to describe, explain and compare instead of only naming everyday things. Keep this answer beside your lesson notes, and read it once more before the next test so the idea stays fresh and ready.

Is this list from a specific Class 7 lesson?

No, this is a general word list for the Class 7 level, covering reading, grammar, school life and daily life, and it is not presented as an official answer key or as the vocabulary of any single lesson in any book. Use the idea in one real sentence or task today, because using knowledge turns it from a fact into a skill you own.

How can I remember grammar terms easily?

Learn each term with one short definition and one example sentence, then spot that term in your textbook the same week, because finding a term in real reading fixes it in memory far better than only memorising a definition. Revise older answers together with new ones, so new learning connects with what you already know instead of replacing it.

What should I do when I forget a word in a test?

Stay calm, use a simpler word with a similar meaning, and continue your answer, because a clear simple sentence earns more marks than a blank space, and you can revise the forgotten word properly after the test. Stay patient with slow progress, because steady small steps in English build more lasting skill than rushed cramming before tests.

How often should I revise my word notebook?

Revise new words the next day, again after three days, and once more at the end of the week, because spaced revision turns short-term learning into lasting vocabulary that stays ready for examinations. End each study session by recalling the main point aloud without looking, since recall practice strengthens memory far more than re-reading.
