Sindh Class 7 English MCQs with Answers

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 513 words · 3 min read

528Subject Guides

Class 7 English MCQs test the small things: the right tense, the right preposition, the meaning of a word in the lesson. Students who only read the lessons lose marks on grammar rules they never practised. This guide shows how to practise Class 7 English MCQs chapter by chapter so the rules stay ready for the paper.

Practise grammar MCQs by rule

Open the Class 7 English book page and work the grammar MCQs one rule at a time: tenses, articles, prepositions, subject-verb agreement. Read the rule, then answer ten MCQs on that rule before moving on.

For every MCQ you get wrong, write the rule in a notebook in your own words. A rule you can write is a rule you will remember in the paper.

Build vocabulary from the lessons

Every lesson gives new words with their meanings. Keep a vocabulary notebook — word, meaning, synonym and one sentence of your own — and add five words after each lesson.

Test yourself weekly from the word side and from the meaning side. Vocabulary MCQs are free marks for students who keep the notebook.

Read the passage before the options

For comprehension MCQs, read the passage twice before looking at the options, and underline the sentence that answers each question. The answer is always in the passage — never guess from memory.

Practise one unseen passage a week the same way as the paper: read, underline, then answer. Speed comes from practice, not from rushing.

Practise a full MCQ set under time

Use the MCQs practice page to test each chapter, then sit with a full mixed-chapter set and a timer in the final weeks, notes closed.

Mark every question you guessed, even the ones you got right, and revise the rule behind each one the same day.

5 key points you must know

  • Practise grammar MCQs by rule — write the rule down for every wrong answer.
  • Keep a vocabulary notebook with meaning, synonym and one sentence for each word.
  • Find the comprehension answer in the passage before looking at the options.
  • Practise one unseen passage a week the same way as the paper.
  • Practise a full MCQ set under time in the final weeks with notes closed.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I practise Sindh Board Class 7 English MCQs?

On the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 7 English book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.

How do I prepare for Class 7 English MCQs?

Revise one grammar topic at a time, build vocabulary from the lessons, find comprehension answers in the passage first, and test yourself under time.

How do I stop guessing in English MCQs?

For every guessed answer, read the explanation and write the rule in your notebook. Guessing without learning the rule repeats the same mistake in the paper.

Are the Class 7 English MCQs on the site chapter-wise?

Yes. Open the Class 7 English book page and use the MCQs practice page to practise chapter by chapter.
