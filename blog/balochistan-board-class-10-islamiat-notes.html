Balochistan Class 10 Islamiat Notes

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 563 words · 3 min read

557Solved Notes

Class 10 Islamiat is the Matric paper where the easiest marks are also the most often lost — students write the translation without the tashreeh, or the Seerat answer without the sequence. This guide shows how to prepare each section of the Balochistan Board Class 10 Islamiat paper so the easy marks are never lost.

Translation first, tashreeh second

Open the Class 10 Islamiat book page and for every selected Ayat learn the translation first, word by word, then the tashreeh in your own words. A correct translation with a short clear tashreeh earns full marks; a long tashreeh with a wrong translation does not.

Write each Ayat's translation in your notebook once a week. Writing it is the only way to know whether you actually know it.

Hadith answers need the matn and the lesson

For every Hadith, write the Arabic matn, then the Urdu translation, then two lines of tashreeh, and finish with one line about what the Hadith teaches in daily life. This four-step format is the safest way to get full marks in the Hadith section.

Learn the matn of the short Ahadith by heart and the meaning of the long ones. In the exam, even one correct line of Arabic shows the examiner the answer comes from the Hadith.

Seerat answers in sequence

Answer Seerat questions in order: event, year, reason, result. For example, the Hijrat: reason (persecution in Makkah), year (13 Nabvi), result (the first Islamic state in Madina). The Sindh Class 8 Islamiat solved exercises practise the same sequence for the shorter Seerat questions.

Practise the sequence on three events until it is automatic. In the exam you will write the answer in order without spending time deciding the order.

Akhlaq: one reference, one example

For Akhlaq chapters, each answer needs two things: one Quranic or Hadith reference and one practical example from daily life. Keep a list of five references — one per Akhlaq topic — and reuse them.

The example should come from a student's own life: honesty at school, respect for parents at home, good manners with neighbours. A real example proves understanding; a memorised line only proves memory.

5 key points you must know

  • Learn the Ayat translation word by word first, then the tashreeh in your own words.
  • Write Hadith answers in four steps: matn, translation, tashreeh, daily-life lesson.
  • Answer Seerat questions in sequence: event, year, reason, result.
  • Keep five references ready — one per Akhlaq topic — and reuse them.
  • Write each Ayat translation in your notebook once a week to test whether you know it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I prepare Class 10 Islamiat for the Balochistan Board?

Translation first, then tashreeh; Hadith in four steps; Seerat in sequence; and one reference plus one daily-life example for every Akhlaq answer.

Should I memorise the Arabic matn of every Hadith?

Learn the short Ahadith by heart and the meaning of the long ones — even one correct line of Arabic strengthens the answer.

What is the best order for a Seerat answer?

Event, year, reason, result — the same order every time, so it becomes automatic in the exam.

How do I revise Islamiat in the last week?

One chapter a day from your notes, writing each Ayat translation once, and revising the five Akhlaq references.
