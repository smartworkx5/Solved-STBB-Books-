Sindh Board Class 8 English Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 740 words · 4 min read

493Solved Notes

Class 8 English is the year reading, grammar and writing come together: longer lessons, real comprehension questions, and composition that counts in the paper. Sindh Board students who work through the solved exercises lesson by lesson — answering in writing and checking against the solved answers — build the habit that carries them through Classes 9 and 10. This guide shows how to use the Class 8 English solved exercises properly.

Work lesson by lesson, in the book's order

Open the Class 8 English book page and work each lesson in the order the book gives it. Read the lesson once for meaning, then answer every exercise question in writing before you look at the solved answer — the comparison is where the learning happens.

Do not skip the reading lessons to reach the grammar. Comprehension questions come from the lessons themselves, and the vocabulary of each lesson returns in the paper. A lesson worked fully is finished; a lesson only read is a lesson you will not remember in the exam.

Grammar: learn the rule, then use it

For every grammar exercise, write the rule in one line before you start: tense, voice, direct and indirect speech, prepositions. The solved exercises show the rule applied — study the pattern in the answer, then cover it and apply the same rule to a fresh sentence of your own.

Keep a grammar notebook with one page per rule and three of your own examples under each. That notebook, not the textbook, is what you revise the night before the paper — and it is built from the exercises you actually worked.

Comprehension and vocabulary

For comprehension questions, find the exact lines in the lesson that answer the question and write the answer in your own words, keeping the lesson's facts exact. Short answers should be complete sentences — a half-line fragment loses marks even when the fact is right.

Learn the vocabulary of each lesson as you go: write each new word with its meaning and one sentence of your own. Ten words a lesson, revised weekly, is a vocabulary that stays with you — and it shows in the composition section too.

Composition and revision

Practise one paragraph or letter a week from the composition exercises, timed, and compare it with the solved model. Good composition is a habit of planning first — three points, in order — and then writing in clear, simple sentences.

In the last two weeks, revise with the Notes page beside you and test each lesson on the MCQs practice page. Every wrong answer points at a lesson to re-read — and the error notebook you kept is the fastest revision you own.

5 key points you must know

  • Work lesson by lesson through the solved exercises: read the lesson, answer in writing, then compare with the solved answer and note what yours missed.
  • Answer comprehension questions in your own words but keep the lesson's facts exact — and write complete sentences, not fragments.
  • For grammar, write the rule in one line first, study the pattern in the solved answer, then apply it to a fresh sentence of your own.
  • Learn ten new words per lesson with a meaning and a sentence of your own, and revise them weekly.
  • Practise one paragraph or letter a week, timed, with a three-point plan before you write — and test each lesson with MCQs in the final weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Sindh Board Class 8 English solved exercises?

On the Class 8 English book page. The solved exercises are free to read lesson by lesson, with notes on the Notes page.

How do I answer the comprehension questions in Class 8 English?

First read the lesson once for meaning, then find the exact lines that answer the question. Write the answer in your own words in a complete sentence, keeping the lesson's facts exact.

How do I prepare the grammar section of Class 8 English?

Learn one rule at a time and practise it until you can apply it to a new sentence. Keep a grammar notebook with one page per rule and three of your own examples under each.

Can I practise Class 8 English MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 8 English book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.
