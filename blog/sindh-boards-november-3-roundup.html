Sindh Boards Roundup: 3 November 2026

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-11-03 · 1490 words · 7 min read

Tuesday 3 November 2026 is a busy day across Sindh and Punjab board news, with examinations beginning, examinations continuing, results settling in, an inquiry window running, and a curriculum review under way. This roundup gathers the day's verified stories in one place, each with its dates and figures stated plainly, so that students, parents and teachers can see the whole picture without chasing rumours. Every fact here comes from the verified snapshot for today, and anything not verified is left out rather than guessed.

Punjab Second Annual Exams Begin Today

The biggest story of the day is the start of the Punjab HSSC second annual examinations. Part-II (12th class) theory papers begin today, Tuesday 3 November 2026, and run to 16 November 2026 across all nine Punjab boards: Lahore, Rawalpindi, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Multan, Sargodha, Sahiwal, Bahawalpur and DG Khan. Morning papers begin at 9:00 AM and evening papers at 2:00 PM, with Friday evening papers at 2:30 PM. Part-I (11th class) theory papers follow from 17 to 28 November 2026, and practical examinations run from 1 to 11 December 2026.

Students appearing today should carry their roll number slip, arrive early, and confirm their centre and session from their own slip rather than a friend's. The admission windows for these examinations, which ran from 24 September to 17 October, are closed, so no fresh applications are expected at this stage. On results, no individual board has announced a supplementary result date in the sources checked. A preliminary schedule reported in Dawn on 18 January 2026 puts a supplementary intermediate result around 12 January 2027, but that is only a reported schedule expectation, not a confirmed board announcement.

BIEK Supplementary Exams Continue, Part-I Results One Week On

In Karachi, the BIEK HSC Part-I and Part-II supplementary examinations continue in their second week, running from 28 October to 21 November 2026 in a single afternoon shift from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, with reporting at 1:30 PM. More than 21,000 students are appearing across 49 centres. Admit cards are available at admitcard.biek.edu.pk using a 16-digit challan number and matric roll number. No supplementary result date has been announced, and a result can only come after the series ends on 21 November 2026.

Meanwhile, the BIEK Part-I Annual Examination 2026 results announced on 26 October 2026 are now one week old, and the picture is settling. Calculated pass-all-papers rates from the verified figures are about 49 percent for Pre-Medical, about 49 percent for Science General, about 41 percent for Commerce Regular and about 35 percent for Arts Regular. Students who did not pass all papers should read their marksheet paper by paper against the 40 percent Ungraded rule, ask their college about process and support, and begin revising weak papers now rather than waiting for a supplementary notice that has not yet been announced for their cohort.

Benazirabad Class 9 Result and the Hyderabad Inquiry Window

BISE Shaheed Benazirabad announced its Class IX Annual Examinations 2026 results on 31 October 2026. In the Science Group, 52,260 students registered, 51,546 appeared and 41,425 were successful, an overall pass rate of 79.02 percent, while the General Group recorded an overall pass rate of 52 percent. Students can check results through the board's official website, www.bisesba.edu.pk, and its Android application. For successful students, the next step is Class 10 preparation; for students with papers below 40 percent, the Ungraded rule means those papers need attention, and teachers and schools are the right first advisers.

In Hyderabad, the inquiry committee headed by Professor Dr Arbeela Bhutto must report within 30 days of its 23 October 2026 order, and that window is now running. The inquiry concerns alleged irregularities in the Class XII pre-medical and pre-engineering results, and those allegations are not proven findings. Results have not been cancelled, and no student should be named in connection with the inquiry. Sources warn that Class IX and XI result compilation could be delayed further, and certificate issuance is delayed by missing signatures, so waiting students should rely only on official announcements and keep their documents ready.

STBB Curriculum Review: Creativity, Critical Thinking and Tolerance

The curriculum story of the day comes from the Sindh Textbook Board, where a curriculum review committee has held its first meeting on the directives of Sindh Minister for Education Syed Sardar Ali Shah, chaired by STBB Chairman Parvez Ahmed Baloch. After reviewing the 12th-grade Sindhi textbook, experts shared observations and suggested revisions in line with contemporary educational needs. The committee emphasised including critical thinking, creativity and tolerance in school curricula, and recommended a comprehensive review of all STBB publications.

The standing context behind the review is large: the STBB 2026-27 textbooks, under the Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Textbook Series from Early Childhood to Grade 10, comprise 195 titles in Sindhi, Urdu and English, with 52 million sets, and distribution was inaugurated on 19 February 2026. The revised curriculum is expected from the next academic year. For students, the practical meaning is that textbooks will gradually reflect more thinking, creativity and tolerance, while current books remain the basis for current examinations. What to Watch Next, and What to Ignore

In the coming days, the stories to watch are concrete and official: the progress of the Punjab Part-II papers through to 16 November, the start of the 11th class papers on 17 November, the continuation of the BIEK supplementary series to 21 November, and any official communication from BISE Hyderabad as the inquiry window advances. Each of these will produce genuine news when the boards themselves announce it, and this roundup series will report it as it is verified.

What to ignore is equally clear. Ignore any Punjab supplementary result date presented as confirmed, since only a reported preliminary schedule around 12 January 2027 exists. Ignore any BIEK supplementary result date, since none has been announced. Ignore any Hyderabad Class IX or XI result date, since none is confirmed. Ignore any claim that Hyderabad results have been cancelled or that any named student is involved in wrongdoing, since the inquiry has made no findings. A roundup is only useful when it separates verified news from noise, and on 3 November 2026, that separation is the most valuable service this article can offer.
