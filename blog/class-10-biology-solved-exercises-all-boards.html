Class 10 Biology Solved Exercises — All Boards Guide

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 735 words · 4 min read

489Solved Notes

Class 10 Biology is one of the highest-scoring science papers — and the one where careful preparation shows fastest, because every question has a model answer that can be learned and reproduced. The chapters are the same across Sindh, Punjab and KPK: inheritance, human physiology, ecosystems and biotechnology. What differs is each board's book, its examples and its exercise questions. This guide shows how to use your own board's solved exercises for the best result.

One subject, three boards — study your own book

Class 10 Biology covers the same ground on every board: inheritance and variation, the nervous and endocrine systems, reproduction, evolution, ecosystems and environment, and biotechnology. The concepts are shared, but each board's book has its own lessons, examples and exercise questions — and the exam is set from your board's book.

Open your board's book page: Sindh, Punjab or KPK. The solved exercises are free to read on each page, with chapter notes and downloadable PDFs on the Notes page.

Inheritance: the chapter that decides the paper

Inheritance and variation is the chapter where the most marks are gained — and lost. Its questions are exact: terms like gene, allele, homozygous and heterozygous, and ratios from genetic crosses. Learn the terms precisely, one pair at a time, and never guess a ratio: draw the cross, write the gametes, fill the Punnett square, and read the ratio from your own diagram.

Practise every exercise question in this chapter in writing. A ratio calculated on paper is a mark earned; a ratio guessed from memory is a mark risked.

Physiology: learn the diagrams

The human physiology chapters — nervous system, endocrine system, reproduction — are diagram chapters. Draw each one yourself: the brain, the nephron, the endocrine glands. Label every part and write one line for what it does. Most exam questions in these chapters ask you to name a part or state its function, and a diagram you drew answers both.

Read the solved exercises for each system after drawing it, and check your labels against the answers. The comparison fixes the names that are easy to confuse — and in Biology, a wrong name is a lost mark.

Ecosystems, biotechnology and the final weeks

Ecosystems and biotechnology are definition chapters: learn the definitions exactly as the book gives them, then add one example from daily life. An ecosystem defined in the book's words, plus the example of a pond or a field near your home, is an answer the examiner can mark full.

In the final weeks, test each chapter on the MCQs practice page for your board's book. On the final day, revise only your error notebook and your glossary of confused terms — they contain exactly what you personally were getting wrong.

5 key points you must know

  • Always study your own board's book — the chapters match across boards, but the lessons, examples and exercise questions do not.
  • Study chapter by chapter in the book's order: inheritance, nervous and endocrine systems, reproduction, evolution, ecosystems, biotechnology.
  • For inheritance questions, draw the cross and read the ratio from your own Punnett square. A drawn cross turns a ratio question into a calculation.
  • Draw the key diagrams yourself — brain, nephron, food chains — label every part and write one line for its function.
  • Test each chapter with MCQs after its solved exercises, and write every missed fact as one line in an error notebook.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Class 10 Biology solved exercises for my board?

On the book pages for each board: Sindh, Punjab and KPK. The solved exercises are free to read on every board's page.

Are Class 10 Biology solved exercises the same for every board?

No. The chapters are the same across boards — inheritance, physiology, ecosystems, biotechnology — but the lessons, examples and exercise questions differ. Always study your own board's book.

How do I handle inheritance and ratio questions in Biology?

Draw the cross: write the gametes, fill the Punnett square, and read the ratio from your own diagram. Never guess a ratio from memory when a square gives it to you.

Can I practise Class 10 Biology MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose your board's Class 10 Biology book and practise chapter by chapter; every MCQ shows the correct answer with an explanation.
