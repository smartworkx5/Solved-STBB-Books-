Sindh Class 8 Social Studies Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 682 words · 3 min read

512Solved Exercises

Class 8 Social Studies covers geography, history and civics in one book — and students lose marks when they treat it as a book to read once and forget. The paper asks for facts, places, dates and reasons, and the solved exercises give all of them chapter by chapter. This guide shows how to learn the book so the facts stay until the paper.

Learn the facts chapter by chapter

Open the Class 8 Social Studies book page and work one chapter at a time. Read the lesson, then answer the exercise questions in writing before checking the solution — names, places, dates and definitions must be learned exactly, and writing them fixes them in memory.

Keep a facts notebook: one page per chapter, with the key names, places and dates listed in order. Revise it every week — a fact revised four times is a fact remembered in the paper.

Draw the maps and learn the places

Geography chapters are tested through maps: rivers, mountains, cities, provinces and neighbours. Draw the map of Pakistan from the notes, mark the places the chapter names, and label each one. Then close the book and draw it again — a map you can draw from memory is a map you know.

Learn places in groups, not one by one: the rivers together, the mountain ranges together, the cities of each province together. Groups are easier to remember than a long list.

Learn history as a story in order

History chapters are easiest to learn as a story: what happened first, what happened next, and why. Read each chapter as a story in order, then write the main events with their dates on a timeline. A timeline you made yourself is easier to remember than a list of dates from the book.

For every event, learn the reason as well as the date. The paper asks why things happened as often as when — and a student who knows the reason can answer even when the exact date slips.

Revise with the notes and MCQs

In the final weeks, revise the chapter notes on the Notes page — one chapter a day, facts notebook open beside you — and test yourself with the solved exercises and the book closed. Whatever you cannot answer goes into a short revision list for the last days.

Finish with the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 8 Social Studies book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question — it catches the small facts on places, dates and civics that long-answer revision misses.

5 key points you must know

  • Answer every exercise question in writing before checking the solved answer.
  • Keep a facts notebook — one page per chapter with key names, places and dates.
  • Draw the map of Pakistan with labelled places until you can draw it from memory.
  • Learn history as a story in order, with a timeline of events and their reasons.
  • Revise one chapter a day in the final weeks and test yourself with the book closed.
