Is That Board Result Real? How to Verify a BIEK Announcement

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-10-26 · 1777 words · 9 min read

Every result season brings the same problem for Karachi's intermediate students: a message, a post or a website claims a board result is out, or names a date for it, and students cannot tell whether the claim is real. As of 26 October 2026, the position for BIEK's Part-I groups is clear but widely misunderstood. The Pre-Engineering Part-I result was announced on 2 October 2026, while the Pre-Medical, Commerce and Humanities Part-I groups have not been officially announced, and no official date has been published for them. Yet aggregator and result websites keep repeating that these results are expected by the end of October, which many students misread as a board date. This guide teaches you how to verify any BIEK announcement in minutes, so you stop acting on rumours and start acting on facts.

What Is Actually Announced — and What Is Not

The first step in verifying any result claim is to separate what the board has said from what other websites are guessing. For BIEK Part-I in 2026, the verified position is that only the Pre-Engineering group has been announced, on 2 October 2026, while Pre-Medical, Commerce and Humanities Part-I remain unannounced as of 26 October 2026. No official date has been published for those remaining groups, and that absence of a date is itself a fact worth remembering. A claim that names a date for one of these groups is therefore either a guess or a fabrication, unless it can be traced to the board itself.

This distinction matters because students make real decisions on the basis of result news: they stop studying, they pay for scrutiny, they book travel, or they worry for days over nothing. When you hear a claim, ask yourself one question before anything else, which is whether the board has actually announced it. If the answer is no, then the claim is at best an expectation and at worst a rumour, and neither should change your plans. Keep your study routine going, keep checking the official source described below, and let the board's own announcement, when it comes, be the thing that changes your schedule. The status guide for remaining BIEK Part-I groups tracks exactly which groups are out and which are pending.

Why Aggregator Dates Are Not Board Dates

Aggregator and result websites exist to attract visitors, and a page that says a result is expected soon attracts far more visitors than a page that honestly says no date is known. That is why sites such as kentoolkit.com and resultinfo.pk describe the pending BIEK Part-I groups as expected by the end of October, and why other sites copy the same wording until it looks like a consensus. It is not a consensus; it is one unverified expectation repeated many times, and repetition does not turn it into a board announcement. The board has published no date, and no number of websites repeating the same guess changes that.

Learning to spot this pattern protects you in every result season, not just this one. Watch for wording such as expected, likely, probably or tentative, which signals a guess rather than a fact, and watch for pages that never link to or quote a board notice. A genuine board announcement carries the board's name, a date, and usually a notice or press release that your college can confirm, while a rumour carries only confidence and urgency. If a page claims a result is out but cannot show you the board's own notice, close the page and check the official source instead. Students who learn this habit early save themselves weeks of false alarms across matric, intermediate and university admissions alike.

A related trap is the screenshot: a cropped image of a result page, a fake notification or an edited board notice can look convincing in a chat group. Screenshots can be edited in seconds, and a forwarded image proves nothing about what the board has announced. Always go to the source yourself rather than trusting a picture of the source, and remember that a result that is genuinely out will be findable on the board's own portal by every student, not only by the one person sharing the screenshot.

The One-Place Check: biek.edu.pk

When you want the truth about a BIEK announcement, there is one place to check first: the board's own portal, biek.edu.pk. Type the address yourself rather than clicking a link in a message, because lookalike domains and misleading search results are part of how rumours spread. Once on the portal, look for the board's notices or result section, and read what is actually there rather than what a search snippet promised you would find. If the group you are waiting for is not listed, then it has not been announced, however confidently other websites claim otherwise.

Make this check a habit with a fixed time, such as once in the morning and once in the evening, rather than refreshing pages all day. Constant refreshing does not make a result appear sooner, and it feeds anxiety that makes waiting much harder than it needs to be. Between checks, your job is to study, and the study plan for waiting students in the guide on using the wait as a head start is a better use of the same hours. If you ever find a claim on the portal itself, read the full notice carefully, note the date and the groups it covers, and then tell your classmates with a link to the notice rather than a screenshot, so they can verify it for themselves.

Why BIEK Groups Come Out Separately

One reason rumours flourish is that students expect all results on a single day, and when one group appears, they assume the rest must follow immediately. BIEK does not work that way: the board releases each group separately, on its own schedule, as the checking and compilation for that group is completed. That is why Pre-Engineering Part-I could be announced on 2 October 2026 while Pre-Medical, Commerce and Humanities Part-I were still pending weeks later, and why the Part-II groups of 2026 came out across July, August and September rather than together. Separate release is normal board practice, not a delay or a problem.

Understanding this pattern changes how you read every rumour. A claim that all remaining groups will appear on one particular day contradicts how the board actually releases results, and should be treated with extra scepticism. Equally, the announcement of one group tells you nothing about the date of the next group, so a friend's Pre-Engineering result is not evidence that your Pre-Medical result is imminent. Judge each group on its own announcement, check the portal for that group specifically, and resist the temptation to convert one group's news into another group's date. Patience here is not passivity; it is the discipline of waiting for facts instead of acting on noise.

Your Daily Two-Minute Verification Habit

Put the whole method together into a habit that takes two minutes and saves days of worry. When a claim reaches you, first ask whether it names the board as its source, then check biek.edu.pk yourself, then confirm with your college notice board if anything is unclear. If all three agree, the claim is real and you can act on it; if any one of them disagrees or is silent, the claim is unverified and your plans should not change. Share only verified news, and when you share it, share the source so your classmates can check it too, because a class that verifies together is much harder to mislead than a class that forwards everything.

Finally, remember that a rumour's damage is not only wasted time. Students have paid fees for processes they did not need, travelled to colleges for results that were not out, and lost sleep over dates that never existed, all because a confident post was mistaken for a board announcement. The verification habit costs almost nothing and prevents all of that, and it is a skill you will use far beyond this result season, in admissions, scholarships and job announcements too. The board will announce your result when it is ready, and when it does, the notice will be on biek.edu.pk for every student to see. Until then, trust the portal, trust your college, and let the rumours pass. For the wider picture of which board handles what, see BSEK versus BIEK.

Frequently asked questions

Has BIEK announced the Pre-Medical Part-I result?

No. As of 26 October 2026, Pre-Medical, Commerce and Humanities Part-I have not been officially announced, and no official date has been published for them.

Is the end of October date on result websites a board date?

No. It is an unverified expectation repeated by aggregator sites, not a date published by BIEK. Treat it as a guess until the board itself announces a date.

Where should I check for a real BIEK announcement?

On the board's own portal, biek.edu.pk, typed by yourself, and on your college notice board. If the group is not listed there, it has not been announced.

Why did Pre-Engineering come out while other groups are still pending?

BIEK releases each group separately as its checking and compilation are completed. Pre-Engineering Part-I was announced on 2 October 2026; the other Part-I groups follow on their own schedule.

Should I trust a screenshot of a result announcement?

No. Screenshots can be edited easily. Always visit the board portal yourself and look for the board's own dated notice before believing or sharing a claim.
