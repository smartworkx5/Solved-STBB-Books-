KPK Class 10 English Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 557 words · 3 min read

546Solved Exercises

Class 10 English is a board paper where grammar and writing carry as much weight as the lessons, yet students spend nearly all their time on the lessons alone. These solved exercises cover lessons, grammar, vocabulary and writing together. This guide shows how to use them so every section of the paper is prepared.

Read the lesson, then answer — never the other way round

Open the Class 10 English book page and read each lesson slowly before touching its exercise, looking up every word you do not know. An exercise answered from a lesson you understood is worth ten answered from memory.

After reading, close the book and say the lesson's main idea in one sentence. If you cannot, read it once more before starting the exercise.

Grammar one rule at a time, with five examples each

Take one grammar rule at a time — tenses, active and passive voice, direct and indirect speech — and write five examples of your own for it. A rule with your own examples stays; a rule only read is forgotten.

Do the grammar exercises in the solved book daily, a few questions at a time. Grammar marks are the easiest to secure because the questions follow fixed patterns.

Vocabulary: learn words in the lesson's sentences

Learn new words inside the sentences where they appear, not from a separate list. A word learnt in its sentence is remembered with its meaning and its use together.

Keep a vocabulary notebook: word, meaning, and one sentence of your own. Revise it every two days — ten minutes is enough.

Writing: outline first, then write

For essays, letters and applications, make a short outline before writing: introduction, three points, conclusion. An outline keeps the writing in order and stops it from wandering.

Practise one piece of writing every week and check it against the solved format — address, date, salutation and closing in a letter; heading and paragraphs in an essay. Format marks are free marks.

5 key points you must know

  • Read each lesson slowly before its exercise, looking up every unknown word.
  • Learn one grammar rule at a time and write five examples of your own for it.
  • Learn vocabulary inside the lesson's sentences, with one sentence of your own per word.
  • Make a short outline before every essay, letter or application.
  • Practise one piece of writing every week and check it against the solved format.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use Class 10 English solved exercises for the board exam?

Read the lesson first, then answer the exercise from understanding — and learn one grammar rule at a time with five examples of your own.

Which part of the Class 10 English paper do students neglect most?

Grammar and writing. Lessons get all the time, but grammar and writing carry as much weight, so give them a daily slot.

How do I learn English vocabulary for exams?

Learn words inside the sentences where they appear, keep a notebook with the word, its meaning and one sentence of your own, and revise it every two days.

How should I write an essay in the board exam?

Make a short outline first — introduction, three points, conclusion — then write in order. An outline keeps the essay from wandering.
