Sindh Boards in November 2026: Supplementary Exams, Punjab Papers and the Hyderabad Report

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-10-31 · 1796 words · 9 min read

November 2026 is a crowded month for board students connected to Sindh. In Karachi, the BIEK supplementary examinations that began on 28 October continue until 21 November, while next door in Punjab, second annual examinations run through the whole month, affecting Sindh students with family or admission links across the province border. Meanwhile, the inquiry into alleged irregularities at BISE Hyderabad, ordered on 23 October, is due to report within 30 days, which places its report in late November. This guide puts the month together in one place: what is happening, when, and what students should watch, without guessing at anything that has not been announced.

BIEK Supplementary Examinations Run Until 21 November

The main Sindh event of November is the continuation of the BIEK supplementary examinations, which began on Wednesday 28 October 2026 and run until Saturday 21 November 2026. All papers are held in a single afternoon shift from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, with reporting at 1:30 PM, covering Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, Science General, Commerce, Arts and Humanities, and Home Economics. For Karachi intermediate students, November is therefore largely a month of sitting papers, with the rhythm of afternoon papers and gap-day revision continuing from late October. Students should keep their admit cards safe and their paper dates marked, because a three-week window is long enough for a date to be forgotten.

Two related BIEK dates also fall in November and deserve attention. The HSC Part-I Pre-Engineering scrutiny window, which opened on 19 October, closes on 18 November 2026, with a fee of Rs 1,000 per paper, so Pre-Engineering students considering scrutiny must act before that deadline passes. And no supplementary result date has been announced by BIEK, which means November will end without a result for supplementary candidates, however much they may hope otherwise. The result can only follow the last paper, and the board's Recent Result Declaration page at biek.edu.pk is where a genuine announcement will appear when it is ready.

Punjab Papers Through November: Why Sindh Students Should Know

Punjab's second annual examinations run through almost the whole of November: Part-II theory from 3 to 16 November, then Part-I theory from 17 to 28 November, with morning papers at 9:00 AM, evening papers at 2:00 PM, and Friday evening papers at 2:30 PM. Practical examinations follow from 1 to 11 December on the Multan schedule. Subject examples from the Multan sheet include 12th English Compulsory on 4 November, Mathematics or Biology on 6 November, Computer Science on 10 November, Chemistry on 12 November, and Physics on 16 November, followed by 11th English Compulsory on 18 November, Computer Science on 23 November, Mathematics or Biology on 25 November, and Physics on 27 November. Other boards share the 3 November start but students must confirm their own board's sheet.

Sindh students may wonder why Punjab dates belong in a Sindh calendar, and the answer is practical: families, admissions, and academic plans cross provincial lines, and many Sindh households follow both provinces' schedules. A student in Karachi with a sibling in Punjab, or a student considering an institution that follows the Punjab calendar, needs both pictures to plan the month. The admission windows for the Punjab examinations, 24 September to 17 October, are closed, so November is purely an examination month there, not a registration month. Students can read the detailed Punjab preparation guide on Blog for the final-days plan.

The Hyderabad Inquiry Report Falls Due in Late November

The third November item is the BISE Hyderabad inquiry, and it must be described with care. On 23 October 2026, the Sindh government suspended the BISE Hyderabad Deputy Controller of the Top Secret Branch, Ijaz Ali Kaka, on the orders of Provincial Minister for Universities and Boards Muhammad Ismail Rahoo, and a three-member inquiry committee was formed, headed by Professor Dr Arbeela Bhutto, with Additional Secretary Amir, also reported as Ameer, Fazal Owaisi and Mehran University Registrar Dr Nasrullah Pirzada as members. The committee's report is due within 30 days of 23 October, which places it in late November, and this guide deliberately does not name a confirmed calendar date, because the notification gives a window, not a day.

Equally important is the status of the matter: the inquiry concerns alleged irregularities in the Class XII annual examination 2026 results for pre-medical and pre-engineering groups, covering position holders, top-10, and A-1 and A grade students, and the checking, tabulation, and announcement of results. These are allegations, not proven findings, and the results have not been cancelled. Students should therefore not treat the inquiry as a change to any result, and no student names should be circulated in connection with it. The sensible stance for November is to watch for the report through proper news and board channels, and to continue normal studies in the meantime. What November Does Not Bring: A Supplementary Result Date

It is worth saying plainly what November does not bring, because false expectations cause the most disappointment. November does not bring a BIEK supplementary result date, since none has been announced, and it should not be expected simply because the papers end on 21 November. Marking and compilation take time after the last paper, and the board will announce the result when it is ready, on its own channels. Students who spend late November refreshing unofficial pages for a date that does not exist will only add anxiety to an already long month, and families should be wary of any page that claims a date without a board source.

November also does not bring any new STBB textbook announcement that has been verified as of 31 October. The standing context is that STBB's 2026-27 textbooks, the Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Textbook Series from Early Childhood to Grade 10, comprise 195 titles in Sindhi, Urdu, and English, with 52 million sets, distribution inaugurated on 19 February 2026, and a revised curriculum from the next academic year. That background matters for the school year, but it is not a November event, and students should not confuse it with the month's examination activity. Keeping the month's real events separate from background context is part of reading board news accurately.

Planning the Month: One Calendar, Three Tracks

The practical way to handle November is to keep one calendar with three tracks. Track one is your own papers: every BIEK supplementary date you must sit, with the 1:30 PM reporting time and the 2:00 PM start, plus the 18 November scrutiny deadline if it applies to you. Track two is any Punjab date that affects your household, such as a sibling's papers, so that family travel and study routines do not clash. Track three is the Hyderabad report window in late November, marked as something to watch, not something to act on, since it does not change any student's own result unless an official decision follows it.

With the calendar in place, the month becomes manageable: revise in paper order, protect sleep during the examination weeks, and use the days after 21 November to rest and then prepare for the next class, rather than waiting anxiously for an unannounced result. Students who plan this way will finish November having sat their papers well, met the scrutiny deadline if relevant, and kept their studies moving. 5 key points you must know

  • BIEK supplementary examinations run until Saturday 21 November 2026, in a 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM afternoon shift.
  • BIEK Pre-Engineering scrutiny closes 18 November 2026, at Rs 1,000 per paper.
  • Punjab second annual theory runs 3–16 November (Part-II) and 17–28 November (Part-I).
  • The BISE Hyderabad inquiry report is due within 30 days of 23 October, in late November; allegations only, results not cancelled.
  • No BIEK supplementary result date has been announced; watch biek.edu.pk, not rumour pages.

Frequently asked questions

What board events happen in November 2026?

BIEK supplementary examinations continue until 21 November, Punjab second annual theory runs from 3 to 28 November, the BIEK Pre-Engineering scrutiny window closes on 18 November, and the BISE Hyderabad inquiry report is due within 30 days of 23 October, in late November. Each event has its own dates and its own audience, so students should follow only the events that apply to them.

When does the Hyderabad inquiry report come out?

The report is due within 30 days of 23 October 2026, which places it in late November, but no confirmed calendar date has been given. The notification gives a window, not a day, so this guide does not name a specific date. The inquiry concerns alleged irregularities, not proven findings, and results have not been cancelled.

Will the BIEK supplementary result come in November?

No supplementary result date has been announced by BIEK, and the result can only follow the last paper on 21 November 2026. Marking and compilation take time after the papers end, so students should not expect a November date that the board has not given. Genuine announcements will appear on biek.edu.pk.

Why are Punjab dates in a Sindh boards calendar?

Families, admissions, and academic plans often cross provincial lines, so many Sindh households follow both provinces' schedules. Punjab second annual theory runs through most of November, and knowing those dates helps families plan travel and study routines. The Punjab admission windows are closed, so November is an examination month there, not a registration month.

How should a student plan November practically?

Keep one calendar with three tracks: your own BIEK paper dates with the 1:30 PM reporting time, any Punjab dates affecting your household, and the Hyderabad report window marked as something to watch. Revise in paper order, protect sleep during examination weeks, and use the days after 21 November to rest and prepare for the next class.
