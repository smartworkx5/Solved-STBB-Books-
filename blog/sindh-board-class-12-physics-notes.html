Sindh Class 12 Physics Notes for Exams

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 513 words · 3 min read

542Solved Notes

Second-year Physics is the toughest paper in the intermediate science group — electrostatics, current electricity, magnetism and modern physics all need full working. Students who memorise formulas without practising the numericals find the paper hard. This guide shows how to use the notes so the formulas and the methods both stay clear.

Master electrostatics and current first

Open the Class 12 Physics book page and give electrostatics and current electricity the most practice time: Coulomb's law, electric field, potential, Ohm's law and circuits. These chapters carry the most numericals in the paper.

For every numerical, write the given data, the formula, the substitution and the answer with units. Practise five of each type until the steps come without thinking.

Learn magnetism with the right-hand rules

Magnetism becomes easy when you learn the right-hand rules as hand movements, not as sentences. Practise each rule with your own hand — force on a wire, field around a wire, direction of the induced current — until the direction comes without thinking.

Draw the field diagrams the notes show and label the direction of current and field on each. A labelled diagram is worth marks on its own in the long questions.

Practise modern physics as definitions plus one example

Modern physics — photoelectric effect, atomic spectra, radioactivity — is mostly definitions and one example each. Learn each definition word for word, then write one example or one numerical beside it.

Keep a list of the formulas that look alike — energy of a photon, de Broglie wavelength, half-life — and write what makes each one different. Confused formulas lose more marks than forgotten ones.

Do a timed numerical round every week

Once a week, solve ten numericals in one hour without the notes. The clock teaches you to write the steps fast and to move past a hard numerical and return to it.

Use the solved notes guides to check the method of any numerical that stops you, then solve it again the next day.

5 key points you must know

  • Give electrostatics and current electricity the most time — they carry the most numericals.
  • Write every numerical as given data, formula, substitution, answer with units.
  • Learn the right-hand rules as hand movements until the direction comes without thinking.
  • Learn modern physics as definitions plus one example, and keep look-alike formulas apart.
  • Solve ten numericals in one hour every week, timed, without the notes.

Frequently asked questions
