Punjab Class 9 Urdu Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 548 words · 3 min read

555Solved Exercises

Class 9 Urdu is the paper where reading the lesson properly matters more than memorising notes. The sabaq, the ghazal, the grammar and the writing section all reward students who understood the text. This guide shows how to work through the solved exercises so each part of the paper is covered.

Read the sabaq before the khulasa

Open the Class 9 Urdu book page and read the full sabaq once before looking at its khulasa. A khulasa written from the lesson you actually read sounds natural; one copied from a guide sounds borrowed, and examiners notice.

After reading, close the book and write the khulasa in your own words in five or six lines. Then compare it with the solved exercise and add any point you missed.

Learn tashreeh in three steps

For every she'r, follow the same three steps: the literal meaning of the difficult words, the meaning of the whole she'r in simple Urdu, and one line about what the poet wants to teach. Students who skip the first step write tashreeh that misses the poet's point.

Keep a small notebook of difficult words with their meanings — one page per ghazal. Revise that page before the paper, not the whole book.

Grammar needs daily practice, not reading

Grammar — mutradif, mutazad, wahid-jama, islah-e-jumla — is learnt by doing, not by reading. Solve five grammar questions every day from the solved exercises and mark them yourself the same day.

A grammar mistake repeated five times becomes a habit. Correct each mistake in writing the day you make it, and it will not appear in the paper.

Practise the writing section every week

The writing section — mazmoon, khat and darkhwast — carries a large share of the paper. Write one mazmoon or letter every week, in the exam format, with a proper heading, introduction and ending.

Ask your teacher to check one piece of writing a week. One corrected mazmoon teaches more than ten read ones.

5 key points you must know

  • Read the full sabaq before looking at its khulasa, then write the khulasa in your own words.
  • Do tashreeh in three steps: difficult words, whole-she'r meaning, the poet's message.
  • Solve five grammar questions daily and correct mistakes the same day.
  • Write one mazmoon or letter every week in the exam format.
  • Keep a notebook of difficult words — one page per ghazal — and revise it before the paper.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a good khulasa in Class 9 Urdu?

Read the sabaq first, close the book, and write the main points in your own words in five or six lines — then compare with the solved exercise.

What is the best way to learn ash'aar ki tashreeh?

Three steps: difficult words first, then the whole she'r in simple Urdu, then one line about the poet's message.

How can I improve Urdu grammar for the board exam?

Solve five grammar questions every day and correct each mistake in writing the same day — grammar is learnt by doing.

How often should I practise essay writing?

One mazmoon or letter every week in exam format, and get at least one checked by your teacher each week.
