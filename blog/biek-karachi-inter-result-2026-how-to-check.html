How to Check the BIEK Karachi Inter Result 2026 (12th Class)

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-10-17 · 1405 words · 7 min read

If you are waiting for the BIEK Karachi Inter result 2026 for 12th class, the most important thing to know is where to check it — and what not to believe. Reports suggest the result will be announced in October 2026, but that timing is NOT officially confirmed. This guide shows you the official way to check your result, explains what to do on result day, and warns you against the rumours that spread every result season.

What Is Confirmed — and What Is Only Reported

Let us be clear from the start. Reports suggest that BIEK Karachi will announce the 12th class result 2026 in October 2026, typically around 10:00 AM on the result day. That timing comes from an unverified report, not from an official BIEK announcement. It must be treated as unconfirmed. Do not plan your day around a result time that the board itself has not announced.

What is safe and general is the checking process itself: students check with their roll number on the official BIEK website or by SMS, and schools may hold result gatherings. The only source that can confirm the result date is the Board of Intermediate Education Karachi itself, through its official website. Until the board announces it, every date and time you see elsewhere is a rumour.

  • Reports suggest an October 2026 announcement — NOT officially confirmed.
  • The reported typical time of around 10:00 AM is also unconfirmed.
  • Only the official BIEK website can confirm the result date.
  • Do not trust dates from social media, forwards, or unknown pages.

How to Check the Result Online

When the result is officially announced, the standard way to check it is on the official BIEK website, biek.edu.pk. Go to the official website — not a copy, not a screenshot, and not a link forwarded in a group — and find the result section for the Inter (12th class) result. Enter your roll number exactly as printed on your admit card or roll number slip, and submit it to see your result.

Keep your roll number slip with you on result day. A single wrong digit will show someone else's result or an error, so double-check the number before you submit it. If the website is slow in the first hour — which is common, because thousands of students check at once — wait and try again rather than panicking. The result is not going anywhere. For general guidance on understanding your result, see our article on how to check the Sindh board result online.

  • Use only the official BIEK website, biek.edu.pk.
  • Find the Inter (12th class) result section.
  • Enter your roll number exactly as printed on your slip.
  • If the site is slow, wait and retry — do not panic.

How to Check the Result by SMS

Students can also check their result by SMS, which is useful when the website is busy or your internet is slow. The general process is to send your roll number by SMS to the board's official result service, following the format the board publishes. Because the exact SMS format and number are announced by the board itself, you must confirm them from the official BIEK website or your school — do not use a number copied from a random post.

SMS results usually arrive within minutes, but during the first rush they can be delayed. If you do not get a reply, wait a little while before trying again, and avoid sending the same message repeatedly. Keep a record of the reply, and cross-check it against the online result when the website becomes reachable.

  • SMS checking is the backup when the website is busy.
  • Confirm the exact SMS format and number from the official BIEK website.
  • Do not use numbers copied from random posts.
  • Keep the SMS reply and cross-check it online later.

What to Do on Result Day

Result day is emotional, so plan it in advance. Check your result in a calm place, ideally with a parent or a trusted family member nearby. Have your roll number ready, a pen and paper to note your marks, and a charged phone. If your school holds a result gathering, attend it — your teachers can explain your marks statement and advise you on next steps.

Whatever the result, remember that it is one result, not a verdict on your future. If you passed, celebrate briefly and then plan your next step — college admission, subject choices, and the documents you will need. If you failed a subject or two, do not panic: the supplementary route exists, and our guide on how to check the Sindh board result online explains the checking process. Talk to a teacher before making any big decision.

  • Check your result calmly, ideally with a parent nearby.
  • Have your roll number, pen, and paper ready.
  • Attend your school's result gathering if one is held.
  • Talk to a teacher before making big decisions about next steps.
  • If you need to improve next time, read how to avoid common exam mistakes.

Avoid Result-Day Scams and Rumours

Every result season brings the same traps: fake result links, pages that ask for your roll number and personal details, and people who offer to change your result for money. The rule is simple: use only the official BIEK website and the official SMS service, and never pay anyone for a result. No one outside the board can change your marks, and anyone who claims otherwise is trying to scam you.

Be careful with your personal details too. Do not post your full result card, roll number, or identity documents publicly on social media. If a website asks for more than your roll number — passwords, CNIC copies, or payments — close it and go to the official site instead. If you are unsure about anything, ask your school: it is the safest source of result-day advice.

  • Use only the official BIEK website and official SMS service.
  • Never pay anyone to change a result — it is a scam.
  • Do not post your roll number or result card publicly.
  • If a site asks for passwords, documents, or payments, close it.
