Sindh Class 9 Biology MCQs with Answers

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 533 words · 3 min read

552Subject Guides

Class 9 Biology MCQs test whether you know the concept behind the fact — the cell's parts, the levels of organisation, how the human body works. Students who only read the chapter lose marks on questions they could answer with a pencil. This guide shows how to practise Class 9 Biology MCQs chapter by chapter.

Practise MCQs with a pencil, not by looking

Open the Class 9 Biology book page and work each MCQ with a pencil: write the term, the definition or the reason before choosing an option. An MCQ solved on paper is learnt; one answered by looking at the options is guessed.

For every MCQ you get wrong, write the correct fact in a notebook and redo it after two days. A question you solve twice is learnt; one you only read is forgotten.

Learn the cell first — it returns everywhere

Give the cell chapter the most practice time: cell structure, organelles and their functions. Every later chapter uses these ideas, so a weak start here costs marks in every chapter that follows.

Draw each organelle and write its function beside it. A diagram with its function written beside it is remembered far longer than a paragraph.

Keep a wrong-answer notebook

For every MCQ you get wrong, write the question, the correct answer and the reason in a notebook, one line each. Revise that notebook every two days — the questions in it are the ones the paper will ask again in another form.

A wrong-answer notebook turns mistakes into marks. Students who keep one stop repeating the same error within a week.

Do a timed MCQ round every week

Do a timed round of twenty MCQs in twenty minutes every week. The clock teaches you to skip a hard question and return to it, instead of losing five minutes on one mark.

After the timed round, check your answers and add every mistake to the wrong-answer notebook the same day. A mistake recorded late is a mistake repeated.

5 key points you must know

  • Work every MCQ with a pencil — write the term, the definition or the reason before choosing an option.
  • Give the cell chapter the most practice time: structure, organelles and their functions.
  • Draw each organelle and write its function beside it.
  • Keep a wrong-answer notebook: question, correct answer and reason, one line each.
  • Do a timed round of twenty MCQs in twenty minutes every week.

Frequently asked questions

How do I practise Class 9 Biology MCQs for the board exam?

Work each MCQ with a pencil before looking at the options, keep a wrong-answer notebook, and do a timed round every week.

Which Class 9 Biology chapter should I prepare first?

The cell — its structure, organelles and functions — because every later chapter uses these ideas.

Should I memorise Biology MCQ answers?

No. Learn the chapter first, then test it with MCQs — a question answered from understanding stays, one answered from memory is forgotten.

How many Biology MCQs should I practise daily?

One chapter a day, plus a timed round of twenty MCQs in twenty minutes once a week.
