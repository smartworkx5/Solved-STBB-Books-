KPK Class 9 Urdu Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 643 words · 3 min read

516Solved Exercises

Class 9 Urdu covers ghazal, nazm, prose, grammar and writing — and the paper rewards students who understand the lesson, not those who memorise the answers. A ghazal's central idea, a prose lesson's message, a grammar rule used correctly: each needs its own way of learning. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises for all of them.

Learn poetry by its central idea

Open the Class 9 Urdu book page and read each ghazal or nazm slowly, line by line, until you can say its central idea in one sentence. Then read the solved explanation — the explanation only makes sense once you have wrestled with the lines yourself.

Learn the difficult words of every poem in a notebook: the word, its meaning, and the line it comes from. The paper asks for word meanings from the poems you studied, and a word learned with its line is remembered far longer than a word learned alone.

Read prose for its message and style

For every prose lesson, write down three things after reading: the main message, two important details, and one line you can quote. The solved exercises give the answers, but your own three-point summary is what you will remember in the paper.

Practise the short questions in writing. Urdu short answers need exact wording — the right term, the right name, the right date. Writing them fixes the wording; reading them once does not.

Grammar and writing need daily practice

Grammar topics — parts of speech, sentence types, idioms, punctuation — are tested through usage. Work the solved grammar exercises in writing and keep a list of the idioms and proverbs from the book with their meanings, revising a few every day.

Write one essay, letter or application every week from the solved exercises, following the model answer's structure. Learn the formats by heart: an application's layout, a letter's greeting, an essay's opening and ending. Format marks are free marks when the format is exact.

Revise with the notes and MCQs

In the final weeks, revise the chapter notes on the Notes page — one lesson a day, poetry notebook open beside you — and test yourself with the solved exercises and the book closed. Whatever you cannot answer goes into a short revision list for the last days.

Finish with the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 9 Urdu book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question — it catches the word meanings, grammar and facts that long-answer revision misses.

5 key points you must know

  • Say each poem's central idea in one sentence before reading the explanation.
  • Keep a poetry notebook — difficult words with meanings and the line they come from.
  • Summarise every prose lesson in three points: message, details, one quotation.
  • Work grammar exercises in writing and revise idioms a few every day.
  • Write one essay, letter or application every week following the model structure.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find KPK Board Class 9 Urdu solved exercises?

On the Class 9 Urdu book page. The solved exercises are free to read lesson by lesson, with notes on the Notes page.

How do I prepare for the Class 9 Urdu paper?

Learn each poem's central idea, summarise prose lessons in three points, practise grammar in writing, and write one essay or letter every week.

How do I learn the difficult words in Class 9 Urdu poetry?

Write each word in a notebook with its meaning and the line it comes from, and revise the notebook regularly. Words learned with their lines are remembered far longer.

Can I practise Class 9 Urdu MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 9 Urdu book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.
