Punjab Class 12 Chemistry Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 455 words · 2 min read

570Solved Exercises

Class 12 Chemistry is dominated by organic chemistry — named reactions, mechanisms and conversions that reward daily written practice. Students who write reactions every day find the paper predictable; students who only read them do not. This guide shows how to work through the solved exercises for the Punjab Board Class 12 Chemistry book.

Reactions written, not read

Open the Class 12 Chemistry book page and write every reaction by hand: reactants, conditions above the arrow, products. A reaction written three times across a week is remembered; a reaction read three times in one evening is not.

Group reactions by family — alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, aldehydes — and learn each family's pattern once. Patterns turn fifty reactions into five rules.

Conversions in two steps at a time

Practise conversions by naming the reagent for each step before writing the product. Most conversion marks are lost on the reagent, not the product.

Keep a reagent list: reagent, what it does, one example reaction. Revise it weekly — it is the single most useful page in organic chemistry.

Numericals with units and steps

Electrochemistry and solution numericals follow the same discipline as Physics: given data, formula, substitution with units, final answer with unit. Write every step — FSc marking rewards working.

Practise two numericals a day rather than ten on Sunday. Daily numericals keep the method automatic.

Short questions from definitions and tests

Short questions lean on definitions, distinguishing tests and uses. Learn each test as: reagent, observation, conclusion — three short lines that answer any test question.

The Class 11 Chemistry notes built the physical-chemistry base this book stands on — revise its formula pages before the final month.

5 key points you must know

  • Write every reaction by hand with conditions above the arrow.
  • Learn reactions by family pattern, not one by one.
  • Name the reagent before writing the product in conversions.
  • Keep a reagent list and revise it weekly.
  • Solve two numericals daily with full steps and units.

Frequently asked questions

How do I prepare Class 12 Chemistry for the Punjab Board?

Write reactions daily by family pattern, practise conversions reagent-first, solve two numericals a day, and learn tests as reagent-observation-conclusion.

How do I memorise organic reactions?

Group them by family and learn each family's pattern. Write every reaction by hand three times across the week — writing fixes memory, reading does not.

What is the best way to practise conversions?

Name the reagent for each step before writing the product, and keep a reagent list revised weekly.

Are numericals important in Class 12 Chemistry?

Yes — electrochemistry and solutions carry steady numerical marks. Full working with units protects those marks.
