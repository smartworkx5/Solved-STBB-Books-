BSEK vs BIEK: Which Board Handles Your Result?

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-10-19 · 1266 words · 6 min read

The BSEK vs BIEK difference which board question confuses many Karachi pupils and parents, because the two boards have similar names and both publish results. The answer is simple. BSEK, the Board of Secondary Education Karachi, handles Matric-level results for Class 9 and Class 10. BIEK, the Board of Intermediate Education Karachi, handles Intermediate-level results for Class 11 and Class 12. Their official websites are bsek.edu.pk and biek.edu.pk. This article explains what each board does, how to check a result with either board, what to do when a result does not show, and how scrutiny and rechecking work. If you know your class, you know your board.

One line that settles it: class decides the board

The rule is one line long. If you are in Class 9 or Class 10, your board is BSEK. If you are in Class 11 or Class 12, your board is BIEK. That single rule answers the most common confusion, including the one pupils feel when they move from Class 10 to Class 11 and discover that their board has changed along with their class.

The confusion is understandable. Both boards serve Karachi, both run examinations, both publish gazettes, and both use roll numbers. But they are separate boards with separate records. Checking the wrong board's website will never show your result, however many times you retry, so the first step on any result day is to be sure which board holds your result.

BSEK: the Matric board

BSEK handles everything at the Matric level in Karachi. That means Class 9 results, Class 10 results, and the records that go with them. A pupil who has just finished Class 9 checks with BSEK. A pupil who has just finished Class 10 checks with BSEK too, and the Class 10 result is the one that decides college admission.

Because BSEK covers two classes, families often deal with it for two result seasons in a row. The checking routine is the same each time: the official website at bsek.edu.pk, the official gazette, and the school. Keeping the roll-number slip safe after each examination makes the next check far easier.

BIEK: the Intermediate board

BIEK handles everything at the Intermediate level in Karachi. That means Class 11 (Part-I) results and Class 12 (Part-II) results, across the Science, Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, Commerce and other groups. A pupil who has moved from Class 10 to Class 11 has moved from BSEK to BIEK, and should check the BIEK website from then on.

BIEK also has an official Android app for checking results, which is a useful alternative when the website is slow on result day. As with BSEK, the official gazette is the second official source, and the school can confirm any result that looks doubtful. How to check a result with either board

The checking routine is the same for both boards. First, be sure which board holds your result, using the class rule above. Second, keep your roll number ready, typed exactly as printed on your slip. Third, check on the board's official website, bsek.edu.pk or biek.edu.pk, and confirm that the name shown matches yours before trusting the marks. Fourth, if the result matters for admission or rechecking, confirm it against the official gazette or through your school.

Both boards also offer scrutiny or rechecking, but the process, fees and deadlines must be confirmed through the board's official notification. Never assume that the process for one board matches the other. If you believe a paper was marked wrongly, read our guide to scrutiny and rechecking in Sindh and confirm the details with the board concerned.

If your result does not show

A result that does not show is usually a small problem, not a disaster. Work through the checks in order. First, recheck the roll number: a single wrong digit shows nothing or someone else's result. Second, check that you are on the right board's website, since a Class 11 pupil on the BSEK site will find nothing. Third, retry later, because board sites get overloaded on result day and often work fine an hour later.

If the result still does not show, use the gazette. The official PDF gazette is an alternative source that does not depend on the portal. Your school can also check the institutional gazette. As a last step, contact the school or the board through verified official channels. Never hand your roll number to a third-party 'result service', and never pay anyone for result information the board publishes itself. 5 key points you must know

  • BSEK (Board of Secondary Education Karachi) handles Matric-level results: Class 9 and Class 10.
  • BIEK (Board of Intermediate Education Karachi) handles Intermediate-level results: Class 11 and Class 12.
  • Official websites: bsek.edu.pk for BSEK and biek.edu.pk for BIEK; BIEK also has an official Android app.
  • Check by roll number on the official site, and confirm against the gazette or school for admission or rechecking.
  • If a result does not show: recheck the roll number, confirm the right board, retry later, use the gazette, or contact the school or board through verified official channels.

Frequently asked questions

Which board handles Class 9 and Class 10 results in Karachi?

BSEK, the Board of Secondary Education Karachi, handles Matric-level results for Class 9 and Class 10. Its official website is bsek.edu.pk.

Which board handles Class 11 and Class 12 results in Karachi?

BIEK, the Board of Intermediate Education Karachi, handles Intermediate-level results for Class 11 and Class 12. Its official website is biek.edu.pk, and BIEK also has an official Android app.

How do I know whether my result is with BSEK or BIEK?

Use the class rule: Class 9 or 10 means BSEK, Class 11 or 12 means BIEK. Checking the wrong board's website will never show your result, so confirm the board before retrying.

What should I do if my result does not show online?

Recheck the roll number, confirm you are on the right board's website, retry later because sites get overloaded on result day, and use the official PDF gazette. If it still does not show, contact your school or the board through verified official channels, never a third-party 'result service'.

Can I apply for rechecking with BSEK or BIEK?

Both boards offer scrutiny or rechecking, but the process, fees and deadlines must be confirmed through the board's official notification. See our guide to scrutiny and rechecking in Sindh and confirm the details with the board concerned.
