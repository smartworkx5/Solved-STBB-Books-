Balochistan Class 10 Chemistry Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 550 words · 3 min read

527Solved Exercises

Class 10 Chemistry rewards students who write every step: the balanced equation, the working, and the answer with units. A correct answer with no working earns little, and a skipped step is a lost mark. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises so the method becomes automatic before the paper.

Work every exercise with the book open, then closed

Open the Class 10 Chemistry book page and work each exercise first with the solved answer beside you, reading every step. Then close the answer and solve the same question again on your own — the second attempt is where the learning happens.

If the second attempt fails, do not just re-read the answer. Cover it, try again, and only peek at the next step. A question solved in small steps you found yourself stays solved.

Balance every equation by hand

Chemical equations are the easiest marks in the paper — and the easiest to lose by a careless count. Balance each equation on paper, count the atoms on both sides, and write the count down until balancing becomes automatic.

Keep a list of the equations you keep getting wrong and rebalance them every second day. The paper repeats the same reactions year after year.

Learn acids, bases and salts as one story

Acids, bases, salts, pH and neutralisation are one connected chapter, not five separate ones. Learn how they link: an acid plus a base gives a salt and water, and pH tells you which side you are on.

Write each definition in the book's words, then test yourself by explaining it aloud in your own words. If you cannot explain it, you do not know it yet.

Practise the numericals under time

In the final weeks, pick five numericals, set a timer, and solve them with notes closed. Write the given data, the formula, the substitution and the answer with units for every one.

Check your answers against the solved exercises and list the steps where marks were lost. Revise only those steps — the rest is already ready.

5 key points you must know

  • Work each exercise twice — once with the answer beside you, once with it closed.
  • Balance every equation by hand and count the atoms on both sides.
  • Learn acids, bases, salts and pH as one connected story, not separate topics.
  • Explain each definition aloud in your own words before calling it learnt.
  • Finish with timed numericals, notes closed, and revise only the steps where marks were lost.

Frequently asked questions

Where are Balochistan Board Class 10 Chemistry solved exercises?

On the Class 10 Chemistry book page, with step-by-step solved exercises for every chapter.

How do I balance chemical equations for Class 10 Chemistry?

Count the atoms of each element on both sides and adjust the coefficients until both sides match. Practise by hand daily — the paper repeats the same reactions.

Which chapters matter most in Class 10 Chemistry?

Chemical equations, acids, bases and salts, and the numericals on mole concept. These carry the easiest marks when the steps are written fully.

Are the Class 10 Chemistry exercises solved step by step?

Yes. The Class 10 Chemistry book page gives the full working for each exercise, not just the final answer.
