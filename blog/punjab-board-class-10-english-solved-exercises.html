Punjab Board Class 10 English Solved Exercises — Full Guide

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 764 words · 4 min read

479Solved Notes

Class 10 English is a compulsory Matric subject in the Punjab Board, and its paper rewards students who prepare all four skills: reading, vocabulary, grammar and writing. Many students prepare only the lessons and lose the grammar and writing marks. This guide shows how to use the solved exercises of the PCTB Class 10 English book to prepare every part of the paper.

What the Punjab Board Class 10 English book and paper cover

The PCTB Class 10 English book contains prose lessons and poems, each with comprehension questions, vocabulary and grammar exercises. Around the book, the paper adds the skills sections: grammar in use (including narration and active-passive voice), translation of sentences and a paragraph, and writing tasks such as essays, letters and applications, stories and dialogues.

Read the solved exercises for every unit on the Punjab Board Class 10 English book page, free on this website. Each unit's questions are solved in the book's own order, with the vocabulary and grammar exercises done, not skipped.

Comprehension and vocabulary: answer from the text

Comprehension answers must come from the lesson, not from general knowledge. Read the question, find the exact lines in the text, and answer in your own words built on those lines. The solved exercises show this discipline: the first sentence of each answer answers the question directly, and the support comes from the passage.

For vocabulary, learn each new word inside a sentence, not as a bare meaning. The book's exercises give the model sentences; make one more sentence of your own for the ten hardest words of each unit. Words learned in sentences stay; words learned as lists fade.

Grammar and translation: a daily fifteen minutes

Grammar in Class 10 English is a practice skill: narration (direct and indirect speech), active and passive voice, and the correct use of tenses and prepositions inside sentences. Give grammar fifteen minutes daily — five sentences converted, five translated. Small daily practice beats a weekly grammar marathon, because conversion rules become automatic only through repetition.

For translation, work from the book's own sentences first. Translate Urdu to English by finding the subject and verb before anything else; every correct translation is built on that skeleton. The solved exercises include the book's translation practice — compare your version with the model and note the difference.

Writing tasks: formats are free marks

Essays, letters, applications, stories and dialogues each have a format, and format marks are the easiest marks in the paper. Learn one clean format for each task and reuse it: the letter's address and date lines, the application's subject line, the essay's introduction-body-conclusion flow, the story's moral at the end.

Prepare two essays and two stories properly rather than ten poorly. A prepared essay written neatly scores more than an unprepared one written longer. Finish your English preparation with the unit MCQs on the MCQs practice page — the objective section tests vocabulary and grammar from the same units.

5 key points you must know

  • Answer comprehension questions from the text itself: direct answer first, support from the passage second. Never import outside knowledge into a passage answer.
  • Learn vocabulary inside sentences — the book's model sentence plus one of your own for hard words. Sentence-learned words survive till the exam.
  • Practise grammar fifteen minutes daily: narration, active-passive and translation, five sentences at a time. Daily conversion practice makes the rules automatic.
  • Learn one format per writing task — letter, application, essay, story, dialogue — and reuse it. Format marks are free marks; never lose them.
  • Prepare fewer writing pieces properly: two essays and two stories, written neatly and memorised in structure, beat ten half-prepared ones.

Frequently asked questions

Are Punjab Board Class 10 English solved exercises free?

Yes. Every unit of the PCTB Class 10 English book is solved free on the book page — comprehension, vocabulary and grammar exercises included.

How should I prepare English grammar for Class 10?

Daily short practice: five narration conversions, five active-passive conversions and five translated sentences. The solved exercises give you the book's own sentences to practise on.

How do I improve translation (Urdu to English)?

Find the subject and the verb first, then build the sentence around them, then check the tense. Practise on the book's sentences and compare with the model answers in the solved exercises.

Can I practise Class 10 English MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. The objective sets cover vocabulary and grammar from the book's units, each with the correct answer and an explanation.
