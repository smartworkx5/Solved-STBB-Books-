Sindh Boards November 2026 Calendar: What to Watch, Date by Date

By Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books Team · Updated 2026-10-30 · 1953 words · 10 min read

November 2026 is a busy month for board students in and around Sindh, with an honest note that no new STBB announcement was verified on 30 October 2026. The month's confirmed landmarks come from neighbouring Punjab's second annual examinations, which run through November, and from the BISE Hyderabad inquiry, whose report is due within 30 days of 23 October 2026, while Sindh's own boards continue their normal post-result processes for the Karachi results announced in late October. This guide pulls those threads into one calendar, gives you the official websites to check, and marks clearly which items are confirmed, which are windows rather than single dates, and which announcements have not yet been made, so that you can plan November without chasing rumours.

The Headline Windows: Punjab Second Annual Examinations

The largest dated item on the November calendar is the Punjab HSSC second annual examination programme, which matters directly to Punjab students and indirectly to Sindh students watching how supplementary examinations are run. Part-II, or 12th class, theory papers run from 3 to 16 November 2026, followed by Part-I, or 11th class, theory papers from 17 to 28 November 2026, with practical examinations from 1 to 11 December 2026. Morning papers begin at 9:00 AM and evening papers at 2:00 PM, with Friday evening papers at 2:30 PM, and practical batches run from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM, with the Friday second batch adjusted to a 2:30 PM start. Sindh students with friends or family in Punjab can use these dates to understand the provincial timetable, but they should not confuse Punjab's schedule with any Sindh board schedule, which follows its own notices.

Subject windows from the BISE Multan date sheet give the programme its shape, and they are worth noting as examples, with the usual warning that other boards' sheets can differ. For 12th class, English Compulsory falls on 4 November, Mathematics and Biology on 6 November, Computer Science on 10 November, Chemistry on 12 November and Physics on 16 November, while for 11th class, English Compulsory falls on 18 November, Computer Science on 23 November, Mathematics and Biology on 25 November and Physics on 27 November. Admission windows for these examinations, which ran from 24 September to 17 October 2026, are closed, so November is examination time, not application time, for Punjab students. The Hyderabad Inquiry Report Window

The second item to watch in November is the report of the three-member committee formed on 23 October 2026 to examine alleged irregularities in the BISE Hyderabad Class XII annual examination 2026 results for pre-medical and pre-engineering groups. The committee, headed by Professor Dr Arbeela Bhutto, with members Additional Secretary Amir, also reported as Ameer, Fazal Owaisi and Mehran University Registrar Dr Nasrullah Pirzada, must report within 30 days of 23 October 2026, which places its reporting window inside November. This guide deliberately presents the window as within 30 days rather than as a confirmed calendar date, because the verified material states it in those terms, and students should watch official channels during that window without treating any particular day as a deadline or believing social media claims of a leaked outcome.

While waiting, the verified position remains unchanged and reassuring: the announced results have not been cancelled, the allegations are allegations and not proven findings, and no student has been named as a beneficiary of manipulation. Students should therefore continue their studies normally, keep official transcripts and mark sheets safe, and use formal rechecking for any individual discrepancy in their own mark sheet rather than waiting for the committee. If the committee's report leads to any action, that action will be announced through official channels, and this site will report it only once it is officially confirmed. Until then, every claim about the inquiry's outcome that cannot be confirmed officially should be treated as unverified and not shared.

Sindh Boards: Post-Result Work After the October Results

For Sindh students themselves, November is principally a post-result month, following two major Karachi announcements in late October. The Board of Secondary Education Karachi announced the Class 9 Science Group result on 28 October 2026, with 123,757 students passing all papers, 53,849 failing, and large partial-pass groups in between, under the new rule that a paper below 40% is ungraded. The Board of Intermediate Education Karachi announced HSC Part-I results for all remaining groups on 26 October 2026, including Science Pre-Medical, Science General, Commerce in Regular and Private categories, Humanities and Arts in Regular and Private categories, Special Candidates in Arts Regular, and Home Economics. Students from both results should spend November on the practical follow-up those results require: reading mark sheets paper by paper, revising open papers, considering scrutiny where marks look surprisingly low, and watching official websites for reappearance notices.

Scrutiny is the most time-sensitive of those follow-ups, and it illustrates why November requires weekly checks of official sources. The BIEK scrutiny fee reported from the board's scrutiny form is Rs 1,000 per paper, but no scrutiny deadline is stated in the verified material used here, so students must check the board's official website and their college notice board for the current notice rather than trusting an unofficial date. BSEK students in the same position should check bsek.edu.pk for their board's notices, since no reappearance date for the Class 9 Science open papers was stated in the 28 October announcement. A weekly habit of checking both official sites takes ten minutes and prevents the commonest November mistake, which is missing a genuine deadline while following a rumoured one.

Official Websites: Your November Checklist of Sources

November is also a good month to save the official websites that govern every item on this calendar, because every rumour in board season can be settled by checking one of them. For Karachi, the Board of Secondary Education Karachi is at bsek.edu.pk and the Board of Intermediate Education Karachi is at biek.edu.pk, with results also available on the official BIEK Android app. For the other Sindh boards, the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education Sukkur is at bisesuksindh.edu.pk and the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education Larkana is at biselrk.edu.pk. Punjab students should use their own board's official website for date sheets, roll number slips and notices, and should confirm their own board's subject-wise sheet rather than relying on another board's sheet, since subject dates can differ between boards.

Make a simple November routine around these sources: check your board's website once a week, read your institution's notice board on the same day, and note anything new in a diary with the date you saw it. That routine catches genuine notices early, whether they concern scrutiny, reappearance, practical examinations or the Hyderabad inquiry, and it protects you from the opposite error of acting on a screenshot whose source cannot be verified. Keep your result, admit card, registration details and any scrutiny or rechecking receipts together, so that any process you need to join can be joined immediately. What Has NOT Been Announced: An Honesty Note

A watchlist is only trustworthy if it says what is missing as clearly as what is present, so this section states the gaps plainly. No new announcement from the Sindh Textbook Board itself was verified on 30 October 2026, so this guide claims no new STBB book, curriculum or examination announcement for that date, and students should treat any such claim circulating online as unverified unless it appears on an official STBB or board channel. The standing STBB context remains the 2026-27 textbooks for Early Childhood to Grade 10, under the Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Textbook Series, with 195 titles in Sindhi, Urdu and English and 52 million sets, whose distribution was inaugurated on 19 February 2026, and a revised curriculum from the next academic year focusing on research, scientific thinking, creativity and related goals, with pupils to be relieved of excessive book burden.

Other gaps matter too, and students should know them before they plan around rumours. No confirmed BSEK General Group or private Science result beyond the 28 October Science Group announcement is part of the verified material, no BIEK Part-II supplementary dates for 2026 beyond what is listed here are confirmed, and no position-holder or topper names for the BIEK or BSEK 2026 results were published in the verified sources. Punjab's second annual admission windows are closed, not open, and the Hyderabad inquiry's outcome is not yet known. November will bring genuine news, but until it arrives through official channels, the honest position is to plan around the confirmed windows above, check official websites weekly, and keep studying. Frequently asked questions

What are the key November 2026 dates for board students?

Punjab Part-II theory papers run 3-16 November 2026, Part-I theory papers 17-28 November, and practicals 1-11 December. The BISE Hyderabad inquiry report is due within 30 days of 23 October 2026. Sindh students should also watch their own boards' post-result notices through November.

Has the Sindh Textbook Board announced anything new on 30 October 2026?

No new STBB announcement was verified on 30 October 2026. The standing context is the 2026-27 textbook series, with 195 titles and 52 million sets, distributed from 19 February 2026, and a revised curriculum from the next academic year. Treat any claimed new STBB announcement as unverified unless it appears officially.

Where do I check BSEK and BIEK results and notices?

BSEK at bsek.edu.pk and BIEK at biek.edu.pk, with BIEK results also on the official BIEK Android app. Other Sindh boards are at bisesuksindh.edu.pk for Sukkur and biselrk.edu.pk for Larkana. Check your own board's site weekly.

Are Punjab second annual admissions still open?

No. The admission windows ran from 24 September to 17 October 2026 across single, double and triple fee stages and are now closed. November is examination time for admitted Punjab students, not application time.

What should Sindh students do in November after the October results?

Read your mark sheet paper by paper against the 40% line, revise open papers, consider scrutiny where marks look surprisingly low (BIEK fee Rs 1,000 per paper, deadline per the official notice), and check your board's official website weekly for reappearance notices.
