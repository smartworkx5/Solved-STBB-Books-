Sindh Class 6 English Solved Exercises

By Tasleem Sanjrani · SolvedBooks.pk · 673 words · 3 min read

514Solved Exercises

Class 6 English is where reading, grammar and writing come together — and students lose marks when they memorise answers without understanding the lesson. The paper asks for comprehension, correct grammar and short written answers, and the solved exercises cover all three lesson by lesson. This guide shows how to use them so the English stays with you until the paper.

Read the lesson first, then answer

Open the Class 6 English book page and read the lesson properly before looking at the answers. Read the passage twice — once for the story and once for the new words — then close the book and answer the exercise questions in your own words before checking the solution.

Keep a vocabulary notebook: every new word with its meaning and one sentence of your own. Ten words a lesson is enough — revise the notebook every week and the words will be there when you need them in the paper.

Learn grammar by using it, not by memorising rules

Grammar chapters — tenses, articles, prepositions, punctuation — are tested through sentences, not through definitions. For every rule, write five sentences of your own using it, then check them against the solved exercises. A rule you have used five times is a rule you will not forget.

Do the grammar exercises in writing, not in your head. Writing shows the small mistakes — a missing article, a wrong verb form — that silent reading hides. Correct each mistake in the notebook and redo the same exercise the next day.

Practise the writing tasks every week

Paragraph writing, letter writing and short compositions carry real marks, and they cannot be crammed. Write one short paragraph or letter every week from the solved exercises, following the model answer's shape: a clear opening line, two or three supporting lines, and a closing line.

Read your writing aloud after finishing it. If a sentence sounds wrong when you hear it, it reads wrong too — fix it before the paper, not during it. Keep your best pieces in one file and revise them before the exam.

Revise with the notes and MCQs

In the final weeks, revise the chapter notes on the Notes page — one lesson a day, vocabulary notebook open beside you — and test yourself with the solved exercises and the book closed. Whatever you cannot answer goes into a short revision list for the last days.

Finish with the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 6 English book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question — it catches the grammar and vocabulary points that long-answer revision misses.

5 key points you must know

  • Read each lesson twice before looking at the solved answers.
  • Keep a vocabulary notebook — every new word with a meaning and a sentence of your own.
  • Write five sentences of your own for every grammar rule you learn.
  • Practise one writing task every week — paragraph, letter or short composition.
  • Revise one lesson a day in the final weeks and test yourself with the book closed.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find Sindh Board Class 6 English solved exercises?

On the Class 6 English book page. The solved exercises are free to read lesson by lesson, with notes on the Notes page.

How do I prepare for the Class 6 English paper?

Read each lesson properly, keep a vocabulary notebook, practise grammar by writing sentences, do one writing task a week, and test yourself with the solved exercises and MCQs.

How do I improve my English vocabulary for Class 6?

Write every new word in a notebook with its meaning and one sentence of your own, and revise the notebook every week. Ten words a lesson is enough if you revise them regularly.

Can I practise Class 6 English MCQs online?

Yes, on the MCQs practice page. Choose the Class 6 English book and practise with the correct answer and explanation shown for every question.
