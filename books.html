How do I use the filters?

  1. Choose a board first, so results stay within that board.
  2. Narrow by class, then by subject and medium.
  3. Set status to Live — Free to Read to hide coming-soon books, or type a class and subject in the search box.
  4. Click Open on a live card to reach its preview page.

What does Coming Soon mean?

Coming Soon means the book is listed but its preview is not live yet. Opening a coming-soon card shows the book's details and a request link. No date is promised for any coming-soon book.
