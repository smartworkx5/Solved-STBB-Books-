Sindh Textbook Board — Solved Books

The Sindh Textbook Board (STBB), based in Jamshoro, publishes the textbooks used in government schools across Sindh. This hub collects every solved book we have prepared for the board: 202 live titles covering Classes 1 to 12, in Arabic, English, Sindhi and Urdu medium. Sindh's catalogue is the largest on SolvedBooks.pk because it includes Sindhi-medium and Arabic titles alongside the Urdu and English editions — each book below is solved in the medium of the textbook itself, so students can revise in the same language they will use in the exam hall.
