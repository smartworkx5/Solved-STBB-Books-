Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board — Solved Books

The Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board (PCTB), based in Lahore, sets the textbooks for Pakistan's most populous province. Our PCTB collection currently holds 119 live solved books for Classes 1 to 12, in Arabic, English, Farsi, Punjabi and Urdu medium. It reaches well beyond the core school subjects: alongside mathematics, science and languages you will find solved notes for Punjab's technical, commerce and elective titles, each prepared from the actual PCTB textbook and free to read online or download as a PDF.
