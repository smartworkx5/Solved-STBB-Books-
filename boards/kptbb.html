Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Textbooks Board — Solved Books

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Textbooks Board (KPTBB), based in Peshawar, publishes the textbooks used across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Our KPTBB shelf is the newest and smallest of the four boards — 9 live solved books so far, for Classes 1 to 11, in English and Urdu medium — and it grows as more of the board's textbooks are solved. Every book listed below is finished and free; classes and subjects that are still being prepared are shown as coming soon on their class pages rather than linked to empty downloads.
