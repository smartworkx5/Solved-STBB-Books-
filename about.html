About SolvedBooks.pk

We are teachers from Pakistan. We saw students struggling to find solved exercises in their own medium, so we started preparing them, chapter by chapter, from the official textbooks of all four boards — Sindh, Punjab, Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Who runs SolvedBooks.pk

SolvedBooks.pk is edited by Tasleem Sanjrani, a government school teacher (Junior Elementary School Teacher — JEST) in Sindh, Pakistan. The solved notes are prepared by teachers, for students and fellow teachers.

What we do

  • Solve every exercise from the official board textbook in the book's own format.
  • Write answers the way board examiners want: translation, explanation, example.
  • Publish everything free to read on this website.

What we do not do

We do not replace the textbook. Solved notes are for learning and revision — always study with your official board textbook open. Textbooks are the property of their respective boards; solved notes here are original explanations of those exercises.

Not an official board website

SolvedBooks.pk is an independent study-help website. It is not an official website of, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, the Sindh Textbook Board (STBB), the Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board (PCTB), the Balochistan Textbook Board (BTBB), the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Textbooks Board (KPTBB) or any board of intermediate and secondary education. Board and textbook names belong to their respective boards.
