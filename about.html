About Solved Sindh Textbook Board Books

We are teachers from Sindh. We saw students struggling to find solved exercises in their own medium — especially Sindhi medium — so we started preparing them, chapter by chapter, from the official Sindh Textbook Board textbooks.

What we do

  • Solve every exercise from the Sindh Textbook Board textbook in the book's own format.
  • Write answers the way board examiners want: translation, explanation, example.
  • Publish everything free to read on this website.

What we do not do

We do not replace the textbook. Solved notes are for learning and revision — always study with your Sindh Textbook Board book open. Textbooks are the property of the Sindh Textbook Board; solved notes here are original explanations of those exercises.
