Sample solved questions

Key Points Gaseous exchange is getting oxygen from air and removing carbon dioxide. In leaves most exchange occurs through stomata; in humans it occurs in alveoli. The trachea divides into two bronchi; alveoli are surrounded by capillaries. Carbon dioxide concentration in blood is the primary stimulus for breathing. Emphysema breaks down alveolar walls and reduces exchange surface. Q: The process of gaseous exchange involves: (a) Breakdown of C-H bonds to yield energy (b) Physical movements that take air in and out of body (c) Getting oxygen from the air and removing carbon dioxide (d) Transport of oxygen by the blood to different parts of the body Correct answer: Getting oxygen from the air and removing carbon dioxide Q: Most of the gaseous exchange in a leaf occurs through: (a) Stomata (b) General surface (c) Cuticle (d) Lenticels Correct answer: Stomata Q: How many bronchi are there in the air passageway? (a) One (b) Two (c) Many (d) None Correct answer: Two Q: Where does the gaseous exchange occur in humans? (a) Pharynx (b) Trachea (c) Bronchi (d) Alveoli Correct answer: Alveoli Q: Which structure actively helps in taking the air out of lungs?
