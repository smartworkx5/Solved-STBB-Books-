Key Points Shading transforms the simple shape of an object into a proper form and creates depth on a flat surface. Pencil tip techniques (Figure 1.1): flat sketch, round sharpened to flat tip, tip of sharp point, side of round and blunt round. Soft pencil marks: 2B scribbles, 3B herringbone and smudged tone, 4B pushed zigzag, 5B irregular dots, 6B rubbed graphite layers. Graphite pencils range from 8B (softest, darkest) to 6H (hardest, lightest); HB is the middle grade. Graphite is a form of carbon; the pencil 'lead' is graphite compressed and baked to varying degrees of hardness. Q: What is shading? Describe the basic shading techniques and the use of the pencil tip. Shading helps to transform the simple shape of any object into a proper form of the object. It helps to create a kind of depth to a flat surface. The basic techniques for pencil strokes should be observed and practiced to achieve different shades with pencils. Experimenting with these strokes students will learn the control over pencil and its strokes. Different techniques of using the pencil tip (Figure 1.1) are: Flat sketch, Round sharpened to flat tip, Tip of sharp point, Side of round and Blunt round. Soft pencil marks (Figure 1.2) include: 2B - Horizontal rows of scribbled shading; Scribbled lines implying a knitted texture and shadow; Vertical scribble, creating a soft texture and shading; 3B - Heavy herringbone texture; Smudged tone (with the finger) to create atmosphere; Random marks marking implying a rough texture; 4B - A pushed zigzag line using the side of the pencil; Rows of vertical scribble, progressing from dark to light; Regular dashes of tone; 5B - Irregular dots, creating an implied texture, perhaps a gravel path; Wooly scribble creating a textured surface; Open zigzag lines create…

Sample MCQs with answers

What does shading do to the simple shape of an object?

Answer: It transforms it into a proper form and creates depth on the flat surface

Which of the following is one of the pencil tip techniques shown in Figure 1.1?

Answer: Blunt round

In the soft pencil marks of Figure 1.2, the smudged tone made with the finger (3B) is used to create:

Answer: Atmosphere

The heavy herringbone texture in Figure 1.2 is made with which pencil?

Answer: 3B

Irregular dots creating an implied texture, perhaps a gravel path, are made with:

Answer: 5B
