Facts to Remember Chemical equilibrium is the state of a reversible reaction in which the forward and reverse rates become equal, so concentrations stop changing although both reactions continue. Dynamic equilibrium is established in a closed vessel when the forward rate falls and the reverse rate rises until the two rates are equal. Law of mass action says the rate of a reaction is directly proportional to the product of the active masses (molar concentrations) of the reactants, each raised to its coefficient. Equilibrium constant for ammonia synthesis N2 + 3H2 ⇌ 2NH3 is Kc = [NH3]^2 / ([N2][H2]^3). Direction of a reaction is predicted by comparing Qc with Kc: Qc less than Kc moves forward, Qc greater than Kc moves backward, Qc equal to Kc is already at equilibrium. Equilibrium is attainable from either side because the same equilibrium state is fixed only by the conditions, whether the reaction starts from reactants or from products. Worked Kc values in this chapter are 24.5 mol/dm^3 for N2O decomposition and 0.02 for HI decomposition.

Sample MCQs with answers

What is chemical equilibrium?

Answer: The state of a reversible reaction in which the forward and reverse rates become equal

How is dynamic equilibrium established in a closed vessel?

Answer: The forward rate falls and the reverse rate rises until the two rates are equal

What does the law of mass action state?

Answer: The rate of a reaction is directly proportional to the product of the active masses of the reactants, each raised to its coefficient

What is the equilibrium constant expression for ammonia synthesis, N2 + 3H2 2NH3?

Answer: Kc = [NH3]^2 / ([N2][H2]^3)

If Qc is less than Kc, in which direction does the reaction move?

Answer: It moves forward
