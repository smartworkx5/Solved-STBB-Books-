Comprehension Questions What is the dying sun? How will it affect the earth? The dying sun means the sun cooling down and losing its heat over millions of years. When the sun dies, the earth will receive no heat and light from it. The oceans will freeze and all life on earth will come to an end. The lesson describes this distant future of the solar system. How did the planets come into existence according to the lesson? According to the lesson, a wandering star once passed close to the sun. Its gravitational pull tore out a filament of matter from the sun. This matter broke into pieces which cooled and became the planets. The earth is one of these pieces, which later cooled enough for life to appear. Why does the writer say that life on earth is a brief incident? Life appeared on earth only after it cooled for millions of years. It will end when the sun cools and the earth freezes again. Compared with the vast age of the universe, the period of life is very short. Man should therefore value life and use his time wisely. What will happen to the sun in the far future? The sun is slowly losing its heat by radiation into space. After millions of years it will become a cold, dark body. The planets will then wander in darkness without heat or light. This is the picture of the dying sun given by Sir James Jeans. Book Exercise — Answer These Questions How is it that a star seldom finds another star near it? The stars travel through a universe so immense that one star very rarely comes near another. In a scale model where the stars are ships, the average ship is well over a million miles from its nearest neighbour. Sample MCQs with answers

  1. What does the 'dying sun' mean in the lesson?

    Answer: The sun is slowly cooling down and losing its heat

  2. According to Sir James Jeans, how did the planets come into existence?

    Answer: A wandering star's tidal pull tore pieces from a mountain raised on the sun

  3. About how long ago did the wandering star come near the sun?

    Answer: Some two thousand million years ago

  4. In Jeans's scale model, if the stars are ships, how far is the average ship from its nearest neighbour?

    Answer: Well over a million miles

  5. Why is there no life on the stars?

    Answer: The stars are far too hot for life to exist on them
