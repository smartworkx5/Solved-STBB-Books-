Sample solved questions

Define the normal distribution and its probability density function. The normal distribution is a continuous distribution with a bell-shaped curve. It is symmetric about its mean. Its probability density function is f(x) = 1/(sigma sqrt(2 pi)) e^(-(x-mu)^2 / (2 sigma^2)). It is the most important distribution in statistics. What is the normal cumulative distribution function? The cumulative distribution function is F(x) = P(X <= x). It is the integral of the density function from -infinity to x. This integral cannot be carried out in a closed form. So tables of the standard normal distribution are used. What is the standard normal random variable? State Theorem 10.1. A normal variable X with mean mu and standard deviation sigma is changed to standard normal by Z = (X - mu)/sigma. Z has mean 0 and variance 1. Theorem 10.1: If X follows N(mu, sigma^2) and Z = (X - mu)/sigma, then Z follows N(0, 1). This change lets us use one table for all normal variables. What is the standard normal probability density function? State Theorem 10.2. The density of the standard normal variable Z is phi(z) = 1/sqrt(2 pi) e^(-z^2/2) for -infinity < z < infinity. phi(z) is the ordinate of the standard normal curve at Z = z. Theorem 10.2: If Z follows N(0, 1), then phi(-z) = phi(z). This means the curve is symmetric about zero.

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. What is the shape of the curve of the normal distribution?

    Answer: A bell-shaped curve

  2. What is the probability density function f(x) of a normal distribution with mean mu and standard deviation sigma?

    Answer: f(x) = 1/(sigma sqrt(2 pi)) e^(-(x-mu)^2 / (2 sigma^2))

  3. About what value is the normal distribution symmetric?

    Answer: Its mean mu

  4. What does the normal cumulative distribution function F(x) represent?

    Answer: F(x) = P(X <= x)

  5. Why are tables of the standard normal distribution used to find normal probabilities?

    Answer: Because the integral of the density cannot be carried out in a closed form
