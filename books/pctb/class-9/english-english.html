Sample solved questions

Pre-reading Before Islam, Arabia was a land of great natural beauty but of social and moral disorder. In the fifth and sixth centuries, mankind stood on the verge of chaos: the civilization built over four thousand years had started crumbling, and people lived in ignorance, superstition, cruelty and injustice, worshipping idols instead of one God. The Arabs themselves were gifted — they possessed remarkable memory and eloquence, expressed in poetry — but they lacked divine guidance, which is why the text presents the coming of the Rasoolullah as the lifting of humanity from ignorance into the light of faith. Pre-reading Islam changed people's lives completely: it replaced idol-worship and superstition with the Oneness of Allah (Tauheed) and the unity of mankind, destroyed the nexus of ignorance and disbelief, and set up a noble conception of life. The text says this produced a thorough transformation of man and society, raising man to the highest possible level in both spiritual and worldly domains, and giving humanity a perfect model to follow in the life and character of the Rasoolullah. Pre-reading Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) is the last Rasool of Allah. He was born in Makkah Mukarramah, received the first revelation in the cave of Hira, and proclaimed Tauheed and the unity of mankind for twenty-three years. The text quotes the Quran (33:45-46): he was sent as a witness, a bearer of good news and a warner, and as one inviting to Allah by His permission, and as a light-giving torch. Pre-reading The world's major religions include Islam (Hazrat Muhammad, peace be upon him, as the last Rasool), Christianity (Hazrat Isa/Jesus), Judaism (Hazrat Musa/Moses), Hinduism (with figures such as Rama and Krishna in its tradition), Buddhism (Gautama Buddha) and Sikhism…

Sample MCQs with answers

According to the notes, how is Makkah Mukarramah described in relation to the Red Sea?

Answer: About fifty miles from the Red Sea

What was held at Ukaz every year, according to the text?

Answer: A fair for poetical competitions

What did Hammad tell Caliph Walid bin Yazid he could recite?

Answer: One hundred long poems for each letter of the alphabet

At what age did Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) spend most of his time in solitude and meditation in the cave of Hira?

Answer: At thirty-eight years of age

Who brought the first revelation to the Rasoolullah (peace be upon him)?

Answer: Hazrat Jibril (Gabriel)
