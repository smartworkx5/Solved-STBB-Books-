Sample solved questions

Key Points Use I, he, she, we, they as the subject of a sentence, and after "and" - "Naureen and I were present." Use me, him, her, us, them as the object - "You are taller than him." Use who for the subject - "It was Salma who called on you." Use a possessive pronoun when nothing follows it - hers, ours, his - "These books are not hers." Use a possessive adjective before a noun - her, our, his - "He lost his bat." Solved Exercises 1. Naureen and ______ were present. (I, me) I 2. It was Salma ______ called on you. (who, whom) who 3. It might have been ______. (he, him) he 4. You are taller than ______. (he, him) him 5. He is a better batsman than ______. (we, us) us 6. Is that Shilla? Yes, it is ______. (she, her) her 7. Nobody but ______ was absent. (she, her) she 8. She and ______ are good friends. (I, me) I 9. Your pen writes better than ______. (her, hers) hers 10. These books are not ______. (her, hers) hers 11. He lost his bat and we ______. (our, ours) ours 12. The bike which has been stolen is not ______. (his, him) his

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. Complete correctly: 'Naureen and ______ were present.'

    Answer: I

  2. Complete correctly: 'It was Salma ______ called on you.'

    Answer: who

  3. Complete correctly: 'You are taller than ______.'

    Answer: him

  4. Complete correctly: 'He is a better batsman than ______.'

    Answer: us

  5. Which pronouns are used as the subject of a sentence according to the notes?

    Answer: I, he, she, we, they
