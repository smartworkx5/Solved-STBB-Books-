Sample solved questions

Key Points Marshall's definition of economics is related to material welfare . The most clear and comprehensive definition of economics is given by Robbins . In economics, goods are limited , scarce and have prices . Adam Smith's book The Wealth of Nations was published in 1776 . Economic resources include land , labour and capital . Economic problem arises because resources are scarce . Robbins criticized the definition given by Alfred Marshall . According to Robbins means have alternative uses. The founder of economics was Adam Smith . The classical school of thought regarded economics as the study of wealth .

Sample MCQs with answers

Whose definition of economics is related to material welfare?

Answer: Alfred Marshall

Who gave the most clear and comprehensive definition of economics?

Answer: Lionel Robbins

In economics, goods are:

Answer: Limited, scarce and have prices

In which year was Adam Smith's book 'The Wealth of Nations' published?

Answer: 1776

Which of the following are economic resources?

Answer: Land, labour and capital
