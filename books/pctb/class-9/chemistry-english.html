(a) Brass (b) Sulphur (c) Graphite (d) Bronze Correct answer: Sulphur Q: 1(v) Which liquid among the following is a colloidal solution? (a) Milk (b) Slaked lime used for white wash (c) Vinegar solution (d) Mixture of AgCl in water Correct answer: Milk Q: 1(vi) Which of the following is a heterogeneous mixture? (a) A solution of calcium hydroxide in water (b) A solution of potassium nitrate in water (c) Hot chocolate (d) Concrete mixture Correct answer: Concrete mixture Q: 1(vii) A state of matter whose properties are between those of liquids and crystalline solids: (a) Liquid crystal (b) Supercritical fluid (c) Plasma (d) Dark matter Correct answer: Liquid crystal Q: 1(viii) When the tiny visible particles of a substance are dispersed through a medium, the mixture is named as: (a) True solution (b) Colloid (c) Suspension (d) Saturated solution Correct answer: Suspension Q: 1(ix) A solution of KClO3 has a solubility of about 13.2 g per 100 cm3 at 40 degrees C.
