Sample solved questions

Emerging Technology Emerging technology is generally referred to as a new technology — a technology that is newly developing and is expected to change the way we live and work. Important examples are Artificial Intelligence , robotics , blockchain , Virtual Reality and 5G. An older technology such as 3G is not an emerging technology. Robotics Robotics is the branch of technology that designs and builds robots . Robotics is replacing human efforts in many dangerous and repetitive jobs. The primary difficulties in robotics are that robots have no feelings or emotions, they are costly to design and maintain, they need a continuous power supply, and building robots that can safely work in every kind of environment is very difficult. Robots also cannot take decisions like humans in unexpected situations. Artificial Intelligence Artificial Intelligence (AI) enables machines to act and think like humans. Applications of AI include self-driving cars, expert systems, chatbots , gaming, marketing, social media, natural language processing, machine vision and navigation control. AI is used in almost every field of life, such as healthcare, education, business and transport. A chatbot is an example of Artificial Intelligence. Machine Learning Machine Learning is a branch of Artificial Intelligence that enables a system to learn and improve itself from its experience without any user interference. A machine learning system improves itself with experience, just as humans learn from practice. Computer Assisted Translation CAT is also known as Computer Assisted Translation . Its main purpose is to help translators translate written and spoken text from one language to another quickly and accurately with the help of software programs.
