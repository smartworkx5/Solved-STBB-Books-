Sample solved questions

Foundation of the Company The East India Company was established by the British traders (merchants) of England in 1600 CE , after getting royal permission (a charter ) from the Queen of England, for the purpose of trade with India and the East . Arrival in India The Company's ships first came to the port of Surat on 24 August 1608 CE . In 1612 CE it defeated the Portuguese in India and won trading concessions from the Mughal Empire, and in 1613 CE a Farman (royal order) was issued by the Mughal emperor Jahangir allowing the Company to establish a factory in Surat . What the Company Traded The Company traded mainly in spices, silk, cotton (cotton and silk goods/cloth), indigo, dye, tea and saltpeter . It bought these Indian goods cheaply and sold them in Europe at huge profits . Indian goods were in great demand in Europe, and Arab and Portuguese traders were already earning huge profits, so the British also wished to trade with India. From Trade to Rule Although the Company was established for trade, it gradually became involved in the politics of the subcontinent . By winning the Battles of Plassey (1757 CE) and Buxar (1764 CE) it became the real ruler of Bengal and then of most of India, until after the War of Independence 1857 CE the British Crown took over India directly in 1858 CE .
