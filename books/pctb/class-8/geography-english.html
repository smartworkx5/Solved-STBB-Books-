Sample solved questions

An ocean is a huge body of salt water on the Earth's surface — the largest body of water. A sea is a part of an ocean partly enclosed by land; it is a smaller body of water located on the margins of an ocean, such as the Arabian Sea and the Mediterranean Sea. The Three Largest Oceans Pacific Ocean — the largest ocean of the world, area about 166 million sq km, average depth about 4,280 m. It lies between Asia and Australia in the west and North and South America in the east, and contains the Mariana Trench, the deepest point on Earth (about 36,201 feet). Atlantic Ocean — the second largest, area about 82 million sq km, average depth about 3,900 m, between the Americas in the west and Europe and Africa in the east. It is the most important and busiest trade route of the world and has the mid-oceanic ridge. Indian Ocean — the third largest, area about 73 million sq km, average depth about 3,900 m, between Africa in the west, Asia in the north and Australia in the east. The Suez Canal links it with the Mediterranean Sea, making it an important trade route between the western and eastern hemispheres.
