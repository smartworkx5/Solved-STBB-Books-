Sample solved questions

Tolerance — The Central Quality Tolerance means to bear pain or hardships with patience. It also means to bear things we do not like — for example, when we are in a position to take revenge but do not do so, we are displaying tolerance. The Rasoolullah (SAW) is the perfect model of tolerance. Key Examples from His Life Taif: The people of Taif threw stones at him, yet he did not curse them. Hazrat Jibrael (AS) said Allah had sent the Angel of the Mountains, who would let Al-Akh-Shabain (the two mountains) fall on them if the Rasoolullah (SAW) wished. He refused, hoping that Allah would let them beget children who would worship Allah Alone. He prayed for his enemies instead of taking revenge. Conquest of Makkah: Abu Sufyan had been the worst enemy of Islam, yet the Rasoolullah (SAW) not only forgave him but honoured him by declaring amnesty for those who took refuge in his house. He declared a general amnesty and forgave all his enemies. Hazrat Ayesha (RA) said that he never took revenge upon anyone. His servant: Hazrat Ans Bin Maalik (RA) said, 'I served the Rasoolullah (SAW) for 10 years. By Allah, he never objected to anything I said or did.' Last address: He said, 'Revenge for bloodshed, as was the custom in the days of ignorance before Islam, is forbidden. I forgive those who murdered my cousin, Ibne-Rabiah.' Word Meanings revenge — deliberate punishment or injury inflicted in return for what one has suffered. conquest — the act of taking control of a place by force; victory. amnesty — a general pardon or forgiveness declared for offenders. troubled — caused pain or worry to someone. custom — a traditional and widely accepted way of behaving in a society. forbidden — prohibited; not allowed. objected — said something to express disapproval or disagreement.

What does tolerance mean?

Answer: To bear pain or hardships with patience

Who had been the worst enemy of Islam before the conquest of Makkah?

Answer: Abu Sufyan

What was declared for those who took refuge in Abu Sufyan's house?

Answer: Amnesty — they would be safe

What did the people of Taif do to the Rasoolullah (SAW)?

Answer: They threw stones at him

Who told the Rasoolullah (SAW) that the Angel of the Mountains had been sent?

Answer: Hazrat Jibrael (AS)
