Sample solved questions

The Alphabet In this unit we learn the alphabet, letter sounds, tracing lines and writing capital and small letters. English is written from left to right. Aa, Bb, Cc, Dd, Ee, Ff, Gg, Hh, Ii, Jj, Kk, Ll, Mm, Nn, Oo, Pp, Qq, Rr, Ss, Tt, Uu, Vv, Ww, Xx, Yy, Zz. Capital letters are big letters. Small letters are little letters. Every letter has its own sound. We say the sound of each letter when we read it. Key Points The alphabet is a set of letters. We can write each letter of the alphabet as a small letter or as a capital letter.

Sample MCQs with answers

How many letters are there in the English alphabet?

Answer: 26

Capital letters are ______ letters.

English is written from ______.

Answer: left to right

What is the small letter for B?

Answer: b
