Sample solved questions

Key Facts The chapter states that in Asia and Eastern Europe temperate grasslands are called steppes . A plain is a broad area of relatively flat land . The chapter's summary states mountains are the highest natural landscapes. The correct fact is Lacustrine Plain . A plain formed by deposits in a former lake basin, as in the Kashmir Valley. The chapter's summary states levee features are formed by the repeated flooding of the river.

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. In Asia and Eastern Europe, temperate grasslands are called:

    Answer: Steppes

  2. A plain is best described as:

    Answer: A broad area of relatively flat land

  3. According to the chapter's summary, the highest natural landscapes are:

    Answer: Mountains

  4. A plain formed by deposits in a former lake basin is called:

    Answer: A lacustrine plain

  5. The Kashmir Valley is given in the chapter as an example of:

    Answer: A lacustrine plain
