Sample solved questions

Emerging Technology Emerging technology is a new technology, or the advancement of an existing technology, that is developing fast and bringing technical, institutional and social change. Its characteristics are innovation , fast growth , consistency and prominent impact. Important examples are Artificial Intelligence , robotics , 3D printing , virtual reality , augmented reality , self-driving cars and 3D holographic imaging. Robotics Robotics is the branch of technology that deals with the design, construction and use of robots — machines that can perform tasks automatically, often tasks that are dangerous, difficult or repetitive for humans. Robots are used in factories for assembling cars and goods, in hospitals for surgery and delivering medicines, in space exploration, in homes for cleaning, and in agriculture and the army for risky work. Artificial Intelligence Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the technology that enables machines and computers to act and think like humans — to learn from experience, recognise speech and images, and make decisions. The goal of AI is to make devices act and think like humans. AI is helpful in voice assistants, medical diagnosis, self-driving cars, online recommendations, translation, fraud detection and doing fast, accurate work in offices, farms and factories. Self-Driving Cars A self-driving car (also called an automatic car) is operated by its own computer system, not by a human driver. Sensors , cameras, radar and AI software observe the road, traffic and obstacles and control the steering, speed and brakes automatically, so the car drives itself safely to the destination.
