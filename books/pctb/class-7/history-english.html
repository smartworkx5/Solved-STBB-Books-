Sample solved questions

The Middle Ages The Middle Ages in Europe lasted from about 500 CE to 1500 CE . The period began with the fall of the Roman Empire in 476 CE , when Germanic tribes invaded its western part and formed many small kingdoms. Historians divide the Middle Ages into three phases : the Early Middle Ages, the High (Later) Middle Ages and the End of the Middle Ages. Early Middle Ages In the early phase, the invading Germanic tribes formed small kingdoms and converted to Christianity . The pope and church leaders became very powerful, and monasteries were the centres of learning. Manorialism became the chief economic system. Charlemagne , king of the Franks, was the most successful king of this phase. Viking invaders attacked many parts of Europe from the 8th to the 10th century CE, and in the 10th century France developed the feudal system to provide security. High Middle Ages Conditions improved in the 11th century CE : people grew more nutritious food, health and population improved, towns grew and trade increased. Kings like Henry II of England and Louis IX of France forced feudal lords to accept their authority. Europeans developed the Gothic style of art and architecture, the first universities were founded, and the Crusades were fought (1095-1291 CE). Chengiz Khan founded the Mongol Empire in 1206 CE , and King John signed the Magna Carta in 1215 CE . End of the Middle Ages The last phase was a time of trouble. England and France fought the Hundred Years' War (1337-1453 CE) , and the Black Death (Bubonic Plague) killed about one-third of the people of Europe between 1347 and 1351 CE.
