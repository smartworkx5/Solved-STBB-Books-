Sample solved questions

The Last Sermon Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) is the last Rasool (Messenger) of Allah. He is Khatam-un-Nabiyyin — the final Prophet, after whom there is no prophet. He delivered his Last Sermon (Khutbah) on the 9th of Dhul Hijjah, 10 years after Hijrat , in the Uranah valley of Mount Arafat, during his last Hajj. Its message was directed to all humanity. Main Teachings of the Sermon The blood (life), property and honour of every Muslim are sacred — as sacred as the day of Arafat, the month of Dhul Hijjah and the city of Makkah. No one may harm another Muslim's life, property or honour. Blood revenge of the Days of Ignorance was abolished. The first claim of blood revenge abolished was that of Ibn Rabi'ah bin Harith. No one may take revenge for a murder on his own; justice is through law. Riba (usury) — the extra amount (interest) charged on a loan — was declared forbidden. All claims of interest were remitted (cancelled), beginning with the interest due to Hazrat Abbas bin Abdul-Muttalib. People were advised to worship Allah, offer the five prayers, fast in Ramadan, pay Zakat willingly and perform Hajj. Zakat is an act of worship and obedience to Allah. The path of righteousness means living according to the commands of Allah, doing good deeds, avoiding sins and injustice, and holding fast to the Quran and the Sunnah. Equality of Mankind The sermon declares: "All mankind is from Adam and Eve. An Arab has no superiority over a non-Arab, nor a non-Arab over an Arab. A white has no superiority over a black, nor a black over a white, except by piety and good actions ." In the sight of Allah, the only standard of superiority is piety and good actions (taqwa). Sample MCQs with answers

  1. When did Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) deliver his Last Sermon?

    Answer: On 9th Dhul Hijjah, 10 years after Hijrat

  2. Where was the Last Sermon delivered?

    Answer: In the Uranah valley of Mount Arafat

  3. What does 'Khatam-un-Nabiyyin' mean?

    Answer: The final Prophet, after whom there is no prophet

  4. In the sermon, the life, property and honour of every Muslim are declared to be:

    Answer: Sacred

  5. What is riba (usury)?

    Answer: The extra amount (interest) charged on a loan
