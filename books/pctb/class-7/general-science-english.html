Sample solved questions

The Root System and Shoot System The root system is the underground part of the plant; it absorbs water and minerals and anchors the plant in the soil. The shoot system is the aerial part above the ground — stem, branches, leaves, flowers and fruits — which prepares food and bears the reproductive parts. Primary and Secondary Roots The primary root is the first root that grows out of the seed when it germinates. It grows straight down into the soil and gives rise to the secondary (lateral) roots . It anchors the plant firmly and absorbs water and minerals from deep in the soil. The secondary roots are the side branches of the primary root; they spread out in the soil in different directions, increase the area of absorption and help to hold the plant firmly in the ground. Internal Structure of the Root The epidermis is the outermost layer of cells of the root; it protects the inner tissues and root hairs arise from it. Root hairs greatly increase the surface area for absorption of water and minerals. The root cap is a protective cap of cells covering the tip of the growing root; it protects the tender tip from injury as the root pushes through soil particles. The endodermis is the innermost layer of the cortex, lying just outside the vascular tissue, and it controls the entry of water and salts into the xylem. The cortex between them stores food. Key Points The primary root grows straight down from the seed; secondary roots branch out from it. Root hairs absorb water and minerals; the root cap protects the growing tip. The endodermis controls the entry of water and salts into the xylem.

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. The innermost layer of the cortex in the root is the:

    Answer: endodermis

  2. The first root that grows out of the seed on germination is called the:

    Answer: primary root

  3. The function of the root cap is to:

    Answer: protect the tender growing tip of the root

  4. Secondary roots are the:

    Answer: side branches that arise from the primary root

  5. Root hairs arise from which layer of the root?

    Answer: epidermis
