Sample solved questions

Why Meat Is an Important Food Meat is considered to be an important food item due to its proteins . It contains 16 to 22% complete protein , which is necessary for growth, repair and maintenance of body cells and tissues. Organ Meat, Bones and Vitamins Organ meat like heart, kidney and liver are excellent sources of proteins, iron, phosphorus and vitamin B12 , which is needed for the formation of red blood cells. All meats are good sources of niacin and vitamin B , which are required for the growth and development of cells and tissues. Bones and lower parts of legs are good sources of calcium and phosphorus , which provide strength and firmness to the bones and teeth. White Meat and Red Meat There are two kinds of meat. White meat (chicken, duck, rabbit, pigeon, turkey and fish) is easily digestible and provides less energy. Red meat (goat, lamb, cow, veal and camel) is a good source of iron and provides more energy. Proteins, minerals and vitamins are present in minced meat. Key Points Meat contains 16 to 22% complete protein for growth, repair and maintenance of the body. Liver, kidney and heart give iron, phosphorus and vitamin B12 for the formation of red blood cells. White meat is easily digestible and gives less energy; red meat gives more energy and is a good source of iron.
