Sample solved questions

The Cell All living things are made of tiny units called cells . A cell has a cell membrane , jelly-like cytoplasm and several small structures called organelles , each doing a special job. The cell membrane is composed of proteins and lipids and controls what enters and leaves the cell. Important Organelles The nucleus is the control centre of the cell. It contains the hereditary material (DNA in chromosomes ) and directs all cell activities such as growth, division and production of proteins. The jelly-like material inside the nucleus is called nucleoplasm , while the material between the membrane and the nucleus is the cytoplasm. Mitochondria produce energy from food, so the site of respiration in a cell is the mitochondria. Chloroplasts are found only in plant cells; they contain the green pigment chlorophyll and make food by photosynthesis. A plant cell has one large central vacuole filled with cell sap, which keeps the cell firm; an animal cell has many small vacuoles. Plant Cell and Animal Cell A plant cell has a cell wall of cellulose outside the membrane, chloroplasts and one large vacuole. An animal cell has no cell wall and no chloroplasts. The rigid cell wall gives the plant cell a fixed shape, support and protection. Key Points The nucleus controls the whole cell activity. Mitochondria release energy from food during respiration. Chlorophyll is the green pigment present in chloroplasts. Plant cells have a cell wall; animal cells do not.
