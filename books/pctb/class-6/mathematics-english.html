Factors “ Factors are the numbers which we multiply to get another number say, product or total”. For example, 6 (factor) × 3 (factor) = 18 (product) . Defining a Factor If we divide a dividend by a divisor, we get quotient and remainder. Now look at the given expressions: 12 ÷ 1 = 12, 12 ÷ 2 = 6, 12 ÷ 3 = 4, 12 ÷ 4 = 3, 12 ÷ 6 = 2, 12 ÷ 12 = 1. From the above examples, it can be seen that the number 12 is completely divisible by 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 12. The remainder in each case is zero (0). We can say that the factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 12 . So, “ A factor is a number which divides the dividend completely leaving no remainder ”. dividend = quotient × divisor + remainder Example 1 — Which of the given numbers are the factors of 42? (i) 3 (ii) 5 (i) 3: 42 ÷ 3 = 14 with remainder ‘0’. As, dividend = quotient × divisor + remainder, 42 = 14 × 3 + 0. Since, remainder is zero (0) so, 3 is a factor of 42. (ii) 5: 42 ÷ 5 = 8 with remainder ‘2’. As, dividend = quotient × divisor + remainder, 42 = 8 × 5 + 2. Since, remainder is not zero (0) so, 5 is not factor of 42. Fun Fact All even numbers will have number 2 as their factor. All numbers end with 5 will have 5 as their factor. All numbers greater than 0 and end with a ‘0’ will have 2, 5 and 10 as their factors. Factors are always whole numbers or integers. Example 2 — Write all the factors of 36 and 40 36 = 1 × 36, 36 = 2 × 18, 36 = 3 × 12, 36 = 4 × 9, 36 = 6 × 6. Sample MCQs with answers

  1. In 6 x 3 = 18, what is 18 called?

    Answer: The product

  2. Which list gives ALL the factors of 12?

    Answer: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

  3. A factor is a number which divides the dividend completely leaving what remainder?

    Answer: Zero (0)

  4. Is 3 a factor of 42?

    Answer: Yes, because 42 divided by 3 is 14 with remainder 0

  5. Why is 5 NOT a factor of 42?

    Answer: Because 42 divided by 5 gives remainder 2
