Sample solved questions

What is ICT? ICT stands for Information and Communication Technology . It is an umbrella term that includes all the computing tools and communication devices — such as computers, cell phones, television, satellite systems and the internet — that let people share information in the digital world. We use ICT to study through distance learning and video lessons, to talk through calls and video conferencing, to work and run businesses, and to get information quickly from anywhere. Data and Information Data is a raw collection of facts and figures which has no proper meaning by itself, for example the marks of students written as 45, 67, 89. When this data is processed and arranged in a meaningful form, it becomes information , for example a result card showing that a student passed with 89 marks in Mathematics. So data is the raw input and information is the useful processed output. Devices of ICT Five common ICT devices are the computer , smart phone , television , scanner and printer . Other ICT devices include tablets, digital cameras, GPS devices and radios. All these devices are used to create, store, process and communicate information. Functions of Some ICT Devices Printer (output device): produces a hard copy of documents and pictures on paper. Keyboard (input device): enters data and instructions by typing letters, numbers and symbols; it also has function keys, cursor control keys and a numeric keypad. Scanner (input device): converts hard copy documents and photographs into digital form so they can be edited, saved, printed or shared by email. Monitor (output device): also called a visual display unit; it displays text, pictures and videos — whatever we type or draw appears on its screen.
