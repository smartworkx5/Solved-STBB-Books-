Sample solved questions

Meaning of the Word Geography The word "Geography" is a combination of two words, Geo and Graphy . Geo means Earth and Graphy means description , so Geography means "Description of the Earth". What Geography Studies Geography is the study of the overall surroundings of the physical and human environment . Modern geography studies the Earth as the home of man. It tries to find out the relationship between man and his environment — in other words, it studies man's adaptation to the environment in which he lives and works. "Geography is the science of places." "Geography is a scientific or systematic study of areal variations and similarities from place to place on the surface of the Earth." What We See Around Us The Earth's surface shows many physical features, such as rivers , mountains , plains , deserts and the atmosphere above it. Geography describes all these features and how people live among them.
