About the Poem The Cricket is a poem by Marjorie Barrows . It tells about a poet and a little bug. What Happens in the Poem When the rain had gone away and the sun was shining everywhere, the poet ran out on the walk to play. The poet found a little bug. The bug was running fast, then it stopped for breath at last. The bug was all black and shiny in the sun. The bug chirped a song for the poet and gave his wings a little tug. The bug showed that he was very glad to be a bug. Key Facts The poem 'The Cricket' is written by Marjorie Barrows. The poet finds a bug while walking. The little bug was glad to be a bug. The little bug gave a little tug to its wings.
