Sample solved questions

What are ordinal numbers? Rule: Ordinal numbers tell the position of things in a row. They answer the question: at what place does this thing stand? The ordinal numbers from first to twentieth are: 1st = first 2nd = second 3rd = third 4th = fourth 5th = fifth 6th = sixth 7th = seventh 8th = eighth 9th = ninth 10th = tenth 11th = eleventh 12th = twelfth 13th = thirteenth 14th = fourteenth 15th = fifteenth 16th = sixteenth 17th = seventeenth 18th = eighteenth 19th = nineteenth 20th = twentieth In the book, honey bees flying to their hive show these positions: the bee nearest the hive is 1st (first), the next is 2nd (second), and so on up to the 20th (twentieth) bee. Remember Counting numbers (1, 2, 3, ...) tell how many . Ordinal numbers (1st, 2nd, 3rd, ...) tell the position .
