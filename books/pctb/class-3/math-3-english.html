Sample solved questions

The rule Roman numerals use letter symbols: I = 1, V = 5, X = 10 . A smaller symbol after a bigger one is added (VI = 5 + 1 = 6). A smaller symbol just before a bigger one is subtracted (IX = 10 − 1 = 9). Worked example: Write the Roman numerals for 2, 5, 12, 16, 7, 9, 19 and 20 (Exercise 1, Q1) Step 1: Build each number from the symbols I = 1, V = 5, X = 10. Step 2: 2 = I + I = II. Step 3: 5 = V. Step 4: 12 = X + I + I = XII. Step 5: 16 = X + V + I = XVI. Step 6: 7 = V + I + I = VII. Step 7: 9 = X − I = IX. Step 8: 19 = X + IX = XIX. Step 9: 20 = X + X = XX. Answer: 2 = II, 5 = V, 12 = XII, 16 = XVI, 7 = VII, 9 = IX, 19 = XIX, 20 = XX. Worked example: Count the chocolates and write the number in Roman numerals (Exercise 1, Q2) Step 1: Count the top row of the picture: 5 chocolates. Step 2: Count the bottom row: 5 chocolates. Step 3: Add: 5 + 5 = 10. Step 4: 10 in Roman numerals is X. Answer: X (ten chocolates). Worked example: Count the dice in the pile and write the number in Roman numerals (Exercise 1, Q3) Step 1: Top layer: 1 die. Step 2: Middle layer: 2 dice. Step 3: Bottom layer: 3 dice. Step 4: Add: 1 + 2 + 3 = 6. Step 5: 6 in Roman numerals is VI. Answer: VI (six dice). Worked example: Fill in the missing number or Roman numeral: 6, IX, 15, XVII, XX (Exercise 1, Q4) Step 1: 6 = VI. Step 2: IX = 9. Step 3: 15 = XV. Step 4: XVII = 17. Step 5: XX = 20. Answer: 6 = VI, IX = 9, 15 = XV, XVII = 17, XX = 20.
