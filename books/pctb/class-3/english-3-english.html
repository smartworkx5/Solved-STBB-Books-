Sample solved questions

Soft Sound of 'c' The letter c has a soft sound like s in these words: city, pencil, face . More words with the soft sound of c: fence, dice, case, cycle, mice . Soft Sound of 'g' The letter g has a soft sound like j in these words: giant, orange, pigeon . More words with the soft sound of g: germ, page, German, ginger . Consonant Clusters A consonant cluster is two consonants together in a word. sh — shop, fish. st — star, stone. Poem and Tongue Twister The poem 'If You're Happy' is recited with actions. Tongue twister: She sells sea shells by the seashore.

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. Which word has the soft sound of 'c', like the sound of 's'?

    Answer: city

  2. Which of these words also has the soft sound of 'c'?

    Answer: face

  3. The letter 'g' has a soft sound, like 'j', in which word?

    Answer: giant

  4. Which word has the soft sound of 'g'?

    Answer: orange

  5. What is a consonant cluster?

    Answer: Two consonants together in a word
