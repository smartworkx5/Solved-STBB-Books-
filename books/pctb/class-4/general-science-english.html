Sample solved questions

What Makes a Thing Living? Living things are different from non-living things because they show special characteristics. The main characteristics of living things are: They breathe . They use food to remain alive and to get energy. They reproduce offspring of their own kind. They can move on their own. They can grow . They can sense changes around them. Living things also show excretion and sensitivity . Both plants and animals are living things, and both need food, sunlight, water and air. Key Facts The characteristic common among a butterfly, a bird and a bat is that they all have wings . Plants provide oxygen to the environment.
