The rule The expanded form of a number shows the value of each digit added together. Read the digits from the left by place value: ten-thousands, thousands, hundreds, tens and ones, and write each digit multiplied by its place. The standard form is the opposite: add the expanded parts together to write the number in its usual short form. Worked example: Numbers in expanded form (Exercise 1, Q1) Step 1: (a) 75,432 = 7 ten-thousands + 5 thousands + 4 hundreds + 3 tens + 2 ones = 70,000 + 5,000 + 400 + 30 + 2 Step 2: (b) 37,911 = 3 ten-thousands + 7 thousands + 9 hundreds + 1 ten + 1 one = 30,000 + 7,000 + 900 + 10 + 1 Step 3: (c) 10,956 = 1 ten-thousand + 0 thousands + 9 hundreds + 5 tens + 6 ones = 10,000 + 0 + 900 + 50 + 6 Step 4: (d) 46,743 = 4 ten-thousands + 6 thousands + 7 hundreds + 4 tens + 3 ones = 40,000 + 6,000 + 700 + 40 + 3 Step 5: (e) 86,594 = 8 ten-thousands + 6 thousands + 5 hundreds + 9 tens + 4 ones = 80,000 + 6,000 + 500 + 90 + 4 Step 6: (f) 19,223 = 1 ten-thousand + 9 thousands + 2 hundreds + 2 tens + 3 ones = 10,000 + 9,000 + 200 + 20 + 3 Step 7: (g) 22,167 = 2 ten-thousands + 2 thousands + 1 hundred + 6 tens + 7 ones = 20,000 + 2,000 + 100 + 60 + 7 Step 8: (h) 57,890 = 5 ten-thousands + 7 thousands + 8 hundreds + 9 tens + 0 ones = 50,000 + 7,000 + 800 + 90 + 0 Step 9: (i) 36,789 = 3 ten-thousands + 6 thousands + 7 hundreds + 8 tens + 9 ones = 30,000 + 6,000 + 700 + 80 + 9 Step 10: (j) 78,324 = 7 ten-thousands + 8 thousands + 3 hundreds + 2 tens + 4 ones = 70,000 + 8,000 + 300 + 20 + 4 Step 11: (k) 41,452 = 4 ten-thousands + 1 thousand + 4 hundreds + 5 tens + 2 ones = 40,000 + 1,000 + 400 + 50 + 2 Step 12: (l) 56,432 = 5 ten-thousands + 6 thousands + 4 hundreds + 3 tens + 2 ones = 50,000 + 6,000 + 400 + 30 + 2 Answer: 75,432 = 70,000 + 5,000 + 400 +…

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. What is the expanded form of 75,432?

    Answer: 70,000 + 5,000 + 400 + 30 + 2

  2. In the expanded form of 10,956, what is written for the thousands place?

    Answer: 0

  3. What is the standard form of 20,000 + 1,000 + 100 + 70 + 1?

    Answer: 21,171

  4. What is the expanded form of 57,890?

    Answer: 50,000 + 7,000 + 800 + 90 + 0

  5. Which number has the expanded form 30,000 + 6,000 + 700 + 80 + 9?

    Answer: 36,789
