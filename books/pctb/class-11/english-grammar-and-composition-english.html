Sample solved questions

Key Points A noun names a person, place, thing or idea. Common nouns name a class — elephant, animal, house. Proper nouns name a particular person or place — Ravi River, Indus River. Collective nouns name a group taken together — crowd, flock, class, furniture. Abstract nouns name a quality or state — truth, honesty, health, happiness. Solved Exercises 1. The crowd is very big. Crowd — Collective Noun. It is the name of a group of people taken together. 2. The elephant is an intelligent animal. Elephant — Common Noun; animal — Common Noun. Both are names of a class of living beings, not of any particular one. 3. The Ravi River is one of the five tributaries of the Indus River. Ravi River — Proper Noun; tributaries — Common Noun; Indus River — Proper Noun. The names of particular rivers are proper nouns, while tributaries is a common noun. 4. The shepherd took the flock of sheep to the field. Shepherd — Common Noun; flock — Collective Noun; sheep — Common Noun; field — Common Noun. A flock is a group of sheep taken together. 5. I bought furniture for my new house. Furniture — Collective Noun; house — Common Noun. Furniture is the name of a collection of household articles taken together. 6. Always speak the truth. Truth — Abstract Noun. It is the name of a quality which cannot be seen or touched. 7. He gave me a bunch of grapes. Bunch — Collective Noun; grapes — Common Noun. A bunch is a group of grapes taken together. 8. The class is studying grammar. Class — Collective Noun; grammar — Abstract Noun. A class is a group of students, and grammar is the name of a subject. 9. We all love honesty. Honesty — Abstract Noun. It is the name of a quality which cannot be seen or touched. 10. Without health, there is no happiness. Health — Abstract Noun; happiness — Abstract Noun.…

Sample MCQs with answers

In 'The crowd is very big', what kind of noun is 'crowd'?

Answer: Collective Noun

In 'The elephant is an intelligent animal', what kind of noun is 'elephant'?

Answer: Common Noun

In the sentence about the Ravi River and the Indus River, what kind of nouns are 'Ravi River' and 'Indus River'?

Answer: Proper Nouns

In 'The shepherd took the flock of sheep to the field', what kind of noun is 'flock'?

Answer: Collective Noun

According to the notes, how is 'furniture' classified in 'I bought furniture for my new house'?

Answer: Collective Noun
