Sample solved questions

Q1.1 Define the word statistics and explain its different meanings? The word 'statistics' is used in two main senses. As a plural noun it means numerical facts or data systematically collected, e.g. the statistics of births, prices or rainfall. As a singular noun it means the science (the body of methods) used for collecting, organising, presenting, analysing and interpreting numerical data and for drawing conclusions from them. The book notes four present-day uses of the word: statistics as numerical data, as a science of methods, as the results of applying those methods, and as estimates or computed values. Q1.2 Define the following terms: (i) Population and sample (ii) Parameter and statistic. Population: the total set of all items or individuals about which information is required, e.g. all students of a college. Sample: a part (a subset) of the population selected for study, e.g. 50 students chosen from the college. Parameter: a numerical quantity computed from the population, e.g. the mean height of all students. Statistic: a numerical quantity computed from the sample, e.g. the mean height of the 50 selected students; it is used to estimate the parameter. Q1.3 Distinguish between qualitative and quantitative variables? Qualitative (categorical) variables describe a quality or attribute that cannot be measured in numbers, e.g. sex, religion, colour, level of satisfaction; they are counted in categories. Quantitative variables take numerical values that can be measured or counted, e.g. height, weight, marks, income. Quantitative variables are further divided into discrete (countable, e.g. number of children) and continuous (measurable on a scale, e.g. height). Sample MCQs with answers

  1. In its plural sense, what does the word 'statistics' mean?

    Answer: Numerical facts or data systematically collected, such as statistics of births, prices or rainfall

  2. What is a population in statistics?

    Answer: The total set of all items or individuals about which information is required

  3. A mean height calculated for ALL students of a college is an example of a:

    Answer: Parameter

  4. A mean height calculated for 50 students selected from a college is an example of a:

    Answer: Statistic
