Sample MCQs with answers

  1. Why was the bus late in the play 'Heat Lightning'?

    Answer: Because of the storm

  2. Where was the Girl coming from when her car stopped?

    Answer: From a party

  3. What did the Girl see the man dragging out of his car?

    Answer: The corpse of a woman

  4. Why could the Girl not describe the killer?

    Answer: She was too frightened and had seen him only in flashes of lightning

  5. Why did the Girl leave her car and start walking down the road?

    Answer: Her car ran out of gas
