Sample solved questions

This book is the Balochistan Textbook Board Class 4 Mathematics book (English medium, based on the National Curriculum of Pakistan 2022-23). It has twelve units: Whole Numbers; Addition and Subtraction; Multiplication and Division; Multiples and Factors; Fraction; Decimals; Patterns; Length, Mass and Capacity; Time; Perimeter and Area; Practical Geometry; and Data Handling and Probability, plus a Glossary at the end. In these easy notes every exercise of the book is worked step by step as teaching notes. Classroom Activities in the book are done in class and are not written out as solved items. Learning Outcomes Count up to 99,999 (5-digit numbers). Read and write numbers up to 99,999 in numerals and in words. Recognise the place value of each digit in 5-digit numbers. Compare numbers using symbols and arrange numbers up to 99,999. Round numbers to the nearest tens, hundreds and thousands, and round numbers to the nearest ten thousands. Read and write Roman numbers up to 100. Numbers up to 99,999: place value, expanded form, words and numerals, comparing, ordering, rounding and Roman numerals.
