Sample solved questions

The Lesson in Brief This unit teaches patience, tolerance and mercy through four events from the life of Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH): the journey to Taif, the old woman of Makkah, the Treaty of Hudaybiyah and the Conquest of Makkah. At Taif, the people threw stones at Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) and badly hurt him when he invited them to Islam. He did not curse them. Instead, he prayed that their future generations would turn to Islam, and his patience was later rewarded when many people of Taif accepted Islam. An old woman in Makkah used to throw garbage on him whenever he passed her house, yet he never became angry and never fought with her. One day she did not come out, so he went to her house, found her sick and cared for her with kindness. Surprised by his good behaviour, she embraced Islam. The Muslims were unhappy with the Treaty of Hudaybiyah because the Quraish set very difficult conditions. Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) still showed great patience and agreed to the terms for the sake of peace, and that patience later brought success to Islam. At the Conquest of Makkah, the people feared revenge for years of cruelty, but he forgave them all and treated them with mercy instead of revenge. Thousands then accepted Islam with love and respect, and his forgiveness united the people and spread peace in Makkah. Key Points Patience means staying calm and kind even when others are cruel. Forgiveness can change hearts more strongly than revenge. Peace agreements may look hard at first yet bring success later.
