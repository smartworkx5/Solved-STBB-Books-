Sample solved questions

Living things grow , take in nutrients , breathe , reproduce , remove waste, respond to their surroundings and finally die. Plants get energy from light through photosynthesis , while animals get energy from the food they eat. Characteristics of Living Things The basic characteristics of living things are growth , nutrition , respiration , sensitivity , reproduction and excretion . Plants and animals both show these life processes, with some similarities and differences between them. Living and Non-Living Things Living things grow in size, take in food for energy, breathe, respond to changes around them, reproduce their own kind and remove waste from their bodies. Non-living things do none of these: they do not grow, eat, breathe, respond or reproduce. Respiration During respiration , a living body exchanges gases with its surroundings to obtain energy from food. Animals take in oxygen from the air and release carbon dioxide . Plants take in carbon dioxide in daytime and release oxygen, and at night they take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide. Sensitivity Sensitivity is the ability of living things to detect and respond to changes in their environment. Plants grow towards light, the leaves of the mimosa plant close when touched, and we blink when bright light shines into our eyes. Habitat A habitat is a place where a plant or an animal lives, such as a forest for many plants, birds, insects and animals. A habitat provides everything a living organism needs, including food, water and space, and helps it to grow, reproduce and increase its population. Sample MCQs with answers

Through which process do plants get energy from light?

Answer: Photosynthesis

Which of these is NOT one of the basic characteristics of living things?

Answer: Sleeping

During respiration, animals take in which gas from the air?

Answer: Oxygen

At night, plants take in ______ and release ______.

Answer: oxygen, carbon dioxide

Sensitivity in living things means the ability to:

Answer: detect and respond to changes in the environment
