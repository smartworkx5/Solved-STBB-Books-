Sample solved questions

This unit teaches that people in a society depend on one another for safety, help and progress, so peaceful living is necessary. Peace brings cooperation and development, while conflict harms everyone and breaks trust. The Charter of Madina When the Holy Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) migrated from Makkah to Madina in 622 AD , he established the Charter of Madina . It laid down rules for living together among the Muslims, Jews and other tribes of Madina. Every community was free to practise its own faith. Disputes had to be settled fairly, and no community could oppress another. All communities had to cooperate and defend the city together. The Charter is still important today because it teaches inclusivity, tolerance, justice and cooperation wherever different communities live together. Key Points Peace is necessary because society members depend on one another. The Charter of Madina was established on the Prophet's arrival at Madina. Its lessons are inclusivity, tolerance, justice and cooperation.

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. Why is peace necessary in a society?

    Answer: Because people depend on one another for safety, help and progress

  2. What does peace bring to a society?

    Answer: Cooperation and development

  3. What does conflict do to a society?

    Answer: It harms everyone and breaks trust

  4. In which year did the Holy Prophet (peace be upon him) migrate from Makkah to Madina?

    Answer: 622 AD

  5. What was established when the Holy Prophet reached Madina?

    Answer: The Charter of Madina
