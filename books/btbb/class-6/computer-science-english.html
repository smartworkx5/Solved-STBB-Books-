Sample solved questions

ICT stands for Information and Communication Technology . It is a connected system of interactive tools and technologies used to send and receive information, with a focus on communication technology. The Computer A computer is an electronic machine that can accept data (input), manipulate the data according to specified rules (process), produce results (output) and store the results for future use. Data and Information Data Information A collection of raw facts and figures. It is unorganized and not useful by itself. It can be words, numbers, images and sounds. Organized, useful data. It is how you understand the facts, and it is the result of data processing. So, processed data is called information , and a set of instructions that tells the computer what to do is called a program . Key Points ICT means Information and Communication Technology. A computer works in four steps: input, process, output and store. Data is raw and unorganized; information is organized and useful.
