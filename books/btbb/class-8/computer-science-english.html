Sample solved questions

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of a computer or machine to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. These tasks include understanding language, recognizing images, solving problems and making decisions. Where AI Is Used Healthcare , finance and education — AI improves efficiency, accuracy and decision-making. Transportation and entertainment also use AI in daily life. Machine Learning (ML) Machine Learning is a branch of AI that enables computers to learn from data and improve their performance on tasks without being explicitly programmed . Instead of following fixed instructions, ML algorithms analyze patterns in data to make predictions or decisions . ML in Daily Life Recommendations on Netflix and Amazon . Image and speech recognition, such as Siri and Google Assistant . Fraud detection by banks. Self-driving cars .

  3. Machine Learning (ML) is a branch of which field?

    Answer: Artificial Intelligence (AI)
