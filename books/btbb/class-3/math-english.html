Sample solved questions

This book is the Balochistan Textbook Board's Class 3 Mathematics book (English medium, based on the National Curriculum 2022-23). It has seven units: Whole Numbers, Number Operations, Fractions, Measurement of Length, Mass, Capacity and Temperature, Time, Geometry, and Data Handling and Probability, plus a Glossary at the end. In these solved notes every lesson is summarised and every exercise is solved step by step. Where the book gives only reading, counting practice or a practical activity, that work is stated clearly so the pupil can do it. Learning Outcomes Students' Learning Outcomes (page 5): Count up to 9999 (4-digit numbers). Read and write up to 999 in numerals and in words. Recognise the place value of each digit in 4-digit numbers. Compare numbers using symbols and arrange numbers up to 9999 using appropriate language. Round numbers to the nearest tens, hundreds and thousands using different concrete and pictorial representations. Read and write Roman numbers up to 20. Recognise and differentiate between even and odd numbers. We have already learned numbers up to 999. Before we start, let's quickly recap.
