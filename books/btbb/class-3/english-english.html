Sample solved questions

use pre-reading strategies to predict some words that might occur in a text by looking at the picture/title; respond to text with reasons and personal opinions; engage in simple conversations that require performing everyday tasks and speech acts, such as introducing themselves and others, giving directions, making a call, making requests; use a dictionary or electronic means to find the spelling and meaning of words; recognize alphabetical arrangement of words based on first two letters as preparation for glossary or dictionary use; understand and use different types of nouns i.e., common, proper, countable, uncountable and collective nouns; develop consistency in the size and proportion of letters and the spacing of words; practise joining letters in handwriting.

Sample MCQs with answers

What should pupils use pre-reading strategies to predict from a picture or title?

Answer: Some words that might occur in a text

How should pupils respond to a text, according to the outcomes?

Answer: With reasons and personal opinions

Which everyday speech act is listed in the outcomes?

Answer: Introducing themselves and others
