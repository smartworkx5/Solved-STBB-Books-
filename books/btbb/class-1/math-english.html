Learning Outcomes Students' learning outcomes (page 5): After completing this unit, students will be able to: count objects and numbers to and across 99 (2 digit numbers) forwards and backwards, beginning from 0, 1 or from any given number; read and write numbers up to 99 (2 digit numbers) in numerals and words; recognize the place value of each digit in 2 digit numbers (tens, ones / units); compare and order numbers up to 99 using appropriate language (for instance: more and less, greater, smaller, equal to, same as, increasing, decreasing, smallest to largest and vice versa etc.); identify numbers before, after and between two numbers; recognize the position of objects and write it using ordinal numbers up to 10.
