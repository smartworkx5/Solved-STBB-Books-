Sample solved questions

Equilibrium is a Latin word meaning balance of forces. It is the state when there seems no change in a system, e.g. a pencil kept on a finger at rest. Two Types of Equilibrium Static equilibrium : the different phases remain at rest, e.g. the fulcrum and the playground seesaw. Dynamic equilibrium : the phases readily interconvert, e.g. in vapour pressure the rate of evaporation equals the rate of condensation, so net change is zero. Water, nitrogen, carbon dioxide and ozone cycles exist in dynamic equilibrium. Reversible Reactions Most chemical reactions are reversible : products can be transformed back into reactants. General form: A + B <=> C + D. The double-headed half arrow shows change in both directions. Chemical equilibrium is where the rate of producing C and D equals the rate of reproducing A and B. Ammonia: 3H2(g) + N2(g) <=> 2NH3(g). NH3 can again give H2 and N2; NH3 is an important constituent of urea fertilizer. N2O4(l) <=> 2NO2(g) at 140C: N2O4 is pale yellow (liquid), NO2 is reddish brown (gas). H2(g) + I2(g) <=> 2HI(g): colourless H2 + purple I2 form colourless HI, which decomposes back. Sulphur trioxide: 2SO2(g) + O2(g) <=> 2SO3(g). In the H2 + I2 graph, H2 and I2 concentrations fall and HI rises, then all become constant. At equilibrium all three species are present, the reaction does not stop, and rate of forward reaction = rate of reverse reaction. All reactions are reversible, but the extent of reversibility varies greatly. Key Points Equilibrium means balance of forces and no apparent change in the system. Static equilibrium is at rest; dynamic equilibrium has equal opposite changes and zero net change. A reversible reaction is written with <=>, e.g. 3H2 + N2 <=> 2NH3. Sample MCQs with answers

What does the word 'equilibrium' literally mean?

Answer: Balance of forces, a state in which there seems to be no change in the system

Which of the following is an example of static equilibrium?

Answer: A pencil balanced on a finger, or the fulcrum and playground seesaw at rest

In dynamic equilibrium, such as vapour pressure over a liquid, what is true?

Answer: The rate of evaporation becomes equal to the rate of condensation, so the net change is zero

How is a reversible reaction written?

Answer: With a double-headed half arrow, e.g. A + B C + D

What is the correct equation for the synthesis of ammonia?

Answer: N2(g) + 3H2(g) 2NH3(g)
