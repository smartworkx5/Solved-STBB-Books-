Sample solved questions

On 16th December, 1971 , Dhaka fell due to internal strife and direct military intervention of India. East Pakistan was separated and emerged as Bangladesh . General Yahya Khan resigned and on 20th December, 1971 transferred power to Zulfiqar Ali Bhutto as Chief Martial Law Administrator . Bhutto, who was foreign minister at that time, forced the Army chief to resign and appointed General Tikka Khan as the new Commander-in-Chief. He strengthened relations with Muslim countries and the international community, signed the Simla Agreement with India (India released all prisoners of war), and in April, 1972 lifted martial law and restored democracy. Economic Reforms: Agricultural Reforms Bhutto promulgated the Agricultural Reforms Ordinance on 1st March, 1972 : Land possession limit reduced from 500 to 150 acres of canal irrigated land, and from 1000 to 300 acres of rainfed land. Tenants would have Haq-e-Shufa'a if the land they cultivate is sold by its owner. No one could evict the tenants, except when the share of produce has not been paid to the landlord. Owners shall supply seeds and pay land revenue and irrigation water charges. Land above the limit shall be confiscated and divided among the tenants, free of cost. A civil servant, during service or after retirement, can possess only 100 acres of land. State agricultural lands shall be divided among landless farmers. Reforms for Industrial Workers To fulfil his slogan "bread, cloth and house for everyone" , Bhutto introduced Labour Reforms — permission for Trade Union activities and job security: Labour Courts (special courts) were set up to protect workers' rights. 5% share for labour in the total profit of the industry was made obligatory as bonus. Sample MCQs with answers

When did Zulfiqar Ali Bhutto take over power in Pakistan?

Answer: 20th December, 1971

Under the Agricultural Reforms Ordinance of 1st March 1972, the land possession limit for canal irrigated land was reduced to:

Answer: 150 acres

What right did the Agricultural Reforms give to tenants if the land they cultivated was sold by its owner?

Answer: Haq-e-Shufa'a (pre-emption right)

Under Bhutto's Labour Reforms, what share of the industry's total profit was made obligatory for labour as bonus?

Answer: 5%

Why did the Bhutto Government devalue the Pakistani rupee on 11th May, 1972?

Answer: To encourage exports
