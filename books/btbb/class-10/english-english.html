Sample solved questions

This unit tells us about Hazrat Muhammad-ur-Rasool Ullah Khatam-un-Nabiyeen (S.A.W) , the last Rasool of Allah. Before Islam, the Arabs had indulged in all sorts of evils: they worshipped gods and goddesses instead of Almighty Allah, they gambled and drank wine, there was no respect for law, and nobody's life and property was safe. Ameen and Sadiq People called the Holy Prophet Ameen (the trustworthy) and Sadiq (the truthful) because of his honesty and truthfulness. The people of Makkah became his enemies because he asked them to worship the one and only Allah and to give up worshipping idols. They gave him a lot of trouble, but he faced all the hardships with courage and determination and kept on preaching the message of Allah. The Journey to Madina The journey from Makkah to Madina was very long and dangerous, because his enemies were looking for him everywhere and wanted to kill him. The Muslims of Madina were overjoyed to see him: many of them had already embraced Islam before he reached there, and Madina became the centre of Islam . Many Muslims of Makkah followed him to Madina to escape the painful treatment of the Kafirs. What Islam Taught Mankind Islam taught mankind to worship one Allah only , to fulfil their duties towards Allah and to serve their fellow men. It taught honest rules of trade, encouraged people to seek knowledge, and gave a complete, practicable and successful way of life. After the conquest of Makkah, the idols in the Kaaba were destroyed and the Arabs gave up gambling, drinking and fighting; nobody stole anything even if it was left by the roadside, and women got their due rights and respect. Sample MCQs with answers

Who is Hazrat Muhammad-ur-Rasool Ullah Khatam-un-Nabiyeen (S.A.W)?

Answer: The last Rasool of Allah

Before Islam, whom did the Arabs worship instead of Almighty Allah?

Answer: Gods and goddesses (idols)

Why was the Holy Prophet (S.A.W) called Ameen?

Answer: Because of his trustworthiness and honesty

What does Sadiq mean?

Answer: The truthful

Why did the people of Makkah become the Holy Prophet's (S.A.W) enemies?

Answer: Because he asked them to worship one Allah and give up idols
