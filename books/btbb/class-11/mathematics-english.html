Sample solved questions

A complex number z = a + bi joins a real part a and an imaginary part b, where i = √(-1) and i^2 = -1. Complex numbers are added, subtracted, multiplied and divided part by part, and every one of them is a point (a, b) of the Argand plane . The conjugate of z is z-bar = a - bi, and the modulus |z| is the distance of the point from the origin. In polar form the same number is written with its modulus r and its angle (argument) theta. Re Im O P (a, b) r = |z| b a Formula: z = a + bi, with i^2 = -1; i^3 = -i, i^4 = 1 Formula: Conjugate: if z = a + bi then z-bar = a - bi Formula: Modulus: |z| = √(a^2 + b^2), and z x z-bar = a^2 + b^2 = |z|^2 Formula: Inverse: 1/z = z-bar / |z|^2 Formula: Polar form: z = r (cos(theta) + i sin(theta)), where r = |z| and theta = tan^(-1)(b/a) Key Points Two complex numbers are equal only when their real parts are equal and their imaginary parts are equal. Division is done by multiplying the top and bottom by the conjugate of the denominator . √(-n) = i √(n) for n > 0 — always take i out first, then multiply. For products and quotients in polar form: moduli multiply or divide, arguments add or subtract . A quadratic with no real roots has two conjugate complex roots.

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. What is the value of i^2, where i = sqrt(-1)?

    Answer: -1

  2. What is the value of i^3?

    Answer: -i

  3. What is the value of i^4?

    Answer: 1

  4. If z = 3 + 4i, what is the modulus |z|?

    Answer: 5

  5. If z = 6 + 7i, what is the conjugate of z?

    Answer: 6 - 7i
