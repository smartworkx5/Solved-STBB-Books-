A thought experiment is a hypothetical scenario in which a hypothesis, theory or principle is presented to consider its consequences. It is a mental laboratory : instead of physically mixing chemicals or using tools, you imagine a scenario related to a scientific principle and apply logical reasoning to it. How It Works Imagine a specific scenario linked to a principle or theory. Apply logical reasoning to predict the outcome if the principle is valid. Use it to predict possible outcomes, understand how a process may work, and identify issues or unexpected results.
