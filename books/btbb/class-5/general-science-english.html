Sample solved questions

The human body is made of organ systems that work together to keep us alive and healthy. The respiratory system brings oxygen into the body, the circulatory system carries oxygen and nutrients to every part, and the nervous system controls everything through the brain, spinal cord and nerves.

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. Which body system brings oxygen into the body?

    Answer: The respiratory system

  2. Which system carries oxygen and nutrients to every part of the body?

    Answer: The circulatory system

  3. Which system controls everything in the body?

    Answer: The nervous system

  4. Through which parts does the nervous system control the body?

    Answer: The brain, spinal cord and nerves

  5. Why do the organ systems of the body work together?

    Answer: To keep us alive and healthy
