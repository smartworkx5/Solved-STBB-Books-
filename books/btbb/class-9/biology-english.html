Sample solved questions

The word science comes from the Latin word Scientia , which means knowledge or learning. Science is the knowledge in which we study the things around us, or the principles of nature, and try to prove these observations by experiments to get logical results. Definition of Biology The word Biology is derived from two Greek words: bios means life and logos means study. So the scientific study of life or living organisms is called biology, or biological science, or life science. Biology is a significant part of everyday life: progress in biology gives us better quality of food, improved health facilities and a hygienic environment. The Quran Instructs Us to Study Life Many Quranic injunctions stress the study of biology. These verses describe the study of organisms and their different aspects of life: Origin of Life — Modern biologists believe that life originated first in oceans and then migrated to land. Allah says in the Holy Quran that every living thing was made from water (Surah Al-Ambiya-21: Verse-30), and that man was made from a clattering clay like the earthen ware (Surah Al-Rehman-55: Verse-14). Pairs of Plants and Animals — Plants and animals have male and female partners, and new generations are formed by sexual reproduction. "And of everything created we opposite kinds (pairs)" (Surah Al-Zariyat-51: Verse-49). Communal Life — Animals of the same species live together in the form of a population, and different populations live in the form of a community (Surah Al-Anaam-6: Verse-38). Human Embryology — Many verses stress the study of the different stages of the embryo: "He creates you in your mother's womb in one manner of creation after the other in three folds shadow" (Surah Al-Zamar-39: Verse-6).
