Sample solved questions

Science is the study of natural phenomena and the laws governing them, built up by observation, experiment and reasoning. Branches of Science Physical sciences — Physics and Chemistry, the study of non-living things. Biological sciences — Botany and Zoology, the study of living things. Earth sciences — the study of the Earth and its surroundings. Physics Physics is the branch of science that deals with the study of matter, energy and their mutual interactions. It explains how and why things move, and the laws that govern heat, light, sound, electricity and magnetism. Branches of Classical Physics Mechanics — the study of motion and forces. Heat (Thermodynamics) — the study of heat energy and its conversion into other forms of energy, or vice versa. Optics — the study of light, its properties, its behaviour on reflection and refraction, and instruments based on it such as lenses, mirrors and microscopes. Sound (Acoustics) — the study of sound waves, their production and properties.
