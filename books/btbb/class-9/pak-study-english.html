Sample solved questions

An ideology is a collection of principles, beliefs and values that influence the worldview of an individual or a group. It affects their attitudes and opinions and provides information for their political, social, economic and moral stances. Ideologies work as conceptual structures through which we interpret reality, understand society's problems and propose remedies for them. Types of Ideology Most of the time, an ideology is based on politics, economics, religion, culture and societal conventions. Well-known examples of ideologies are nationalism, religion, feminism, capitalism and socialism . Ideologies may be obvious or hidden, but they strongly affect human conduct, decision-making and the identity of individuals and groups. Importance of Ideology Ideology influences a nation's governance structure , policy direction and societal values. It affects political systems, economic policies, social and cultural values, and even foreign relations and healthcare. It serves as a compass for a nation's aspirations and decides its identity — promoting unity among citizens, or inciting discord. In democratic societies it shapes public discussion and elections; in authoritarian regimes it may be used to solidify authority. Key Points An ideology is a set of principles, beliefs and values that guides a person or a group. Nationalism, religion, feminism, capitalism and socialism are well-known ideologies. Ideology is the compass that decides a nation's identity, governance and direction.
