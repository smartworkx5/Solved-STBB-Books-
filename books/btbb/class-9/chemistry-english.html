Sample solved questions

Chemistry is the study of matter , including its composition, properties, structure and interactions with other matter and energy. A scientist who understands chemistry is called a chemist . Chemists work in many subfields, such as physical, biochemistry, organic, inorganic and analytical chemistry. Chemistry is crucial for understanding the natural world: Earth (solids) — minerals in the Earth. Air (gases) — atmospheric gases. Sea (liquids) — properties of seawater. Sky (plasma) — ions and molecules in plasma states. Chemists study the composition, structure and changes of matter in these environments. These subjects connect with geology, meteorology and oceanography, but chemistry explains the interactions and transformations of matter within these systems. This interdisciplinary approach improves our understanding and our ability to address environmental challenges on our planet. Plasma , sometimes called the fourth state of matter, makes up over 99% of the visible universe, including stars and lightning. Remember: melting of ice is a physical change , but rusting of iron is a chemical change . Do not confuse the two. Key Points Chemistry is the study of matter, its composition, properties, structure and interactions with other matter and energy. A person who understands chemistry is a chemist. Chemistry explains Earth (solids), air (gases), sea (liquids) and sky (plasma). Plasma makes up over 99% of the visible universe. Chemistry plays a vital role in medicine, agriculture, materials science and environmental protection.
