Sample solved questions

The periodic table can be divided into four blocks : s, p, d and f. The classification of elements into blocks is based upon the valence orbital of the element involved in bond formation, that is, the sub-shell into which the last electron enters. The Four Blocks Block Groups Last electron enters Elements per period General configuration s-block Group 1, Group 2 and Period 1 s-subshell 2 ns1-2 p-block Groups 13 to 18 (except helium) p-subshell 6 ns2 np1-6 d-block Groups 3 to 12 d-subshell 10 (n-1)d1-10 ns1-2 f-block Lanthanides and actinides f-subshell 14 (n-2)f1-14 (n-1)d0 or 1 ns2 The number of elements in each block per period is fixed by the capacity of the sub-shell: s holds 2 electrons, p holds 6, d holds 10 and f holds 14. Sample MCQs with answers

On what basis are elements classified into s, p, d and f blocks?

Answer: The sub-shell into which the last electron enters

Which groups belong to the s-block?

Answer: Group 1 and Group 2

What is the general configuration of the p-block?

Answer: ns2 np1-6
