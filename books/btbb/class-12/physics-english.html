Sample solved questions

Electric charge is the fundamental property of matter by virtue of which a body experiences a force in an electric field. There are two kinds: positive charge , as on a glass rod rubbed with silk, and negative charge , as on an ebonite rod rubbed with fur. Properties of Charge Like charges repel and unlike charges attract each other. Charge is quantized - it occurs only in whole multiples of the elementary charge. Charge is conserved - it can neither be created nor destroyed, only transferred. Charge is additive - the total charge is the algebraic sum of all charges. Q = n e Q - total charge, unit C; n - whole number of electrons or protons; e = 1.6 x 10 -19 C, the elementary charge A point charge is a charged body whose size is negligible compared with the distances involved. A test charge is a very small positive charge used to explore a field without disturbing it. Worked Example: Electrons in One Coulomb How many electrons are contained in 1 C and 3 C of charge? Step 1: Use Q = n e, so n = Q / e, with e = 1.6 x 10 -19 C. Step 2: For 1 C: n = 1 / (1.6 x 10 -19 ) = 6.25 x 10 18 . Step 3: For 3 C: n = 3 / (1.6 x 10 -19 ) = 1.875 x 10 19 , about 1.9 x 10 19 . Answer: 1 C contains 6.25 x 10 18 electrons and 3 C contains about 1.9 x 10 19 electrons.
