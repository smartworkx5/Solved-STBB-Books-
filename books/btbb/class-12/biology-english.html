Sample solved questions

Respiration is the exchange of respiratory gases and the release of energy from food. Breathing or ventilation is simply taking fresh air, with more O₂, into the lungs and removing air with more CO₂ out of the lungs. This lesson introduces the human respiratory system and its first parts, the nose and the pharynx. 14.1 Respiratory System of Man External Respiration Internal Respiration Exchange of respiratory gases (O₂ and CO₂) between the organism and its environment, and between the circulatory fluid and the external environment A biochemical process within the cell that oxidises food molecules; exchange of gases between the cell and the circulatory fluid No ATP or water is formed ATP, CO₂ and water are produced It is aerobic It may be aerobic or anaerobic 14.1.1 Properties of Respiratory System A respiratory surface shows three characteristics for ready exchange of gases through diffusion: It should be large, moist and highly permeable , so respiratory gases can pass through it easily. It should be a thin epithelium (1 mm or less) and possess a network of blood capillaries, which facilitates rapid transport and diffusion of gases between the surface and the blood. A good ventilation mechanism should be present to maintain a steep diffusion gradient. 14.1.2 Components of Respiratory System The respiratory system consists of the respiratory tract (the air passageway) and the respiratory organs , a pair of lungs. The upper respiratory tract includes the nostrils, nasal cavities and pharynx; the lower respiratory tract includes the larynx, trachea, bronchi and bronchioles. The air sac or alveolus is the functional unit of the lungs. 14.1.3 Nose The nose is the only externally visible part of the respiratory system, made of bones, cartilages and fatty muscle tissue. Sample MCQs with answers

What is respiration, as defined in the notes?

Answer: Exchange of respiratory gases and release of energy from food

Breathing, or ventilation, means:

Answer: Taking fresh air with more oxygen into the lungs and removing air with more carbon dioxide out

Which statement about external respiration is correct?

Answer: It is the exchange of gases between the organism and its environment, and no ATP or water is formed

Internal respiration differs from external respiration because internal respiration:

Answer: Is a biochemical process within the cell that oxidises food and produces ATP, carbon dioxide and water

A respiratory surface should be large, moist and highly permeable so that:

Answer: Respiratory gases can pass through it easily
