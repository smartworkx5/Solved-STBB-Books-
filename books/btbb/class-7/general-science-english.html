Sample solved questions

A plant has two systems: the root system below the ground and the shoot system above the ground. The root system absorbs water and nutrients from the soil. Parts of the Root System Primary root (tap root) — grows downwards from the radicle of the embryo. Secondary roots — smaller roots growing from the primary root. Tertiary roots — further branches of secondary roots. Grasses and strawberry have threadlike fibrous roots spread in the soil, not growing deep. Tip of the Root Root meristem — rapidly dividing cells at the tip. Root cap — covers and protects the meristem. Root hairs — tiny hair-like outgrowths of the epidermis. They increase the surface area for absorption of water and nutrients. The root has three regions: region of meristematic activity, region of elongation and region of maturation. The cortex in roots also stores food. Key Points Root hairs increase surface area for absorption. The root cap protects the growing tip.

Sample MCQs with answers

Which plant system grows below the ground?

Answer: The root system

What is the main job of the root system?

Answer: To absorb water and nutrients from the soil

The primary root of a plant grows from which part of the embryo?

Answer: The radicle

Smaller roots that grow out of the primary root are called:

Answer: Secondary roots

Grasses and strawberry plants have which kind of roots?

Answer: Threadlike fibrous roots spread in the soil
