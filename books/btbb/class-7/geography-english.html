Sample solved questions

Plains are one of the major landforms on Earth. They are found on every continent and cover more than one-third of the world's land area. A plain is a vast expanse of relatively flat land with low relief — there is only a small difference in elevation across it. Physical Features of Plains The landscape is flat or gently sloping. Relief is low, with little to no hills or mountains. Plains are often intersected by rivers and their river valleys. Soils are frequently fertile, because plains are formed by sediments deposited by rivers or glaciers. Plains often support grassland ecosystems, helped by favourable climate and fertile soil. Plains Around the World Steppe — the temperate grasslands of Asia and eastern Europe, which receive little rain and grow grass instead of trees. Savana — grasslands with scattered trees, found in Africa, Australia and South America. Tundra — in the Arctic region, the plains of frozen ground. Indus Plain — in Pakistan, known for its fertile soil. West Siberian Plain — one of the world's largest and flattest plains, covering almost 1 million square miles (2.6 million km2). Prairie Plains — with rich soil and favourable climate, often called breadbaskets because they produce so much food. The Nile River flows through the Egyptian Plain and makes it fertile; Egypt is called the Gift of the Nile . Key Points Plains are flat, low-relief land covering more than one-third of the world's land area. Steppe, Savana and Tundra are plains known by special names in different regions.
