Sample solved questions

Key Points The electronegativity of beryllium is approximately equal to that of aluminium. Lithium forms only the normal oxide with oxygen, while the larger alkali metals form peroxides or superoxides. Fluorine, F2, is the strongest oxidizing agent among the halogens, ahead of Cl2, Br2 and I2. Barium sulphate is not soluble in water. NaF has the highest melting point among the sodium halides NaCl, NaBr, NaF and NaI. HF has the highest boiling point among the hydrogen halides because of the highest electronegativity of fluorine. The inert pair effect dominates in lead, the heaviest Group-IVA element listed. Carbon and silicon have no tendency to form dichlorides in Group-IVA. Which one of the following metal sulphates is not soluble in water Options: (a) Na2SO4 (b) K2SO4 (c) BaSO4 (d) ZnSO4 Correct answer: BaSO4 Q: 5.
