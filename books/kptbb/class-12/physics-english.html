Sample solved questions

Key Points The repulsion between two parts of a divided charge is maximum when the charge is split equally, q equals Q divided by 2. A conductor in electrostatic equilibrium has the same potential throughout and its excess charge resides on the outer surface. The potential of earth is taken as zero because earth is a very large good conductor whose potential does not change appreciably. The kinetic energy gained by a charge accelerated through a potential difference is q times deltaV, independent of its mass, giving 1 keV for a proton through 1 kV. For an isolated charged capacitor, inserting a dielectric keeps the charge unchanged while the potential difference and stored energy decrease. The energy stored in a capacitor is one half C V squared, which gives 8 joule for 1 microfarad at 4000 volt. Capacitors in series carry the same charge, so a 2, 3 and 6 microfarad series combination across 10 volt carries 10 microcoulomb on each. Electric flux through a closed surface depends only on the charge enclosed, by Gauss law, and the work done by the field is minus q deltaV. Solved Exercises Exercise - Multiple Choice Questions Q: 1. Sample MCQs with answers

1. A charge Q is divided into two parts q and Q-q and separated by a distance R. The force of repulsion between them will be maximum when:

Answer: q = Q/2

2. Some charge is being given to a conductor. Then its potential

Answer: Is remain same throughout the conductor

3. Electric potential of earth is taken to be zero because the earth is good:

Answer: Conductor

4. A proton is about 1840 time heavier than an electron. When it is accelerated by a potential difference if 1 kV, its kinetic energy will be:

Answer: 1 keV

5. A capacitor is charged with a battery and then it is disconnected. A slab of dielectric is now inserted between the plates, then

Answer: Potential difference between the plates decreases, stored energy decreases and charge remains unchanged
