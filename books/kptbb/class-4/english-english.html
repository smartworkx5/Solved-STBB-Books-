The Rashidun Caliphs The first caliphate, the Rashidun Caliphate , was ruled by the four Rashidun (rightly-guided) caliphs: Hazrat Abu Bakr , Hazrat Umar , Hazrat Usman and Hazrat Ali . Caliphs were religious and political leaders of the Muslim world. They were close companions of Rasoolullah (peace be upon him). The Rashidun Caliphate lasted for 30 years and is a role model for all Muslim rulers. There are four Rashidun Caliphs: Hazrat Abu Bakr, Hazrat Umar, Hazrat Usman and Hazrat Ali. The First Man to Accept Islam Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique was the first among men to embrace Islam. The Title 'Al-Farooq' Al-Farooq means the one who distinguishes between the right and the wrong. Rasoolullah gave Hazrat Umar this title because he became a distinguishing factor between Muslims and disbelievers. Hazrat Usman Accepts Islam Hazrat Usman accepted Islam instantly when his friend Hazrat Abu Bakr invited him to Islam. The First Child to Accept Islam Hazrat Ali was the first among children to embrace Islam; he was at that time only ten years old . Qualities of Hazrat Umar Hazrat Umar's justice and discipline are greatly liked. He ruled with justice, granted scholarships to the poor, and served Islam and the Muslims for ten and a half years . He is a role model for Muslim leaders who want to rule with justice. The Life of Hazrat Ali Hazrat Ali was the son of Hazrat Abu Talib, the uncle of Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him). From his early childhood he lived with Rasoolullah and was brought up in his care. He was the first child to embrace Islam, at the age of ten. He wrote letters and treaties for Rasoolullah, and he was a very courageous man, known as Asadullah , the Lion of Allah.
