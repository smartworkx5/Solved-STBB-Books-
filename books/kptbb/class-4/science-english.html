Sample solved questions

Key Points Living things use food to remain alive, breathe, move on their own, grow and produce living things of their own kind, and they have the ability to sense. Plants make their own food and generally cannot move from one place to another; animals depend on plants and other animals for their food and can move from place to place. Vertebrates are animals having a backbone, for example fish, frog, lizard, pigeon and cat. Invertebrates have no backbone, for example cockroach, honey bee, butterfly, starfish and snail. There are four types of teeth: incisors for biting and cutting, canines for piercing and tearing, premolars for chewing and grinding, and molars also for chewing and grinding. Bones and muscles work together: when muscles contract, they pull the bones, so the bone moves at the joint. The lungs exchange oxygen between blood and air; the heart works like a pump and circulates blood all over the body. Many plants produce fruits to protect the seeds. Fish have fins and a tail. Non-flowering plants have no flower; pine is a non-flowering plant. Solved Exercises Q: Which one is common among butterfly, bird and bat?
