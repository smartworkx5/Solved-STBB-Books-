Key Points One degree equals pi divided by 180 radians, about 0.01745 radian, and a full circle is 2 pi radians. The metric prefix micro stands for 10 to the power minus 6, that is 0.000001. In subtraction the absolute uncertainties add, so a temperature difference of 30 degrees C from two readings each uncertain by 0.5 degrees C carries an uncertainty of 1 degree C. In multiplication and division the percentage uncertainties add, and for a power the percentage uncertainty is multiplied by the power. The number 0.00708600 has six significant figures, because leading zeros are not significant but captive and trailing zeros are. An addition result is rounded to the least number of decimal places, while a multiplication result is rounded to the least number of significant figures. The precision of a measurement is fixed by the place of its last readable significant digit, so 385,000 km is precise to 1000 km. Torque has dimensions M L squared T to the power minus 2, while strain, refractive index and magnification are dimensionless. Solved Exercises Exercise - Multiple Choice Questions Q: Q1.
