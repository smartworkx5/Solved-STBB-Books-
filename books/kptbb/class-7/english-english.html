Sample solved questions

Pre-reading Q: Q1. Why was Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) called Sadiq and Amin? Ans: As a grown-up man, Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) was so honourable and trustworthy that people would call him Al-Sadiq and Al-Amin. Al-Sadiq means the truthful one and Al-Amin means the trustworthy one, because he always spoke the truth and people could fully trust him. Q: Q2. Why is Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) called Rehmatul-lil-Aalamin? Ans: Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) is called Rehmatul-lil-Aalamin, a mercy for all the worlds, because his teaching was not for one tribe or nation only. His words in the Last Sermon were directed to the entire humanity, and he taught everyone to be kind and just to all, so his message is a mercy and guidance for the whole of mankind. Q: Q3. What was the impact of Hazrat Muhammad's (peace be upon him) personality on people? Ans: His personality had a deep impact on people. Because he was honourable and trustworthy, people called him Al-Sadiq and Al-Amin and listened to him with full attention. At the Last Sermon, when he asked the assembled people, "Have I fulfilled my mission?", the hundreds of pilgrims replied together, "In truth you have," which shows how completely people believed in him and accepted his message. Comprehension — A. Answer the following questions: Q: Q1. Describe a few character traits of Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him). Ans: Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) was honourable and trustworthy, so people called him Al-Sadiq and Al-Amin. He was firm and steadfast: he spared no effort in preaching the true religion Islam and suffered great hardships, but nothing could move him from his chosen path. He was also kind and just to everyone, as his Last Sermon teaches kindness to wives, servants and all…

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. What is the basis of superiority in the eyes of Allah?

    Answer: goodness and piety

  2. In 'There is no preference for Arab over the non-Arab', the word 'preference' means:

    Answer: liking for

  3. In 'Allah revealed to him the true religion, Islam', 'him' refers to:

    Answer: Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him)

  4. In 'Have I fulfilled my mission?', what mission is the Rasool (peace be upon him) referring to?

    Answer: Preaching of Islam

  5. The phrase 'lend me an attentive ear' means:

    Answer: listen to me attentively
