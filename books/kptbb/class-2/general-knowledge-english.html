Sample solved questions

Our Country Pakistan has four provinces : Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab and Sindh. The federal areas are Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Gilgit Baltistan and Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT). The capital of Pakistan is Islamabad . Balochistan is the largest province by area, and Punjab is the largest province by population. Gilgit Baltistan is situated in the north of Pakistan. Solved Questions and Answers Q: Look at the map of Pakistan carefully and write the names of four provinces and federal areas. Ans: The four provinces of Pakistan are Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab and Sindh. The federal areas are Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Gilgit Baltistan and Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT). Q: Draw/Make the flag of Pakistan in your notebook under the guidance of your teacher and colour it. Point out the colours representing the Muslim and other religions. Ans: Note: Drawing and colouring the flag is a practical activity and has no written answer. The colours are: the green colour stands for the Muslims, and the white colour represents the people of other religions. The Moon (Crescent) is the symbol of progress and the star is the symbol of light and knowledge (learning). Q: Collect the pictures showing the culture of different areas of Pakistan in group, show it to your class mates and discuss them among yourselves. Ans: Note: This is a group collection and discussion activity in the book and has no written answer. Q: In the guidance of your teachers, trace the following given provinces on a chart paper in group and make their cut outs. Now combine these cut outs and complete the map of Pakistan from it. (Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Gilgit Baltistan) Ans: Note: This is a practical tracing and cut-out activity in the book…
