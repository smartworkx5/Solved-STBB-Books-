Sample solved questions

Pre-reading Q: Q1. Why did non-Muslims trust Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him)? Ans: Non-Muslims trusted Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) because he was always truthful, honest and just. Even before Prophethood he remained away from the moral degeneration of society, opposed oppression and injustice, and gave every person his or her due without favour, so people of all faiths trusted his word and his decisions. Q: Q2. How did Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) set high and noble ideals for all mankind? Ans: He set high and noble ideals by his own practical example. He based the justice system on the principle of giving a person his or her due, whatever his or her stature might be. He decided every case on merit, with justice and fairness, without fear or favour, even when a case was against his own interest or the interest of those near and dear to him. Q: Q3. How can people achieve perfection in the moral, spiritual and social areas of life? Ans: People can achieve perfection in the moral, spiritual and social areas of life by following the commands of Allah and the excellent pattern of the life of Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him). They should stand firm for justice, speak the truth, give everyone his or her due, and never let personal desires, hatred or relationships make them unjust. Sample MCQs with answers

Why did non-Muslims trust Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him)?

Answer: He was always truthful, honest and just

In the case of the Quraish woman found guilty of stealing, what did Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) refuse to do?

Answer: Accept intercession to spare her from punishment

What did Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) call 'a piece of fire'?

Answer: Gains made by deceiving a judge to get a positive decision

Whom did Hazrat Muhammad (peace be upon him) assign to handle the land dispute case?

Answer: Huzayfa bin Yemin

Why was an inquiry initiated in the land dispute case?

Answer: So that the case could be decided according to evidence
