Sample solved questions

Key Points Living things are classified into groups on the basis of similar characteristics. Birds that live in water, like the duck, have webbed feet to help them swim, while land birds like the hen have separate toes with claws. The cat is a mammal; mammals give birth to their young ones and feed them on milk. A fish respires through gills. Seeds with one cotyledon are monocots (wheat, rice, maize) and seeds with two cotyledons are dicots (pea, bean, gram). Monocot leaves have parallel veins and dicot leaves have net-like veins. Echinoderms are the group found only in the ocean. Solved Exercises Q: Which one of the following is the foot of an aquatic bird? (Options are pictures labelled A, B, C and D) Options: (a) A — webbed foot (b) B — foot with thick separate toes and claws (c) C — foot with thin separate toes and claws (d) D — foot with thin separate toes and claws Correct answer: A — webbed foot Q: The cat belongs to which group? Options: (a) Amphibians (b) Reptiles (c) Birds (d) Mammals Correct answer: Mammals Q: A fish respires through which organ? Options: (a) Gills (b) Lungs (c) Air sacs (d) Skin Correct answer: Gills Q: What is the number of cotyledons in a gram seed? Options: (a) One (b) Two (c) Three (d) Four Correct answer: Two Q: Out of these which group is found only in ocean? Options: (a) Fish (b) Insects (c) Echinoderms (d) Amphibians Correct answer: Echinoderms
