Sample solved questions

Getting Started — Let's Talk Q: What behaviour do we need to show while working in a group? Ans: While working in a group we should be patient, polite and helpful. We should listen to others, share things with them, finish our own part of the work on time and never misbehave with group members. Q: What is the importance of patience while working with friends? Ans: Patience is very important while working with friends. If we stay patient, we do not get angry over small delays or mistakes. Like Ayesha in the story, we can help a friend finish his work instead of scolding him, and the group work is completed happily and on time. Q: What would you do if you were Azlan? Ans: If I were Azlan, I would not hide my laziness or misbehave with my group members. I would start my part of the project on time, and if I was late, I would say sorry to my friends and work hard to finish it quickly. Pre-reading Q: Have you ever faced any situation in which you showed patience? How? Ans: Yes. Once my younger brother broke my pen by mistake. I wanted to scold him, but I stayed calm and patient. I forgave him and shared another pen with him. In this way I showed patience and avoided a quarrel. While-reading Q: If someone misbehaves with you, what would be your reaction? Ans: If someone misbehaves with me, I will stay patient like Rasoolullah (PBUH). I will not scold or fight with that person. I will speak politely, forgive him and pray for his guidance, because patience always brings a good result. Post-reading Q: Who is your role model and why? Discuss. Ans: My role model is Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH). He is complete in all aspects. Sample MCQs with answers

  1. How long did Rasoolullah (PBUH) stay in Taif?

    Answer: ten days

  2. Why was Rasoolullah (PBUH) called Saadiq?

    Answer: He always spoke the truth

  3. How did the people of Taif treat Rasoolullah (PBUH)?

    Answer: They mocked him and a mob pelted him with pebbles

  4. What did Rasoolullah (PBUH) do for the people who hurt him at Taif?

    Answer: He prayed to Allah to guide them to the right path

  5. In 'The farmer is cutting wheat', which letters make the digraph?

    Answer: wh
