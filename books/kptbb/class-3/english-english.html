Sample solved questions

First Day in Class III - My Speech: Pre-reading, While-reading and Post-reading Q: Pre-reading: What is your favourite subject? Ans: Sample answer: My favourite subject is English. I like to speak with my friends in English and to learn new words through games and activities, as the speaker does in the speech. Q: Pre-reading: Do you like your school? Give reason. Ans: Sample answer: Yes, I like my school. I come to school every day to learn new things, and it is fun to learn and play in the school with my friends. Q: While-reading: Do you want to add something to your speech? Ans: Sample answer: Yes. I want to add that we should listen to our teachers, follow the class rules, and keep our classroom clean so that we always stay healthy and happy. Q: Post-reading: There are three paragraphs in the speech, which one do you like the most? Ans: Sample answer: I like the last paragraph the most. It tells us about a very important rule, to keep our classroom clean, and it says this habit will help us to keep our school and home clean and to always be healthy and happy. Q: Post-reading: Name two rules for your classroom which you will always follow. Ans: Sample answer: Two rules I will always follow are: 1) Listen when others are talking. 2) Keep your classroom clean. The poster in the speech also gives rules such as follow directions, work quietly and do not disturb others, and show respect for school and personal property. Reading Comprehension Q: What makes you happy on the first day of new class? Ans: Sample answer, from the speech: Meeting all my friends makes me happy on the first day of the new class.     Answer: English

  3. Why should we listen to our teachers?

    Answer: They help us learn new things

    Answer: A mop
