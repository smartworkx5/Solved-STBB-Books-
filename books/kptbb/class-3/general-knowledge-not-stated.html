Key Points A shadow is formed on the opposite side of the light, where the light cannot reach because something blocks it. There are four cardinal directions : North, East, South and West. The shadow is long in the morning and evening and short at noon. We can guess the time with the help of the size of a shadow. Writing the names of places around your home and drawing your school are practical activities — every student does them for his or her own home and school. Activity (book p. 3) Q: Below is the picture of your home. Write names of places surrounding it. Ans: Note: This is a personal activity with no single written answer. Every student will write the names of the places around his or her own home. The picture in the book shows the directions North and East to help the student find the direction of each place. Activity (book p. 4) Q: i. Shadow is formed on which side of the candle? Ans: The shadow is formed on the opposite side of the light. The book says a shadow is always formed on the other side, where the light cannot reach because something blocks it. So the shadow of the hand appears on the wall on the opposite side of the candle light. Q: ii. What happens to the size of the shadow when hand is brought closer to the candle? Ans: Note: The book does not state what happens to the size of the shadow when the hand is brought closer to the candle. The book only states that the shape and size of a shadow depends on the direction and angle of light. This answer is to be found by doing the classroom observation. Q: iii.
