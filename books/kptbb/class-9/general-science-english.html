Sample solved questions

Key Points The word science comes from the Latin word 'scientia', which means 'to know'. The Muslim period of science ranges from the 7th to the 13th century A.D. Al-Razi wrote the famous medical book 'Al-Havi'. Bu-Ali-Sina wrote 'Al-Qanoon Fil-Tib' and was the first physician to describe meningitis. Al-Bairuni wrote 'Qanoon-al-Masoodi Fil Hayat wan-Najoom'. Muslim scientists solved scientific problems by the practical and experimentation approach. Astronomy is the branch of science that deals with heavenly bodies. A biopsy is a medical test in which cells or tissues are removed for examination. John Dalton developed the atomic theory of matter. Physical sciences deal with matter and energy and describe the material universe in terms of weight, mass etc. Solved Exercises Exercise A: Select the best answers for the following questions. Q: 1. Al-Havi was the name of book written by: Options: (a) Jabir-bin-Hayyan (b) Bu-Ali-Sina (c) Al-Bairuni (d) Al-Razi Correct answer: Al-Razi Q: 4. Astronomy is the branch of science, which deals with: Options: (a) Heavenly bodies (b) Earthen bodies (c) Bodies in oceans (d) Anti-bodies Correct answer: Heavenly bodies Q: 5. What was the most important approach of Muslim Scientists for solving scientific problems?
