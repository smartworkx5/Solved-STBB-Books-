Sample solved questions

Key Points 10 cm equals 100 mm, because 1 cm = 10 mm. A micrometer (screw gauge) measures length, and a vernier caliper best measures the internal diameter of a pipe. Prefix order for large multiples: mega < giga < peta < exa, with exa the largest of those listed; atto is the smallest prefix among atto, pico, nano and femto. 6 x 10^2 shows one significant digit, while 900.8 shows four significant digits. 9.460 x 10^15 m has four significant figures, the digits 9, 4, 6 and 0. 0.2 mm = 0.0002 m = 2 x 10^-4 m. KITAB UL MANAZIR was written by Ibnal Haitham. Solved Exercises Exercise — Multiple Choice Questions Q: 1. How many millimeters are there in 10 cm? Options: (a) 100 mm (b) 200 mm (c) 50 mm (d) 10 mm Correct answer: 100 mm Q: 2. Which of the following quantity can be measured using a micrometer? Options: (a) current (b) force (c) length (d) mass Correct answer: length Q: 3. The instrument best measures the internal diameter of a pipe is Options: (a) screw gauge (b) vernier caliper (c) meter rule (d) measuring tape Correct answer: vernier caliper Q: 4. Which prefix represents a largest value? Options: (a) mega (b) giga (c) peta (d) exa Correct answer: exa Q: 5. Which of the following is the smallest prefix? Options: (a) atto (b) pico (c) nano (d) femto Correct answer: atto Q: 6. Which of the following numbers shows one significant digit? Options: (a) 1.1 (b) 6.0 (c) 7.1 (d) 6 x 10^2 Correct answer: 6 x 10^2 Q: 7. Which of the following numbers shows 4 significant digits? Options: (a) 900.8 (b) 4 (c) 5174.00 (d) 0.0002 Correct answer: 900.8 Q: 8. A light year is distance traveled by light in one year. It travels about 9.460 x 10^15 m.
