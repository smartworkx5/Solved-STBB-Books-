Sample solved questions

Glossary — Word Meanings Q: Meaning of 'foster-mother': Ans: A woman who takes another person's child into her home for a period of time, without becoming his or her legal parent. Q: Meaning of 'widow': Ans: A woman whose husband has died. Q: Meaning of 'proposal': Ans: An act of formally asking somebody to marry you. Q: Meaning of 'orphan': Ans: A child whose parents are dead. Q: Meaning of 'AD': Ans: Anno Domini, a Latin phrase meaning 'in the year of the Lord', which is used when referring to the year after Hazrat Essa (AS) was born. Q: Meaning of 'slave': Ans: A person who is legally owned by someone else and has to work for that person. Comprehension — A. Answer the following questions Q: Q1. Why was Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) called Sadiq and Amin? Ans: Since childhood, Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) was loved and respected by all. He never told a lie, so people called him 'Sadiq', the truthful one. He was always honest and fair in his dealings with people, so people also called him 'Amin', the trustworthy one. Q: Q2. Why is Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) called Rahmatul-lil-Aalamin? Ans: Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) was kind, gentle and merciful to everyone. He loved the poor, treated orphans and widows with kindness, helped the slaves and the helpless, was kind to birds and animals, and treated even his enemies with kindness. His mercy was for all, so he is called Rahmatul-lil-Aalamin, a mercy for all the worlds. Q: Q3. What was the impact of Hazrat Muhammad's (PBUH) personality on people? Ans: People loved and respected Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH) from his childhood because he never told a lie and was always honest and fair. Hazrat Khadija was so pleased with his honesty and truthfulness that she wished to marry him. He won the hearts of his friends and enemies by love, kindness and mercy…
