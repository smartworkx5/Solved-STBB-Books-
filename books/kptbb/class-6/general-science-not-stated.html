Sample solved questions

Key Points A cell is the structural and functional unit of living organisms. Muscle is a tissue: cells of the same kind group together to perform one function and make a tissue. The circulatory system transports digested food to all parts of the body. The nucleus controls all the activities of the cell, just as the brain controls the body. Centrioles are present in animal cells; plants prepare food in their chloroplasts. Euglena is a unicellular organism; it can be seen clearly with a microscope. Mitochondria are called the power house of the cell. Solved Exercises Exercise A: Fill in the blanks: Q: 1. A cell is the ______ and functional unit of living organisms. Ans: structural Q: 2. Centriole is present in ______ cell. Ans: animal Q: 3. Cell membrane is the outer most covering in ______ cells. Ans: animal Q: 4. Euglena is a ______ organism. Ans: unicellular Q: 5. Plants prepare food in their ______. Ans: chloroplast Exercise B: Choose the correct answer: Q: 1. Muscle is a type of ______. Options: (a) cell (b) tissue (c) organ (d) system Correct answer: tissue Q: 2. A branch on a stem is a part of ______ system. Options: (a) root (b) branch (c) shoot (d) conducting Correct answer: shoot Q: 3. Which system of your body is responsible for the transport of digested food? Options: (a) digestive system (b) circulatory system (c) respiratory system (d) skeletal system Correct answer: circulatory system Q: 4. Brain controls the body just like the ______ controls activities of a cell. Options: (a) mitochondria (b) cytoplasm (c) nucleus (d) ribosomes Correct answer: nucleus Q: 5.
