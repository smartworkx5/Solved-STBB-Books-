Sample solved questions

Pre-reading Q: Look at the mind-map and predict what the lesson is about. (Mind-map words: truthful, trustworthy, forgiving, tolerant, honest, courageous, loyal, patient, just) Ans: The mind-map shows the noble qualities of Hazrat Muhammad (SAW) - truthful, trustworthy, forgiving, tolerant, honest, courageous, loyal, patient and just. The lesson is therefore about his character, especially his simplicity and humility, and how he lived a simple, humble life and treated everyone kindly and equally. Comprehension A. Answer the following questions Q: 1. What is meant by simplicity and humility? Ans: Simplicity means living a plain and unadorned life, without show, luxury or waste, and being content with simple food, dress and living. Humility means being humble and modest, not proud or arrogant, and never making others feel small, unwanted or embarrassed. The lesson says simplicity in life is part of faith. Q: 2. Describe in a few sentences, the life style of Hazrat Muhammad (SAW) in the light of the text that you have read. Ans: Hazrat Muhammad (SAW) lived a very simple and hard life in lifestyle, diet and manners. He never saw bread made from fine flour, his mattress was made of leather stuffed with the fibre of date tree, and when he died he left neither money nor anything else except his white riding mule, his arms and a piece of land left to charity. He milked his goat, mended his clothes, repaired his shoes and helped with household work, although the Muslim treasury was at his disposal. Q: 3. How can simplicity and humility do away with the culture of extravagance and waste? Ans: If we adopt the sunnah of simplicity and humility, we stop squandering our hard-earned resources on unwanted luxurious items to satisfy pride and superiority. Simplicity teaches contentment…

Sample MCQs with answers

1. The mattress on which Hazrat Muhammad (SAW) slept was made of leather, stuffed with the fibre of date tree. This shows that he (SAW) __________.

Answer: led a simple life

2. The word that is opposite in meaning to affluent is __________.

Answer: poor

3. According to the text Hazrat Muhammad (SAW) and Hazrat Fatima (RA) greeted each other __________ way.

Answer: in the same

4. The word closest in meaning to impoverished is __________.

Answer: without money

5. According to the text of the lesson Hazrat Muhammad (SAW) used to milk his goat, this shows that __________.

Answer: humility was essential part of the Rasool's (SAW) character
