Key Points Zulfiqar Ali Bhutto took over on December 20, 1971 as the country's first Civilian Martial Law Administrator. The nationalization of industries was announced on January 2, 1972, and Martial Law ended in April 1972. The 1973 Constitution was drawn up by a committee of 25 members of the National Assembly. After taking power in 1977, General Zia-ul-Haq promised to hold elections within 90 days; he became President in September 1978. Prime Minister Muhammad Khan Junejo announced the Five-points Programme. Benazir Bhutto took the vote of confidence on December 12, 1988 and launched the Peoples' Works Programme. Nawaz Sharif became Prime Minister for the first time on November 6, 1990. Pakistan became a nuclear power on May 28, 1998.
