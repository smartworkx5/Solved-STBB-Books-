Sample solved questions

Key Points Opposite charges attract each other. An object that is negatively charged has gained electrons. Static electricity means an imbalance of electrons. A glass rod becomes positively charged when rubbed with silk. An ebonite rod and a glass rod brought together after being rubbed with fur attract each other. A person who gains a positive charge on a carpet feels a shock when electrons flow from a neutral doorknob to the hand. Lightning hits buildings and homes because they are the closest point to thunder clouds. An electrostatic precipitator is used in pollution control. Like charges repel each other while opposite charges attract each other. The path on which current flows is called a circuit. Solved Exercises Exercise A: Select the correct answers for the following questions Q: 1. Opposite charges __________ each other. Options: (a) Attract (b) Repel (c) Cancel (d) None of them Correct answer: Attract Q: 2. An object that is negatively charged has: Options: (a) gained protons (b) lost protons (c) gained electrons (d) lost electrons Correct answer: gained electrons Q: 3. The term "electricity" in referring to static electricity, Means: Options: (a) An imbalance of protons (b) An equal number of atoms (c) An imbalance of electrons (d) An imbalance of neutrons Correct answer: An imbalance of electrons Q: 4. Which one rod will become positively charged when rubbed with silk: Options: (a) Glass rod (b) Carbon rod (c) Silver rod (d) Ebonite rod Correct answer: Glass rod Q: 5. If an ebonite rod and a glass rod are brought together after being rubbed with fur, which of the following will happen? Options: (a) They will remain stationery (b) They will attract each other (c) They will repel each other (d) None of them Correct answer: They will attract each other Q: 6.…
