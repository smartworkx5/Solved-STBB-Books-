Key Points In one complete cycle a spot on a transverse wave on a string travels a total distance of 4A, where A is the amplitude. The period of a mass-spring system depends on the mass and the spring constant, but not on the amplitude of vibration. Because the period of a mass-spring system is proportional to the square root of the mass, doubling the period requires four times the mass. Adding passengers or a heavy load to a car on shock absorbers increases the mass, so the frequency of oscillation decreases. The time period of a simple pendulum does not depend on the material of the bob, so an iron bob and a lead bob of the same size give the same period. A wave transports energy but not matter.
