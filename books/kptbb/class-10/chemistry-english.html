Sample solved questions

Key Points At dynamic equilibrium the amounts of liquid water and water vapour become constant because the forward and reverse rates are equal. A reversible reaction proceeds in the forward and reverse direction and does not go to completion. Kc is the ratio of the two rate constants, Kc = k1/k2. For A + B <=> C + D, Kc = [C][D] / [A][B]. A very small Kc, such as 10^-30 for N2 + O2 <=> 2NO, means the forward reaction does not proceed appreciably. For PCl5 <=> PCl3 + Cl2, the unit of Kc is mol / dm^3. When Kc = 1, the reaction is 50% complete. The law of mass action states that the rate of a reaction is proportional to the product of the active masses of the reacting substances.
