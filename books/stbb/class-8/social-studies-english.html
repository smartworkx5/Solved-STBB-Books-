What the Renaissance Means Renaissance means the rebirth of intellectual learning, art, music and culture in Europe. It lasted from the early 14th century to the late 16th century and bridged the long gap between the Middle Ages and modern civilization. During this period Europe experienced a new wave of learning in philosophy, literature, science and art. Famous People of the Renaissance Leonardo da Vinci — a famous artist of the Renaissance. Raphael — a famous artist of the Renaissance. William Shakespeare — a famous English writer and dramatist. Importance of the Printing Press Johannes Gutenberg put the printing press into operation in 1450. It spread knowledge to other parts of the world quickly and cheaply. Religious messages could now be printed and spread in the form of books, and people started to read in different languages. Education became reachable for every person, so the Renaissance spread faster. Three Things Artists Focused On Painting portraits — showing real human faces and feelings. The natural world — painted as it actually looked. The technique of perspective — giving depth and a real-life look to paintings. Sample MCQs with answers

  1. What does the word 'Renaissance' mean?

    Answer: The rebirth of intellectual learning, art, music and culture in Europe

  2. The Renaissance lasted from:

    Answer: The early 14th century to the late 16th century

  3. Who put the printing press into operation in 1450?

    Answer: Johannes Gutenberg

  4. How did the printing press help the Renaissance?

    Answer: It spread knowledge quickly and cheaply, so education reached every person

  5. Two famous artists of the Renaissance were:

    Answer: Leonardo da Vinci and Raphael
