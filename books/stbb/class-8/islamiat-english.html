Sample solved questions

Introduction to the Holy Quran The Holy Quran is the last revealed Book of Allah Almighty. It was revealed to the last Prophet, Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ, gradually over a period of twenty-three (23) years. It has thirty (30) Paras and one hundred and fourteen (114) Surahs. The purpose of its revelation is to show mankind such a way of living that, by acting upon it, they can lead a life of honour and success in this world and in the Hereafter. Memorizing the Holy Quran is a great act of worship, and there is great reward in reciting it: according to the saying of the Holy Prophet ﷺ, ten virtues are received for reciting every single letter of the Holy Quran. Aims of this Chapter Students should be able to read the Holy Quran correctly, with proper accent, in Salah and in daily life. They should memorize a part of it and read the translation of a part of it. Love for pondering over the Quran, acting upon it and preaching it should be created in them. (a) Nazra Quran: Para 21 to Para 30 (Ten Paras) In this class, ten Paras of the Holy Quran (from Para 21 to Para 30) will be read as Nazra with Tajweed. Their names are: Para 21: أُتْلُ مَا أُوحِيَ (Utlu Ma Uhiya) Para 22: وَمَنْ يَقْنُتْ (Wa Manyaqnut) Para 23: وَمَالِيَ (Wa Maliya) Para 24: فَمَنِ اظْلَمُ (Famaniz Zalam) Para 25: إِلَيْهِ يُرَدُّ (Ilaihi Yuraddu) Para 26: حم (Ha Meem) Para 27: قَالَ فَمَا خَطْبُكُمْ (Qala Fama Khatbukum) Para 28: قَدْ سَمِعَ اللهُ (Qad Sami'ullah) Para 29: تَبَارَكَ الَّذِي (Tabarakallazi) Para 30: عَمَّ يَتَسَاءَلُونَ (Amma Yatasa'aloon) Important Points about Nazra An oral examination of the Nazra and Hifz portions is taken in the mid-term and annual examinations, and the marks obtained are included in the result sheet. Sample MCQs with answers

  1. In Class 8, which Paras of the Holy Quran are read as Nazra?

    Answer: Para 21 to Para 30

  2. What is the name of Para 21?

    Answer: Utlu Ma Uhiya

  3. What is the name of Para 29?

    Answer: Tabarakallazi

  4. What is the name of the last Para, Para 30?

    Answer: Amma Yatasa'aloon

  5. What is the name of Para 25?

    Answer: Ilaihi Yuraddu
