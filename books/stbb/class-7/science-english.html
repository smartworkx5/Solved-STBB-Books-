Sample solved questions

The Digestive Tract Digestion starts in the mouth and ends at the anus. The tube through which food passes is called the digestive tract (alimentary canal). Food moves through it in this order: Order Organ What happens there 1 Mouth Teeth chew the food; saliva starts changing starch 2 Oesophagus The food pipe that pushes food down to the stomach 3 Stomach Gastric juice starts digesting protein; food is mixed and softened 4 Small intestine Digestion is completed and digested food is absorbed into the blood 5 Large intestine, rectum, anus Water is absorbed and waste leaves the body Accessory Organs: Liver and Pancreas The liver and the pancreas are NOT part of the digestive tract, because food does not pass through them. They are accessory organs — they only send their juices into the tract. The liver makes bile , a greenish juice that goes into the small intestine and breaks fat into small droplets. The pancreas sends pancreatic juice into the small intestine. The gallbladder is a small green sac below the liver. The Epiglottis — A Safety Flap The epiglottis is a small flap that closes the windpipe during swallowing, so food cannot enter the windpipe. This is why we should not talk while eating : while talking, the windpipe passage stays open and a piece of food may go into it, causing coughing or choking.

Sample MCQs with answers

Which juice comes out of the liver?

Answer: Bile

Which is the correct order of the organs of the digestive tract?

Answer: Mouth ← Oesophagus ← Stomach ← Small intestine

Which organ stops food from entering the windpipe while eating?

Answer: Epiglottis

Why are the liver and the pancreas not counted as part of the digestive tract?

Answer: Food does not pass through them; they only send juices into the tract

Why should we not talk while eating?

Answer: While talking the windpipe stays open and food may enter it, causing choking
