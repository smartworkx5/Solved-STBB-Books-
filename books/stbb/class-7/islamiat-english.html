Sample solved questions

This is an oral unit of the book (Nazra and Hifz). There is no written exercise for it in the book; these notes give the facts printed in the book's own pages of this chapter. What is Nazra? Nazra means reading the Holy Quran by looking at it. In Class 7, the fixed Nazra portion is eight paras: Para 13 to Para 20 . The student reads these paras with correct pronunciation (talaffuz) and tajweed. The eight paras and their names Para 13 — Wa Ma Ubarri'u Para 14 — Rubama Para 15 — Subhanallazi Para 16 — Qal Alam Aqul Para 17 — Iqtaraba Para 18 — Qad Aflaha Para 19 — Wa Qalallazina Para 20 — Amman Khalaqa About the Holy Quran (from the chapter's introduction) The Holy Quran is the last Book of Allah, revealed to His last Messenger, Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ. It was revealed in stages over about twenty-three years. The earlier holy books are also mentioned in the Holy Quran. By acting upon the Holy Quran, a person gains success in this world and in the Hereafter. Reciting even one letter of the Holy Quran brings ten rewards. We should read the Holy Quran with tajweed, understand its meaning, and act upon it. Exam facts given in the book Students should learn the names of their paras by heart. The Nazra and Hifz oral test is held in the mid-term and in the annual examination. Out of the total 100 marks of Islamiat, 40 marks are fixed for the Nazra part. Passing the Nazra Quran is compulsory for passing Islamiat.
