The Story in Short The story "Can We Do Anything?" is about pollution around us and what we, as citizens and students, can do to stop it. Ali is excited on the first day of school after the long summer vacations because he wants to meet his classmates and teachers. He travels in a van with his best friend Saleem. On the way, the boys talk about their holidays, but loud horns disturb them and the fumes from vehicles enter the van through the windows, so some students start coughing. In class, Miss Sana shows the students some posters on pollution. The students discuss pollution and admit that they had only been complaining about it — they had done nothing to stop it. The class then works in groups and makes a plan to fight pollution. Ali and his group arrange a campaigning programme in their neighbourhood: they make and show posters, distribute handbills listing the steps young people can take, present role-plays, give speeches and share an action plan for cleaning the neighbourhood. The programme is a big success because almost everyone agrees to do something. Ali comes home tired, but very happy, because he has taken a big step in the right direction. The Three Sections of the Story Section 1 — Air Pollution: Ali's morning and his journey to school. Section 2 — Pollution Free Environment: the class discussion and the group plan. Section 3 — A Task Completed: the neighbourhood campaign. "Social Hazard" is the extra title that does not belong to any section. Word Meanings enthusiastic — very excited or interested social campaigns — activities to make people aware about something involved — made them take part honked — blew the horn fumes — dangerous smoke having a strong smell emitted — produced purposeless — without any purpose or reason assign — give (work) deadlines…

Sample MCQs with answers

What is the story 'Can We Do Anything?' mainly about?

Answer: Pollution around us and what we can do to stop it

Next to whom did Ali sit in the van?

Answer: His best friend Saleem

Why did some students cough in the van?

Answer: The fumes from the vehicles were entering the van through the windows

Who showed the posters on pollution to the class?

Answer: Miss Sana

What did Miss Sana's raised hand mean in the class?

Answer: It was her signal for complete silence and full attention
