Sample solved questions

The Story Memna is a little baby goat . She likes to go to the pond every day to drink water and play there. At the pond she meets Jabir , the big bad wolf . Jabir tells lies. He says the pond is his alone and he wants to eat Memna. Memna is small but brave and clever . She does not believe the wolf's lies and calls her friends for help. The Characters Jabir - the big bad wolf. He tells lies and tries to frighten the small animals. In the end he is taught a lesson and runs away. Memna - the baby goat. She is small, brave and clever. Mano - a fluffy black cat. She is kind and quick to help. She says, "Don't worry." Moti - a fat brown dog. He is strong and friendly. He says, "Don't be upset, I'll help you." Kumet - a big red horse. He is gentle and caring. He says, "Don't cry." Who Said These Words? "Don't be upset, I'll help you." - Moti the dog said this. "Don't cry!" - Kumet the horse said this. "Don't worry!" - Mano the cat said this. "The big bad wolf is coming to eat me!" - Memna said this. "I've come to eat you." - Jabir the wolf said this. Questions and Answers Memna likes to go to the pond every day. At the pond, Memna meets Jabir , the big bad wolf. In the end, her friends Mano, Moti and Kumet help her, and together they teach the wolf a lesson . Key Points Memna is a baby goat who goes to the pond every day. Jabir is a big bad wolf who tells lies. Memna's friends help her and teach the wolf a lesson.
