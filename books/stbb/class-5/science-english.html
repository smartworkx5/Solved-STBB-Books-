Sample solved questions

What Is Classification? Classification means dividing living things into groups on the basis of their similar characteristics . Living things that share similar characteristics are placed in the same group. Why Do Scientists Classify? There are millions of kinds of living things. It is very difficult to remember every living thing separately. Scientists classify living things so that they can be identified and understood easily. Classification also makes the characteristics of each living thing and its relation with other living things clear. Key Points Classification groups living things by similar characteristics. There are millions of kinds of living things. Classification makes living things easy to identify and understand. It shows how living things are related to each other.

Sample MCQs with answers

What does classification mean?

Answer: Dividing living things into groups by similar characteristics

Why is it difficult to study living things without classification?

Answer: It is difficult to remember every living thing separately

Scientists classify living things so that they can be what?

Answer: Identified and understood easily

On what basis are living things placed into groups?

Answer: On their similar characteristics

What does classification make clear about a living thing?

Answer: Its characteristics and its relation with other living things
