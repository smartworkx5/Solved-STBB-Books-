Sample solved questions

Groups of Three To read a big number, split it into groups of three digits from the right. The groups are ones , thousands , millions and billions . Reading a Number Read each group like a small number, then say the group name. For example, 171,283,680 is read as one hundred seventy one million , two hundred eighty three thousand , six hundred eighty. Writing in Figures One million two thousand six hundred has parts 1,000,000, 2,000 and 600, which together make 1,002,600. One billion is written as 1,000,000,000. Empty groups are filled with zeros.
