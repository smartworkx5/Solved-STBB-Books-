Nazira Recitation (Reading by Looking) This lesson is a memorisation and recitation lesson. For Nazira (reading the Quran by looking at it), the Surahs of the thirtieth Juz are prescribed: Surah Al-Fil, Surah Quraysh, Surah Al-Ma'un, Surah Al-Kauthar, Surah Al-Kafirun, Surah An-Nasr, Surah Al-Lahab, Surah Al-Ikhlas, Surah Al-Falaq and Surah An-Naas . Along with these, the first five verses and the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah and the last three verses of Surah Al-Hashr are also read by looking. Hifz (Memorisation) For memorisation: Ayat-ul-Kursi from Surah Al-Baqarah, Surah Al-Falaq and Surah An-Naas . Besides these, Surah Al-Mulk is taught to the students by heart, and the first sixteen (16) verses of Surah Ar-Rahman are also given for memorisation. Sample MCQs with answers

For Nazira recitation in Class 5, the Surahs of which Juz are prescribed?

Answer: The thirtieth Juz

Along with the Surahs of the thirtieth Juz, which verses of Surah Al-Baqarah are read in Nazira?

Answer: The first five verses and the last two verses

The last three verses of which Surah are included in the Nazira portion?

Answer: Surah Al-Hashr

Which of these is prescribed for Hifz (memorisation)?

Answer: Ayat-ul-Kursi

How many verses of Surah Ar-Rahman are given for memorisation?

Answer: The first sixteen verses
