Sample solved questions

Roman numbers We use Roman numbers with symbols I = 1 , V = 5 and X = 10 . Our normal numbers like 3, 7 and 15 are called Hindi-Arabic numbers . 1 = I, 2 = II, 3 = III, 5 = V, 10 = X, 15 = XV, 20 = XX When a smaller symbol comes after a bigger symbol, we add it: VI = 5 + 1 = 6 and VIII = 5 + 3 = 8. When I comes before V or X, we subtract it: IV = 5 - 1 = 4 and IX = 10 - 1 = 9. Worked example Write 14 in Roman numbers. Step 1: Split 14 as 10 + 4. Step 2: 10 is X and 4 is IV. Step 3: Join them: X + IV = XIV. Answer: 14 = XIV. In the same way 16 = XVI, 17 = XVII and 19 = XIX. Key points I = 1, V = 5, X = 10. Add when a small symbol is after a big symbol. Subtract for IV = 4 and IX = 9.
