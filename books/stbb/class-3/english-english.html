Sample solved questions

About This Lesson This is a speaking lesson . You talk in class and introduce yourself and your friends. There are no written questions in this lesson, so practise speaking with your partner. What to Say Say your name : "My name is ..." Say your age : "I am ... years old." Say your class : "I am in class three." Tell about your eyes and hair . Tell what you like and do not like. A Model Introduction In the Writing lesson of this unit, Ali introduces himself like this: "My name is Ali . I am eight years old . I am in class three . My eyes are brown and my hair is black . I like reading books and playing football. I do not like fighting and telling lies." Key Points We introduce ourselves by telling our name, age and class. We can also tell about our eyes, hair and the things we like. Speak clearly and politely when you introduce yourself and others.
