Sample solved questions

Reading the Holy Quran The first part of this chapter teaches us to read the Holy Quran correctly. For correct reading, we must first know the Arabic Alphabets and their marks. Arabic Alphabets (Huroof-e-Hijaa) There are 29 Arabic Alphabets , called Huroof-e-Hijaa . Another name of these alphabets is Huroof-e-TaHajji . These alphabets (huroof) are joined together to make words. The 29 huroof in order are: ا ب ت ث ج ح خ د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ع غ ف ق ک ل م ن و ہ ء ی Letters Without Dots Some huroof have dots (nuqta) and some have no dots at all. Five huroof which have no dots are: Alif (ا), Haa (ح), Daal (د), Seen (س) and Kaaf (ک). Tha is spelled as ثا , just as Baa is spelled with Alif. Suad is written as ص , Seen is written as س and Tha is written as ث . The harf which comes just before Meem (م) is Laam (ل) . Key Points Arabic Alphabets are 29 in number and are also called Huroof-e-TaHajji. Huroof join together to make words. Alif, Haa, Daal, Seen and Kaaf have no dots (nuqta).
