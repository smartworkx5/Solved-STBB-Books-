Sample solved questions

Summary The lesson is about the migration and teachings of the Holy Prophet (S.A.W). Born in 571 AD at Makkah in a respected Quraish family, he asked the Quraish to worship the One true God. Most refused, thinking he was against their gods and their ancestors, and opposed him. He migrated to Yathrib in 622 AD. The day he entered Yathrib marks the start of the Islamic calendar. He stopped first at Quba, outside the city, and built a mosque there. Yathrib was renamed Madinat un-Nabi, then shortened to Madina. Key Points In Madina he drew up the Charter of Madina, united the Arab tribes under Islam, and carried out social and religious reforms. The Charter covered the rights and duties of Muslims and Jews, freedom of religion, and no alliance with outside enemies; disputes were referred to the Prophet (S.A.W). Groups in Madina: Muslims, pagans, hypocrites and Jews. His farewell pilgrimage in the 10th year of Hijra was his first and last Hajj; he taught how to perform Hajj and delivered the Last Sermon in the Uranah valley of Mount Arafat. Messages of the Last Sermon Hurt no one, so you may not be hurt in return. Men and women have rights over each other. All Muslims are tied in the bond of brotherhood; life and property of every Muslim are a sacred trust. No Arab is superior to a non-Arab and no white to a black except by piety and good action; all mankind is from Adam, who was created from dust. Return things kept in trust to their owners; beware of Satan in small matters; carry these words to those who are absent.
