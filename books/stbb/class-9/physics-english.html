Sample solved questions

Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy and their interaction. It observes nature, represents it mathematically and concludes with experiment. The word 'physics' comes from the ancient Greek word 'physicos', meaning knowledge of nature. Branches of Physics Mechanics — motion of objects (e.g. riding a bicycle). Thermodynamics — heat and energy (e.g. cooking a barbecue). Electricity — electric charges and circuits (e.g. turning a bulb on). Astrophysics — stars, galaxies and the universe (e.g. looking for giant galaxies). Acoustics — sound (e.g. producing a loud sound). Atomic Physics — the structure of the atom (e.g. describing an atom). Geophysics — the Earth and energy obtained from it. Why Study Physics? Physicists are categorised into two groups: those who do experiments from home to large-scale experiment labs, and those who work on theories. Physics lies in the laws that govern the whole universe, from an atom to large-scale galaxies.
