Before You Read Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah was the founder of Pakistan — a great leader, a brilliant lawyer and the leader of the All-India Muslim League, whose struggle gave Muslims of the sub-continent a separate homeland on 14th August 1947. He became the first Governor General of Pakistan. His main contribution was the creation of Pakistan. Through constitutional and political struggle, he united the Muslims, presented their case for a separate state, and achieved an independent homeland where they could live according to their own faith, culture and laws. His vision for Pakistan was a democratic, prosperous and just Pakistan where all citizens — majority and minorities — enjoy equal rights, where law and order is maintained, bribery, corruption, nepotism and black-marketing are crushed, and everyone is free to practise his or her religion. Famous sayings: “Unity, Faith and Discipline”; “No power on earth can undo Pakistan”; “Work, work and only work”; and his guiding words that he would always be guided by the principles of justice and fair play without prejudice or ill-will. Summary On 11th August 1947 the Quaid addressed Pakistan's first Constituent Assembly — the first sovereign legislative body of the country, bestowed with full powers. He felt honoured because the Assembly had conferred a great honour upon him by electing and trusting him to lead it, and he thanked the members with the utmost sincerity. The Assembly's first task was framing a full and complete sovereign constitution, and its second was to function as the Federal Legislature. The Quaid called the change a cyclonic, unprecedented revolution that created two independent sovereign dominions in the sub-continent. Who was Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, according to the lesson?

Answer: Founder of Pakistan and its first Governor General

On which date did the Quaid address Pakistan's first Constituent Assembly?

Answer: 11th August 1947

What was the first task of the Constituent Assembly?

Answer: Framing a full and complete sovereign constitution

What was the second function of the Constituent Assembly?

Answer: To function as the Federal Legislature

According to the Quaid, what is the first duty of a government?

Answer: To maintain law and order
