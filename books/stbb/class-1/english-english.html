Sample solved questions

Greetings in the Classroom When the teacher comes into the class, she says: Assalam-o-Alaikum , students. The students stand up and reply: Walekum-o-Salaam , teacher. The teacher says: Thank you all. Please, sit down. The students say: Thank you, teacher , and sit down. Telling Your Name The teacher says: My name is Asma . I am your teacher. Kabir says: My name is Kabir. I am your student, teacher. Sara says: My name is Sara. I am your student. Ali says: Teacher, my name is Ali. I am also your student. To tell our own name, we say: My name is ... Key Points We stand up to greet the teacher. We tell our name with the words: My name is ... Asma is the teacher in this lesson.

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. When the teacher says Assalam-o-Alaikum, what do the students reply?

    Answer: Walekum-o-Salaam

  2. What is the name of the teacher in this lesson?

    Answer: Asma

  3. Kabir says: My name is ______.

    Answer: Kabir

  4. What do the students say when the teacher asks them to sit down?

    Answer: Thank you, teacher

  5. Who is a student in this lesson?

    Answer: Sara
