Allah Made Everything Allah is the Creator of everything in the world. Allah created the mountains , the trees , the sun , the moon and the stars. Allah also created birds, flowers, animals and all the people. Things made by Allah are made by nature . Things Made by People Some things are made by people, not by nature. A chair, a pencil, a dice and a sewing machine are made by people . Key Points Allah is the Creator of everything. Mountains, trees, the sun, a bird and a flower are created by Allah. A chair, a pencil and a dice are made by people.
