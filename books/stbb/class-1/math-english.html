Sample solved questions

Counting things To count things, touch each thing one by one and say the counting words in order: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine . The last word you say tells how many things there are. In the book we count: 1 mango, 2 bananas, 3 apples, 4 lemons, 5 pomegranates, 6 stars, 7 candles, 8 pencils and 9 hens. The number zero When a cage has no parrot at all , we write the number 0 . Zero means nothing . Four cages with 3, 2, 1 and 0 parrots show the numbers going down to zero. Worked example Count the cricket balls in the picture and write the number. Step 1: Touch each ball and count: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9. Step 2: The last number is 9, so there are 9 balls. Answer: 9 Key points Count slowly, touch each thing once. 0 means nothing. Number words: One, Two, Three, Four, Five, Six, Seven, Eight, Nine.
