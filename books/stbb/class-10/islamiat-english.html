Selected Verses - Translations Verse about fearing for weak offspring: وَلْيَخْشَ الَّذِينَ لَوْ تَرَكُوا مِنْ خَلْفِهِمْ ذُرِّيَّةً ضِعَافًا خَافُوا عَلَيْهِمْ فَلْيَتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَلْيَقُولُوا قَوْلًا سَدِيدًا Translation: And those (who fear Allah Almighty) should fear - those who, if they left weak offspring behind them, would fear for their (ruin) - so they should fear Allah and speak straightforward words. Verse about consuming the wealth of orphans: إِنَّ الَّذِينَ يَأْكُلُونَ أَمْوَالَ الْيَتَامَى ظُلْمًا إِنَّمَا يَأْكُلُونَ فِي بُطُونِهِمْ نَارًا وَسَيَصْلَوْنَ سَعِيرًا Translation: Indeed, those who consume the wealth of orphans unjustly only consume fire in their bellies, and they will enter a blazing Fire. Verse about handing over the orphans' wealth: وَآتُوا الْيَتَامَى أَمْوَالَهُمْ وَلَا تَتَبَدَّلُوا الْخَبِيثَ بِالطَّيِّبِ وَلَا تَأْكُلُوا أَمْوَالَهُمْ إِلَى أَمْوَالِكُمْ إِنَّهُ كَانَ حُوبًا كَبِيرًا Translation: And hand over to the orphans the wealth that is under your care, and do not exchange their pure and fine wealth for your defective and bad wealth, nor consume their wealth by mixing it with your own wealth, for that is a very great sin. Verse about women's dower (mahr): وَآتُوا النِّسَاءَ صَدُقَاتِهِنَّ نِحْلَةً فَإِنْ طِبْنَ لَكُمْ عَنْ شَيْءٍ مِنْهُ نَفْسًا فَكُلُوهُ هَنِيئًا مَرِيئًا Translation: And give women their dower (mahr) happily. Yes, if they willingly give up a part of it for you, then consume it with pleasure and delight. Verse about trade by mutual consent and not killing yourselves: يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تَأْكُلُوا أَمْوَالَكُمْ بَيْنَكُمْ بِالْبَاطِلِ إِلَّا أَنْ تَكُونَ تِجَارَةً عَنْ تَرَاضٍ مِنْكُمْ وَلَا تَقْتُلُوا أَنْفُسَكُمْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ كَانَ بِكُمْ رَحِيمًا Translation: O believers! Sample MCQs with answers

According to the Holy Quran, what do those who consume the wealth of orphans unjustly actually consume in their bellies?

Answer: Fire

When an orphan reaches the age of marriage and sound judgment (rushd) is seen in him, what must his guardian do with the orphan's wealth?

Answer: Hand over the wealth to the orphan

In the rules about the orphan's wealth, what does 'rushd' mean?

Answer: Sound judgment in managing wealth and business

According to the Holy Quran, how may a poor guardian use the wealth of the orphan under his care?

Answer: He may consume from it according to custom, in a fair and reasonable way

What does the Holy Quran command about giving women their dower (mahr)?

Answer: It should be given to them happily
