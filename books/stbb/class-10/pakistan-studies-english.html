Sample MCQs with answers

  1. What does an ideology consist of?

    Answer: Organised ideas about life and thought

  2. Which is the first and basic source (origin) of the Islamic ideology?

    Answer: The Holy Quran

  3. Which is the second basic source of the Islamic ideology?

    Answer: The Sunnah of the Prophet (PBUH)

  4. In the principles of democracy in Islam, what does justice mean?

    Answer: Putting everything in its right place

  5. According to the lesson, equality in Islam means that:

    Answer: There is no distinction on the basis of colour, race, language, belief or wealth, and all people are equal before the law
