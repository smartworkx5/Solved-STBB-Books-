Sample solved questions

Summary Unit 1.1 has two reading texts. The poem The Voice of God by Louis I. Newman tells of a man who climbed the topmost steeple (the tallest part of a religious building) to hear the voice of God, but God told him to go down again, because God dwells among the people. The story The Wise Caliph is about Caliph Haroon-ur-Rashid, who was known and respected for his justice, wisdom and concern for the welfare of his people. He used to walk through the streets of Baghdad at night in disguise to gain first-hand knowledge of the people's difficulties. One day the Qazi brought him a difficult dispute: a well-dressed rich man and a beggar in rags both claimed that a beautiful white horse belonged to them. The rich man said he had given the lame beggar a lift out of pity, but at the city gate the beggar refused to get down and claimed the horse was his. The beggar told the opposite story. The wise ruler asked each man to place his hand on the horse one by one. The horse winced at the beggar's touch, because he was a stranger, but it snorted and neighed with pleasure when the rich man touched it, because it recognised its true master. The ruler gave the horse to the rich man, and forgave the beggar on the condition that he begged the rich man's forgiveness. The rich man readily forgave him and even gave him a handful of gold coins. The Voice of God: Key Points The poet climbed the tall tower of the place of worship because he wanted to hear the voice of God. He was told to come back down because God lives among the people, not only high up in a tower. Message of the poem: God is found among people — by serving and caring for people, we come close to God. Pre-reading: a good person is honest, kind, fair, helpful, patient and caring; a bad person is dishonest, selfish, cruel…

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. In the poem 'The Voice of God', where did the man climb to hear the voice of God?

    Answer: The topmost steeple

  2. Why did God tell the man to go down again?

    Answer: Because God dwells among the people

  3. What is the main message of the poem 'The Voice of God'?

    Answer: God is found among people, by serving and caring for them

  4. Caliph Haroon-ur-Rashid was known and respected for his:

    Answer: justice, wisdom and concern for the welfare of his people

  5. Why did the Caliph walk through the streets of Baghdad at night in disguise?

    Answer: To gain first-hand knowledge of the people's difficulties
