Sample solved questions

The Story in Short One bright sunny morning, Hamza and Abdullah go to their neighbourhood park. It is Sunday and both friends are in high spirits. They stop outside the park to buy some ice cream from a small shop near the gate. Hamza wants to throw his ice cream wrapper on the ground. Abdullah stops him and says we should keep Pakistan clean and beautiful. They read the park rules written on a big board at the park gate. They decide to put up posters and place dustbins with signs of 'Keep Pakistan Clean' on them. Word Meanings trash — garbage heap — pile high spirits — lively and cheerful behaviour or mood chatting — talking wrapper — a piece of paper or plastic that covers something, like a sweet or a packet of biscuit realize — understand or know something City Park Rules You must not write or draw on the walls of the park. You must not throw trash around the park. Use the provided dustbins. You must not cut or harm the plants and trees. You must not write on or damage the benches. You must follow the park timings: from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm daily. Children must come with an adult. Key Points The most suitable title for this text is 'Keeping Public Places Clean'. Public places are for all of us to use. If we do not take care of them, we will have no place left for fun and enjoyment.

Sample MCQs with answers

On which day did Hamza and Abdullah go to the park?

Answer: Sunday

How was the morning when the two friends went to the park?

Answer: Bright and sunny

What did the friends buy from the small shop near the gate?

Answer: Ice cream

What did Abdullah tell Hamza not to do?

Answer: Not to throw his ice cream wrapper on the ground

Why did Hamza want to throw the wrapper on the ground?

Answer: Because everyone else throws trash around there
