Sample solved questions

The Holy Quran — Introduction Islam is a universal and everlasting religion. Its foundation is the Holy Quran and the Sunnah of the Holy Prophet ﷺ. The Holy Quran is the last Book of Allah Almighty. Allah Almighty revealed it to the last Prophet, Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ, through Hazrat Jibrail Ameen (AS). The Holy Quran is guidance and a complete code of life for the welfare of all mankind. The solution of the problems of every walk of life is present in the Holy Quran. The Holy Quran is the source of guidance and the foundation of the religion of Islam. What is Nazra? Nazra means reading the Holy Quran by looking at it. For this class, six paras are fixed for Nazra reading: Para 7 to Para 12. Para 7: Wa Idha Sami'u Para 8: Wa Lau Annana Para 9: Qalal Mala'u Para 10: Wa A'lamu Para 11: Ya'tazirun Para 12: Wa Ma Min Da'bbah These paras must be read with correct pronunciation and vowels (I'rab), with Qira'at and Tajweed, in a pleasant voice and with fluency. After learning the translation of the Holy Quran, students will be able to join its teachings with their practical life. Important Points about the Exam An exam of this part will be taken during the year, and at the annual exam there will be an oral (viva) exam of it. A total of forty percent (40%) marks are fixed for this part. The marks obtained in this part will be entered separately in the result sheet. Passing this part is compulsory for passing the subject of Islamiat. The teacher should teach the students to read the Holy Quran with correct pronunciation. Rights of the Holy Quran The Holy Quran has four great rights upon us: To recite the Holy Quran with Qira'at and Tajweed. To think and ponder over the Holy Quran. Answer: Para 7 to 12

How many paras in total have to be read in the Nazra part?

Answer: Six paras

Para 7 begins with which words?

Answer: Wa Idha Sami'u
