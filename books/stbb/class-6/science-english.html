Sample solved questions

Cells Are Very Small All living things are made of tiny units called cells . Cells are so small that they cannot be seen with the naked eye. That is why scientists use a microscope to see them. The Microscope The objective lens (the lens on the side of the object) makes the object look bigger. Focusing is NOT the work of the objective lens. Focusing is done by the adjustment knobs , which focus the microscope with the help of the objective lens. Building Blocks of Life Cells are called the building blocks or units of life because: All living things are made of cells. The cell is the basic unit of living things. All the processes of life take place inside cells. Chromosomes Chromosomes are present inside cells. They contain the instructions which are transferred from parents to their offspring (children).

Sample MCQs with answers

Why do scientists use a microscope to see cells?

Answer: Cells are so small they cannot be seen with the naked eye

Which part of the microscope makes the object look bigger?

Answer: Objective lens

What is the work of the adjustment knobs of a microscope?

Answer: To focus the microscope

Cells are called the building blocks of life because:

Answer: All living things are made of cells

Where do all the processes of life take place?

Answer: In cells
