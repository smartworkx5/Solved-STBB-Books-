Sample solved questions

Nazira Recitation In Class 4, the first three parts (paras) of the Holy Quran are prescribed for Nazira recitation : Alif Laam Meem , Sayaqool and Tilkal Rusul . One part is to be learnt in three months , and all three parts are completed in the academic year. Out of the total 100 marks in Islamiat, 40 marks have been allocated for the Nazra section. Success in Nazra Quran is compulsory for success in the Islamiat subject. Surahs to Memorize (Hifz) Surah Al-Kausar : إِنَّا أَعْطَيْنَاكَ الْكَوْثَرَ ۝ فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَانْحَرْ ۝ إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْأَبْتَرُ Its simple meaning is: Allah Almighty has given the Nabi Karim (PBUH) Al-Kausar , that is, abundance of goodness. So we should pray to our Lord and offer sacrifice for Him alone. Surely, the enemy of the Prophet (PBUH) is the one who is cut off from all good. Surah Al-Asr : وَالْعَصْرِ ۝ إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ لَفِي خُسْرٍ ۝ إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِ Its simple meaning is: By the time, surely man is in loss, except those who believe , do good deeds , advise one another to follow the truth and advise one another to be patient . Surah Al-Ma'oon : أَرَءَيْتَ الَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِالدِّينِ ۝ فَذَٰلِكَ الَّذِي يَدُعُّ الْيَتِيمَ ۝ وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ الْمِسْكِينِ ۝ فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْمُصَلِّينَ ۝ الَّذِينَ هُمْ عَن صَلَاتِهِمْ سَاهُونَ ۝ الَّذِينَ هُمْ يُرَاءُونَ ۝ وَيَمْنَعُونَ الْمَاعُونَ It tells us about the people who deny the Day of Judgment : they push away the orphan and do not urge feeding the poor. It also warns those who are careless about their Salah, who make a show of their deeds and who refuse to lend even small useful things to others. Surah Al-Kafiroon : قُلْ يَٰٓأَيُّهَا الْكٰفِرُونَ ۝ لَآ أَعْبُدُ مَا تَعْبُدُونَ ۝ وَلَآ أَنتُمْ…

Sample MCQs with answers

  1. Which parts (paras) of the Holy Quran are prescribed for Nazira recitation in Class 4?

    Answer: The first three parts

  2. In how much time is one part (para) to be learnt?

    Answer: In three months

  3. Out of the total 100 marks in Islamiat, how many marks are allocated for the Nazra section?

    Answer: 40 marks

  4. According to Surah Al-Kausar, what has Allah Almighty given to the Nabi Karim (PBUH)?

    Answer: Al-Kausar, that is, abundance of goodness

  5. According to Surah Al-Asr, who is NOT in loss?

    Answer: Those who believe, do good deeds, advise one another to follow the truth and to be patient
