Sample solved questions

Numbers in words and figures We can write a number in figures using digits, or in words using number names. To change words into figures, break the words into parts such as thousands, hundreds, tens and ones, write each part, and then add the parts together. To change figures into words, read the thousands part first, then the hundreds, tens and ones. Worked example Write in figures: Four hundred fifty six thousand and nine hundred twenty six. Step 1: Four hundred fifty six thousand = 456,000. Step 2: Nine hundred twenty six = 926. Step 3: Add the parts: 456,000 + 926 = 456,926. Answer: 456,926. Key points Break number words into thousands, hundreds, tens and ones parts. Fill empty places with zero, for example 729,006 has zeros in the hundreds and tens places. Read figures in groups: thousands first, then the last three digits.
