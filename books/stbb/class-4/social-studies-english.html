Sample solved questions

Map and Globe A globe is a model of the Earth . It is made in the shape of a ball and shows the continents and oceans on it. A map is a drawing of the whole Earth or a part of it, drawn on paper as it looks from above. Map and Globe — the Differences A globe shows the Earth in three dimensions like a ball; a map is flat and two-dimensional . A globe shows the true shape of the Earth; a map can show one country or city in a bigger size. A globe is difficult to carry; a map can be folded and carried easily. Elements of a Map Title — tells what the map shows. The title of the Sindh map in this lesson is Sindh Districts . Legend or key — explains the symbols used in the map, such as the international boundary, the provincial boundary, the district boundary and the River Indus. Compass rose — shows the directions. Scale — helps us find actual distances from the map. Directions The four cardinal directions are North, East, South and West. The four intermediate directions are Northeast, Southeast, Southwest and Northwest. From Shaheed Benazirabad: Naushahro Feroze is in the north, Sanghar in the east, Matiari in the south and Jamshoro in the west. Khairpur is in the northeast, Tando Allahyar in the southeast, Hyderabad in the southwest and Dadu in the northwest. Scale and Distance On the Sindh map, 1 centimetre = 50 kilometres . The map distance between Jamshoro and Sanghar is about 3.5 centimetres, so the actual distance is about 175 kilometres . When a drawing is made twice its size, both its length and width become double: a pencil box 18 cm long and 6 cm wide becomes 36 cm long and 12 cm wide.
