Sample solved questions

Meeting a New Friend Amjad says: Good morning , Abbas. How are you? Abbas replies: Good morning, Amjad. I am fine . Thank you. Zaib is a new boy in class two. Abbas tells Amjad about him. Amjad welcomes Zaib warmly: Hello Zaib. Welcome to class two. Zaib says: Hello Abbas. Nice to meet you . At the end Abbas says: Thank you. We are friends now . Practice Note This is a speaking-practice dialogue. Practise it in groups with your friends, taking the roles of Amjad, Abbas and Zaib. Key Points We say 'Good morning' when we meet someone in the morning. We welcome a new friend with kind and happy words.
