Temperature and the Average Kinetic Energy of Molecules Temperature is a direct measure of the average translational kinetic energy of the molecules, as shown by the relation average K.E = (3/2) kT. When the absolute temperature doubles, the average kinetic energy of the particles also doubles. Temperature therefore rises only when the molecules move faster on average. Kinetic theory gives the average translational kinetic energy of one molecule as (3/2) kT, where k is Boltzmann constant and T is the absolute temperature. Each of the three directions of motion contributes (1/2) kT. The value depends only on temperature and not on the mass or nature of the gas. The root mean square speed is proportional to the square root of the absolute temperature, vrms = sqrt(3 kT / m). If T becomes 3T, vrms becomes sqrt(3) times its original value. It does not become 3 times, because speed depends on the square root of temperature, not on temperature directly. Average K.E of one molecule = (3/2) kT Pressure, Volume and Molecular Speed At constant temperature Boyle law gives P1 V1 = P2 V2, so pressure and volume are inversely proportional. If the volume becomes 2 V1, the pressure becomes P2 = P1 V1 / (2 V1) = P1 / 2. The gas spreads into twice the space, so its molecules strike the walls half as often. Kinetic theory derives P = (1/3) rho times the mean square speed of the molecules, where rho is the density of the gas. Pressure is produced by molecular collisions with the walls of the container. A denser gas, or one whose molecules move faster, therefore exerts a greater pressure. At the same temperature all gases have the same average kinetic energy, so the lighter particles move faster, with speed proportional to 1 / sqrt(molar mass). Sample MCQs with answers

What is the average translational kinetic energy of one molecule of an ideal gas at absolute temperature T?

Answer: (3/2) kT

If the absolute temperature of a gas is doubled, what happens to the average kinetic energy of its molecules?

Answer: It doubles

How much does each single direction of molecular motion contribute to the average kinetic energy?

Answer: (1/2) kT

The absolute temperature of a gas is increased from T to 3T. By what factor does the root mean square speed change?

Answer: sqrt(3) times

At constant temperature the volume of a gas is doubled from V1 to 2V1. What is the new pressure?

Answer: P1/2
