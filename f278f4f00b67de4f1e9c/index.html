How it works

  1. Ask for help — write what you need: a labourer, a driver, an electrician, a tailor… People from your own area see it.
  2. A neighbour accepts — when the work is done, you give them the secret code shown in your app.
  3. Coins move, work done — the helper earns coins and can use them for their own help. No code, no coins — payment only after the work.
